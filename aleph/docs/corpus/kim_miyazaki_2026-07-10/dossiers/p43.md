## Summary
The paper asks how the viscoelastic shear moduli (storage G′ and loss G″) of a crosslinked semiflexible actin network arise from microscopic mechanisms — thermal fluctuations, filament bending/stretching, and crosslinker (ACP) bending/stretching — and how these depend on crosslink density, crosslinker type, and prestrain. The authors build a 3-D **Brownian dynamics (BD)** model in which F-actin is a chain of rigid cylindrical segments with extensional + bending harmonic potentials, and actin-crosslinking proteins (ACPs) are explicit two-armed cylindrical elements with their own stretch + two bending stiffnesses; steric repulsion between cylinders is included. Networks are grown by a first-order irreversible polymerization/crosslinking process (from ref [35]) to 99% monomer incorporation, then coarse-grained (N_C = 10 monomers → one 70 nm cylinder). Two ACP types are used: **ACPᴮ** (bundling, equilibrium crosslink angle 0) and **ACPᶜ** (orthogonal crosslinking, angle π/2). Viscoelasticity is probed two ways: **bulk rheology** (impose oscillatory shear strain on the top boundary, measure wall stress → G′, G″) and **segment-tracking rheology** (track MSD of individual actin segments, convert via a modified Mason–Weitz microrheology formula). Key findings: (i) crosslinker-free networks give G′∝f^0.75 (transverse thermal undulation signature); (ii) orthogonal ACPᶜ raises G′ and flattens its frequency slope (toward the cell-like 0.15–0.3 exponent) far more effectively than bundling ACPᴮ, which percolates poorly; (iii) increasing prestrain γ makes the network dramatically more elastic — at γ=0.55, G′ is ~100× larger and nearly frequency-independent, with G′∝τ₀^0.85 above a threshold prestress τ₀≈0.1 Pa; (iv) three prestrain regimes emerge — low-γ dominated by filament bending (k_b,A) with thermal fluctuations mattering only when persistence length l_p ≲ crosslink spacing l_c; intermediate-γ dominated by ACP bending (k_b,ACP); high-γ dominated by filament + ACP stretching (k_s,A, k_s,ACP); (v) a small percolating "**supportive framework**" (~25% of ACPs, ~28% of filaments, selected by highest ACP bending force) carries ~70% of the stress; (vi) thermal fluctuations are largely unimportant under physiological conditions (l_c ≪ l_p), so a crosslinked F-actin network behaves essentially as a deterministic overdamped elastic solid. Model G′,G″ agree semi-quantitatively with a matched in-vitro filamin/gelsolin bulk-rheometer experiment (overlap 1–10 Hz).

## Model & Methods
- **Model type:** 3-D Brownian dynamics (Langevin, overdamped) agent-based particle/bond network. Explicit actin filaments (chains of rigid cylindrical segments) + explicit two-armed ACP crosslinkers + steric repulsion. This is a **fine-grained mechanistic** model (every filament segment and every crosslinker is an explicit element with bending + stretching potentials) — directly analogous to the FF (Cytosim-style) engine in ffn_cellsim.
- **Integrator:** Euler forward integration of the overdamped Langevin equation (inertia dropped). Nondimensionalized using k_BT, ζ_C,A (segment friction), and L_C,A (segment length) as primary units.
- **Timestep:** Δt = 6.17×10⁻⁹ s (dimensionless 2.0×10⁻⁵).
- **Boundary conditions / geometry:** cubic simulation box, linear dimension **2.8 µm** (<5 µm 3-D domain). Periodic BC during network generation. For bulk rheology, filaments severed at top+bottom faces, periodicity deactivated there; segments within 70 nm of bottom surface held fixed; segments within top 70 nm driven by imposed strain. Filaments longer than the box width (2.8 µm) are severed to reduce finite-size effects.
- **Network construction:** start from uniform G-actin + randomly dispersed ACPs; polymerize + crosslink by a first-order irreversible (Brownian) bonding process to 99% monomer incorporation (model from ref [35]); then coarse-grain (spheres → cylinders, N_C=10). Two "master" networks (one ACPᴮ-bundled, one ACPᶜ-crosslinked); lower-R networks made by randomly deleting active ACPs while holding geometry fixed. No polymerization/depolymerization and **no ACP unbinding** during measurement (permanent crosslinks) — the primary stated limitation.
- **Friction:** anisotropic cylinder friction coefficients (Eq. 6) computed, but the perpendicular value ζ_C,A,⊥ (only 1.64× the parallel ζ_C,A,∥ at L/σ=10) used isotropically for simplicity; hydrodynamic interactions neglected (actin volume fraction ~0.1%, high aspect ratio).
- **Measurement protocols:**
  - *Bulk rheology:* translate top boundary at constant strain rate to set prestrain γ, superpose small 5% sinusoidal strain (differential moduli, as in Gardel 2006 [14]); sum forces on top-attached filament ends / top area = stress; add fluid shear stress τ_zx = η dγ_x/dt (Eq. 9); G′,G″ from stress/strain amplitude ratio and phase (Eq. 10). Linear regime strain kept ≤2% (experiment) / 5% oscillation (sim).
  - *Segment-tracking rheology:* divide domain into N_MSD=512 cells, pick one cylindrical element per cell, record MSD of its center, convert to G* via modified Mason particle-tracking formula (Eq. 11).
- **Compute cost:** ~16 days on an Intel Xeon 2.66 GHz CPU to reach 1 s of simulated time; hence frequency overlap with experiment is narrow (1–10 Hz).
- **In-vitro companion experiment:** AR-G2 (TA Instruments) rheometer, 40 mm parallel-plate; rabbit skeletal-muscle actin (10 mg/ml, −80°C, thaw 37°C); recombinant filamin A (from Sf9 lysates) + recombinant human gelsolin (E. coli). Mixed, loaded <10 s, polymerized 2 hr RT; G′,G″ measured 0.1–10 Hz at strain <2%. Gelsolin used to cap length to ⟨L_f⟩≈1.5 µm to match simulation.

## Governing Equations
1. **(Eq. 1) Full Langevin equation:** m_i d²r_i/dt² = F_i − ζ_i (dr_i/dt − v_∞) + F_i^B.
   *Meaning:* Newton–Langevin for element i (actin segment or ACP): deterministic force F_i, Stokes drag relative to medium velocity v_∞ with friction ζ_i, plus stochastic thermal force F_i^B satisfying the fluctuation–dissipation theorem. Inertia (LHS) is set to zero (overdamped).
2. **(Eq. 2) Overdamped Euler update (nondimensional):** r̃_i(t̃+Δt̃) = r̃_i(t̃) + (1/ζ̃_i)(F̃_i^B + F̃_i) Δt̃ + ṽ_∞ Δt̃.
   *Meaning:* explicit forward-Euler position update; medium velocity v_∞ (set by the imposed shear velocity profile) advects the elements — this is how the applied strain rate couples in.
3. **(Eq. 3) Harmonic stretch + bend potentials:** Ũ_s = ½ k̃_s (r̃₁₂ − r̃₀)² ; Ũ_b = ½ k̃_b (θ − θ₀)².
   *Meaning:* extensional (bond-length) and bending (angle) elastic energies for both actin segments and ACP arms; subscript 0 = equilibrium value.
4. **(Eq. 4) Steric repulsion potential:** Ũ_r = ½ k̃_r [r̃₁₂ − (σ̃_{C,i,1}+σ̃_{C,i,2})/2]² if r̃₁₂ < (σ_{C,i,1}+σ_{C,i,2})/2, else 0.
   *Meaning:* one-sided harmonic (WCA-like) excluded-volume repulsion between two cylinder segments based on minimum inter-cylinder distance r₁₂; only active on overlap. (Analogous to LJ-repulsive EV in ffn_cellsim.)
5. **(Eq. 5) Repulsive-force distribution to segment endpoints:** F̃_{r,α} = F̃_r (L_{C,i} − y)/L_{C,i} ; F̃_{r,β} = F̃_r · y/L_{C,i}.
   *Meaning:* the repulsive force acting at contact point Y on a cylinder is split between its two endpoints α,β by lever-arm fraction y/L (y = distance from α to Y).
6. **(Eq. 6) Cylinder friction coefficients:** ζ_{C,i,⊥} = 3πησ_{C,i}·(3 + 2L_{C,i}/σ_{C,i})/5 ; ζ_{C,i,∥} = 3πησ_{C,i}·(4 + L_{C,i}/σ_{C,i})/5.
   *Meaning:* perpendicular and parallel translational drag for a cylinder of length L and diameter σ in a medium of viscosity η (from Clift/Grace/Weber [50]). At L/σ=10, ⊥ is 1.64× ∥; ⊥ used isotropically.
7. **(Eq. 7) ACPᴮ (bundling) equilibrium geometry + stiffnesses:** L̃_{C,ACP}=0.7, σ̃_{C,ACP}=0.143, θ₁,eq=π, θ₂,eq=π/2; k̃_s,ACP=5000, k̃_b,ACP,1=250, k̃_b,ACP,2=1000.
   *Meaning:* dimensionless equilibrium arm geometry and stiffness set for the bundling crosslinker; θ₁ = angle between the two ACP arms (π ⇒ two crosslinked filaments parallel, angle 0 between them ⇒ bundle).
8. **(Eq. 8) ACPᶜ (orthogonal) equilibrium geometry + stiffnesses:** L̃_{C,ACP}=1.45, σ̃_{C,ACP}=0.143, θ₁,eq=0.417π, θ₂,eq=π/2; k̃_s,ACP=5000, k̃_b,ACP,1=250, k̃_b,ACP,2=1000.
   *Meaning:* same for the orthogonal crosslinker; θ₁,eq=0.417π chosen to keep 70 nm equilibrium spacing between the two crosslinked filaments; arm longer than ACPᴮ. k_b,ACP,1 acts between the two arms; k_b,ACP,2 acts between an arm and the actin axis (eq. π/2).
9. **(Eq. 9) Added fluid shear stress:** τ_zx = η dγ_x/dt.
   *Meaning:* on top of filament-borne stress, add the viscous stress of the medium assuming an instantaneous linear velocity profile (water-diffusion time ~10 µs ≪ oscillation period).
10. **(Eq. 10) Moduli from bulk rheology:** |G*(f_s)| = |τ_zx|/|γ_x| ; G′(f_s) = |G*| cos φ ; G″(f_s) = |G*| sin φ.
    *Meaning:* complex modulus magnitude = stress-amplitude/strain-amplitude; φ = stress–strain phase delay (φ=0 perfectly elastic, φ=π/2 perfectly viscous). tan⁻¹(G″/G′)=φ is the "phase delay."
11. **(Eq. 11) Moduli from segment-tracking (modified Mason microrheology):** |G*(ω)| ≈ k_BT / [π r_b ⟨Δr²(1/ω)⟩ Γ(1+χ(ω))] ; G′(ω)=|G*|cos(πχ(ω)/2) ; G″(ω)=|G*|sin(πχ(ω)/2).
    *Meaning:* generalized Stokes–Einstein microrheology, with r_b the *effective* actin-segment radius satisfying ζ_{C,A,⊥}=6πηr_b, Γ the gamma function, and χ(ω)=d ln⟨Δr²(t)⟩/d ln t |_{t=1/ω} the local log-slope (power-law exponent) of the MSD. Modified from spherical bead to cylindrical segment.

## Force Laws & Potentials
- **Extensional (stretch):** harmonic, F = −k_s (r₁₂ − r₀). k_s,A (actin) = 1.69×10⁻² N/m; k_s,ACP = 4.23×10⁻³ N/m (= 1/40 of actin's, for compute efficiency). Also tested k_s,A = 3.38×10⁻³, 6.76×10⁻⁴ (=0.0068) N/m.
- **Bending:** harmonic in angle, F ∝ −k_b (θ − θ₀). Actin k_b,A = 1.056×10⁻¹⁸ N·m (also 1.056×10⁻¹⁹, 1.056×10⁻²⁰ tested). ACP two bending stiffnesses: k_b,ACP,1 = 1.04×10⁻¹⁸ N·m (arm–arm) and k_b,ACP,2 = 4.14×10⁻¹⁸ N·m (arm–filament axis).
- **Steric repulsion:** one-sided harmonic (Eq. 4), strength k_r = 1.69×10⁻³ N/m; acts only when cylinder minimum distance < mean radius sum; force split to endpoints by lever rule (Eq. 5).
- **Thermal (Langevin) force:** F_i^B Gaussian white noise obeying fluctuation–dissipation with drag ζ_i and T=300 K. Turning it off drops effective temperature to ~10 K (residual from forced oscillation).
- **Torsional force:** a torsional-angle stiffness existed in the generation model [35] but is **neglected** in the dynamics here (the two bending forces already preclude free torsional rotation).
- **No motor (myosin) force, no bond off-rate:** crosslinks are permanent (no Bell/catch unbinding), no active contraction — this is a *passive* crosslinked network.

## Relevance flags
- **DCM?** Weak/indirect. This is a fine-grained filament model, not a surface-mesh continuum. But it supplies the *material law* that a cortex/cytoskeleton continuum would need: crosslinked-actin G′,G″ magnitudes (~0.1–100 Pa passive, ~10³ Pa prestressed), the prestress-stiffening exponent (G′∝τ₀^0.85), and the emergent "supportive framework" idea (a few percolating elements carry the load) — useful as a sanity target for DCM cortical-tension/stiffness values but not a direct engine input.
- **FF?** **Yes — strongly.** This is the archetypal FF-engine physics: explicit actin filaments as bending+stretching cylinder chains, explicit two-armed crosslinkers with their own bending/stretch stiffnesses, harmonic steric repulsion (EV), overdamped Langevin/BD with fluctuation–dissipation thermal force, and the exact parameter set (k_s,A=1.69e-2 N/m, k_b,A=1.056e-18 N·m ⇒ l_p~10–20 µm, k_s/k_b for ACPs, N_C=10 coarse-grain, box 2.8 µm). It's the direct methodological ancestor of Cytosim-style Kim-lab actin BD. Directly informs FF crosslinker stiffness, EV potential form, and the bulk-rheology (oscillatory shear) + segment-tracking (MSD microrheology) validation protocols.
- **TAG-validation?** **Yes.** Rich quantitative validation targets: G′∝f^0.75 (no crosslink), G′∝f^0.3 (crosslinked, cell-like 0.15–0.3), G′ ~100× stiffening at γ=0.55, threshold prestress ~0.1 Pa, G′∝τ₀^0.85, supportive-framework (25% ACP → 70% stress), l_c=0.393 µm vs l_p transition for thermal-fluctuation relevance, and the permanent-crosslink stress-catastrophe caveat (~1–30 Pa). All are candidate acceptance-oracle values for FF network-rheology tests. Note: k_s,A here is deliberately 1/40 of the real actin value for compute efficiency — do NOT KB-register 1.69e-2 N/m as the physiological actin stretch stiffness without that ÷40 caveat.
