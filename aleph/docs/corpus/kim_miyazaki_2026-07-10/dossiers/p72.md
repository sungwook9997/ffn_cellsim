## Summary
The authors ask whether heterogeneous (two-component) de novo protein assemblies can be engineered into dynamic, reversible tubular structures that recapitulate the flexibility and stimulus-responsiveness of natural cytoskeletal filaments. Their design fuses a natural heterodimeric coiled-coil "heterolinker" pair — MBD3L2 (M3L2) and p66α (from the NuRD complex, dimer Tm ≈ 35 °C, Kd ≈ 268 nM) — to the four C-terminal vertices of a C4-symmetric homotetrameric scaffold, *Pseudomonas fluorescens* PuuE allantoinase (PDB 3CL6), producing two units: PuuE-M (flexible M3L2 orientation) and PuuE-p (rigid p66α orientation). The rigid angular interface biases assembly toward *closed* structures (rings/tubes) rather than sheets. Mixing the two units (each 250 nM–12.5 µM) in 50–200 mM NaCl at 30–40 °C for hours-to-days produces "chessboard-patterned" PuuE nanotubes several µm long (up to ~5 µm at 48 h), verified by nsTEM and cryo-EM. Cryo-EM 2D/3D reconstruction reveals a *microtubule-like diversity* of tube diameters/symmetries (C4 29.2 nm, C5 34.2 nm, C6 40.0 nm, with C7–C10 at lower resolution). TIRFM of Alexa488-labeled tubes shows thermally driven 2D bending; worm-like-chain fitting gives a persistence length Lp = 19.7 µm — comparable to actin filaments (measured here 12.5 µm; literature 9–20 µm), far below microtubules (0.1–10 mm) and far above intermediate filaments (<1 µm). Assembly is reversible: salt exchange (100→0→100 mM NaCl) disassembles and reassembles tubes. Grafting the actin D-loop onto PuuE-M ("PuuE(D-loop)-M") introduces actin-like *helical* double/triple intertwined tubes with a thinner C3-symmetric (23.6 nm) building block, and this helical form is temperature-reversible (unwinds on ice, reforms at 30 °C). The work is a proof-of-concept that artificial two-component scaffolds can biomimetically reproduce cytoskeletal flexibility, reversibility, and morphological diversity.

## Model & Methods
- **Model type**: EXPERIMENTAL. No computational dynamics model. Components: (1) rational/computational protein design (scaffold + heterolinker fusion, AlphaFold2/AlphaFold-Multimer prediction), (2) in-vitro self-assembly reconstitution, (3) structural biology (nsTEM + cryo-EM single-particle/helical reconstruction), (4) single-filament biophysics (TIRFM + worm-like-chain persistence-length fit).
- **Scaffold**: PuuE allantoinase homotetramer, C4 symmetry, ~5 nm (side) × ~10 nm dimensions, one C-terminus at each vertex (PDB 3CL6).
- **Heterolinker**: anti-parallel coiled-coil heterodimer M3L2 / p66α (from M3L2-NuRD complex); Tm ≈ 35 °C, Kd ≈ 268 nM.
- **Constructs**: N-6xHis-TEVcs–PuuE–M3L2 (PuuE-M); N-6xHis-TEVcs–PuuE–p66α (PuuE-p); N-6xHis-TEVcs–PuuE(D-loop)–M3L2 (PuuE(D-loop)-M, actin D-loop grafted into the back-side loop of PuuE).
- **Expression/purification**: *E. coli* BL21(DE3) + pGro7 chaperone; IPTG induction 0.1 mM, 16 °C, 16–20 h; HisTrap FF Ni-affinity (imidazole gradient 20–400 mM); dialysis into NaCl(+) (50 mM Tris pH 8.0, 100 mM NaCl, 0.5 mM EDTA) or NaCl(–) (0 mM NaCl) buffer; conc. by absorbance at 280 nm.
- **Assembly protocol**: mix PuuE-M + PuuE-p at equal (tetramer-equivalent) concentration in a 200 µL volume, ThermoMixer/orbital shaking 300 rpm at target temperature; standard optimum = 100 mM NaCl, 40 °C, 24 h (PuuE tube) or 30 °C (D-loop tube).
- **nsTEM**: 2% uranyl acetate stain, Hitachi HT-7700, 80 kV.
- **Cryo-EM**: Glacios, 200 kV, Falcon4EC; pixel 1.22 Å (PuuE tube, 120,000×) / 0.925 Å (D-loop tube, 150,000×); RELION 5.0beta + cryoSPARC 4.1.1; CTFFIND4; helical refinement from featureless hollow-cylinder reference (avoid reference bias); 3D classification with symmetry search (C1) then refinement imposing C4/C5/C6 (and C3 for D-loop). 4346 movies / 709,722 segments (PuuE tube); 4871 movies / 126,987 segments (D-loop tube, 25 °C). EMDB: EMD-60617/60618/60619 (PuuE C4/C5/C6); EMD-60620/60621/60622/60623 (D-loop C3/C4/C5/C6).
- **TIRFM**: Alexa Fluor 488 succinimidyl-ester labeling (0.7 mM); methylcellulose (1500 cP, 0.2% w/v) depletion to constrain tubes to the evanescent field; IX-71 Olympus, 60× NA1.45 oil, iXon3 EMCCD, 488 nm laser; frames every 40 ms; 25 ± 1 °C. Skeletonization via ImageJ JFilament v1.02; contour length L and end-to-end distance R measured per frame with custom Python (3.10.12).
- **Molecular modelling**: AlphaFold 2.2/2.3 multimer (DeepMind); pLDDT/PAE via af2visualizer; cartoon rendering PyMOL 2.5 / ChimeraX 1.7; isoelectric points + APBS electrostatics + PDB2PQR; surface hydrophobicity via Color_h (Eisenberg scale).
- **BCs / geometry / integrator / timestep**: NOT APPLICABLE — no simulation dynamics. The only quantitative physical model is the equilibrium worm-like-chain relation fit to TIRFM data (below).

## Governing Equations
1. **Worm-like-chain (Kratky-Porod) mean-square end-to-end distance for a thermally fluctuating semiflexible 2D polymer** — Eq. (1):
   ⟨R²⟩ = 4·Lp²·[ 2·exp(−L / 2Lp) − 2 + L / Lp ]
   *Meaning*: relates the ensemble mean-square end-to-end distance ⟨R²⟩ to contour length L and persistence length Lp for a thermally driven (2D-confined) semiflexible filament; fit by nonlinear least squares (scipy.optimize.curve_fit) to extract Lp. (This is the SAME persistence-length physics FF uses for actin/tube bending stiffness κ = Lp·kB·T.)

No other governing equations (no force law, motor law, or bond-kinetics equation) appear in the paper.

## Force Laws & Potentials
- None explicit. Assembly is driven by (implicit, not parameterized) M3L2/p66α coiled-coil binding (electrostatic + hydrophobic), tuned by NaCl electrostatic screening and by temperature relative to the dimer Tm. No functional force form, potential, or rate constant is written. The persistence length Lp is the only mechanical parameter, related to bending rigidity via κ = Lp·kB·T (standard WLC, implied not stated).
