## Summary
The paper asks how **covalent cross-linking** of basement-membrane (BM)-like matrices affects the ability of invasive breast cancer cells (MDA-MB-231) to extend invadopodia-like protrusions, independent of proteases. Experimentally, two biomaterial systems are used: (1) reconstituted BM (rBM/Matrigel, 8 mg/mL) covalently cross-linked with tissue transglutaminase (tTg, up to 500 µg/mL), and (2) rBM–alginate interpenetrating networks (IPNs) with bio-orthogonal tetrazine–norbornene click cross-links tuned to tumor-like stiffness (~2 kPa). Rheology + creep–recovery tests show covalent cross-linking **reduces matrix mechanical plasticity** (permanent strain: rBM ~80% → rBM+tTg ~10%; HP IPN ~30% → LP-CC IPN <10%) **without significantly changing stiffness** (Young's modulus ~100 Pa for rBM, ~2 kPa for IPN). In low-plasticity, cross-linked matrices cells become rounded (high circularity), spread less, and lose actin–cortactin colocalization; protease inhibition (GM6001 or a broad cocktail) does not rescue, so the effect is **protease-independent and physical**. An **agent-based Brownian-dynamics computational model** by Kim — fibers + force-dependent (Bell's-law) cross-linkers + a simplified serially-connected cell membrane exerting protrusive force — reproduces both the creep/plasticity behavior and the cell-shape phenotypes purely mechanically: lowering the cross-linker zero-force unbinding rate `k0,u` and/or the force sensitivity `λu` lowers matrix plasticity, and in low-plasticity matrix the modeled cell makes short blunt protrusions with ~10% max / ~0% permanent strain vs. long thin protrusions with ~40% max / ~30% permanent strain in high-plasticity matrix. Conclusion: reduced mechanical plasticity from covalent cross-linking is *sufficient* to physically block invasive protrusions, no mechanotransduction/signaling needed.

## Model & Methods
**Model type:** Agent-based **Brownian dynamics** of a coarse-grained cross-linked fiber network (Taeyoon Kim's canonical actin/ECM cytoskeleton framework, here applied to ECM), with an added simplified cell membrane. Fibers and cross-linkers are represented by **cylindrical elements**; the membrane is a chain of serially connected rectangular-solid (wall) elements.

**Governing dynamics:** Motions of all cylindrical (fiber, cross-linker) and membrane elements are regulated by the **Langevin equation** (over-damped Brownian dynamics). Bending and extensional (spring) forces maintain equilibrium angles and equilibrium lengths of the cylindrical elements.

**Fiber assembly:** A fiber nucleates from one cylindrical element of length **1.4 µm**, elongated by adding identical cylindrical elements; the max number of elements addable to the first is capped at **two**, giving average fiber length **~4 µm** (deliberately short fibers).

**Cross-linkers:** Bind to sites spaced every **140 nm** along fibers; unbind force-dependently by **Bell's law** (Eq. 1). Reference kinetics `k*0,u = 3×10⁻⁶ s⁻¹`, `λ*u = 1.0×10⁻¹⁰ m (=100 pm)`. Only **tensile** spring force increases the off-rate above baseline (a slip bond; under compression `ku = k0,u`).

**Creep-test setup:** 3D domain **40 × 40 × 1 µm**, periodic BC in x and y. A cross-linked network is pre-assembled by dynamic fiber/cross-linker events, then loaded. Chains crossing the y-boundaries are severed and clamped to the boundary. The **top boundary is displaced in +x** (shear); bottom boundary fixed. Actual stress = Σ(forces on clamped chains)/(top-boundary area); top-boundary position is feedback-adjusted to match a goal shear stress. Goal stress = **100 Pa for the first hour** (creep), then **0 Pa** (recovery/plastic evaluation). Initial modulus and permanent strain computed as in the experiments.

**Cell-invasion setup:** Domain **100 × 60 × 1 µm**. Membrane = serially connected rectangular solid elements of height = domain z-width (1 µm), forming a wall separating intracellular/extracellular space. Initial **circular membrane radius = 10 µm** at domain center; **158 membrane elements**, initial element length **400 nm**. Membrane elements carry bending forces + extensional forces preventing them being **too long (>800 nm)** or **too short (<4 nm)**. Protrusive force: **25 pN applied radially outward to 40 membrane nodes** on the right side for the first hour (they converge to concentrate force, giving an effective **total force ~1 nN** — the paper describes the invasion runs as "total force of 1 nN"). Volume-exclusion/repulsive forces between membrane and matrix let the membrane push and deform the matrix without crossing it. After 1 h protrusive force is removed; recovery/plastic deformation evaluated.

**Integrator/timestep:** Brownian/Langevin dynamics; explicit timestep not given in the main text (in Supplementary Table 2 / supplementary material, not in this file).

**Compute:** Ran on the Comet supercomputer at SDSC via XSEDE.

**Experimental methods (Chaudhuri lab):**
- Rheology: AR2000EX stress-controlled rheometer, 25 mm plate, G′/G″ at **0.15 Hz, 1% strain**; mineral oil seal.
- Creep-recovery: constant shear stress **10 Pa (rBM)** or **100 Pa (IPN)** for 1 h, then unload (0 Pa), ~2 h recovery.
- rBM: growth-factor-reduced Matrigel diluted to **8 mg/mL**; tTg (guinea-pig liver, 2.2 UN/mg) activated with 2 mM DTT + 5 mM CaCl₂.
- IPN: **4.4 mg/mL rBM + 10 mg/mL alginate**; HP uses low-MW (35 kDa) alginate, LP-CC uses high-MW (265–280 kDa) tetrazine/norbornene click alginate; ionic Ca²⁺ cross-linking (CaSO₄).
- Cells: MDA-MB-231, **0.5×10⁶ cells/mL**, serum-starved, EGF-stimulated (**50 ng/mL EGF**, 10% FBS), 24 h.
- Protease inhibition: **10 µM GM6001**, or cocktail (**20 µM Marimastat, 20 µM Pepstatin A, 20 µM E-64, 0.7 µM Aprotinin, 2 µM Leupeptin**).
- IHC: anti-paxillin, anti-β1-integrin, anti-cortactin (1:300); Alexa-488 phalloidin; DAPI; Leica 63×/1.4 NA.
- Image analysis: ImageJ, **circularity = 4π·area/perimeter²**.

## Governing Equations

**(1) Bell's law — cross-linker force-dependent unbinding rate:**
```
        ⎧ k0,u · exp( λu · |F_s,x1| / (kB·T) )   if r ≥ r0,x1   (tensile)
  ku =  ⎨
        ⎩ k0,u                                    if r <  r0,x1   (compressive)
```
Meaning: cross-linker off-rate rises exponentially with tensile spring force; a slip bond. `k0,u` = zero-force unbinding rate, `λu` = force sensitivity (length), `kB·T` = thermal energy, `F_s,x1` = spring force on the cross-linker element, `r0,x1` = equilibrium length of the cross-linker element. Reference: `k*0,u = 3×10⁻⁶ s⁻¹`, `λ*u = 1.0×10⁻¹⁰ m`.

**(2) Matrix strain around the modeled cell:**
```
  ε = (d − r0,m) / (2 · r0,m)
```
Meaning: matrix deformation metric. `d` = distance from membrane center to the *farthest* of 120 nearest fiber nodes sampled at 3° angular resolution (these 120 nodes delineate the matrix boundary around the cell); `r0,m` = initial membrane radius (10 µm).

**(3) Elastic (Young's) modulus from complex shear modulus (experimental):**
```
  E = 2(1 + ν) · G*,   with ν = 0.5  (incompressible) → E = 3G*
  G* = ( G'² + G''² )^(1/2)
```
Meaning: converts measured storage/loss shear moduli to Young's modulus assuming Poisson's ratio 0.5.

**(4) Cell circularity (experimental morphology metric):**
```
  circularity = 4π · area / perimeter²
```
Meaning: 1 = perfect circle; low values = elongated/protrusive cells.

## Force Laws & Potentials
- **Cross-linker unbinding:** Bell slip-bond, Eq. (1) above. Off-rate only elevated under tension.
- **Bending forces:** harmonic angular restoring forces maintaining equilibrium angles between consecutive cylindrical elements (fibers, cross-linkers, and membrane elements). (Functional constants in Supplementary Table 2, not in this file.)
- **Extensional (stretching) forces:** harmonic spring forces maintaining equilibrium lengths of cylindrical elements; membrane elements additionally constrained to length window 4 nm–800 nm.
- **Cross-linker binding:** stochastic binding to discrete sites every 140 nm along fibers.
- **Volume exclusion / repulsion:** repulsive membrane–matrix interaction so membrane and matrix elements do not interpenetrate; enables protrusive pushing/deformation.
- **Protrusive force:** constant 25 pN per node, radially outward, on 40 right-side membrane nodes for 1 h (effective total ~1 nN).
- Thermal (Langevin) random force + drag on every element.

## Relevance flags
- **DCM?** Partial-yes. This is fundamentally an FF/ECM-network model, but the **simplified serially-connected membrane wall exerting radial protrusive force + volume-exclusion against a matrix** is conceptually adjacent to DCM's membrane/node-face contact against an environment; the strain metric ε=(d−r0,m)/2r0,m and the circularity readout are useful cell-shape observables.
- **FF?** **Yes, strongly.** This is a Taeyoon-Kim agent-based Brownian-dynamics cross-linked fiber network with **Bell's-law slip-bond cross-linkers** (`k0,u`, `λu`), exactly the FF crosslinker/off-rate mechanism. Reference constants (`k*0,u=3×10⁻⁶ s⁻¹`, `λ*u=100 pm`, binding sites every 140 nm, ~4 µm fibers, protrusion 25 pN×40 nodes ≈ 1 nN) are directly relevant to FF's ECM/collagen-Mikado + protrusion-into-ECM work and to matrix mechanical-plasticity emergence. Directly maps to the FF crawl-on-substrate / protrusion-into-collagen line.
- **TAG-validation?** **Yes.** Quantitative targets: matrix plasticity (permanent strain 80%→10% rBM; 30%→<10% IPN), stiffness invariance (~100 Pa, ~2 kPa), model creep permanent-strain/modulus curves, and invasion max/permanent strains (HP ~40%/~30%, LP ~10%/~0%) are clean validation anchors for an FF ECM-plasticity + protrusion module and for a Bell-law crosslinker off-rate parameterization (KB candidate: crosslinker k_off0 and force sensitivity λ).
