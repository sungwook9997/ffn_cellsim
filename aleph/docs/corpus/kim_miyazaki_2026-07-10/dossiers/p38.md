## Summary
The paper asks how the **geometry, density, and type** of actin-nucleation sites on a membrane control the architecture and growth of self-assembling actin networks. The authors build an in-vitro reconstitution platform: a photopolymerized DiynePC lipid-bilayer template with 10 µm × 10 µm (and other sizes/shapes) empty regions, into which laterally **fluid POPC bilayers doped with 2% Ni-lipid** are incorporated by SUV fusion. His-tagged nucleation-promoting factor (His-VVCA, the VVCA domain of mouse N-WASP) or His-tagged formin nucleation domain (His-mDia1ΔN, FH1-FH2) is anchored on the fluid patches via His–Ni affinity, then a purified actin mixture is added. With **VVCA + Arp2/3** the network grows as dense **pillars perpendicular** to the membrane (barbed-end-out, filaments push against the membrane); pillar cross-section copies the pattern shape/size; pillar length and actin density scale with pattern size and with VVCA density (tuned by Ni-lipid % over an order of magnitude, ~10 000–30 000 molecules/µm² — matching cellular NPF densities). With **mDia1ΔN + fascin** the network instead forms **vortex-like bundled** networks growing **horizontally along the pattern contour** (filopodia-like). The platform also works with crude **Xenopus egg extracts** by patterning **PI(4,5)P₂**-containing bilayers, producing finger-like networks and recapitulating the physiological cascade from lipid-signaling (step i) onward. Key methodological advance over prior PEG-templated systems: both interior and exterior of nucleation zones are lipid bilayer (negligible 4 nm/4 nm height gap, energetically stabilized edges, no nonspecific adsorption), giving biologically relevant boundary conditions and controllable NPF lateral diffusion.

## Model & Methods
- **Model type:** Experimental **in-vitro reconstitution** — purified protein mixtures + crude cytoplasmic (Xenopus egg) extracts on a micropatterned biomimetic supported lipid bilayer. No mathematical/simulation model; no integrator/timestep. Observables are optical (confocal 3D reconstruction, TIRF, pyrene bulk-polymerization assay).
- **Membrane template fabrication:** Photopolymerized DiynePC lipid bilayer on a coverslip, patterned by photolithography (UV irradiation) into arrays of empty regions (Morigaki lab method, refs 32–34). Polymerized DiynePC has intrinsic UV/vis excitation/emission for QC (Figure S1). Observation chamber = 1 mm thick PDMS sheet with 4 mm diameter holes on the coverslip.
- **Fluid-bilayer incorporation:** Small unilamellar vesicles (SUVs) of POPC + 2% (mol/mol) Ni-lipid spontaneously spread/fuse into the empty regions; unfused SUVs washed out. Result = laterally mobile fluid bilayer patches embedded in a continuous polymerized-bilayer frame (both are 4 nm tall → negligible boundary height gap).
- **NPF/nucleator anchoring:** His-tagged protein (His-VVCA from mouse N-WASP; or His-mDia1ΔN, the C-terminal FH1-FH2 nucleation domain of mouse diaphanous-1) binds Ni-lipid via His–Ni²⁺ affinity; unbound protein washed out. Density controlled by Ni-lipid mol% (0.66/2/6%). His-VVCA remains laterally mobile (FRAP, Movie S2, Fig S3), retained ≥60 min (Fig S4), and is excluded from pillar interiors (Fig S5).
- **Nucleation trigger (purified, Arp2/3 route):** actin mixture = 5 µM monomeric actin, 50 nM Arp2/3 complex, 15 µM profilin, 25 nM capping protein (CP). Profilin suppresses spontaneous bulk nucleation so nucleation is membrane-restricted.
- **Nucleation trigger (purified, formin route):** actin mixture = 5 µM actin, 15 µM profilin, 50 nM fascin (cross-linker/bundler), with His-mDia1ΔN anchored.
- **Extract route:** Xenopus egg extracts supplied to PI(4,5)P₂-containing patterned fluid bilayers (POPC/POPS carrier); Alexa647-actin added to 5 µM final to visualize.
- **Two-color pulse-chase:** pillar grown with TMR-actin, then Alexa647-actin added → maps where new subunits incorporate (barbed-end location).
- **Geometry sweep:** square patterns of side 2, 4, 8, 16 µm; shapes (circle, octagon, hexagon, pentagon, square, triangle, cross, star, concave) each of equal area = 100 µm².
- **Readouts:** 3D confocal reconstruction (pillar morphology, cross-sections at z = 0 and z = 15 µm), pyrene-actin bulk polymerization kinetics, fluorescence-intensity (FI) quantification of anchored NPF and of actin in pillars; statistics by Mann–Whitney U test.

## Governing Equations
No governing physics equations are presented (experimental paper). The only quantitative relation is an **empirical calibration fit**, transcribed below.

1. **His-VVCA density vs Ni-lipid fraction (empirical linear fit, Fig 3b):**
   `FI_VVCA = 157.7·x − 2.32`,  R² = 0.88,
   where `x` = % Ni-lipid (mol/mol) and `FI_VVCA` = fluorescence intensity ∝ His-VVCA surface density. Meaning: anchored NPF density is linearly proportional to Ni-lipid mole fraction over ~1 order of magnitude.

2. **Maximum NPF surface-density estimate (derivation stated in text, not boxed):**
   Using area per POPC molecule `a_POPC = 0.64 nm²` (König 1997, ref 39), the maximum His-VVCA densities were estimated as ≈ **10 000 molecules/µm²** (0.66% Ni-lipid) and ≈ **30 000 molecules/µm²** (2% Ni-lipid). (Implied: molecules/µm² ≈ (Ni-lipid fraction)·(1/a_POPC)·10⁶ nm²/µm² × occupancy, i.e. at 2%, 0.02/0.64 nm² ≈ 0.031 nm⁻² = 31 000 µm⁻².)

## Force Laws & Potentials
No explicit force laws. The mechanistic force concept invoked qualitatively:
- **Polymerization (barbed-end) pushing force:** new actin-monomer addition at barbed ends localized near the VVCA area and pointing toward the bilayer generates a pushing force that elongates pillars perpendicularly from the membrane (Brownian-ratchet-type, invoked by analogy to lamellipodia; refs 26, 38). No functional form given.
- **Tethering vs pushing (cited from Boujemaa-Paterski 2017, ref 26):** NPF density modulates (i) polymerization force pushing the network away from the NPF area and (ii) tethering that pulls the network toward the NPF area — two competing effects, stated qualitatively.
