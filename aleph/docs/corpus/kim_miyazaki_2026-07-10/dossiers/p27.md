## Summary
The paper asks how the sparse, dynamically remodeling homeostatic cortical actin array of plant (Arabidopsis hypocotyl) epidermal cells reaches a dynamic steady state characterized not by treadmilling but by "stochastic dynamics" (de novo + branched nucleation, rapid barbed-end growth, slow pointed-end depolymerization, prolific severing, and capping). The authors build a 3D agent-based Brownian-dynamics model in which actin filaments and the Arp2/3 complex are serially connected cylindrical segments whose endpoints obey an inertia-neglected Langevin equation, with harmonic extensional/bending/torsional/repulsive (volume-exclusion) potentials. Onto this mechanics they layer stochastic reactions: profilin binding/unbinding of monomers, de novo nucleation (profilin-free monomers only), barbed-end polymerization (both monomer states), ATP→ADP aging, pointed-end depolymerization (ADP only), angle-dependent severing (k_sev = k0,sev·exp(λsev·θ)), barbed-end capping/uncapping, Arp2/3 side-branch nucleation at 70°, and processive-formin-enhanced (3×) polymerization. In a 20×20×1 µm periodic domain over 2000 s, a single "reference condition" reproduces experimental observables within ~2-fold: polymerization ~2.2 µm/s, depolymerization ~0.2 µm/s, filament length ~9.9 µm, severing ~0.01 events/µm/s, nucleation ~1.2×10⁻⁴ events/µm²/s, and ~99% profilin-bound monomers. Parameter sweeps show severing and capping are essential for turnover (their inhibition recapitulates the PTI/immune-response filament-abundance increase and depletes the monomer pool), Arp2/3 and formin cooperatively set filament density, and — reconciling a discrepancy with in-vivo data — enhanced de novo nucleation upon dual Arp2/3+formin inhibition only appears in the model if profilin activity is additionally down-regulated (raising k−,prof to free monomers), predicting profilin as the key regulator of compensatory nucleation.

## Model & Methods
- **Model type:** 3D agent-based / coarse-grained **Brownian dynamics** (overdamped Langevin, inertia neglected) with stochastic Monte-Carlo-style reaction events (nucleation, polymerization, depolymerization, severing, capping, branching, state transitions). Actin filaments = serially connected cylindrical segments with polarity (barbed vs pointed end); Arp2/3 = two cylindrical segments joined at a center point.
- **Integrator:** explicit **Euler** update of positions from velocities (Eq. 4).
- **Timestep:** Δt = 1.15×10⁻⁵ s.
- **Simulated duration:** 2000 s per run; steady-state analysis uses data from 1000 s onward. Data averaged over **5 independent simulations** per condition.
- **Domain / BCs:** 3D box **20 × 20 × 1 µm** (thin z because the central vacuole occupies up to ~90% of plant cell volume). **Periodic boundary conditions in x and y**; the two z-normal walls exert repulsive forces on out-of-domain elements. Reference actin concentration C_A = 1 µM (deliberately low for computational efficiency; real plant ~40–50 µM — noted as a limitation).
- **Initialization:** because k_n,A is very low (de novo nucleation is rare), 10 filament seeds (one actin segment each) are randomly placed at t=0 to reach steady state faster.
- **Reference condition:** C_A = 1 µM, R_Arp2/3 = 0.00007; all other parameters per Table 2. Swept parameters: k0,sev, k+,cap, k+,form, k−,prof, R_Arp2/3.
- **Analysis:** counts of actin in 5 states sampled once/s (profilin-bound monomer, profilin-free monomer, formin-elongated filament, Arp2/3 filament, de novo filament). Polymerization & depolymerization rates from average-length change over 100-s intervals (only free/uncapped barbed ends for poly). Severing rate = (#events per 100-s interval)/(avg length × interval), events/µm/s.
- **Visualization:** VMD (Visual Molecular Dynamics, UIUC); filaments colored by origin (de novo/Arp2/3/formin-elongated), Arp2/3 as small green spheres.
- **Code:** public at github.com/ktyman2/plantactin.
- **Experimental support:** variable-angle epifluorescence microscopy (VAEM) of dark-grown Arabidopsis hypocotyl epidermal cells expressing GFP-fABD2 reporter; original timelapse data from Xu et al. 2024.

## Governing Equations
1. **(Eq. 1) Overdamped Langevin (force balance, inertia neglected):**
   F_i − ζ_i·(dr_i/dt) + F_iᵀ = 0
   — deterministic force + drag + thermal force sum to zero at endpoint i.
2. **(Eq. 2) Drag coefficient of a cylindrical segment (Clift et al. 1978 approximation):**
   ζ_i = 3πμ·r_c,i · (3 + 2·r0,i/r_c,i) / 5
   — μ = medium viscosity, r0,i = segment length, r_c,i = segment diameter.
3. **(Eq. 3) Fluctuation–dissipation (thermal force correlation; Underhill & Doyle 2004):**
   ⟨F_iᵀ(t)·F_jᵀ(t)⟩ = (2·k_B·T·ζ_i·δ_ij / Δt) · δ
   — δ_ij Kronecker delta, δ second-order tensor, Δt timestep.
4. **(Eq. 4) Euler position update:**
   r_i(t+Δt) = r_i(t) + (dr_i/dt)·Δt = r_i(t) + (1/ζ_i)·(F_i + F_iᵀ)·Δt
5. **(Eq. 5) Extensional harmonic potential:** U_s = ½·κ_s·(r − r0)²
6. **(Eq. 6) Bending harmonic potential:** U_b = ½·κ_b·(θ − θ0)²
7. **(Eq. 7) Torsional harmonic potential:** U_t = ½·κ_t·(ϕ − ϕ0)²
8. **(Eq. 8) Repulsive volume-exclusion potential (Kim et al. 2009):**
   U_r,A = ½·κ_r·(r₁₂ − r_c,A)²  if r₁₂ < r_c,A;  = 0 if r₁₂ ≥ r_c,A
   — r₁₂ = minimum distance between two neighboring actin segments; acts only on overlap.
9. **(Eq. 9) Angle-dependent severing rate:** k_sev = k0,sev·exp(λ_sev·θ)
   — k0,sev zero-angle severing rate constant, λ_sev angle sensitivity, θ local bending angle. k0,sev extremely low, λ_sev high, so thermal-fluctuation θ alone barely severs; crosslinkers/motors that bend filaments (e.g., myosin XI buckling) would sharply raise k_sev. Only k0,sev is varied here.

**Reaction-kinetics rules (non-equation but mechanistic):**
- Profilin partition: monomer transitions profilin-free↔profilin-bound at k+,prof / k−,prof; set so >90% (≈99%) profilin-bound.
- De novo nucleation: rate = k_n,A × [profilin-free monomer]; emergence of one segment.
- Polymerization: barbed end adds one segment at k_p,A, using both monomer states; force-independent. New actin is ATP; after t_ATP it becomes ADP.
- Depolymerization: pointed end removes one segment at k_d,A, ADP state only; force-independent.
- Capping: barbed end free→capped at k+,cap, capped→free at k−,cap; formin-bound ends cannot cap; barbed ends formed by severing are immediately capped.
- Arp2/3 branching: binds anywhere on mother filament at k+,Arp2/3, adds one segment on the other side (daughter); daughter elongates by polymerization; unbinds at k−,Arp2/3m (mother) and k−,Arp2/3d (daughter, easier).
- Formin: free barbed end → formin-bound at k+,form; elongation ×α_form (=3); reverts to free after dwell t_form; formin-bound end cannot cap.

## Force Laws & Potentials
- **Stretch:** harmonic, U_s = ½κ_s(r−r0)² — κ_s,A = 1.69×10⁻² N/m (actin), κ_s,Arp2/3 = 2×10⁻³ N/m.
- **Bend:** harmonic, U_b = ½κ_b(θ−θ0)² — κ_b,A = 2.64×10⁻¹⁹ N·m (actin, from Isambert 1995); four Arp2/3 bend constants (see table).
- **Torsion:** harmonic, U_t = ½κ_t(ϕ−ϕ0)² — κ_t,Arp2/3 = 1×10⁻¹⁸ N·m (keeps mother+daughter coplanar; ϕ0 = 0).
- **Excluded volume:** one-sided harmonic (Eq. 8), κ_r,A = 1.69×10⁻³ N/m, active only when segment separation r₁₂ < actin diameter r_c,A = 7 nm.
- **Thermal:** Gaussian white noise per fluctuation–dissipation (Eq. 3).
- No explicit motor force law (myosin excluded from this study); severing is the only mechanochemically coupled process (via θ, Eq. 9).

- **Fig 11:** Profilin sweep with both nucleators ON — de novo filaments increase and dominate, branched filaments modestly decrease; profilin-bound monomer fraction falls with higher k−,prof.

## Relevance flags
- **DCM? — NO (low).** This is fine-grained filament kinetics in a plant cortical array; DCM is a surface-mesh continuum cell model. No membrane/turgor/junction physics here. Not directly applicable.
- **FF? — YES (high).** Directly a Taeyoon-Kim agent-based fine-grained actin model — the exact lineage of the FF Cytosim-style engine. Transcribed force laws (harmonic stretch/bend/torsion Eqs 5–7, one-sided repulsion Eq 8, cylindrical drag Eq 2, FDT thermal force Eq 3) and the full reaction kit (de novo + Arp2/3 branch nucleation at 70°±config, capping k+/k−, angle-dependent severing k_sev=k0,sev·exp(λ_sev·θ) Eq 9, formin 3× enhancement, profilin monomer partitioning, ATP→ADP aging) map onto FF's formin/Arp2/3/severing/capping modules. The 140 nm actin segment, κ_b,A=2.64×10⁻¹⁹ N·m, κ_s,A=1.69×10⁻² N/m, Δt=1.15×10⁻⁵ s are usable engine-parameter references (note: plant, C_A=1 µM efficiency choice, not MCF7). Arp2/3 branch geometry (mother 90°, daughter 20°, branch 70°, coplanar torsion) and the barbed/pointed-end asymmetry are structurally identical to FF cortex needs.
- **TAG-validation? — YES (moderate).** Table 1 provides clean, quantitative actin-dynamics validation targets (poly 2.2 µm/s, depoly 0.2 µm/s, length ~10 µm, severing ~0.01 events/µm/s, nucleation ~1.2×10⁻⁴ events/µm²/s, formin 3×) with explicit experiment-vs-model comparison — good acceptance-oracle values for FF filament-turnover kernels. Caveat: values are PLANT cortex (Arabidopsis), not mammalian/MCF7; register as plant-actin context, not as MCF7 cortex parameters. Angle-dependent severing law is a citable mechanistic KB claim.

**Notes:** Model is force-independent for polymerization/depolymerization (only severing is mechanochemically coupled via bend angle). Myosin and crosslinkers explicitly excluded (future work). λ_sev numerical value and μ (medium viscosity) are not tabulated in Table 2 — a minor provenance gap. C_A=1 µM is an acknowledged computational-efficiency compromise (real ~40–50 µM), so absolute densities are under-estimates. Public code: github.com/ktyman2/plantactin.
