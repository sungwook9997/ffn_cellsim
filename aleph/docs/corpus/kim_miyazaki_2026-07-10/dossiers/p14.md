## Summary

The paper asks how the same minimal actomyosin toolkit (F-actin + non-muscle myosin II + a single crosslinker, α-actinin) can drive distinct membrane morphological transitions — no deformation, weak deformation, single bleb, multiple blebs — and what selects the two bleb-initiation mechanisms (cortex **detachment** from membrane vs. cortex **rupture**). They combine a bottom-up reconstitution (purified actin, smooth-muscle myosin SMM, His-tagged α-actinin, ZIP kinase, encapsulated in cell-sized liposomes ~20 µm diameter via inverted emulsion, with myosin activity temporally gated by ZIPK phosphorylation) with an agent-based Brownian-dynamics model (Taeyoon Kim's fine-grained actin/ACP/motor network coupled to a triangulated deformable membrane with area+volume conservation). Two independently-tuned control parameters emerge: **actin-membrane coupling strength R_C = C_H/C_A** (His-α-actinin/actin molar ratio) and **network connectivity R_X = C_X/C_A** (total crosslinker/actin). Key findings: (1) R_C is the *primary* determinant of both the transition mode and deformation magnitude (increasing R_C: no-deformation → weak → single bleb, saturating at high R_C); R_X barely shifts the mode. (2) The bleb-initiation mechanism is set by the *balance* of R_C and R_X — low R_C / high R_X favors **Detachment** (membrane peels off intact cortex, governed by Bell-kinetics unbinding of His-α-actinin from F-actin at a critical pressure ΔP*), high R_C / low R_X favors **Rupture** (catastrophic successive F-actin severing at a critical tension). An analytic model derives γ_C^{D*} ∝ R_C for the detachment threshold, and γ_C^{R*} increasing in R_X (R_C-independent) for rupture; the phase diagram matches experiment. (3) A sparse 3D volume-spanning bulk network (methylcellulose omitted) promotes **multiple blebs** over a wider parameter range because randomly-oriented bulk myosin generates inward antagonistic contractile forces. (4) Simulations quantify separable network vs. membrane tension (network plateaus ~200 pN/µm before rupture; membrane rises during bleb expansion) and show local motor density drives local rupture. (5) Local perturbation of R_C or R_X can position blebs deterministically.

## Model & Methods

**Two coupled models plus experiment.**

### A. Experimental (in-vitro reconstitution)
- Inverted-emulsion encapsulation of purified proteins into cell-sized liposomes. Membrane lipid = 85% egg-PC : 10% DOPG : 5% DGS-NTA(Ni). Actin-membrane linkage = His-tag on recombinant α-actinin I ↔ Ni-NTA lipid (defined, tunable coupling; replaces native PH-PIP2 binding).
- Myosin (chicken gizzard smooth-muscle myosin, SMM) activated by ZIP kinase (ZIPK) phosphorylation of the regulatory light chain — temporal gating that mimics in-vivo regulation and lets cortex assembly finish before contraction.
- Buffer A50 (50 mM HEPES-KOH pH 7.6, 50 mM KCl, 5 mM MgCl₂, 1 mM EGTA); 1 mM ATP with regeneration (phosphocreatine/CPK). Isotonic (|Δosm| < 17 mOsm, mean −0.23 mOsm). 25 ± 1 °C.
- Methylcellulose (0.2% w/v, 1500 cP) as crowding/depletion agent → 2D cortical network; omit it → 3D volume-spanning network.
- Imaging: confocal (equatorial), epifluorescence time-lapse (up to 2 h), TIRF (myosin minifilament length). Biochemical assays: pyrene actin polymerization, urea/glycerol PAGE phosphorylation kinetics, co-sedimentation for K_d.

### B. Analytic theory (Supplementary Note 1) — the "Detachment" mechanism
Pressure balance ΔP+F=0, Young-Laplace ΔP, Bell-kinetics ODE for actin-bound His-α-actinin surface density ρ_H^b, solved for critical detachment pressure ΔP* and cortical tension γ_C^{D*}.

### C. Agent-based Brownian-dynamics model (Supplementary Note 2) — Taeyoon Kim's engine
- **Model type:** overdamped Langevin (Brownian) dynamics, inertia neglected, explicit particle/segment agents. This is directly the FF-engine paradigm.
- **Elements = nodes + cylindrical segments.** F-actin = serial polar cylindrical segments (barbed/pointed ends). ACP (α-actinin analog) = 2 segments joined at a center. Motor (NM-II minifilament) = backbone of 7 segments/8 points, 16 arms, each arm = ensemble of 4 heads (64 heads/motor ≈ 56 in real NM-II minifilament). Membrane = triangulated mesh with per-triangle area conservation + global volume conservation.
- **Integrator:** explicit Euler (Eq. S4), Δt = 1.15×10⁻⁴ s.
- **Drag:** per-node cylindrical drag (Eq. S3). Medium viscosity μ = 8.6 Pa·s (note: this is ~in-vivo cytoplasm-scale, higher than the ~0.9 mPa·s buffer — an explicit model choice, contrasting with the low-viscosity buffer used experimentally).
- **Thermal noise:** fluctuation-dissipation (Eq. S2).
- **Forces:** harmonic extensional (Eq. S5) + harmonic bending (Eq. S6) + repulsion (actin-actin Eq. S7; membrane-membrane Eq. S10; cyto-membrane Eq. S10 variant) + membrane volume/area (Eq. S8/S9) + ACP-membrane attractive coupling (Eq. S11).
- **Actin dynamics:** nucleation (k_n,A) + barbed-end polymerization (k_+,A); NO depolymerization; deterministic severing when tensile force on a segment > F_sev = 300 pN.
- **ACP dynamics:** binding to sites every 7 nm at k_+,ACP; NO unbinding from F-actin (deliberate — forces rupture to occur only via severing; justified since severing (200–600 pN) dominates over α-actinin/actin unbinding (1.4–80 pN) when ≥5–15 crosslinkers bind a filament; ≥6 in all conditions, 124 in reference).
- **Motor dynamics:** parallel cluster model (Erdmann-Schwarz) — walking k_w,M and unbinding k_u,M load-dependent (catch-bond); binding k_+,M = 40·N_h s⁻¹; unloaded v ≈ 140 nm/s, stall ≈ 5.7 pN per arm.
- **Membrane-cortex coupling:** fraction of ACPs (= R_C/R_X) get an attractive harmonic force to the nearest membrane triangle (normal direction; ACP slides freely along surface); ACP dissociates from membrane if separation > r_0,a, at which point it feels ~30 pN. Membrane frozen during assembly, then released.
- **Geometry/BCs:** spherical membrane, diameter 8 µm default (Nc-cheap), 16 µm validated. 2D network assembled in shell 0.9·r_MB → r_MB (27.1% of volume); 3D network fills whole interior. C_A = 10 µM fixed. R_X = C_X/C_A, R_M = C_M/C_A, R_C = C_H/C_A. Reference: R_X = R_C = 0.08, R_M = 0.01.
- **Measurement protocols:** bleb detection via discrete mean curvature (Voronoi area) every 5 s; tension via summed F_{s,i}·cos φ_i over segments crossing xy/yz/zx centroid cross-sections, normalized by contour length; local ACP areal density on spherical-coordinate surface elements.

## Governing Equations

**Main-text (analytic detachment theory):**

1. **(Eq. 1)** ΔP + F = 0 — pressure balanced by elastic restoring force of coupling just before bleb.
2. **(Eq. 2)** ΔP = 2(γ_m + γ_c)/R − 2γ_{m0}/R₀ — Young-Laplace with membrane tension γ_m, cortex tension γ_c, radius R; subscript 0 = just after formation.
3. **(Eq. 3)** ΔP ≃ 2γ_c/R — near threshold γ_c ≫ γ_m (confirmed in silico), so internal pressure set by cortex tension.
4. **(Eq. 4)** dρ_H^b/dt = k_on(ρ_H − ρ_H^b)(ρ_A − ρ_H^b − ρ_N^b) − k_off·ρ_H^b — force-free kinetics of actin-bound His-α-actinin surface density.
5. **(Eq. 5)** dρ_H^b/dt = k_on(ρ_H − ρ_H^b)(ρ_A − ρ_H^b − ρ_N^b) − k_off⁰·ρ_H^b·exp[(r₀/k_BT)(ΔP/ρ_H^b)] — Bell-kinetics: force per bond f = ΔP/ρ_H^b accelerates unbinding.
6. **(Eq. 6)** ΔP* ≃ 0.7·(k_BT·ρ_H/r₀)·ln(ρ_A/K_m^{2D}) — critical detachment pressure; K_m^{2D} = k_off⁰/k_on.
7. **(Eq. 7)** γ_C^{D*} ≃ 0.35·(k_BT·ρ_H·R/r₀)·ln(ρ_A/K_m^{2D}) — critical cortical tension for detachment (from Eq. 3 & 6).
8. **(Eq. 8)** γ_C^{D*} ∝ R_C — since R_C ≃ ρ_H/ρ_A, detachment threshold scales linearly with coupling strength.
   - Rupture counterpart (stated, not derived): γ_C^{R*} = increasing function of R_X, independent of R_C.

**Motor orientation (Methods):** θ_MB = 90° − cos⁻¹( |r⃗_MB·r⃗_OM| / (|r⃗_MB||r⃗_OM|) ) — motor angle relative to membrane circumference.

**Supplementary Note 1 (detailed detachment derivation):**
- **(S1–S3)** same kinetic ODE; with ρ_A ≫ ρ_H^b+ρ_N^b reduces to dρ_H^b/dt = g(ρ_H^b) − h(ρ_H^b,ΔP), g = k_on(ρ_H−ρ_H^b)ρ_A, h = k_off⁰·ρ_H^b·exp(r₀ΔP/k_BT ρ_H^b).
- **(S4–S5)** equilibrium ρ_H^b(0) = ρ_A·ρ_H/(ρ_A + K_m^{2D}).
- **(S6–S9)** tangency (fold-bifurcation) conditions for critical pressure ΔP*.
- **(S10)** α*·exp(1+α*) = ρ_A/K_m^{2D}; **(S11)** α* = (r₀/k_BT·ρ_H)·ΔP*; **(S12)** α* ≃ 0.7·ln(ρ_A/K_m^{2D}); **(S13)** ΔP* ≃ 0.7·(k_BT·ρ_H/r₀)·ln(ρ_A/K_m^{2D}).

**Supplementary Note 2 (agent-based dynamics):**
- **(S1)** F_i − ζ_i·(dr_i/dt) + F_i^T = 0 — overdamped Langevin.
- **(S2)** ⟨F_i^T(t)F_j^T(t)⟩ = (2k_BT·ζ_i·δ_ij/Δt)·δ — FDT noise.
- **(S3)** ζ_i = 3πμd_i·(3 + 2l_i/d_i)/5 — cylindrical drag (Underhill-Doyle).
- **(S4)** r_i(t+Δt) = r_i(t) + (1/ζ_i)(F_i + F_i^T)Δt — Euler update.
- **(S5)** U_s = ½κ_s(l − l₀)² — extensional harmonic.
- **(S6)** U_b = ½κ_b(θ − θ₀)² — bending harmonic.
- **(S7)** U_r,A = ½κ_r,A(r₁₂ − d_A)² for r₁₂ < d_A, else 0 — actin-actin repulsion.
- **(S8)** U_V,MB = ½κ_V,MB(V_MB − V_0,MB)² — volume conservation.
- **(S9)** U_A,MB = ½κ_A,MB(A_MB − A_0,MB)² — per-triangle area conservation.
- **(S10)** U_r,MB = κ_r,MB(r₁₂ − r_0,r)²·[½ + ⅓(1 − r₁₂/r_0,r)⁴] for r₁₂ < r_0,r, else 0 — steep membrane/cyto-membrane repulsion (r_0,r = d_MB).
- **(S11)** U_a,MB = ½κ_a,MB(r₁₂ − r_0,a/2)² for r_0,a/2 ≤ r₁₂ < r_0,a, else 0 — ACP-membrane attractive coupling (r_0,a = d_ACP + d_MB).

## Relevance flags

- **DCM? YES (high).** The deformable-membrane half is exactly DCM physics: a triangulated surface mesh with per-triangle area conservation (κ_A,MB, S9), global enclosed-volume conservation (κ_V,MB, S8), bending on dihedral angles (κ_b,MB), extensional chain stiffness (κ_s,MB), self-repulsion (κ_r,MB, S10), and node-face-style repulsion between cytoskeleton and membrane. Concrete parameter set (l₀,MB=70 nm, d_MB=50 nm, A₀=2.12e-15 m², V₀=2.68e-16 m³, κ_V=1e3 N/m²) is directly usable as a DCM membrane-mesh calibration/cross-check, and the bleb-formation phenomenology (volume-conserved shell + internal contractile pressure → protrusion) is a DCM validation scenario. Cortical-tension γ_c and Young-Laplace ΔP=2γ_c/R are the same relations DCM uses.
- **FF? YES (very high — this is the FF engine's exact paradigm).** Overdamped Langevin (S1) + FDT noise (S2) + cylindrical per-segment drag (S3) + Euler integration; explicit cylindrical actin segments (l₀=140 nm), harmonic extension/bending, actin-actin excluded volume, formin-free nucleation+barbed-end polymerization, **deterministic F-actin severing at F_sev=300 pN**, α-actinin-analog ACP crosslinkers, and NM-II minifilament motors driven by the **parallel cluster model (Erdmann-Schwarz)** with catch-bond load dependence (v₀≈140 nm/s, stall≈5.7 pN). Bell kinetics for adhesion off-rate. This is Taeyoon Kim's canonical model (his refs 6, 8) — the direct literature anchor for FF's filament/crosslinker/motor physics. Tables S1+S2 give a fully-specified, citable native-scale parameter set. Note κ_s,A=1.69e-2 N/m and κ_b,A=2.64e-19 N·m (→l_p~9 µm) for FF actin-stiffness calibration; F_sev=300 pN (Tsuda 1996) is a direct severing datum.
