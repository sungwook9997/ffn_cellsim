## Summary
The paper asks *why* the Arp2/3 complex (which nucleates the branched-actin lamellipodium) is specifically required for cell migration on **soft** elastic substrates but not stiff ones — a question raised by prior corticogenesis work (Wang et al. 2016). Combining experiment (H4 human glioblastoma cells on laminin-coated polyacrylamide gels of 500 Pa / 4 kPa / 90 kPa, with pharmacological Arp2/3 inhibition by 40 µM CK-869) with two computational models, they show Arp2/3 inhibition converts lamellipodia into filopodia-like protrusions (FLPs), reducing spreading area and aspect ratio (on stiff gels), raising migration directionality, and **significantly lowering migration speed on every stiffness — the deficit being largest on the softest gels**. Traction force microscopy (90 kPa) shows inhibited cells exert **higher** traction (localized to FLPs) than controls. Two models explain this: (1) an **agent-based cross-linked actomyosin network** (Taeyoon Kim's Brownian-dynamics framework) coupled to a triangulated elastic substrate via molecular-clutch focal-adhesion (FA) sites, run over 6 FA-area conditions (A_FA = 0.05–1) — wider lamellipodia (large A_FA) distribute force over more, smaller, more transient adhesions, raising total mean force but **decreasing substrate stress** (force/area); (2) a **parallel focal adhesion (PFA) model** extending the classic Chan–Odde 2008 motor-clutch model to 1/3/6/10/15/20/30 adhesions sharing a fixed total clutch pool on a single substrate spring. The PFA model reveals **Parallel Focal Adhesion Compensation (PFAC)**: with enough parallel FAs, a surviving adhesion ramps its force to rescue the load when neighbors fail, preventing the catastrophic all-clutch load-and-fail collapse of a single filopodial adhesion — prolonging cell–substrate interaction time (no failure ever occurs at 30 FAs). SIM-TIRF imaging confirms real lamellipodia have ~37 small nascent FAs on average (max 89, min 6), matching the high-FA/low-stress regime; FLPs have single large mature FAs transmitting high stress over a small area. Net mechanistic claim: Arp2/3-built lamellipodia give two mechanical advantages on soft substrate — lower substrate stress (spatial force distribution) and prolonged adhesion lifetime (PFAC) — enabling efficient mesenchymal glioma migration.

## Model & Methods

### Model 1 — Agent-based cross-linked actomyosin network + elastic substrate (Taeyoon Kim framework)
- **Model type**: agent-based / coarse-grained Brownian (Langevin) dynamics of a cytoskeletal network — actin filaments as segmented cylinders, actin cross-linking proteins (ACPs) and motors as explicit binding agents, coupled to a deformable elastic substrate mesh via molecular clutches. Framework and equations are those of prior Kim-lab work (Kim et al. 2009; Jung et al. 2015; Mak et al. 2016; Li et al. 2017; Kim 2015); details deferred to Supplemental Material + Suppl. Tables S1/S2.
- **Domain / geometry**: very thin slab mimicking lamellipodium geometry, **5 × 5 × 0.1 µm** rectangular domain (Figure 1A). Substrate = 2-D triangulated mesh at z = 0.
- **Boundary conditions**: **Periodic (PBC)** on the ±x lateral boundaries; **repulsive boundary (no PBC)** at the +y (top, leading-edge) side; **"sticky" boundary (no PBC)** at the −y (y = 0, rear/nucleation) side.
- **Network assembly**: nucleation + growth of seed F-actins from the −y boundary (y = 0); Arp2/3 binds the side of existing F-actins and nucleates daughter filaments (branch, ~70° per introduction) so the network grows toward +y. F-actins cross-linked by ACPs. Motors initially distributed near the −y boundary (biased 0 ≤ y ≤ 1.25 µm; unlike actin/ACP which are uniform).
- **Substrate coupling**: clutches form between substrate mesh nodes and points on F-actin **within 300 nm** of an F-actin barbed end; each clutch is a **linear spring**. Equilibrium length of chains between mesh nodes = **50 nm**.
- **FA region**: adhesions restricted to a designated FA region of width W_FA out of total width W; **A_FA = W_FA / W** is the control parameter (fraction of width that can adhere), swept over **6 conditions A_FA = 0.05, 0.1, 0.17(≈0.2), 0.33, 0.5, 1.0**. Filopodia-like condition = smallest A_FA = 0.05.
- **Clutch-count control**: each FA site has an upper limit N_C^max on clutches; the product **A_FA × N_C^max is held constant** across conditions (values shown in Fig 1D: A_FA=0.05→N_C^max=20; 0.1→10; 0.17→6; 0.33→3; 0.5→2; 1.0→1) so the maximum total clutch number is matched. Under the filopodia-like (small A_FA) condition, extra clutches per site keep max total clutches independent of FA area.
- **Dynamics driver**: motor (myosin) activity contracts the network in the −y direction over time; clutch-based substrate interactions slow contraction and load/deform the substrate. Bonds (clutch links) break at a rate proportional to applied force → **slip bond** (baseline). Catch-slip variant tested in Suppl. Fig S3.
- **Measurement protocol**: to avoid the confound of slowing contraction (no F-actin turnover/polymerization in the model, so contraction decays as the network compacts), **all quantities measured over the first 20 s** where contraction rate is ~constant. Actin flow speed measured from actin segments initially at y = 4.5–5.0 µm. Stress = (sum of all clutch forces in FA region) / (FA region area).
- **Integrator/timestep**: not stated in main text — deferred to Supplemental Material (standard Kim-lab Langevin/Brownian-dynamics Euler scheme with sub-µs dt in the cited framework papers).

### Model 2 — Parallel Focal Adhesion (PFA) motor-clutch model
- **Model type**: extension of the classic **Chan–Odde (2008)** stochastic motor-clutch model of filopodia to **multiple parallel adhesions** sharing one substrate.
- **Three domains** (Figure 1E / 6E): (i) **substrate domain** = a single elastic spring (stiffness k_sub); (ii) **molecular-clutch domain** = N_FA adhesions in parallel, each a bundle of clutches (linear springs, stiffness k_clutch) that stochastically bind/unbind, off-rate increasing with force (slip bond, each FA with its own internal off-rate); (iii) **actomyosin domain** = "motors" driving actin retrograde flow whose velocity slows with load (force-velocity, per Chan–Odde).
- **Conditions**: 1 FA (= filopodia), 3, 6, 10, 15, 20, 30 parallel FAs. **Total clutch number held constant** and divided equally among the FAs (1-FA = all clutches in one adhesion). 10 simulations per condition.
- **All parameters** in Suppl. Table S3 (not in this PDF).

### Experimental methods (H4 glioma)
- **Cells**: H4 human glioblastoma line (ATCC), DMEM + 10% FBS + 1% pen-strep.
- **Substrates**: laminin (25 µg/ml, Sulfo-SANPAH conjugation)–coated polyacrylamide gels, Young's moduli **500 Pa, 4 kPa, 90 kPa** (formulations in Suppl. Table S4; 40–120 µm thick for microscopy; modulus from uniaxial compression, MTS system, 5–10% strain linear fit).
- **Arp2/3 inhibition**: **CK-869, 40 µM** (binds serendipitous pocket on Arp3, destabilizes Arp2–Arp3 interface), applied 30–60 min before imaging.
- **Migration**: 4×10⁴ cells/gel; live imaging every 5 min for 4 h, 15 fields; TrackMate (ImageJ) tracking; speed + directionality index; Cohen's d effect size.
- **Traction Force Microscopy**: 90 kPa gels, 200-nm crimson fluorospheres, Zeiss LSM780 (63×/1.4NA), Han et al. 2015 PIV/force reconstruction.
- **FA imaging**: SIM-TIRF (GE OMX SR), vinculin (anti-vinculin V9131 + AF488) + phalloidin actin; per-lamellipodium FA count/size via custom MATLAB.

## Governing Equations
The main text does **not** print the model's differential equations explicitly (they are in Supplemental Material and the cited Kim-lab + Chan–Odde papers). Transcribing the relations that ARE stated/implied:

1. **Clutch as linear (Hookean) spring** — clutch force f_c = k_clutch · x_clutch(i), where x_clutch(i) is the stretch of clutch i (Fig 1E labels k_off, k_on, x_clutch(i)). *Meaning*: each engaged molecular clutch transmits force proportional to its extension.

2. **Substrate as single elastic spring (PFA model)** — F_sub = k_sub · x_sub. *Meaning*: aggregate substrate deformation resists the summed clutch load (Fig 1E, "Substrate Domain" spring; Fig 3/4 report substrate position x_sub in nm).

3. **Force-dependent (slip-bond) clutch off-rate** — k_off = k_off,0 · exp(F_clutch / F_b) (Bell form; stated as "off rate increases with tension", "break at a rate proportional to an applied force"). *Meaning*: loaded clutches detach faster → drives the load-and-fail instability.

4. **Catch-slip variant (Suppl. Fig S3 test)** — bond strengthens with force up to a threshold then behaves as slip (referenced from Bangasser et al. 2013). *Meaning*: tested robustness; conclusions unchanged.

5. **Motor force–velocity (Chan–Odde)** — actin retrograde-flow velocity v_actin = v_unloaded·(1 − F_motor/F_stall) (linear motor-clutch force-velocity, "velocity of actin flow driven by myosin slows in response to force"; F_m labeled in Fig 1E). *Meaning*: the actomyosin "motor" drives retrograde flow that loads clutches; flow stalls under load.

6. **Substrate stress definition** — σ = (Σ f_clutch within FA region) / (A_FA region area). *Meaning*: the paper's central observable — force distributed over a wider FA area lowers stress even as total force rises (Fig 3C vs 3D).

7. **FA-area control parameter** — A_FA = W_FA / W. *Meaning*: fraction of network width permitted to adhere (lamellipodium width proxy).

8. **Clutch-budget constraint** — A_FA × N_C^max = const. *Meaning*: normalization so total maximum clutch count is matched across FA-area conditions.

9. **Network Langevin dynamics (agent-based, from Kim framework, not printed here)** — γ (dx/dt) = F_bend + F_stretch + F_motor + F_xlink + F_clutch + F_thermal, with thermal force satisfying fluctuation–dissipation. *Meaning*: overdamped Brownian dynamics of every actin segment/ACP/motor/clutch — see Kim et al. 2009 / Mak et al. 2016 for exact potentials.

## Force Laws & Potentials
Functional forms are those of the referenced Kim-lab agent-based framework (extensional + bending harmonic potentials on actin, harmonic ACP/motor/clutch springs, Bell-type unbinding, Chan–Odde motor force-velocity); explicit constants are in Suppl. Tables S1–S3 (not in this PDF). Stated in main text:
- **Clutch spring**: linear, force = k_clutch·(extension); clutch links form within 300 nm; inter-node chain equilibrium length 50 nm.
- **Substrate spring** (PFA): linear, single lumped spring; (agent-based) triangulated elastic mesh with Hookean edges.
- **Slip-bond off-rate**: exponential increase with clutch force (Bell/Evans-type).
- **Motor**: myosin motors bias-loaded near −y; drive contraction / retrograde flow; force-velocity per Chan–Odde.
- **Arp2/3 branch**: daughter filament nucleated at ~70° off the mother filament side.

## Relevance flags
- **DCM?**: **Weak/indirect.** No cortex/membrane/nucleus continuum surface-mesh cell. The elastic substrate is a 2-D triangulated Hookean mesh — conceptually similar to DCM's node/spring meshing but it models the SUBSTRATE, not a deformable cell. Migration/traction physics is not DCM's aggregate-compaction focus. Low priority for DCM.
- **FF?**: **YES — strongly relevant.** This is a Cytosim-class fine-grained agent-based actomyosin network (explicit F-actin segments, Arp2/3 ~70° branches, ACP cross-linkers, myosin motors, molecular clutches with Bell-type slip off-rates and Chan–Odde force-velocity) coupled to a substrate through adhesion clutches — precisely the FF engine's domain (native cortex, adhesion clutches, Arp2/3 lamellipodium, Bell-Evans off-rates, Hill/Chan–Odde motors). The lamellipodium-vs-filopodium FA-area sweep, PFAC compensation, and stress-vs-force distribution are directly reproducible/validatable in FF. The parameter values live in Suppl. Tables S1–S3 (need SI to seed FF adhesion-clutch parameters).
- **TAG-validation?**: **YES.** Concrete quantitative validation targets: mean force ↑ / stress ↓ vs lamellipodium width (Fig 3C/D), U-shaped clutch force vs N_FA and time-to-failure crossover ≥10 FA / no-failure at 30 FA (Fig 3H, 4H), ~37 FAs/lamellipodium (max 89, min 6), FA lifetime ~2–11 s vs A_FA, traction control<inhibited on 90 kPa, migration-speed Cohen's d 0.9→0.25 across 500 Pa→90 kPa. Register the PFAC emergent phenomenon + these numbers as an FF adhesion-clutch validation gate. Note SI-dependent params should be flagged NO_SI until Suppl. Tables ingested.
