## Summary
The authors ask what extracellular forces a cell generates *after* mitotic rounding, when it must elongate and cleave while mechanically confined by neighbors in an epithelial monolayer. Growing MDCK (and MCF10A) monolayers on soft (~1 kPa) collagen-I–coated polyacrylamide gels with embedded fiducial beads, they use traction force microscopy (TFM) and monolayer stress microscopy (MSM), averaged over >100 aligned division events, to resolve three chronological force stages: (1) **mitotic rounding** (net inward cell–matrix stress / tensile cell–cell stress), (2) **division elongation** (outward/protrusive stress strongly anisotropic along the division axis — perpendicular stress <25 % of axial), and (3) **postdivision spreading** (uniform outward stress). Through a battery of perturbations they rule out neighbor pulling (E-cadherin extracellular-domain truncation *increases* rather than removes protrusive stress; adjacent nuclei randomly oriented; cells divide against both edges and vertices), neighbor migration/de-adhesion (adjacent nuclei do not consistently move; focal-adhesion count unchanged metaphase→cytokinesis), and establish that the **dividing cell itself pushes outward** (adjacent cells and nuclei show reduced cross-sectional area and increased cell–cell-boundary curvature κ along the division axis). Force is transmitted to the substrate via adhesions of the *adjacent* cells (single isolated cells generate no consistent elongation traction). FEM modeling of measured adjacent-cell 3D deformation implies real forces of **300–500 nN** — far above TFM/MSM readings (which under-report because division occurs at the mid-plane while tractions are read basally). Mechanistically, **cytokinetic ring contraction**, not interpolar-spindle elongation, drives elongation: cell volume drops only ~5 % across cytokinesis, and a Brownian-dynamics triangulated-membrane simulation shows that with such near-volume-conservation, ring constriction yields ~60 % axial elongation (a ~40 % volume drop would be needed to abolish it); blebbistatin (myosin II), BTO-1 (Plk1), and anillin siRNA each abrogate elongation (~60 % reduction) and impair chromosome segregation, while kinesin-5 perturbation (BRD9876/STLC) affects elongation only partially and laser ablation of the interpolar spindle causes retraction only sometimes. The protrusive elongation signature (adjacent-cell area strain <0, boundary curvature change >0) is reproduced across many in-vivo epithelia (mouse, Drosophila, Xenopus, zebrafish, C. elegans), making division-elongation force a universal mechanism.

## Model & Methods

### Experimental
- **Cells:** MDCK type II G (WT + stable lines: FUCCI cell-cycle sensor, LifeAct::RFP/GFP, E-cadherin::DsRed, truncated E-cadherin T151⁻, vinculin::GFP, α-tubulin::GFP); MCF10A (WT + LifeAct::RFP). Drosophila embryo (utrophin::GFP cortex marker + tubulin::mCherry), adult Drosophila intestine (from prior data).
- **Substrate:** polyacrylamide gel, ~1 kPa (4 % acrylamide / 0.06 % bis) with 0.5-µm carboxylate fluorescent beads, functionalized with collagen-I via sulfo-SANPAH; single cells on stiffer 7.43 kPa (10 %/0.06 %). Glass–collagen-I for non-flexible experiments.
- **Gel mechanics:** AR-G2 rheometer, cyclic strain amplitude 0.01 @ 1 rad/s; shear modulus G\* = (G′² + G″²)^½; converted to elastic modulus with Poisson ratio 0.457 (Takigawa 1996).
- **TFM:** remove cells (trypsin), reference bead image, PIV (PIVLab; 64×64 & 32×32 px windows, 50 % overlap; 32/16 for MCF10A), Gaussian filter SD 6 µm, Fourier-transform TFM (Trepat 2009; validated vs pyTFM).
- **MSM:** MATLAB, monolayer height 9 µm (Tambe 2011).
- **Alignment/averaging:** >100 events per condition rotated so division axis = horizontal x, centered; stresses differenced between two division-stage timepoints; fit to 8.6-µm grid (11.4 µm MCF10A). Peak traction = mean of L/R peaks within 60-µm-diameter region of center. Outward traction defined negative, inward positive.
- **Perturbations:** blebbistatin 100 µM (myosin II), BRD9876 40 µM (↑ kinesin-5 binding → inhibits spindle elongation), S-trityl-L-cysteine (STLC) 25 µM (↓ kinesin-5 binding), BTO-1 50 µM (Plk1), anillin siRNA (20 nM, MCF10A). Laser ablation of interpolar spindle (Mai Tai multiphoton 800 nm). Cell-volume by confocal voxel counting. FEM (Abaqus loft → Ansys) of adjacent-cell deformation with applied forces 10/50/100/300/500 nN; SSIM against experiment.

### Computational membrane model (T. Kim; Fig. 7 b–e, Fig. S4 b–f) — the DCM-relevant core
- **Model type:** coarse-grained triangulated-**membrane surface mesh**, **Brownian (overdamped Langevin) dynamics**, inertia neglected. Membrane-only continuum shell with volume + surface-area constraints — SimuCell3D/DCM-style.
- **Mesh:** 10,242 nodes; pentagon/hexagon dual cells around each node; initial inter-node chain length ~0.5 µm; initial spherical radius R = 10 µm; domain 100×100×100 µm.
- **Integrator:** explicit **Euler** (Eq. 4), timestep Δt.
- **Cytokinesis mimic:** 522 equatorial nodes within a 1-µm-wide band displaced inward at constant speed v_c toward the cytokinetic axis; they stop once their distance to the axis < 0.5 µm.
- **Sweep:** volume- and area-conservation strengths varied; elongation = (leftmost–rightmost membrane extent)/(initial diameter). FEM adjacent-cell material: isotropic, Young's 1 kPa, density 1000 kg/m³, Poisson 0.38 (Brückner 2017; Trickey 2006).

## Governing Equations
1. **Overdamped Langevin (BD) equation of motion:** `F_i − ζ_i (dr_i/dt) + F_i^T = 0` — deterministic force, drag, and thermal force on node i balance (no inertia).
2. **Fluctuation–dissipation for thermal force:** `⟨F_i^T(t) F_j^T(t)⟩ = (2 k_B T ζ_i δ_ij / Δt) δ` — variance of stochastic force set by drag and temperature (δ = 2nd-order unit tensor, δ_ij Kronecker).
3. **Node drag coefficient:** `ζ_i = λ A_i` — drag proportional to instantaneous area A_i of the node's surrounding polygon (λ constant).
4. **Euler position update:** `r_i(t+Δt) = r_i(t) + (dr_i/dt) Δt = r_i(t) + (1/ζ_i)(F_i + F_i^T) Δt`.
5. **Extensional (chain-length) potential — piecewise spring with a slack window:**
   `U_s = ½ κ_s (r − r0,L)²  if r < r0,L ;  0  if r0,L ≤ r ≤ r0,H ;  ½ κ_s (r − r0,H)²  if r > r0,H` — no force while chain length r is in the slack range [r0,L, r0,H]; linear restoring spring outside it.
6. **Bending (dihedral) potential:** `U_b = κ_b [1 − cos(θ − θ_0)]` — penalizes deviation of dihedral angle θ between adjacent triangles from θ_0 = 0; forces applied to the 4 nodes of the two triangles.
7. **Global volume-conservation potential:** `U_v = κ_v (V − V0)² / (2 V0)` — harmonic in enclosed volume; forces move triangles normal to surface (out if V<V0). V computed by summing signed tetrahedra (triangle + membrane center).
8. **Global surface-area-conservation potential:** `U_a = κ_a (A − A0)² / (2 A0)` — harmonic in total area; forces resize individual triangles toward A0. (Area *can* increase via membrane reservoirs in cells — Gauthier 2011, Raucher & Sheetz 1999 — unlike lipid vesicles.)

## Force Laws & Potentials
- **Extensional force:** derivative of Eq. 5 — dead-band spring, stiffness κ_s, slack window r0,L = 0.05 µm, r0,H = 1.25 µm (so chains free to roam between 0.05 and 1.25 µm, spring beyond).
- **Bending force:** −dU_b/dθ from Eq. 6, stiffness κ_b, equilibrium θ_0 = 0 rad (flat).
- **Volume force:** −∂U_v/∂r_i from Eq. 7, magnitude scaled by κ_v; normal to triangles.
- **Area force:** −∂U_a/∂r_i from Eq. 8, magnitude scaled by κ_a.
- **Thermal force:** Gaussian, variance per Eq. 2.
- **Drag force:** −ζ_i (dr_i/dt), ζ_i = λ A_i (Eq. 3).
- **Cytokinetic driving:** kinematic (prescribed constant-velocity inward displacement of 522 equatorial nodes), not a potential.
- **FEM (continuum, separate):** linear-elastic isotropic solid, E = 1 kPa, ν = 0.38; external point/area loads 10–500 nN.

## Relevance flags
- **DCM? YES (high).** The computational core is essentially a DCM: a triangulated surface-mesh membrane with harmonic global volume (Eq. 7) and surface-area (Eq. 8) conservation, dihedral bending (Eq. 6), extensional edge springs (Eq. 5), and overdamped Langevin/Brownian dynamics (Eqs. 1–4) — the same SimuCell3D-style architecture as ffn_cellsim's DCM. Directly usable: the volume-conservation → axial-elongation coupling (5 % volume loss ⇒ ~60 % elongation), area-reservoir modeling (κ_a 10⁻⁴–10⁻⁶ N/m), and cytokinetic-ring constriction as prescribed equatorial node displacement. Cross-references DCM division work (memory: DCM division stable inset) and the node-area drag ζ=λA convention.
- **FF? PARTIAL/LOW.** No explicit filament/motor/clutch mechanics; myosin II, kinesin-5, anillin appear only as pharmacological knobs, not fine-grained mechanisms. Conceptually relevant to FF cytokinetic-ring/actomyosin roadmap but provides no filament-scale force laws.
- **TAG-validation? YES.** Rich quantitative targets for a division/DCM contract: 5 % volume loss ⇒ 60 % elongation (40 % to abolish); cytokinesis force 300–500 nN; cell–cell boundary curvature 0.035→0.092 µm⁻¹ (in-vivo −0.005→0.11); division-elongation traction anisotropy (perp <25 % axial); mitotic-rounding AFM ~500 Pa / 50–100 nN. All measured/simulated with figure provenance — suitable as ValidationGate acceptance oracles. Note: Nam & Chaudhuri 2018 (Nat. Phys.) is the closely related single-cell-in-alginate precursor and a natural companion KB source.
