## 1. Mechanisms to adopt  (marginal–moderate for DCM)
- **Boundary force transmission breaks symmetry → large-scale organized flow.** A confining boundary (droplet membrane) converts a disordered active network into a rotating vortex: elongating bundles push on the boundary, the non-slipping end generates a moment of force, and the whole network rotates. For DCM this is a reference for how the cortex/membrane BOUNDARY actively shapes internal organization (contractile-ring positioning, cortical rotation) — the boundary is not passive.
- **⟨ω⟩ ∝ 1/R, constant boundary flow speed.** Angular velocity scales inversely with cell radius; boundary flow speed is size-independent (~3.19 µm/min ≈ bundle elongation rate). A candidate DCM confined-flow scaling law.
