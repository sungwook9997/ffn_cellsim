# FF lens — p54

**Paper:** Sakata, Matsubara, Gomi, Miyazaki (2026), "Lipid composition effects on the number and size of liposomes formed by the inverted emulsion method," *Biophys. J.* 125:731–744. DOI 10.1016/j.bpj.2025.12.013.

**Verdict for FF: essentially NULL (relevance 0.5/5).** This is a purely experimental in-vitro *reconstitution-methods* study from the Miyazaki lab on how phospholipid net charge and headgroup size control the **yield (number)** and **size distribution** of protein-free giant liposomes made by the inverted (water-in-oil droplet transfer) emulsion method. It contains **no cytoskeletal filaments, no motors, no crosslinkers, no adhesion clutches, no persistence length, no bending/stretch modulus, no bond kinetics, and no force law**. There is nothing in the Cytosim-physics (Nédélec–Foethke) fine-grained filament engine that this paper can advance, port, parity-check, or validate. The two equations in the paper are empirical single-exponential *fit curves* for yield-vs-temperature and yield-vs-incubation-time — phenomenological descriptors of a lab protocol, not mechanistic force/kinetic laws. I record this negative result explicitly and flag the single marginal, out-of-scope use (encapsulation container geometry) below so the finding is auditable rather than silently dropped.

---

## 1. Mechanisms to adopt
**None.** No FF module (filament, crosslinker, myosin, formin, Arp2/3, clutch, turnover, membrane containment, turgor) has a counterpart mechanism here. The paper's mechanistic content (Fig. 7) is a *qualitative* cartoon of three levers that set droplet→liposome conversion yield: (i) abundant lipid supply to the oil–water interface, (ii) electrostatic repulsion between charged lipids on the nascent bilayer and on the interface monolayer, (iii) water intrusion at the connecting "neck" facilitated by large-head steric hindrance or small-head negative-curvature stabilization. These govern *how a vesicle detaches during manufacture*, not how a cytoskeletal network generates or relaxes stress. Nothing beats a current FF mechanism because there is no overlapping mechanism.

## 2. Force laws / kinetic schemes to port
**None.** The only two closed-form expressions in the paper are empirical fits with no physical force content:
- Yield vs centrifugation temperature (Fig. 4B): `y = a·exp(−b·x)`, a = 68.6 liposomes/mm², b = 0.0855 °C⁻¹ (x = T in °C, y = liposomes/mm²).
- Yield vs pre-centrifugation incubation time (Fig. 4D): `y = c − a·exp(−b·x)`, a = 25.6 liposomes/mm², b = 0.249 min⁻¹, c = 102 liposomes/mm² (plateau).

Neither maps to any FF state variable (no bond force F, no off-rate k_off, no motor duty cycle, no branching angle, no unbinding spectrum). They are protocol descriptors and must **not** be entered into any FF config or oracle.

## 3. Parameters / rate constants to set
**None applicable to FF.** All numeric parameters concern lipid chemistry and a manufacturing protocol: total lipid in oil 1 mM; 12,000×g / 30 min; A50 buffer (50 mM HEPES-KOH pH 7.6, 50 mM KCl, 5 mM MgCl₂, 1 mM EGTA); inner 150 mM sucrose + 350 mM glucose, outer 500 mM glucose density steps; egg PC mean MW 770.123 Da; per-lipid net charge / head MW (e.g. DOPA −1/96, DOPG −1/170, PEG2000-PE −1/2177 Da); counted liposome diameter 5–40 µm. **None supersede any FF value** (k_off0, F_b, stall force, step size, duty ratio, ℓ_p, link stiffness, nucleation rate) — the FF engine has no lipid-headgroup or vesicle-yield parameter to update. Do **not** register any of these against a filament/motor/crosslinker module.

## 4. Network architecture / protocol to reproduce
**None as an FF scenario.** The reconstitution geometry is a bulk W/O emulsion transferred across a planar oil–water interface — there is no filament network, no confinement geometry a cytoskeleton is built into, no density/connectivity of a mechanical network. The only structurally adjacent concept is the **cell-sized (5–40 µm diameter, MCF7-comparable R≈7.5 µm) unilamellar container**: *if* the project ever moves to encapsulating a reconstituted actomyosin cortex inside an artificial-cell liposome (a future bottom-up validation), this paper is the correct methods reference for how to make and size that container. That is a wet-lab manufacturing recipe, not an FF simulation scenario, and it does not touch the native-scale cortex build.

## 5. Validation targets for FF observables
**None.** The paper reports no G′(ω)/G″(ω), no stress-relaxation modes/times, no contraction rate or extent, no network tension, no motor velocity-vs-load, no buckling threshold, no mesh size, no scaling exponents. Its measured quantities are liposome yield fold-changes (e.g. 10% DOPA → 13.7× yield in natural sedimentation; DOPG/DOPE peak ~10× at 10% replacement, non-monotonic collapse beyond) and diameter distributions — manufacturing metrics with **zero** mechanistic-simulator overlap. There is nothing here for the FF engine to reproduce.

## 6. Risks / caveats
- **Primary risk = false adoption.** The single temptation is to treat the "container geometry" (5–40 µm liposome, cell-sized) as an FF input. Guard: FF's physiological baseline is already the MCF7 cell (R=7.5 µm, native 38000-filament cortex, membrane + turgor + nucleus). This paper's protein-free vesicle has *no cortex and no active mechanics*; grafting its lipid-chemistry parameters onto the FF membrane-containment or cortex modules would violate the fine-grained-mechanistic rule (it is a lumped chemistry recipe, not a filament/motor mechanism) and inject non-physical, non-cytoskeletal numbers. Do not do it.
- **Regime mismatch is total.** Purified lipid-in-oil, room-to-cold-temperature, gravity/centrifugal droplet transfer — none of it is a living cortex, a 3D actomyosin network, or any FF regime. There is no adaptation that preserves the native-scale/physiological-baseline rules because there is nothing to adapt from.
- **Correct disposition:** this paper belongs (weakly) to TAG/reconstitution-methods provenance cataloguing only — Miyazaki-lab inverted-emulsion protocol constants for a possible future encapsulated-cytoskeleton artificial-cell experiment. It should carry **no FF ModelContract, no ValidationGate, no Parameter, and no CodeMapping** against the filament engine. Record the negative finding and move on.
