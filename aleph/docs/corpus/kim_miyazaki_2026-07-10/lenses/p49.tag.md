# TAG-validation lens — p49

**Paper:** Kinetic Scheme of Myosin Phosphorylation by ZIP Kinase — Yamaguchi, Nakagawa, Tran, Shimizu, Miyazaki. *Biochemistry* 2025, 64, 4805−4817. DOI 10.1021/acs.biochem.5c00495.

## Paper kind

**experimental** (in-vitro enzyme biochemistry) — with a light **analytic/simulation** overlay (two coupled Michaelis–Menten ODEs, "Model 1"/"Model 2", numerically integrated to reproduce time courses). The kinetic constants (kcat, Km, KI) are directly *measured* (LC-MS/MS PRM + urea/glycerol PAGE densitometry + cosedimentation); the time-course curves are *model output* from those constants. Under our philosophy the measured constants are **overlay / parameter-source / cross-check targets, never fit targets**; the MM/competitive-binding equations are closed-form and could serve as a **candidate oracle** IF a phosphorylation-gated myosin-activation layer is ever built.

Critically: this is an **upstream biochemical-regulation** paper (how myosin II gets *switched on*), not a **mechanics** paper. It contains **no force law, no motor force–velocity, no off-rate under load, no bending/tension observable**. It touches neither the DCM cortical-σ continuum nor FF filament mechanics directly. Our engines currently model myosin as *already-active* Hill minifilaments (FF) or a lumped active σ (DCM) — there is no phosphorylation state variable to gate. So every candidate below is **latent / PI-gate review**, contingent on a future activation-fraction module.

## Coverage vs existing gates

**No existing gate covers any of this data.** The KB has no myosin-*activation* / kinase-kinetics gate. The closest current rows are all downstream mechanics that assume myosin is already contractile:

- `VG-H3-KU35-cortex-tension`, `VG-H7-gate-a`, `VG-H7-gate-b`, `VG-H3-composite-tension` (γ_active cortical tension 0.35–0.65 mN/m) — these are **force outputs** of active myosin; p49 supplies the *upstream on-switch kinetics* (seconds-timescale activation), a different physical quantity/units (s⁻¹, µM vs mN/m). **No overlap, no conflict.** p49 could one day inform the *time constant* with which the γ-generating myosin pool turns on, but it does not touch the tension band.
- `PARAM-F_stall_motor` (2 pN/motor), `PARAM-v_unloaded` (100 nm/s) — Hill motor mechanics; p49 gives no force/velocity numbers, so it neither confirms nor challenges these.
- `VG-U2-catch-slip-bond`, Bell-Evans oracle — bond off-rate under load; unrelated (kcat here is a catalytic turnover, not a mechanical off-rate).

Verdict: p49 is **additive, not confirming/tightening/challenging** any current contract. It opens a *new observable class* (myosin regulatory-light-chain phosphorylation kinetics) that our model does not yet represent.

## New gate candidates

All **latent — PI-gate review, contingent on a future phospho-gated myosin-activation module.** Do NOT author these now; stage as candidates. They are measurable/defensible *if* such a module exists; none is measurable against the current engines (which have no phosphorylation state).

- **`VG-MYO-activation-rate` (candidate, latent).** Observable: mean MRLC di-phosphorylation (activation) timescale of the active-myosin pool. Band/value: characteristic turnover kcat 0.40–0.60 s⁻¹ (ZIPK-FL) → di-phospho activation on **~1–10 s** at physiological [kinase], with Km 4.2 µM setting half-saturation. Engine: FF (myosin activation fraction) or an activation-oracle. Source: quotes #1–5, Table 1 p4810. Rationale: sets the *physiological seconds-scale* on which cortical/contractile force can ramp — a real, defensible timescale contract, but only once activation is a state variable.
- **`VG-MYO-sequential-order` (candidate, latent, mechanism check).** Observable: phosphorylation-site order (Ser19 before Thr18). Value: monophospho Ser19:Thr18 ≈ 35:1 → sequential, Ser19-first (not random). Engine: activation-kinetics oracle only. Source: quote #11, Fig 4 p4809. Rationale: a qualitative mechanism gate (ordered 2-step), defensible; but purely biochemical, no mechanics.
- **`VG-MYO-substrate-sequestration` (candidate, latent).** Observable: intact-filament (SMM) phosphorylation is strongly suppressed vs free light chain due to kinase–MHC competitive binding. Value: Total-P native SMM 31.5% vs isolated MRLC 111% (≈3.5× slower); KI(MHC) = 0.30 µM ≈ 10× tighter than Km(MRLC). Engine: activation-oracle. Source: quotes #9,#14,#15, Fig 6/7 p4811–12. Rationale: captures that *assembled* myosin activates slower — physiologically real, but far from current model scope.

## Oracle candidates

**Candidate closed-form (latent, PI-gate).** The paper gives two exact closed forms suitable for an analytic cross-check *if* a phospho-activation layer is built:

1. **Sequential two-step Michaelis–Menten (Model 1):**
   V(0P→1P) = kcat_S19·[E]·[0P] / (Km_S19 + [0P]); V(1P→2P) = kcat_T18·[E]·[1P] / (Km_T18 + [1P]).
   Cross-check: given the 4 constants, the integrated 0P/1P/2P(t) and Total-P(t) curves are deterministic — an activation module could be validated against these ground-truth trajectories.
2. **Competitive MHC sequestration (Model 2):** denominator gains (Km/KI)·[MHC] term — closed-form for the intact-filament slowdown.

These are genuine analytic laws (bulk well-mixed kinetics), but they are **not mechanics oracles** in the sense of Helfrich/Young-Laplace/Hill — they belong to a biochemical-activation sub-layer that does not currently exist. Recommend: hold as a *documented candidate oracle*, do not implement until a myosin-activation-fraction state variable is on the roadmap.
