## Paper kind

**simulation** (primary). The paper is an agent-based Brownian-dynamics model of a quasi-2D myosin **motility assay** (gliding F-actin on a surface of anchored HMM), with a *qualitative* in-vitro TIRF reconstitution as verification. All figure time-series (v_A, v_M, polar order P, nematic S, phase-separation M/M₀, velocity correlation, bound-motor fraction) are **model output**, so under our philosophy they are **comparison-only, never a truth source**. The experimental panel (Fig. 4) is qualitative (short+dense → clusters, long+dilute → bundles) with **no quantitative curve to overlay** — filament-length and concentration values are stated as ranges, not measured observables with error bars. The only genuinely *analytic* content is the standard slender-body drag closed-form (A3) and the order-parameter definitions (Eqs. 1–3), which are candidate cross-checks, not new physics.

**Scale mismatch (decisive):** This is a **surface motility assay in a 50×50×0.1 µm periodic box** — no membrane, no turgor, no cortex tension, no 3D cell shape. It is **not** at our single-cell DCM scale and touches the FF engine only at the level of shared primitives (semiflexible filament + explicit motor + Brownian dynamics + excluded volume). Nothing here maps onto an existing single-cell gate band.

## Coverage vs existing gates

No existing gate covers this paper's observables. Nearest neighbors, and why none match:

- **VG-U5-velocity-correlation** (`372120daec5d81568aeccf1833ae7dee`): defines a velocity-correlation length ξ_v ~50–200 µm in **epithelial monolayers** (cell-scale collective). This paper's ⟨cos α⟩ (Eq. 3) is a **filament-endpoint** velocity correlation within a 1 µm radius in a motility assay — same *name*, entirely different scale and object. **Not the same observable; no confirm/tighten/challenge.**
- **VG-U3-cortex-multifilament** (`372120daec5d81f388e5d43867b7539f`) and **MC-U3-cell-integration**: our cortex is a fine-grained multi-filament network with motors — the FF engine shares the *primitives* used here, but no gate band overlaps (this paper reports emergent active-matter order parameters, not cortical tension in mN/m).
- **PARAM-F_stall_motor / PARAM-v_unloaded / MC-U2-fa-motor-clutch**: our motor model is **Hill force–velocity + Bell-Evans off-rate** (architectural rule). This paper uses the **Parallel Cluster Model (PCM, Erdmann–Schwarz)** — a *different mechanistic motor kinetics scheme*. It neither confirms nor challenges our Hill/Bell-Evans bands; it is an **alternative motor oracle lineage** worth noting but not adopting (our architectural table pins Hill + Bell-Evans as the sanctioned mechanisms).

**Net: 0 confirmed, 0 tightened, 0 challenged.** The paper adds no pressure on any current band.

## New gate candidates

Be conservative — this is a simulation paper off our scale, so nothing here is a defensible single-cell contract. I stage **one** candidate for PI review, and explicitly recommend it **not** become a gate unless the FF engine is ever used to model a reconstituted motility assay.

- **proposed_vg_id: VG-FF-mobile-motor-clustering (CANDIDATE — PI-gate review, LOW priority, likely DECLINE)**
  - observable: emergent polar order P and phase-separation M/M₀ of gliding F-actin as a function of motor mobility D̄_M, in an FF quasi-2D motility-assay reconstruction.
  - band/value: qualitative monotonic trend — mobile motors (D̄_M≳1) → round polar clusters (P ~0.5–0.6, M/M₀ ~3); fixed motors (D̄_M=0) → nematic bundles (P ~0.2–0.3, M/M₀ ~1). Fig. 1d,f, p4.
  - engine: FF (cross-check only).
  - **Why staged, not proposed as a real gate:** (1) target values are *simulation output*, forbidden as a truth source; (2) the assay is not our single-cell physiological baseline (no cortex/turgor/membrane) — validating on it would violate the "validate at native + full compartments" rule; (3) the underlying motor kinetics is PCM, not our Hill/Bell-Evans mechanism, so an FF match would test a *different* motor model. This is a **qualitative sanity direction**, not a contract. **Recommend: do not create a gate; keep as a documented FF cross-reference.**

No other gate candidates. The length/density crossovers (rows 8–9) and order parameters are emergent active-matter phenomenology, not measurable observables with defensible bands at our scale.

## Oracle candidates

1. **Slender-cylinder drag closed-form (candidate analytic cross-check):** ζ = 3πμ·r_c·(3 + 2 r₀/r_c)/5 for a cylindrical segment of length r₀, diameter r_c in medium viscosity μ (Eq. A3, p9). This is a **standard Broersma/slender-body drag law** and could serve as an analytic cross-check for the **per-segment drag** used in the FF engine's overdamped dynamics (compare to Cytosim's drag convention). Useful because our γ-floor / bulk-drag work (memory: FF native crawl "grid-dependent drag Σγ∝Nc") turns on getting per-filament drag right — an independent closed-form for cylindrical-segment drag is a legitimate dimensional/sanity oracle. **Recommend: register as an FF drag cross-check oracle (analytic), NOT a truth band.** Note the ⅗ prefactor and (3 + 2 r₀/r_c) form are geometry-specific — verify against our segment aspect ratio before use.
2. **Order-parameter definitions (Eqs. 1–3):** nematic S = ⟨2cos²θ⟩−1, polar P = ⟨cos θ⟩, velocity correlation ⟨cos α⟩. Standard 2D active-matter estimators — usable as **measurement-protocol references** if FF ever computes filament order parameters, but not oracles in the ground-truth sense (they are definitions, not laws).

No Young-Laplace / Helfrich / Hill / Bell-Evans-class closed form is added or challenged.
