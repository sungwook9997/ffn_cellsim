# TAG-validation lens — p21

**Paper:** Yadav, Banerjee, Tabatabai, Kovar, Kim, Banerjee, Murrell — *Filament Nucleation Tunes Mechanical Memory in Active Polymer Networks*, Adv. Funct. Mater. 2019, 29(49), 1905243. DOI 10.1002/adfm.201905243.

## Paper kind

**experimental** (primary) — quasi-2D in-vitro **reconstitution** of a purified actin/formin "model cortex" (G-actin + profilin + His-mDia1 tethered to an NTA-Ni supported bilayer, methylcellulose-crowded), imaged by spinning-disk confocal + FRAP. Coupled to a **simulation** (stochastic filament-assembly, finite shared monomer pool) and an **analytic** continuum growing-elastica theory.

Consequence for use: every number here is a **reconstitution / overlay / on-demand cross-check** value, NOT a fit target and NOT a truth source (per the "oracle-is-crosscheck-not-truth" and "no-fit-to-experimental" hard rules). It is *not* MCF7 and *not* at our single-cell cortex operating point (25 °C, quasi-2D membrane layer, methylcellulose depletion, no myosin drive in the base condition). The one closed-form worth lifting (filament-arc bending energy) is an analytic **oracle candidate**. All engine relevance is **FF** (fine-grained filaments); DCM relevance is nil.

## Coverage vs existing gates

- **VG-U3-gactin-pool** (`372120daec5d818fa306cc1acaee725e`, KB-3.21, status=blocked, Phase-2 entry): "Total actin mass conserved; lamellipodia self-limiting via c_G depletion." → **CONFIRMS the framework.** This paper's whole point (rows 3–6) is that a *finite shared monomer pool* sets length, number, and the biphasic R₁→R₂ growth crossover (~20 min) via depletion. It is direct experimental + agent-based support for the c_G-conservation contract and its "self-limiting via depletion" clause. Does not tighten the band (the gate has no numeric band); adds an empirical crossover timescale (~20 min at these concentrations) as overlay context. **No band change** — flag only as supporting evidence.
- **VG-U3-cortex-multifilament** (`372120daec5d81f388e5d43867b7539f`, KB-3.18/3.19/3.20): the length/number-vs-nucleator control (rows 3–5) and turnover (rows 8–10) are the mechanistic inputs a multi-filament cortex must reproduce; paper is **supporting evidence**, not a band change.
- **No existing gate covers**: single-filament elongation rate, actin persistence length, F-actin FRAP turnover time, or filament bending-energy. These are FF per-filament observables currently unvalidated → new-candidate territory below.
- **PARAM-ell_p** (`372120daec5d81d7994bf34947e13905`, 17 µm, range 9–22) is **collagen type-I** persistence length, NOT actin. Row 11 (actin l_p ≈15 µm) is a *distinct* constant — do not overwrite; register separately.
- **PARAM-v_unloaded** (retrograde flow, 100 nm/s) is unrelated to barbed-end polymerization velocity (rows 1–2); no conflict.

## New gate candidates (all **FF engine**, all **candidate — PI-gate review**; overlay/cross-check, reconstitution-scale)

> Caveat carried on every candidate: reconstituted minimal system (purified actin/formin, 25 °C, quasi-2D bilayer, methylcellulose crowding) — a per-filament / network cross-check, **never a fit target**, and to be read as an OVERLAY on FF, not a physiological MCF7 contract. These validate FF's barbed-end growth kernel and filament mechanics against a clean reconstituted ground truth.

1. **VG-FF-formin-elongation-twomode** *(candidate)*
   - observable: FF barbed-end elongation velocity, formin-processive vs free mode.
   - band/value: fast (formin+profilin) ~150 subunits s⁻¹ (≈0.37 µm/s, tol ±30%); slow (profilin-actin, post-release) ~10 subunits s⁻¹ (≈0.027 µm/s, tol ±40%).
   - condition: single filament, saturating profilin, 25 °C.
   - source: Fig 1B,C (p<10⁻⁷ separation). Cross-checks the FF barbed-end growth kernel (memory: project-ff-filament-length-growth / KB-3.6 ratchet).

2. **VG-FF-actin-persistence-length** *(candidate)*
   - observable: emergent bending persistence length l_p of an FF actin filament from thermal shape fluctuations, l_p = κ/k_BT.
   - band/value: 15 µm (accept 9–20; canonical Gittes 1993 15–18 µm).
   - condition: single F-actin, thermal, no load.
   - source: Param table (cites Gittes 1993 [25]). A clean FF sanity/verification gate on filament stiffness parameterization.

3. **VG-FF-actin-FRAP-turnover** *(candidate, weaker)*
   - observable: F-actin network turnover time τ from FRAP-analog (fluorescence/occupancy recovery) in an FF cortex patch.
   - band/value: order 1–20 min, **decreasing-then-increasing (non-monotonic) with nucleation density**: 1.7 / 21.8 / 12.6 min at 10 / 100 / 1000 nM (large scatter ±60–80%).
   - condition: finite shared monomer pool, formin-nucleated.
   - source: Fig 2D. Weaker as a contract (wide error bars, concentration-specific); best used as a qualitative *non-monotonic ordering* check + overlay, not a tight band.

Not proposing gates for the τ₂/τ₁ decoupling (row 16), K₃₃ (row 14), or bending-energy magnitude (row 12) as *contracts*: the mechanical-memory / nematic-defect regime is specific to the intermediate-nucleation reconstitution and has no clean analog at the MCF7 single-cell cortex operating point. Bending energy is better handled as an oracle (below).

## Oracle candidates

- **Filament-arc bending energy (analytic cross-check):** `E_bend = (κ/2)·(L/R²)` with `κ = k_B T · l_p` (Eq. S2). This is a closed-form ground-truth for the elastic energy stored in a curved FF filament of contour length L bent to radius R — directly implementable as an FF **oracle** cross-check (the filament analog of the existing Helfrich `κL/2R²` membrane-bending oracle; same functional form). Use: given an FF filament's measured (L, R, l_p), assert stored bending energy matches `k_BT·l_p·L/(2R²)`. Ground-truth-primary, no reconstitution number needed. **Strongest single takeaway of this lens for the oracle layer.**
- Not oracle-grade: the continuum growing-elastica buckling law (⟨R⟩/⟨R_max⟩ peaks at intermediate N_f/N_p) — full form lives only in the SI Notes and is regime-specific; record as qualitative model context, not a cross-check.
