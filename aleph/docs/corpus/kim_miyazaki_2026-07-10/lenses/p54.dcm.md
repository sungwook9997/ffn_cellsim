# DCM lens — p54

**Paper:** Sakata, Matsubara, Gomi, Miyazaki (2026) *Lipid composition effects on the number and size of liposomes formed by the inverted emulsion method*, Biophys. J. 125:731–744. DOI 10.1016/j.bpj.2025.12.013.

**Bottom line for DCM:** This is a purely experimental in-vitro **reconstitution-method** study (Miyazaki lab). It quantifies how phospholipid headgroup charge and molecular weight change the **yield (number)** and **size** of protein-free giant liposomes made by droplet transfer. It contains **no governing physics model, no bending modulus, no membrane tension, no cortex, no active mechanics, and no continuum constitutive law** — only two empirical exponential fit curves for temperature/time trends and a qualitative curvature cartoon (Fig. 7). Consequently there is essentially nothing to *port* into DCM's surface-mesh/actomyosin/turgor physics. The only genuinely transferable items are (a) the cell-sized (5–40 µm) vesicle geometry that matches DCM's single-cell scale, (b) the qualitative link between headgroup size and local membrane spontaneous curvature (a passive-membrane notion DCM does not currently resolve), and (c) reconstitution-protocol metadata useful only if the project ever encapsulates a DCM/FF cytoskeleton in a real liposome. **Relevance to the DCM engine is low (1/5).** I flag the marginal levers honestly below rather than manufacture applicability.

---

## 1. Mechanisms to adopt

Almost none apply. Assessed candidly:

- **(Marginal, optional) Headgroup-dependent spontaneous curvature c₀ as a passive membrane property.** Fig. 7 argues large headgroups (steric hindrance) and small headgroups (negative-curvature stabilization) both bias the local membrane curvature at the liposome "neck." DCM's membrane force is currently area-elasticity K_A + bending κ_m with **implicit c₀ = 0** (Helfrich with zero spontaneous curvature). If DCM ever needs to represent an asymmetric-leaflet or lipid-sorting effect, this paper motivates a per-face spontaneous-curvature term c₀ in the bending energy. **Why it does NOT beat what DCM does now:** DCM's cortical tension is actomyosin-generated (γ = ΔP·R/2, myosin-borne), not lipid-composition-borne; a passive c₀ term addresses a phenomenon (bare-lipid neck detachment during vesicle *formation*) that DCM never simulates. There is no MCF7 c₀ datum here to set the value, so adopting it would violate the no-magic-number rule. **Recommendation: do not adopt now; note as a latent extension only.**

- **(Not a mechanism, a caveat) The membrane in DCM is not a bare bilayer.** This paper's entire physics is the bare-lipid monolayer→bilayer zipping and neck rupture. DCM's membrane is a coarse continuum shell coupled to a cortex; none of the neck/detachment/transfer mechanics has a DCM analogue. Nothing to adopt.

**Net:** no DCM module (membrane K_A/κ_m, cortex γ, cytoplasm η/Π₀, nucleus, contact/cadherin, remesh, T1) gains a mechanism from this paper.

## 2. Equations to port

**None are physics equations.** The paper contains only two empirical phenomenological fits, neither of which is a mechanistic law and neither maps to DCM state:

1. Yield vs centrifugation temperature (Fig. 4B): `y = a·exp(−b·x)`, a = 68.6 liposomes/mm², b = 0.0855 °C⁻¹, x = T [°C]. — A count-vs-temperature trend for a fabrication process. No DCM symbol (γ, Π, η, K_A, κ_m, nodes, faces) corresponds to it. **Do not port.**
2. Yield vs pre-centrifugation incubation time (Fig. 4D): `y = c − a·exp(−b·x)`, a = 25.6, b = 0.249 min⁻¹, c = 102 liposomes/mm², x = t [min]. — A monolayer-saturation kinetics summary for interface preparation. **Do not port.**

The Helfrich/spontaneous-curvature idea from Fig. 7 is stated only in words; the paper gives **no functional form** for it, so there is no equation to transcribe.

## 3. Parameters to set

**None supersede any DCM config field.** All parameters are for the vesicle-fabrication protocol (lipid-in-oil concentration 1 mM, 12,000×g/30 min, A50 buffer, sucrose/glucose density steps, egg PC mean MW 770.123 Da, headgroup MW/charge table). They feed no DCM force term. Specifically:

- egg PC mean MW = 770.123 Da, headgroup MW (96–2177 Da) and net charge (0 or −1) per lipid — **membrane-chemistry inputs with no DCM continuum equivalent**; DCM does not resolve individual lipids or leaflet asymmetry.
- Δρ (inner vs outer ≈ 24.3 mg/mL; inner vs lipid-oil ≈ 282 mg/mL) — density steps that drive sedimentation in the assay; DCM has no oil phase and its gravity/buoyancy is the cell's own ρ, not a lipid-oil emulsion.
- Cell-sized diameter range **5–40 µm, counting threshold ≥5 µm** — the *only* number that overlaps DCM's regime, and only as a geometry sanity bound (see §4), not a config value.

No field in the DCM YAML (K_A, κ_m, γ_interphase = 0.15 mN/m, γ_mitotic = 1.5 mN/m, η = 65 Pa·s, Π₀ = 40 Pa, E_nuc, cadherin catch-bond) is informed or superseded.

## 4. Geometry / protocol to reproduce

- **(Weak, geometry only) Cell-sized spherical vesicle, R ≈ 5–20 µm, passive membrane, no cortex.** The paper's liposomes are the *R = 7.5 µm single-cell* scale DCM targets. A **passive-membrane-only DCM limit test** — a closed mesh with cortex γ→0 and myosin off, held spherical purely by bending κ_m + area K_A + enclosed-volume turgor Π₀ — would be dimensionally comparable to one of these liposomes. Useful as a **degenerate baseline** (does the shell stay a smooth sphere with the cortex disabled?), not as a validation target with a number attached.
- **No aspiration, AFM, division, durotaxis, or aggregate geometry** is present in this paper to reproduce. The only "protocol" is emulsion transfer, which DCM does not model.

## 5. Validation targets for DCM observables

**None.** The paper's measured quantities are: liposomes per mm² (yield), yield fold-changes (3–14×), diameter distributions, droplet→liposome transformation efficiency, and temperature/time trends. **None is a DCM observable** — there is no tension, shape index, division force, ring-closure rate, compaction fraction, contact angle, or cortical-flow speed. The size distribution (5–40 µm, mode ~15–25 µm) is the closest thing, and it only confirms that the studied objects live at DCM's length scale; it is a fabrication-yield statistic, not a mechanical target. Do **not** register any number from this paper as a DCM gate.

## 6. Risks / caveats

- **Regime mismatch (fundamental):** in-vitro **purified bare-lipid vesicles being manufactured**, vs. DCM's **living MCF7 cell at physiological baseline**. The paper has no cortex, no myosin, no turgor set point, no nucleus — the entire physiological-baseline stack DCM mandates is absent. Any "adoption" would import a non-physiological zero-cortex object, directly conflicting with the physiological-baseline HARD rule.
- **Different physics question:** the paper is about *vesicle formation kinetics/yield* (neck rupture, monolayer zipping, sedimentation), a process DCM never simulates — DCM starts from an already-formed, already-pressurized cell. There is no shared dynamical variable.
- **No mechanistic parameters:** zero bending modulus, zero tension, zero viscosity for the bilayer — so even the one conceptually-adjacent idea (spontaneous curvature c₀) cannot be set without inventing a magic number, which is forbidden.
- **How to adapt without violating the baseline rule:** the correct adaptation is **not to adapt**. If DCM later needs leaflet-asymmetry/spontaneous-curvature membrane physics, source c₀ and κ_m from a dedicated bilayer-mechanics paper (e.g. an X-ray/flicker-spectroscopy κ measurement), not from this yield study. This paper's role in the project is **reconstitution-method / KB-provenance metadata** (Miyazaki-lab inverted-emulsion constants), relevant only to a future "cytoskeleton-in-a-liposome" reconstitution — not to the DCM engine.

---

**Verdict:** No mechanism, equation, parameter, or validation target from p54 advances the DCM surface-mesh engine. Logged as low-relevance; the sole latent hook is a passive spontaneous-curvature membrane term that is neither needed nor parameterizable now.
