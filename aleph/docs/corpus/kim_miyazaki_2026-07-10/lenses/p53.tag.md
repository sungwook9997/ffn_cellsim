# TAG-validation lens — p53

**Paper:** Costache et al. 2022, *Rapid assembly of a polar network architecture drives efficient actomyosin contractility*, Cell Reports 39(9):110868. DOI 10.1016/j.celrep.2022.110868.
**System:** early *C. elegans* embryo cortex (1–4 cell). **Lab:** Robin (experiment) + **Taeyoon Kim** (agent-based actomyosin model, Fig 6 / Table S4).

---

## Paper kind

**experimental** (primary validation-grade content = single-molecule TIRF measurements of formin kinetics), with two secondary layers:
- an **analytic** layer — a closed-form filament-length law and a barbed-end half-life estimate (→ candidate oracles), and
- a **simulation** layer — the canonical Kim Brownian-dynamics agent-based actomyosin model with a complete parameter table (Table S4). Its outputs (tensile forces, contraction ratios) are **comparison-only, never a truth source**; but its inputs are lit-sourced constants (Ferrer 2008, Isambert 1995, Meyer & Aebi 1990) usable as Parameter rows.

Use rule: measured formin/actin kinetics → **overlay/cross-check** targets for FF (model-organism cortex, not MCF7 — universal actin biophysics, so defensible as a cross-check, not a fit target). Simulated forces → comparison only.

**Engine relevance: FF (strong-yes)** — this is a Cytosim-class fine-grained actomyosin engine that maps directly onto FF's filament-beam + Bell-Evans crosslink clutch + PCM/Hill motor architecture and its formin/barbed-end-growth kernel. **DCM (partial)** — RhoA-pulse / cortical-flow phenomenology only.

## Oracle candidates

- **A. Filament-length law (min-of-two-exponentials)** — `L = V_elong / (1/τ_actin + 1/τ_formin)`. Closed-form: elongated length = elongation speed × effective off-time, where effective off-rate is the sum of actin-monomer turnover and formin detachment (whichever ends growth first). **Strong analytic-oracle candidate for FF**: given FF's elongation velocity and turnover rates, predicts the emergent mean filament length (~6 µm here) as a ground-truth cross-check on FF's length distribution. Mechanistic, grid-invariant, no free fit.
- **B. Barbed-end half-life** — `t½ = ln2/(k_on·[F])` (≈3 s at k_on=0.029 nM⁻¹s⁻¹, [F]=10 nM). Simple self-consistency oracle for barbed-end saturation kinetics; lower value than A, useful as a sanity relation.
- **C. Bell's law** `k_u = k_u⁰·exp(F·x_u/k_BT)` — **already an oracle** (Bell-Evans) in the KB; this paper only instantiates it with ACP constants. No new oracle.
- **D. MSD anomalous-diffusion `MSD = 2D·tᵅ`** — a *classification method* (α cutoffs), not a physics ground-truth; note as a diagnostic, not an oracle.
