# DCM lens — p33

**Paper:** Miyazaki, Kinosita & Shiroguchi (2013), "Accurate polarity control and parallel alignment of actin filaments for myosin-powered transport systems," *RSC Adv.* 3, 8728–8733. DOI 10.1039/c3ra41112e.

**Verdict up front: near-zero DCM applicability (relevance 0.5/5).** This is an in-vitro reconstitution + nanofabrication method paper. It builds an isopolar, flow-aligned array of purified actin filaments on a glass coverslip (gelsolin plus-end capping + biotin/streptavidin cross-linking + shear-flow orientation), and reads out polarity with myosin-V-coated beads. There is **no cell, no membrane, no cortex-as-continuum, no turgor/osmotic pressure, no enclosed-volume mechanics, no surface-mesh, no governing PDE/ODE, no constitutive law, and no motor force–velocity model.** The DCM engine models a cell as a closed triangulated *surface* enclosing a volume; this paper models neither a surface nor a volume. Nothing here ports to the DCM force stack. The sections below record the (thin) usable residue honestly rather than manufacture leverage.

---

## 1. MECHANISMS to adopt
**None for DCM.** No DCM module (membrane K_A/κ_m, cortex γ, cytoplasm η/Π, nucleus E_nuc, node-face contact, cadherin junction, aggregate-σ, remesh, T1) has a counterpart here.

- The paper's only "mechanisms" are (a) gelsolin plus-end capping/nucleation, (b) biotin–streptavidin (and α-actinin) cross-linking, (c) shear-flow filament orientation, and (d) myosin-V processive gliding. These are single-filament / crosslinker / motor-track phenomena. DCM does not resolve individual cortical filaments (that is the FF engine's domain, and even there this paper supplies only constants, not dynamics). At the DCM continuum/surface scale, actin polarity, filament alignment σ, and single-motor run length are sub-grid and lumped into γ — so there is nothing to graft.
- The nearest *adjacent* DCM open problem is **cortical-tension γ generation**. This paper does NOT help it: it gives no myosin stall force, no duty ratio, no Hill relation, no filament areal-tension datum, no actomyosin contractility measurement. Myosin-V is a *cargo-transport* motor, not the *tension-generating* non-muscle myosin II minifilament that sets cortical γ. Do not import its 0.62 µm/s into any γ or contractility term.

## 2. EQUATIONS to port
**None.** The paper contains no dynamical equations, potentials, or constitutive laws (dossier "Governing Equations: None"). The only relation stated is a trivial geometric proportionality used for density bookkeeping:

- (G1) line-density ∝ area-density (used to convert 0.2 µM → 2.9 µm⁻¹). This is a counting convenience, not a mechanics equation, and maps to **no** DCM state (nodes, faces, γ, Π, η, K_A, κ_m).

If one insists on a *cross-check* identity (not from this paper, but consistent with its cited constants): the FF-side bending stiffness κ = k_B T · l_p. That belongs to the FF filament lens, not DCM — DCM has no per-filament bending; membrane bending κ_m is a separate surface quantity and is unrelated to actin l_p.

## 4. GEOMETRY / PROTOCOL to reproduce
**No DCM scenario.** The setup is a quasi-2D glass flowchamber (~50 µm spacers, ~5 mm gap, ~15 µL, shear flow ~15 µL/s) with filaments laid flat in the image plane. There is no confinement of a *cell*, no aspiration, no AFM indentation, no division, no aggregate, no durotaxis — nothing mappable to a DCM single-cell or N≈400 aggregate scenario. The shear-flow-alignment protocol acts on free filaments in solution, not on a cell surface, so it does not even serve as a membrane-shear or flow-BC template at our scale.

## 6. RISKS / caveats
- **Regime mismatch is total, not partial.** Purified in-vitro proteins on glass vs. a living MCF7-like cell; 2D substrate vs. 3D closed surface + enclosed volume; single filaments/crosslinkers vs. continuum cortex; myosin-V transport motor vs. myosin-II tension motor. Every axis differs.
- **Physiological-baseline rule:** importing any of these constants into a DCM tension/contractility term would inject a non-physiological, wrong-mechanism value (a cargo-motor speed or a synthetic biotin crosslink) — a direct violation. The correct action is to import **nothing** into DCM.
- **Where this paper legitimately lives:** it is a FF-lens / TAG-citation source only — actin l_p = 15–20 µm and diameter 6 nm as filament-bending / EV cross-checks, and myosin-V 0.62 µm/s as a transport-motor sanity reference. Even there it is NOT a myosin force-velocity or contractility source. For DCM specifically, file it as **not applicable** and spend no engine effort on it.
