# TAG-validation lens — p54

**Paper:** Sakata, Matsubara, Gomi, Miyazaki (2026) — "Lipid composition effects on the number and size of liposomes formed by the inverted emulsion method." *Biophys. J.* 125, 731–744. DOI 10.1016/j.bpj.2025.12.013.

## Paper kind

**experimental** (in-vitro reconstitution / methods-optimization study). Miyazaki lab, bottom-up synthetic biology. It measures the production **yield (number/field or /mm²)** and **size distribution (diameter)** of cell-sized giant liposomes made by the inverted-emulsion (droplet-transfer) method, as a function of phospholipid composition, temperature, and incubation time. No governing dynamical model, no force law, no constitutive law — the only equations are two empirical exponential *fit* curves used to summarize trends. Under our philosophy this makes every number here at best an **overlay/cross-check target**, never a fit target — and in practice none of them map onto a single-cell mechanobiology observable we simulate.

## Coverage vs existing gates

**No overlap with any current gate.** Our ValidationGates cover: ECM network mechanics (G0, σ(r), strain-stiffening, Mikado topology), FA motor-clutch (traction, catch-slip), cortex/whole-cell tension (VG-H3-KU35, VG-H7-gate-a/b — the 0.35–0.65 mN/m γ band), the additive compartment modules (H.8 membrane tether/tension, H.9 nucleus modulus, H.10 cytoplasm viscoelasticity), junction cadherin, and the U5 collective/necrosis spheroid gates. Every one of these is a **mechanical/kinetic observable of a live single cell or its ECM**.

This paper contributes **none** of those. It has:
- no membrane bending modulus κ_m, no membrane/apparent tension, no K_A → does **not** touch VG-H8-membrane-tension (the closest gate by subject). It is protein-free vesicle *formation* yield, not membrane elasticity.
- no cortex, no myosin, no actin → nothing for VG-H3/VG-H7 or the cortex contract.
- no adhesion/clutch/junction kinetics → nothing for U2/U4.

The one nominal adjacency — cell-sized (5–40 µm) unilamellar vesicle geometry — is a target *container* for a future encapsulated-cytoskeleton reconstitution, not a validation observable. **CONFIRMS / TIGHTENS / CHALLENGES: none.**

## New gate candidates

**None proposed.** Every measured quantity is a technique-yield or count statistic specific to the inverted-emulsion protocol (a lab production metric), not a defensible single-cell mechanobiology observable. Fabricating a "liposome yield" gate would violate the philosophy: it is (a) not measurable in DCM/FF (we do not simulate droplet-transfer emulsion physics — no oil phase, no monolayer-zipping, no sedimentation), (b) not an analytic ground truth, and (c) an experimental protocol number that could only ever be an overlay, of which we have no producing run. Nothing here is a contract-grade observable at our scale.

## Param candidates

**None for the runtime.** Values here are reconstitution-protocol constants (1 mM lipid-in-oil, 12,000×g/30 min, A50 buffer, sucrose/glucose density steps, egg PC MW 770.123 Da, lipid head-MW/charge table). They anchor no runtime parameter — DCM/FF carry no lipid-chemistry, monolayer, or emulsion parameters. If the project ever reconstitutes an encapsulated cytoskeleton in a GUV, these become **methods/reference metadata** (a protocol card), not a `parameter` row that feeds a gate. Recommend: register as SourceEvidence/methods provenance only, **not** as a Parameter. No conflict with, and no supersession of, any existing Parameter row.

## Oracle candidates

**None.** The two closed forms (yield-vs-T exponential a·e^(−bx); yield-vs-time saturating c − a·e^(−bx)) are **empirical phenomenological fits** to a specific protocol's output, not mechanistic closed-form laws with derivable constants. They fail the oracle test on two counts: the constants are fit, not first-principles (a=68.6, b=0.0855, etc. are magic numbers by our rule), and they describe an emulsion-transfer process we do not model. They are not candidate cross-checks for FF/DCM.
