# TAG-validation lens — p60

**Paper:** Mak, Zaman, Kamm & Kim (2016) "Interplay of active processes modulates tension and drives phase transition in self-renewing, motor-driven cytoskeletal networks." *Nat Commun* 7:10323. DOI 10.1038/ncomms10323.

---

## Paper kind

**Hybrid → classified `simulation` (primary), with an embedded `experimental` overlay layer.**

The paper's central quantitative product is the output of a **3D Brownian-dynamics agent model** (Taeyoon-Kim-lab engine, refs 35–39) — the stress-vs-time curves, phase maps (peak stress, sustainability S, clustering factor CF), and the rate-ratio master-curve collapse are all **model outputs**. Under our philosophy these are **comparison-only, NEVER a truth source** — they are an *emergent-behavior* target for the FF engine (same physics class), not a band we fit to.

Embedded within it are **live-cell fluorescence measurements** (MDA-MB-231 LifeAct, drug perturbation) that yield two genuinely *measured* time constants (τC, τD) — these are **experimental overlay** targets. A **reduced "active kinetic spring" analytic model** (Suppl. Note 6) is present but is a qualitative constitutive reduction, **not a closed-form oracle** (no clean law we can implement as a cross-check; the only transcribed closed forms are two empirical sigmoid fits).

Consequence for use: emergent BD behaviors → FF qualitative/scaling comparison gates (no imposed magnitude band from simulation); τC/τD and the TFM stress reference → overlay-only.

## New gate candidates (all: candidate, PI-gate review)

**G1 — FF: actin turnover stabilizes the network against motor-driven aggregation (qualitative emergent).**
- proposed_vg_id: `VG-FF-turnover-antiaggregation`
- observable: network morphology state (homogeneous vs aggregated) + sustained internal prestress as a function of actin treadmilling rate, at fixed ACP% and motor%.
- band/value: **QUALITATIVE contract** — above a critical turnover rate the FF cortex stays homogeneous with *sustained* prestress; below it the network collapses into actomyosin foci with a transient stress peak that then decays. Sustained prestress **decreases monotonically** with turnover rate. NO magnitude band imposed (Mak's 300→0 Pa is simulation, comparison-only).
- engine: FF (native cortex; turnover + Bell ACP + PCM/Hill motors already present).
- source: Fig 2a, Fig 3a, p4–5. "below a critical turnover rate the network collapses into aggregates; above it, it stays homogeneous."

**G2 — FF: critical-turnover-rate phase boundary scaling.**
- proposed_vg_id: `VG-FF-critical-turnover-boundary`
- observable: critical turnover rate k_crit vs crosslinker:motor ratio.
- band/value: **QUALITATIVE/SCALING** — k_crit **decreases with ↑ACP** (→ ~0 as ACP:motor → ~100) and **increases with ↑motor%**. Directional contract, not a numeric band.
- engine: FF.
- source: p4, "critical turnover rate falls with more ACPs (→0 near ACP:motor ≈ 100) and rises with more motors."

**G3 — FF/DCM: pulsed actomyosin-foci formation/dissolution timescale (experimental overlay).**
- proposed_vg_id: `VG-FF-foci-pulse-timescale`
- observable: characteristic time for actomyosin cluster formation (τC) and dissolution (τD) under turnover suppression/restoration.
- band/value (OVERLAY-ONLY, never fit): formation **τC ≈ 28 ± 5 s**, dissolution **τD ≈ 155 ± 42 s** (MDA-MB-231, 2.5 µM CytoD); consistent with a **~100 s** pulse timescale. Note formation ≈ 5–6× faster than dissolution (asymmetry is the testable shape).
- engine: FF (foci emergence from turnover pulsing); DCM cortex-pulse relevance.
- caveat: MDA-MB-231, not MCF7; drug-perturbed (turnover blocked), so it probes the *no-turnover* aggregation branch, not the physiological homogeneous baseline. Overlay context only.
- source: Fig 7d,e, p10–11; Fig 4, p6.

**G4 (weak, optional) — FF: rate-ratio master-curve collapse (scaling cross-check).**
- proposed_vg_id: `VG-FF-turnover-motor-ratio-collapse`
- observable: peak-stress-vs-turnover-rate curves at different motor-walking rates.
- band/value: **dimensionless scaling** — curves collapse onto one master curve when turnover rate is scaled by the relative motor-walking rate (constant turnover:walking ratio ⇒ same stress profile). No magnitude.
- engine: FF.
- source: Suppl Fig 9, p6. Flag: derived from simulation; treat as a self-consistency/scaling check, not a lab target.

All four are **measurable at our single-cell FF scale** (native cortex has the three active processes) and defensible as *qualitative/scaling* contracts. None imposes a fit magnitude. **PI authors gates; these are staged candidates only.**

## Oracle candidates

**None strong.** 
- The only transcribed closed forms (Eqs 1 & 2) are **empirical sigmoid fits** used to extract τC/τD — curve-fitting artifacts, not mechanistic laws; not oracle material.
- The "active kinetic spring" reduced model (Suppl Note 6) is a qualitative constitutive reduction (effective k ∝ number of bound springs; wall force = Σ contractile forces), reproduces phase maps *qualitatively* only — no clean analytic law with a defensible ground-truth value to cross-check against.
- The mechanistic laws it *uses* — Bell's equation off-rate (ref 41) and Erdmann-Schwarz parallel-cluster-model / Hill-type motor kinetics (ref 42) — are **already oracles in our system** (Bell-Evans, Hill). This paper adds no new closed form.
