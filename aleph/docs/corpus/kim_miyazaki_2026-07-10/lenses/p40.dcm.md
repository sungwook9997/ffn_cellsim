# DCM lens — p40

**Paper:** Zhou, Jung, Farhana, Fujimoto, Kim, Yokokawa (2022) *ACS Nano* 16, 14765−14778 — "Durability of Aligned Microtubules Dependent on Persistence Length Determines Phase Transition and Pattern Formation in Collective Motion." DOI 10.1021/acsnano.2c05593.

**Up-front honesty (regime mismatch is severe).** This is an **in-vitro 2D MT–kinesin gliding-motility assay** + agent-based Brownian-dynamics of self-propelled filaments on a glass surface. There is **no closed cell shell, no turgor/osmotic pressure, no cortical surface tension, no cadherin junction, no volume constraint** — none of DCM's actual primitives. The physics is nematic collective motion of purified microtubules. The dossier's own flag is "DCM? No (weak/indirect); FF? Yes." I concur: this is a **FF-engine paper** (Cytosim-style beams + motors + Langevin), not a DCM surface-continuum paper. Its DCM value is (a) **one transferable conceptual principle** (durability, not formation-probability, sets the emergent timescale — which directly reinforces DCM's open cadherin-T1-compaction framing), (b) **microtubule bending/persistence-length parameters** if/when DCM's `--microtubules` inclusion is given an explicit bending law, and (c) **one measurement-protocol analog** (coupling-time = bond-lifetime) for cadherin junctions. Everything below is scoped to those. **Overall DCM impact: LOW.** Do not build a DCM production run off this paper; register it as parameter/concept provenance and route the mechanistic content to the FF lens.

## 1. MECHANISMS to adopt

**M1 — Durability-not-probability principle for the cadherin junction / aggregate compaction rate (CONCEPTUAL, high transfer value).**
The paper's central, cleanly-established result: three filament groups have **identical alignment/bundle-*formation* probability** (~30% snuggle probability, dispersion η_d = 16.3°, crossover ~70% — all independent of persistence length Lp and depletant MC), yet form radically different large-scale patterns. What differs is the **durability (coupling time / disintegration time) of the aligned state**. In the agent-based model (Fig 7e): *formation* time is statistically equal (p>0.05) between soft and stiff filaments, but *disintegration* time differs (p<0.05), and disintegration time — not formation — is what sets bundle size and the emergent phase-transition rate.
- **DCM module it touches:** cell–cell cadherin catch-bond junction (Rakshit KU-4.2, load-sharing clusters, contact-age maturation) and the **loose→compact aggregate compaction RATE** open problem.
- **Why it beats what DCM does now:** DCM's memory record ([[project-dcm-compaction-turgor-blocked]], [[project-dcm-ipc-large-dt]]) already found that aggregate-σ compaction reaches the correct mechanical *end-state* but its *rate* is drag-limited (~100 s) whereas real MCF7 compaction is 24–48 h and requires **cadherin T1 rearrangement escalation**, not σ-pull. This paper is an **independent, quantitative precedent from the same lab (Taeyoon Kim)** that in a coupled active system the emergent timescale is governed by the **lifetime/disintegration of the coupled state**, not by how easily it forms. That is exactly the reframing the DCM open item needs: the compaction rate-limiter must be modeled as a **junction disintegration/rearrangement law (durability)**, and tuning junction *formation* (engagement probability, contact-age maturation gain) will NOT move the compaction rate — mirroring "formation time equal, disintegration time differs." It de-risks the (b) T1-rearrangement direction and warns against tuning the wrong knob.
- **Concrete DCM change:** when the T1/rearrangement escalation is scoped, make the compaction-rate observable depend on a **junction lifetime τ_junction** (governed by catch-bond off-rate + neighbor-swap rate), and validate that *engagement-side* parameters leave the rate invariant — a direct port of Fig 7e's "formation invariant, disintegration decisive" test as a DCM sanity gate.

**M2 — Bending-rigidity ↔ orientational-noise coupling for the microtubule inclusion (PARAMETER-level, low-moderate transfer).**
Tip thermal fluctuation ∝ 1/Lp; larger Lp = stiffer filament = smaller orientational noise = longer-lived coupling. If DCM's native `--microtubules` compartment (used in the structurally-stable native cell) is ever given an **explicit Euler–Bernoulli bending law** rather than a lumped stiffener, this paper supplies the **measured MT persistence length** (0.65–3.54 mm) to set the bending modulus κ = Lp·k_BT (see §2, Eq 3). This is the only place a *number* from this paper feeds a DCM force law.
- **Why it beats current:** DCM currently treats microtubules as a stiff inclusion without a literature-anchored bending modulus per unit length; Lp gives one (van den Heuvel / Zhou-2021 lineage). Note this is native-scale (mm Lp ≫ cell), so it enters as a stiffness parameter, not a shape driver.

**M3 — Depletion-force bundling as a weak attractive cohesion (REJECT for DCM, note only).** MC depletion force = 0.11 pN — explicitly weaker than the kinesin stall force 5–7 pN, and the paper argues it acts on *durability* not *formation*. This is a purified-colloid mechanism with **no MCF7 physiological analog**; do NOT introduce a depletion-attraction term into DCM's aggregate-σ or junction model (would be a non-physiological magic-number force). Recorded here only so it is not mistaken for an adhesion mechanism.

## 2. EQUATIONS to port

Only **Eq 3** is genuinely portable to DCM, and only as the thermal↔bending equipartition relation (it is generic Euler–Bernoulli, not MT-specific). The nematic-order (Eq 4) and skewness (Eq 5–6) are 2D-image collective-motion metrics with **no DCM observable** and are NOT ported. The core Brownian-dynamics update, propelling-force form, extensional/bending/excluded-volume potentials live in the **SI (Table S7, Fig S6a) — not in this text** and are FF-engine content anyway.

**Eq 3 (port target) — persistence length from bending-mode equipartition:**
```
L_p = (1 / var(a_n)) · (L / q_n)^4
```
Rearranged to the DCM-usable form via L_p = κ / k_BT (κ = bending modulus, EI):
```
κ_bend = L_p · k_BT
```
- **Symbol → DCM state mapping:**
  - `L_p` (persistence length, 0.65–3.54 mm) → sets the **microtubule-inclusion bending modulus** κ_bend if that compartment gets an explicit beam law. NOT the membrane bending κ_m (membrane bending is a separate literature anchor; do not overwrite κ_m with an MT number).
  - `k_BT` at T = 298 K (assay 25 °C) = 4.11 × 10⁻²¹ J → the thermal-energy scale for any DCM Langevin/BAOAB bending-fluctuation term.
  - `L` = filament length (9.6 µm here) → segment length in an explicit-MT model; **not** a surface-mesh quantity.
  - `q_n` = clamped-free beam eigenvalue roots 1.875, 4.695, 7.855, (n−0.5)π — only needed if DCM ever *measures* Lp from mode fluctuations (validation), not for the forward model.
- **What it does NOT map to:** DCM's γ (cortical tension), Π (turgor), K_A (area elasticity), η (cytoplasm viscosity), or node/face penalty forces. There is no surface-continuum equation in this paper to port to those.

**Eqs 4/5/6 (NOT ported):** S (nematic order) and Sk (skewness) are pixel-orientation statistics of a 2D filament field. DCM's compartments do not produce a directional filament field at the surface-continuum level; these have no DCM analog. Recorded as absent by design.

## 4. GEOMETRY / PROTOCOL to reproduce

**None are DCM scenarios.** The paper's setups — (a) high-density collective gliding (ρ ≈ 5.5 filaments/µm², 2D), (b) low-density binary-collision assay (ρ ≈ 0.022 filaments/µm²), (c) 100×100 µm 2D agent-based domain — are **2D open-surface filament fields with no cell boundary, no volume, no confinement**. They are not confinement/aspiration/AFM/division/aggregate/durotaxis geometries and cannot be run as DCM cell scenarios.

**One transferable *protocol analog* (measurement, not geometry):**
- **P1 — Coupling-time / bond-lifetime protocol (Fig 6a).** Coupling time is defined as the interval between contact moment **t0** and the moment the coupled tips **deviate (t1)**. Port this as the **cadherin-junction lifetime measurement** in DCM: t0 = node-face contact establishment / catch-bond engagement, t1 = junction disengagement (cluster m→0). This gives a clean, literature-precedented **τ_junction observable** for the durability-side validation of M1, and the "formation time vs disintegration time" separation (Fig 7e) becomes a **two-observable DCM sanity gate**: measure τ_form (engagement) and τ_disint (lifetime) separately, and show the compaction rate tracks τ_disint, not τ_form.

## 6. RISKS / caveats

- **R1 — Wrong engine.** This is fundamentally an FF paper (fine-grained filaments + motors + Langevin/excluded-volume). Routing its mechanistic content into DCM's surface-continuum would be a category error. The mechanistic equations that matter (extensional/bending/excluded-volume/propelling potentials) are **in the SI (Table S7), not this text**, and belong to the FF lens. **Adapt for DCM only the M1 principle and the P1 protocol analog.**
- **R2 — Purified in-vitro ≠ living MCF7.** Purified porcine-brain tubulin + E. coli kinesin on piranha-cleaned glass at 25 °C, no cytoplasm (η=water, not 65 Pa·s), no turgor, no membrane. Any number imported (Lp) is a **material property of the filament** that survives context transfer; any *process* (depletion bundling, gliding, nematic ordering) is regime-specific and must NOT be imported as a DCM force. Importing the depletion-force attraction (0.11 pN) would **violate the physiological-baseline rule** (no MCF7 depletant exists) and inject a magic number.
- **R3 — 2D, no confinement, no volume.** All geometry is planar with free boundaries. There is no cell-scale confinement BC, no enclosed-volume constraint — so nothing in §4 is a DCM scenario. Do not attempt to reproduce nematic patterns in a DCM cell; the mechanism does not exist there.
- **R4 — Scale mismatch of Lp.** MT Lp = 0.65–3.54 mm ≫ cell R = 7.5 µm. At cell scale an MT is effectively a rigid rod; Lp enters as a **stiffness parameter of the microtubule inclusion**, never as a shape-determining flexibility. Framing it as an orientational-noise driver (as the paper does for gliding) is invalid inside a cell.
- **R5 — Correlational Lp→pattern link.** The paper's Lp is co-varied with lattice-defect density (growth-rate control); the mechanistic isolation rests on the simulation. For DCM purposes only the **durability-vs-formation dissociation** is robust and transferable; the specific Lp→pattern mapping is not a DCM claim.

**Adaptation that respects the hard rules:** take (i) the M1 durability principle to *frame and gate* the cadherin-T1 compaction-rate work already open in DCM (no new force, no magic number — it constrains which knob to model), (ii) the P1 t0→t1 lifetime protocol as the junction-lifetime measurement, and (iii) the MT Lp values only if an explicit MT bending law is later added, at their measured physiological values. Reject depletion force, gliding velocity, stall force, and all 2D nematic metrics for DCM (they are FF or non-transferable).
