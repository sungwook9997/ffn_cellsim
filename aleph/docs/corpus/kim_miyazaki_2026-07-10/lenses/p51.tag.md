# TAG-validation lens — p51

**Paper:** Hassan, Biel & Kim (2019), "Mechanical Model for Durotactic Cell Migration," *ACS Biomater. Sci. Eng.* 5(8):3954–3963. DOI 10.1021/acsbiomaterials.8b01365.
**Lab:** Taeyoon Kim (Purdue Weldon BME) — the same lab whose fiber-ECM work anchors our `project-dcm-ecm-kim-1r-gap` (1/r force propagation) comparison.

---

## Paper kind

**simulation** (2D coarse-grained agent-based biomechanical model; overdamped quasi-static `Mu=f` linear solve each step). Every quantitative result in this paper is a **model output**, not a laboratory measurement. Under our validation philosophy this means **comparison-only, never a truth source and never a fit target**. The paper's cell drag coefficients (η, α, β, λ, γ) are themselves *fit to* experimental velocity + MSD (their ref 39), so the paper is one abstraction layer *above* the experiment — its numbers cannot supersede any sourced Parameter row and cannot become a hard band.

The one exception worth noting: the paper reports several **emergent behavioral laws** (durotaxis emerges purely mechanically with no decision rule; DI biphasic in soft-region stiffness; speed set by local stiffness independent of orientation; distance-before-reorientation ∝ 1/gradient). Those are *qualitative mechanism cross-checks* that FF could reproduce — valuable as behavior targets, but staged as PI-gate candidates only.

Not analytic (no closed-form law we could implement as an oracle). Not experimental. Not review/method.

## Coverage vs existing gates

- **Migration speed (rows 5–8)** — no existing gate covers whole-cell crawl speed. Nearest is `VG-U2-biphasic-traction` (mean traction vs substrate stiffness) and `VG-U2-traction-magnitude` — both about *force*, not *speed*. Our FF crawl record (`project-ff-crawl-mechanism`) reports emergent ~28–45 nm/s ≈ **100–160 µm/hr**, i.e. ~3–10× faster than this paper's fibroblast 5–35 µm/hr — a cross-check discrepancy worth noting (different cell type + FF's grid-drag caveat), NOT a contract.
- **Durotaxis / stiffness-directed migration (rows 1–4, 9)** — **not covered**. The only durotaxis-adjacent gate is `VG-U5-wetting-dewetting` ("stiff substrates favor wetting") which is a spreading, not a directional-migration, observable. `PARAM-E_substrate` is annotated "key durotaxis/wetting sweep parameter," so the project intends a durotaxis line but has no gate yet.
- **Substrate strain-stiffening (row 11)** — conceptually the SAME phenomenon our `VG-U1-strain-stiffening` already validates (exponent [1.5,2.5], status=passing) and `VG-U1-force-propagation` (r⁻¹ decay, passing). This paper **CONFIRMS qualitatively** that a bending+stretching fiber network is linear-then-stiffening; it adds no tighter band (it's a different network — triangular lattice with p-removal, not our Mikado). No change to existing bands.
- **Substrate 1/r-type force spread (row 10, dipole)** — resonates with `VG-U1-force-propagation` (passing, r⁻¹ for sub-isostatic Mikado) and our Kim 1/r reconciliation. Confirms direction; no tightening (simulation, and force *magnitude* 0.5 nN is model-scale, not our scale).

**Net:** this paper CONFIRMS (qualitatively) two passing U1 substrate gates and CHALLENGES nothing. It exposes one genuine **coverage gap: durotaxis / directional migration on a stiffness gradient**, which our KB flags as intended (E_substrate) but has never gated.

## New gate candidates

All **candidate, PI-gate review** — and all flagged as *behavioral/qualitative* because the source is simulation, so bands are directional-sense, not tight numeric contracts. Best fit is the **FF** engine (fine-grained crawl on a stiffness-graded ECM), since the paper's central claim — durotaxis EMERGES from mechanics with no decision rule — is exactly the mechanistic bar FF should clear.

1. **proposed_vg_id: VG-FF-durotaxis-emergent** (candidate, PI-gate)
   - observable: net displacement bias (or DI = fraction of forward progress toward stiff) of an FF crawling cell on an ECM with a stiffness gradient / sharp step, with **no stiffness-sensing rule** in the cell.
   - band_or_value: DI > 0.5 (biased toward stiff) on a step/gradient; DI → 0.5 (unbiased) on uniform substrate. Sign/direction test, not a numeric band.
   - condition: FF native cell + collagen-Mikado ECM with spatially varied crosslink stiffness; clutch traction only, no decision logic.
   - engine: **FF**.
   - source: rows 1–4; "durotaxis ... purely mechanical ... without any cell decision-making" (abstract; Fig 3b/4b, p6–7).

2. **proposed_vg_id: VG-FF-speed-orientation-independence** (candidate, PI-gate)
   - observable: crawl speed as a function of local substrate stiffness vs cell orientation.
   - band_or_value: speed correlates with **local stiffness only**, statistically **independent of orientation** — i.e. speed is not the durotaxis driver (directional bias must come from reorientation, not a fast/slow axis).
   - condition: FF cell on graded ECM.
   - engine: **FF**.
   - source: row 8; Fig S10C,D + text p8.

3. **proposed_vg_id: VG-FF-reorientation-distance-gradient** (candidate, PI-gate, weak)
   - observable: mean distance a cell travels before reorienting toward stiff vs the stiffness gradient magnitude.
   - band_or_value: **inverse** monotonic relation (steeper gradient → shorter distance). Scaling-sense only; the ~200→~50 µm numbers are model-scale and NOT a band.
   - condition: FF gradient crawl, long run (their 36 h).
   - engine: **FF**.
   - source: row 9; Fig 5d (p7).

*Not proposed:* a migration-speed band (5–35 µm/hr). It is calibrated model output (fit to their ref-39 experiment), cell-type-specific (fibroblast), and conflicts in magnitude with FF's own emergent ~100–160 µm/hr. Registering it as a contract would be fitting to a simulation — declined. Keep it only as an **overlay note** for FF crawl-speed sanity.

## Param candidates

**None registered.** Every numeric constant in this paper is either (a) the paper's own *model convention* (κ_b,M=1 nN·µm, p=0.4, n=4×10⁴ mm⁻², domain 500 µm, ξ grid, κ_s,M sweep) — not a sourced physical property we could adopt, or (b) locked in **SI Table S1** which is not in the ingested text (radii, torques τ_F/τ_R, κ_s,FR, drag coeffs η/α/β/λ/γ) — **incomplete provenance**, cannot register.

- `T = 6 min` lamellipodium lifetime is cited from their ref 35 (~10 min lamellipodia) and is *physically* meaningful, but it is a second-hand citation, not this paper's measurement — flag as **weak candidate only if the primary (ref 35, Burnette) is ingested**. Do not register from p51.
- No conflict with, and no supersession of, any current Parameter row (`PARAM-E_substrate`, `PARAM-k_xl`, `PARAM-kappa_fiber`, etc.). The paper's substrate is a triangular-lattice spring network in its own (nN/µm, nN·µm) units, not directly commensurable with our Mikado WLC κ_fiber or E_substrate kPa.

## Oracle candidates

**None.** The paper provides no closed-form analytic law implementable as a ground-truth cross-check. Its results are numerical solutions of a linear system, and the two "laws" it states — DI biphasic in κ^L, distance ∝ 1/gradient — are *empirical scaling observations from the simulation*, not derived closed forms. This does not qualify as an analytic oracle under our philosophy (oracles must be closed-form ground truth like Helfrich, Young–Laplace, Bell–Evans, Hill). The one closed-form pieces used (harmonic springs U=½k x², bending U=½κθ²) are already standard in our ECM/oracle stack.
