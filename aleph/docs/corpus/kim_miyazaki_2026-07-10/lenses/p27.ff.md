# FF lens — p27

**Paper:** Kim, Zhang, Iyer-Pascuzzi, Staiger, Kim — "Agent-Based Computational Modeling of the Stochastic Dynamic Behavior of Actin Filaments Recapitulates the Homeostatic Cortical Array in Plant Epidermal Cells." *Cytoskeleton* 2026;83:407–426. DOI 10.1002/cm.70014. Public code: github.com/ktyman2/plantactin.

**Why this paper matters for FF (bottom line up front):** This is a **Taeyoon-Kim-lab agent-based Brownian-dynamics actin model** — the *exact lineage* of FF's Cytosim-style fine-grained engine (the same lab whose DCM⊗ECM 1/r work FF already validates against). The force laws are the same harmonic-segment family FF already uses, so this is a **1:1 parameter + kinetic-scheme donor**, not a physics translation. Its unique, directly-portable contribution is a **complete, self-consistent stochastic turnover kit** — de-novo nucleation gated by a profilin monomer pool, ATP→ADP aging, pointed-end depolymerization, **angle-dependent severing `k_sev = k0,sev·exp(λ_sev·θ)`**, barbed-end capping/uncapping, Arp2/3 branch with a full 4-angle geometry, and processive formin ×3 enhancement — all mutually calibrated to hit live-cell numbers within 2×. FF currently has these as scattered, partly-stubbed modules; p27 gives the **assembled, mass-conserving recipe with rate constants**.

**Two hard caveats up front (do not violate the physiological-baseline rule):**
1. **Plant, not mammalian.** All numbers are *Arabidopsis hypocotyl cortex*. C_A = 1 µM is an explicit **computational-efficiency compromise** (real plant 40–50 µM; MCF7 different again). Register as **plant-actin context / mechanism donor**, never as MCF7 cortex parameters. Absolute densities/lengths are acknowledged underestimates.
2. **No motors, no crosslinkers, force-independent (de)polymerization.** Myosin XI and ACPs are explicitly excluded; only severing is mechanochemically coupled (via bend angle θ). So this paper informs FF's **turnover/nucleation/severing/capping** layer, *not* its contraction/γ-generation layer.

## 1. MECHANISMS to adopt

### 1.1 Angle-dependent severing (`turnover` / `filament` module) — HIGHEST-VALUE lever
**Change:** Replace any force/length-uniform fragmentation stub with the McCullough-2011-derived **local-curvature-gated** severing: a filament segment severs at rate `k_sev = k0,sev·exp(λ_sev·θ)`, where θ is the local bend angle at that segment junction (Eq. 9). Only ADP-state segments are severable.

**Why it beats current FF:** FF's open problems list "filament turnover/fragmentation" and "buckling/condensation symmetry-breaking." This law makes severing **emergent from mechanics** — a filament buckled by myosin or a crosslinker (high θ) severs preferentially, exactly the coupling FF's fine-grained-over-lumped rule demands. It is the *single mechanochemically-coupled channel* in the paper and the natural hook for FF's myosin: when Stam-Hocky minifilaments buckle cortical filaments, `k_sev` spikes locally → contraction-facilitating fragmentation (the Matsuda-2024-style "fragmentation facilitates contraction" mechanism, here made native). k0,sev is set "extremely low" and λ_sev "quite high" so thermal θ alone barely severs — meaning severing is **essentially motor/crosslinker-gated**, which is precisely the physiological behavior FF wants and cannot get from a rate-only fragmentation model.

### 1.2 Profilin-gated monomer pool + de-novo nucleation (`turnover` / new `monomer_pool` state)
**Change:** Add an explicit, finite, conserved G-actin pool with two states — profilin-bound and profilin-free — interconverting at `k+,prof`/`k−,prof`. **De-novo nucleation consumes profilin-FREE monomers only**; formin-enhanced polymerization consumes profilin-BOUND monomers. Nucleation rate `= k_n,A · [profilin-free monomer]`.

**Why it beats current FF:** FF nucleation is currently rate-driven without a monomer budget, so it cannot exhibit **pool-depletion-limited assembly** (the paper's key homeostatic mechanism — Fig 6: kill severing+capping → pool empties → poly rate collapses to depoly rate). This is the mechanism that makes cortical density **self-limiting** rather than needing a magic cap on filament count. It also gives FF a single knob (profilin availability) that the paper *predicts* controls compensatory nucleation — a clean, mechanistic replacement for any ad-hoc density clamp.

### 1.3 ATP→ADP aging as the depolymerization/severing gate (`filament` state)
**Change:** Every polymerized segment is born ATP; after dwell `t_ATP = 3 s` it flips to ADP. **Pointed-end depolymerization and severing act on ADP segments only.** Barbed-end growth is ATP.

**Why it beats current FF:** Encodes the real barbed/pointed asymmetry mechanistically — new (barbed, ATP) ends grow, old (pointed, ADP) ends shrink and sever — reproducing **non-treadmilling stochastic dynamics** (growth 10× faster than shrinkage, filament lifetime 15–20 s emerges) instead of an imposed treadmill velocity. Directly serves FF's "stress relaxation / turnover" open problem.

### 1.4 Barbed-end capping with severing coupling (`turnover` / `cap` state)
**Change:** Free barbed end → capped at `k+,cap`, capped → free at `k−,cap` (kept ~1000× lower, so caps are near-terminal). **Formin-bound ends cannot cap; severing-created new barbed ends are immediately capped.** Capped ends cannot polymerize.

**Why it beats current FF:** Gives FF a real growth-termination mechanism ("length ceases → capping → disassembly") and the experimentally-observed severing↔capping interplay (most severed ends cap). This is the density regulator paired with severing; the paper shows capping alone tunes array sparseness (Fig 5).

### 1.5 Arp2/3 branch with full 4-angle geometry (`Arp2/3` module) — supersede the single-angle stub
**Change:** Represent Arp2/3 as **two cylindrical segments joined at a center**, constrained by **four** harmonic angles, not one 70° branch angle:
- mother↔Arp segment: θ0 = 90° (1.57 rad), κ_b = 1×10⁻¹⁸ N·m
- daughter↔Arp segment: θ0 = 20° (0.35 rad), κ_b = 1×10⁻¹⁸ N·m
- **mother↔daughter branch: θ0 = 70° (1.22 rad)**, κ_b = 1×10⁻¹⁸ N·m
- two-Arp-segment internal angle θ0 = 0, κ_b = 1×10⁻¹⁹ N·m
- **torsion mother↔daughter ϕ0 = 0** (coplanar), κ_t = 1×10⁻¹⁸ N·m

Daughter unbinds much more slowly than mother (`k−,Arp2/3d` ≪ `k−,Arp2/3m`) → stable branches. Branch nucleates ATP-only.

**Why it beats current FF:** FF's brief lists "Arp2/3: angle-harmonic branch 70°±35°." The paper shows the mechanistically-correct construct is **not one spring** but a rigid two-segment nucleator with a torsion term keeping the branch coplanar and *two* asymmetric off-rates. The 70°±35° thermal spread emerges from κ_b,f under kT, and the differential mother/daughter off-rate is what makes branches persist — FF's current single-angle rigid branch cannot reproduce branch stability vs. debranching.

### 1.6 Processive formin as loose-coupled ×3 elongation with dwell (`formin` module)
**Change:** Free barbed end → formin-bound at `k+,form`; while bound, polymerization rate ×`α_form = 3`, **using profilin-bound monomers**; reverts to free after dwell `t_form = 5 s`; formin-bound end cannot cap.

**Why it beats current FF:** Makes formin a **stochastic processive state with finite dwell** feeding off the profilin-bound pool, not a constant elongation multiplier. This is the loose-coupling-to-polymerization behavior (AtFH14, Zhang 2016) and it cleanly separates the two nucleation channels FF needs (Arp2/3 = side-branch; processive formin = barbed-end acceleration), reproducing the ~30% formin-elongated / ~50% branched / ~20% de-novo class split.

## 2. FORCE LAWS / KINETIC SCHEMES to port

All symbols map onto FF filament endpoint state (`r_i`), segment (r0), and angle triples.

**Overdamped Langevin (already FF's regime — cross-check):**
`F_i − ζ_i·(dr_i/dt) + F_iᵀ = 0`  (Eq. 1) → Euler `r_i(t+Δt) = r_i + (1/ζ_i)(F_i + F_iᵀ)Δt` (Eq. 4).
FF uses constrained BAOAB (superior to this explicit Euler) — **do not adopt Euler**; keep BAOAB. Port only the *forces* below.

**Cylindrical-segment drag (Clift 1978):**
`ζ_i = 3πμ·r_c,i·(3 + 2·r0,i/r_c,i)/5`  (Eq. 2). μ = medium viscosity, r0 = segment length, r_c = segment diameter. → FF per-segment drag for a rod of finite aspect ratio; use at FF's **physiological cytoplasm μ ≈ 65 Pa·s (MCF7)**, NOT the paper's (untabulated) plant μ.

**Fluctuation–dissipation thermal force (Underhill & Doyle 2004):**
`⟨F_iᵀ(t)·F_jᵀ(t)⟩ = (2 k_B T ζ_i δ_ij / Δt)·δ`  (Eq. 3). Standard; FF's BAOAB already encodes this.

**Harmonic potentials (map 1:1 onto FF bead-rod/bead-spring):**
- Stretch: `U_s = ½ κ_s (r − r0)²`  (Eq. 5)
- Bend:   `U_b = ½ κ_b (θ − θ0)²`  (Eq. 6)
- Torsion: `U_t = ½ κ_t (ϕ − ϕ0)²`  (Eq. 7) — **FF should add a torsion term for Arp2/3 coplanarity if not present.**

**One-sided excluded volume (Kim 2009 form) — supersede LJ for filament-filament:**
`U_r,A = ½ κ_r (r₁₂ − r_c,A)²` if `r₁₂ < r_c,A`, else 0  (Eq. 8). r₁₂ = min distance between two segments. This is a **soft harmonic overlap penalty**, cheaper/stiffness-controlled vs. LJ; FF may keep LJ but this is the Kim-lineage-consistent choice and avoids LJ's hard core / timestep pressure.

**Angle-dependent severing (Eq. 9) — the key new kinetic law:**
`k_sev = k0,sev · exp(λ_sev · θ)`
θ = local bend angle at a segment junction; evaluate per-junction each reaction step, ADP-only. Draw uniform u∈[0,1] per junction per Δt_react, sever if `u < k_sev·Δt_react`. New barbed end from severing → immediately capped.

**Reaction scheme (Gillespie-style / per-step Bernoulli, all first-order in the listed reactant):**
- Profilin: free↔bound at k+,prof / k−,prof.
- De-novo nucleation: `rate = k_n,A · [profilin-free monomer]` → emit 1 segment (ATP, de-novo class).
- Polymerization: barbed end adds 1 segment at k_p,A (free-barbed elongation) or ×α_form at formin ends; consumes appropriate monomer state; **force-independent** (paper's choice — FF may later make it force-dependent, flag as extension).
- Depolymerization: pointed ADP end removes 1 segment at k_d,A; force-independent.
- Capping: k+,cap / k−,cap as above.
- Arp2/3: binds mother anywhere at k+,Arp2/3, spawns daughter; off-rates k−,Arp2/3m (mother), k−,Arp2/3d (daughter, ~30000× slower).
- Formin: k+,form on/off, dwell t_form, ×α_form.
- Aging: ATP→ADP after t_ATP.

## 4. NETWORK ARCHITECTURE / PROTOCOL to reproduce

**FF scenario "Kim-plant-cortex-turnover" (a quasi-2D turnover benchmark, NOT the native MCF7 cortex):**
- Domain: **20 × 20 × 1 µm slab**, periodic in x,y; two z-normal repulsive walls (thin-slab because plant vacuole occupies ~90% volume). This is a good **isolated turnover/homeostasis testbed** — a flat cortical patch where nucleation/severing/capping balance can be measured without membrane/turgor confound.
- Init: **10 single-segment seeds** randomly placed at t=0 (because k_n,A is tiny, seeding speeds steady-state).
- Reference: C_A = 1 µM (efficiency), R_Arp2/3 = 7×10⁻⁵, Table-2 params.
- Run 2000 s, analyze from 1000 s (or ≥500 s where steady state is reached), average **5 seeds** — matches FF's seed-robustness-ensemble discipline.
- Parameter sweeps as FF validation scenarios: sever (k0,sev = 0, 4, 24 ×10⁻⁵), cap (0, 0.005, 0.04), Arp2/3 (0, 3×10⁻⁵, 9×10⁻⁵), formin (0, 0.045, 0.105), profilin release (0.1, 4, 10), and **dual Arp2/3+formin OFF** (de-novo becomes sole source).

**How this plugs into FF's native cortex:** Use this slab as a **unit-test / calibration harness** for the turnover kernels, then transplant the *calibrated kernels* (not the geometry) into the native `--cortex-fil 38000 --from-resting --microtubules` MCF7 shell. Per the native-scale HARD rule, **no conclusion is drawn from the 20×20×1 slab** — it is a dev-scale kernel calibrator; every finding is reconfirmed on the native full cell.

## 6. RISKS / caveats

1. **Plant ≠ mammalian (biggest).** Arabidopsis hypocotyl cortex; isoforms (AtFH14 formin, plant profilin PRF1/PRF3, plant Arp2/3) differ from MCF7. **Adopt the mechanism *forms* (angle-severing law, profilin-pool gating, 4-angle Arp2/3, ATP-aging, formin-dwell) and the *scheme*, but re-anchor rate constants to mammalian sources where FF has them.** Register p27 values as plant context in the KB (like the existing "plant-actin context, not MCF7" precedent), never as MCF7 cortex parameters. Do NOT let these supersede FF's Ferrer/Bell-Evans/Rakshit mammalian values.

2. **C_A = 1 µM is a stated efficiency compromise** (real 40–50 µM). Absolute densities/lengths are underestimates. FF native cortex must use its own physiological concentration/density (KB-3.18 ~100/µm²); take from p27 only the **rate constants and the pool-depletion *mechanism***, not the sparse absolute density.

3. **Force-independent (de)polymerization.** The paper deliberately omits force-dependence in growth/shrinkage — only severing couples to mechanics. FF's mechanistic-over-lumped rule *prefers* force-dependent kinetics; treat p27's force-free growth as a **baseline to extend**, not a ceiling. Flag: adding load-dependent barbed-end growth (Brownian ratchet, already in FF filopodium ratchet) on top of this scheme is a legitimate FF improvement, not a deviation.

4. **No motors, no crosslinkers.** The contraction/γ-generation open problems are *out of scope* here. The value is the **substrate** severing gives motors: when FF's Stam-Hocky myosin buckles a filament (raising θ), `k_sev` rises → motor-driven fragmentation. That coupling is the paper's own stated future direction ("study myosin XI buckling raising k_sev") and is exactly FF's contraction lever — but it must be *added* by FF; the paper does not demonstrate it.

5. **Explicit Euler + Δt=1.15×10⁻⁵ s.** Inferior to FF's constrained BAOAB. **Port forces, not the integrator.** The tiny Δt reflects the stiff κ_s; FF's BAOAB + soft-EV (Eq. 8) may allow a larger stable step — verify CFL including k_turgor per FF's known CFL caveat.

6. **Provenance gaps:** λ_sev (severing angle sensitivity) and μ (medium viscosity) are **not in Table 2** — must be recovered from the public code (github.com/ktyman2/plantactin) before the severing law is quantitatively usable. Do not guess λ_sev (magic-number rule). κ_b,A=2.64×10⁻¹⁹ N·m implies ℓ_p≈6.4 µm at this segment convention vs FF's 17 µm — a **discretization/convention discrepancy to reconcile**, not a datum to blindly overwrite FF's persistence length.

7. **Native-scale rule:** the 20×20×1 µm slab is a **dev-scale kernel calibrator only**. Any conclusion (turnover balance, homeostasis, severing-driven fragmentation) must be reconfirmed on the native `--cortex-fil 38000 --from-resting --microtubules` MCF7 cell before it is reported or acted on.
