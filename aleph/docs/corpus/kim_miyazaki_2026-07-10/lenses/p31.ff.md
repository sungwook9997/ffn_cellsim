# FF lens — p31

**Paper:** Slater, Sciortino, Bausch, Kim, "Cluster formation and phase separation driven by mobile myosin motors in the motility assay," *Phys. Rev. Research* **7**, 013312 (2025). DOI 10.1103/PhysRevResearch.7.013312.

**One-line relevance:** This is the **same Kim-lab agent-based Brownian-dynamics lineage** (refs [29–31], already cited in-project) that FF's fine-grained filament+explicit-motor primitives descend from. It is an in-vitro **quasi-2D gliding motility assay** (purified actin + HMM on a lipid bilayer), NOT a cortex, so its *biology* (clusters/phase separation) does not transfer to our single-cell cortex. What DOES transfer, and is high value, is the **numerics scaffold**: the explicit-motor PCM kinetics (a mechanistic alternative/cross-check to our current Hill+Stam-Hocky), the closed-form cylindrical-segment drag law, the harmonic force-law set, and a clean set of quantitative anchors + a reusable collective-behavior diagnostic (nematic/polar order, velocity correlation, phase-separation metric). Treat as a **motor-kinetics + numerics oracle**, not a mechanism to graft into the cortex.

---

## 1. MECHANISMS to adopt

### 1a. Motor kinetics — Parallel Cluster Model (PCM) as an explicit-ensemble cross-check for Stam–Hocky/Hill (moderate value, oracle-grade)
- **FF module:** `myosin` (Stam–Hocky bipolar minifilament; Hill 1938 force–velocity; duty ratio).
- **The change:** Adopt the **PCM (Erdmann–Schwarz)** as a *validation oracle* for the ensemble walking/unbinding rates our minifilament emits. Each motor = ensemble of N_H heads; PCM assumes each head is in one of **3 mechanochemical states** with **5 transition rates** (Table S2 in their SI — not in this PDF, flag for retrieval), and predicts the ensemble unbinding rate k_{u,M} and walking rate k_{w,M} of a small head cluster **including load-dependence and duty-ratio emergence**.
- **Why it beats/complements current FF:** Our Hill force–velocity is a single-motor closed form; the multi-head bipolar minifilament produces ensemble behavior emergently but we have no *independent* rate oracle for the ensemble k_off / k_walk under load. PCM is exactly that — a published, benchmarked (their ref [29]) small-ensemble rate model. Use it to check that our Stam–Hocky minifilament reproduces the PCM ensemble unbinding vs load curve. This is the CLAUDE.md pattern: PCM is an **acceptance oracle**, the runtime stays the fine-grained minifilament.
- **Caveat / do NOT replace:** PCM here *lumps* N_H=4 heads into one segment with a single stall force — that is MORE lumped than our Stam–Hocky per-head model. So PCM feeds validation, it does not become the runtime. Keep the mechanistic minifilament.

### 1b. Explicit-motor over implicit-motor — confirms our architectural principle (no change, corroboration)
- The paper's central methodological point (§IV Discussion): explicit motors (reaction force on the motor, binding-site kinetics) give results "which cannot be reproduced with implicit motors" (constant propelling force). This is a literature datum **directly endorsing the FF hard rule** (mechanistic over lumped). Useful to cite when defending FF's explicit-clutch/minifilament cost.

### 1c. Motor-side reaction force / motor mobility (informational — NOT a cortex change)
- Their key knob is **motor drag ζ_M** → the motor is pushed back by reaction force when it loads F-actin. In FF the analog is the **adhesion-clutch / membrane anchor drag** on the myosin minifilament tail and on cross-linkers. The lesson worth carrying: a motor/anchor with finite drag experiences reaction and can be displaced, changing the force it delivers. Our clutches already do this; the paper is a reminder that the **anchor drag coefficient is a first-class force-setting parameter**, not a numerical nuisance. Do not port the *bilayer-fluid* mobility (there is no fluid bilayer under the cortex; the cortex anchors to membrane + focal adhesions, i.e. our ζ_M → high/fixed regime, D̄_M≈0).

## 2. FORCE LAWS / KINETIC SCHEMES to port

All of these are the **same primitives FF already uses** — value is as a **written cross-check / parity target**, plus the drag closed-form which is worth adopting verbatim.

**(A3) Cylindrical-segment drag coefficient (adopt/cross-check):**
```
ζ_i = 3π μ r_{c,i} · (3 + 2 r_{0,i}/r_{c,i}) / 5
```
- μ = medium viscosity; r_{0,i} = segment length; r_{c,i} = segment diameter.
- **Map to FF state:** per-bead/per-segment translational drag in the overdamped Langevin step. FF currently uses per-node drag derived from η=65.9 Pa·s cytoplasm (see MEMORY project-dcm-physio-nodeface-contact). This is the standard slender-cylinder form; use it to **sanity-check FF's segment drag** against a published closed form (dimensional-analysis gate). Note μ here = in-vitro buffer (~water), NOT our 65 Pa·s cytoplasm — swap μ, keep the geometry factor.

**(A1) Overdamped Langevin (force balance, no inertia):**
```
F_i − ζ_i (dr_i/dt) + F_i^T = 0
```
**(A2) Fluctuation–dissipation noise:**
```
⟨F_i^T(t) F_j^T(t)⟩ = (2 k_B T ζ_i δ_ij / Δt) · δ
```
- Standard FDT white noise. **FF cross-check:** our BAOAB integrator must reproduce this variance in the overdamped limit. This is the Euler-Maruyama form (their integrator); FF's BAOAB is strictly better (mechanistic-integrator rule), so this is a **low-order reference**, not a target to match step-for-step.

**(A5) Actin extensional (Hookean):** `U_{s,A} = ½ κ_{s,A}(r − r_{0,A})²`
**(A6) Motor extensional (Hookean):** `U_{s,M} = ½ κ_{s,M}(r − r_{0,M})²`, r_{0,M} ≈ 13.5 nm rest.
**(A7) Actin bending (angular Hookean):** `U_{b,A} = ½ κ_{b,A}(θ − θ_{0,A})²`, θ_{0,A}=straight — encodes persistence length ℓ_p.
**(A8) Excluded volume (one-sided harmonic):**
```
U_r = ½ κ_r (r₁₂ − r_c)²   if r₁₂ < r_c ;   0 otherwise
```
- r₁₂ = min inter-segment distance, r_c = actin diameter r_{c,A}.
- **Map to FF:** A5/A7 = our stretch + bending beam; A8 = our excluded-volume repulsion. FF uses **LJ repulsive** (mechanistic-EV rule) — soft harmonic (A8) is a *cheaper proxy* our rule already rejects; keep LJ, note A8 as the coarser sibling. **Note:** exact κ_{s,A}, κ_{s,M}, κ_{b,A}, κ_r, μ, r_0, r_c numeric values are in Supplemental Material [32], NOT this PDF — retrieve SI if a parity run needs them.

**Motor propulsion (kinetic scheme, emergent — the mechanistic core):**
- Binding sites on F-actin every **7 nm**; binding rate **k_{+,M} = 40·N_H s⁻¹** (=160 s⁻¹ at N_H=4).
- After binding, heads walk toward barbed end at k_{w,M} (PCM), unbind at k_{u,M} (PCM); force delivered through the motor spring (A6); reaction force displaces the (drag-ζ_M) motor.
- **Map to FF:** binding-site quantization (7 nm) and the binding rate are directly usable as **FF myosin-attachment parameters** (see §3).

## 4. NETWORK ARCHITECTURE / PROTOCOL to reproduce (as an FF scenario)

**Scenario "gliding motility assay" — a quasi-2D FF cross-check bench (NON-cortex, oracle scenario):**
- Domain **50 × 50 × 0.1 µm**, PBC in x,y; F-actin nodes pinned to plane z=13.5 nm; motors at z=0 (gap = motor rest length). Displacements in x,y only.
- **Two-phase protocol** (directly maps to FF's init→production):
  1. *Initiation:* nucleate + polymerize F-actin (no depoly) to a frozen length distribution; motors bind but **do not walk**. This sets ⟨L_f⟩ via nucleation rate — same as FF barbed-end growth then freeze.
  2. *Production:* freeze filament lengths; motors walk toward barbed end.
- Sweeps to reproduce: motor mobility D̄_M ∈ {0, 0.0625, 0.25, 1, 4}; ⟨L_f⟩ ∈ {1.29, 2.58, 3.53, 7.43} µm; density ∈ {903, 1806, 3612, 6020, 12040} monomers/µm².
- **Use for FF:** a **decoupled 2D bench** to validate FF's explicit-motor + excluded-volume + bending machinery against a published emergent phenomenology (polar vs nematic ordering, cluster vs bundle vs ring) BEFORE trusting it inside the 3D cortex. This is a "unit test at native motor fidelity" — cheap, 2D, purified, with a known answer.
- **Do NOT** promote this to a cortex conclusion: no membrane, no turgor, no 3D, water viscosity. It is explicitly labelled **non-authoritative for the cell** per the native-full-compartment rule; it is a mechanism-isolation bench only.

**Experimental reconstitution (if we ever want the in-vitro overlay):** rabbit skeletal actin + biotinylated HMM on 98% DOPC / 2% DSPE-PEG(2000)-biotin SLB via streptavidin; gelsolin:actin ratio sets length (1:50 short ~0.5–1 µm, 1:190 medium, 1:500 long ~1–2 µm); 2 mM ATP + 0.2% methylcellulose crowding + O₂ scavenger; TIRF ~30 min post-ATP.

## 5. VALIDATION TARGETS for FF observables

Reusable **diagnostics** (adopt the metrics into FF's collective-behavior analysis toolkit) + **numbers** (for the 2D oracle bench):

**Metrics to add to FF analysis (Eqs 1–3):**
- Nematic order `S = ⟨2cos²θ⟩ − 1` over pairs within 1 µm (θ∈[0°,180°]).
- Polar order `P = ⟨cos θ⟩` over pairs within 1 µm.
- Velocity correlation `⟨cos α⟩ = ⟨(v_i·v_j)/(|v_i||v_j|)⟩` over endpoints within 1 µm, sampled every 1 s.
- Phase-separation metric `M/M₀` on a 100×100 grid (per cell: Σ motors × Σ actin-segments, grid-averaged, normalized to t=0).
- These are directly useful for FF **cortex flow / condensation / symmetry-breaking** diagnostics (polar sorting, motor-actin demixing) even outside this assay.

**Numeric targets (2D oracle bench only):**
| observable | target | condition |
|---|---|---|
| F-actin glide speed v_A | plateau ~1–2 µm/s (↓ with motor mobility) | after ~50 s |

## 6. RISKS / caveats

1. **Regime mismatch — purified 2D gliding assay ≠ living 3D cortex.** No membrane, no turgor, no ECM, water-viscosity buffer, filaments pinned to a plane, PBC. The *emergent biology* (clusters, rings, polarity sorting, +1/2 defect trapping) is a surface-assay phenomenon and MUST NOT be reported as a cortex finding. Only the **numerics + motor kinetics + metrics** transfer.
2. **Motor lumping is coarser than FF.** N_H=4 heads → one cylindrical segment with a single stall force is *more lumped* than our Stam–Hocky per-head minifilament. Adopting it as runtime would VIOLATE the mechanistic-over-lumped rule. Use PCM only as an ensemble-rate **oracle** (retrieve Table S2 from SI first); keep the per-head minifilament as runtime.
3. **Soft-harmonic EV (A8) vs LJ.** Their excluded volume is a one-sided harmonic proxy; FF's rule mandates LJ repulsive. Do not downgrade FF to A8 for speed.
4. **Explicit-Euler + FDT (A2, A4) is a lower-order integrator** than FF's constrained BAOAB. Match variance in the overdamped limit only as a sanity check, not as a target trajectory.
5. **Viscosity substitution.** The drag law (A3) is portable but μ must be swapped from in-vitro buffer (~10⁻³ Pa·s) to the physiological ~65 Pa·s MCF7 cytoplasm for any cortex use — the physiological-baseline HARD rule. Using their μ in a cortex run would reintroduce the water-viscosity failure mode.
6. **No motor–motor repulsion / no severing** (authors' own stated limitations). If FF reproduces this bench, it should turn motor–motor EV OFF to match, then note that our production cortex keeps it ON. Severing/turnover is absent here — do NOT infer turnover physics from this paper.
7. **SI-gated numbers.** κ_{s,A}, κ_{s,M}, κ_{b,A}, κ_r, μ, r_0, r_c are in Supplemental Material [32], not this PDF. A quantitative parity run needs SI retrieval; without it, only the in-text anchors (§3) are usable.
