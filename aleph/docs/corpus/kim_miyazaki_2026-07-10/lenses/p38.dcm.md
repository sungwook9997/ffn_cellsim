# DCM lens — p38

**Paper:** Yamazaki, Miyata, Morigaki, Miyazaki (2024), *Controlling Physical and Biochemical Parameters of Actin Nucleation Using a Patterned Model Lipid Membrane*, Nano Lett. 24(5):1825–1834, DOI 10.1021/acs.nanolett.3c02742.

**Bottom line for DCM: LOW direct relevance (score ~1.5/5).** This is a purified/extract in-vitro reconstitution of actin nucleation on a 2D supported lipid bilayer. There is NO continuum surface-mesh, NO cortex-tension γ, NO turgor/osmotic pressure, NO cell-aggregate, NO cell-cell junction, NO division, NO durotaxis content. It has one empirical calibration fit (a fluorescence-intensity ↔ Ni-lipid line), qualitative force concepts (barbed-end pushing normal to the membrane; pushing-vs-tethering competition), and a set of physiological **membrane-bound nucleator surface densities**. The only things that legitimately cross to DCM are (a) a citable physiological areal density for a membrane-anchored actin-generation field, and (b) a mechanistic justification for making cortex/protrusion generation a *membrane-surface-localized, normally-directed* source term rather than a bulk scalar. Everything else is FF-engine territory (filament-resolved nucleation/branch geometry), where the dossier already scores it moderate/high. Below I keep only what survives the "our-scale single-cell surface-mesh, no magic numbers, physiological-baseline" filter, and I explicitly flag the speculative items so they are not over-adopted.

---

## 1. MECHANISMS to adopt

### M1 — Cortex/protrusion generation as a MEMBRANE-SURFACE-LOCALIZED, normally-directed source (not a bulk scalar). *(weak-moderate, conceptual)*
- **DCM module:** cortex tension γ generation + any future protrusion force on the membrane mesh.
- **Change:** the paper's central mechanistic result is that actin nucleation is spatially restricted to the membrane, that barbed ends sit **at the membrane and point toward it** (two-color pulse-chase, Fig 1f), and that new-monomer addition at those barbed ends generates a **pushing force perpendicular to the membrane** (p.4). Mapped to DCM: any actin-derived generation term (cortex tension source, or a protrusive normal stress) should be attached to membrane **faces** with a **face-normal direction**, driven by a per-face nucleator/NPF surface-density field σ_NPF(face), not applied as a spatially-uniform bulk scalar.
- **Why it beats current DCM:** DCM's cortex tension is currently a single near-uniform surface tension γ (interphase 0.15 / mitotic 1.5 mN/m) set globally. This paper is direct experimental evidence that generation is *heterogeneous and membrane-templated* — density and geometry of the membrane nucleation zone set where and how strongly the network grows. This is the mechanistic hook for (i) polarized cortex (front/rear γ asymmetry for crawl/durotaxis), (ii) bleb repair (cortex regeneration is a membrane-localized nucleation event), and (iii) confinement/geometry response. It does NOT require resolving filaments — it is a per-face scalar field σ_NPF that scales the generation term.
- **Caveat (do not over-claim):** the paper gives no rate law linking σ_NPF to a force magnitude — the density→length/density→actin-density relationships are non-monotonic and range-dependent (Fig 3c,d). So adopt the *architecture* (surface-localized, normal-directed, density-weighted source) but NOT a fitted density→force coefficient. See Risks R2.

### M2 — Nucleator-TYPE selects cortex architecture (branched-isotropic vs bundled-anisotropic). *(weak, conceptual / FF-primary)*
- **DCM module:** cortex anisotropy / surface tension tensor (currently isotropic γ).
- **Change:** Arp2/3+NPF → dense branched network pushing **perpendicular** (isotropic areal buildup); formin(mDia1ΔN)+fascin → bundled network growing **parallel to the contour** (anisotropic, tangential). At DCM's coarse-graining this is the difference between an *isotropic normal-pushing* cortex contribution and a *tangential/anisotropic contractile* contribution. If DCM ever adds a cortex tension tensor (γ_normal vs γ_tangential) this paper motivates two distinct generation modes keyed to nucleator identity.
- **Why:** justifies, mechanistically, that "cortex tension" is not one number — the same actin pool produces normal-protrusive vs tangential-bundled stress depending on nucleator/crosslinker identity. But this is genuinely FF-scale (branch angle, fascin bundling); for DCM it is at most a two-mode switch and should be treated as a future refinement, not a near-term port.

### M3 — Turnover-dynamics / geometry-size sensing as a candidate γ-heterogeneity driver. *(speculative, flag-only)*
- The Discussion (p.8) hypothesizes that NPF binding/unbinding/lateral-diffusion turnover on a finite membrane domain makes generation size-dependent (unbind-then-rebind-to-same-domain probability scales with domain area). For DCM this is only a *narrative* for why a small confined cell or a small contact patch might generate cortex differently than a large one — no equation is provided. **Do not implement**; note as a mechanism-source citation if DCM later models size-dependent cortex.

## 2. EQUATIONS to port to the surface-mesh / continuum level

There are **no governing physics equations** in this paper (it is experimental). The single quantitative relation is an empirical *calibration line*, and it is NOT a physics law — do NOT port it into a force term.

### E1 — Empirical NPF-density calibration (Fig 3b) — reference only, DO NOT use as a DCM force law
```
FI_VVCA = 157.7 · x − 2.32        (R² = 0.88)
```
- `x` = Ni-lipid mole fraction [% mol/mol]; `FI_VVCA` = fluorescence intensity ∝ anchored His-VVCA surface density.
- **DCM mapping:** none as a force. It only tells you anchored NPF areal density is linear in the anchor-lipid fraction over ~1 decade. Useful solely to convert the paper's density conditions into molecules/µm² (see E2). The intercept −2.32 and slope 157.7 are instrument-specific (arbitrary FI units) → **magic-number-forbidden** if injected anywhere.

### E2 — Areal-density estimate (text derivation, p.5) — this IS portable as a physiological areal density
```
σ_NPF ≈ (f_anchor / a_lipid) · occupancy
      = (0.02 / 0.64 nm²) ≈ 0.031 nm⁻² = 3.1×10⁴ µm⁻²   at 2% anchor lipid
```
- `f_anchor` = anchor-lipid mole fraction (0.66% / 2%), `a_lipid = a_POPC = 0.64 nm²` (area per lipid, König 1997), `occupancy ≤ 1`.
- **DCM mapping:** if DCM adds a mechanistic membrane-anchored cortex-generation field σ_NPF(face) [molecules/µm²], this gives the physiological *ceiling* to normalize it against. It is a per-area surface density → naturally a **per-face** quantity on the membrane mesh (face area A_f in µm² → N_nucleators(face) = σ_NPF·A_f). This is grid-invariant (density, not count) and derivable → passes the magic-number block.

**No** pushing-force functional form, off-rate, elastic modulus, or bending term is given to port. The "Brownian-ratchet pushing force" is invoked by analogy only (refs 26, 38) — no expression.

## 4. GEOMETRY / PROTOCOL to reproduce

DCM is a closed 3D deformable cell; this paper is a flat 2D open membrane patch. Almost none of the protocol maps to a DCM scenario. The only transferable geometry idea:

### G1 — Heterogeneous (patterned) membrane generation field as a DCM scenario. *(usable, low priority)*
- **Setup:** on the DCM membrane mesh, paint a per-face σ_NPF(face) field that is high on a defined patch (area 2–100 µm² in the paper's sweep; on our R=7.5 µm cell, an O(1–20 µm²) patch = a localized cap of faces) and ~zero elsewhere, then let the M1 generation term act. This reproduces the paper's core observation — *generation is spatially confined to the patterned zone and its footprint copies the patch shape* — but at the continuum cortex level (localized cortex thickening / normal protrusion over the patch).
- **Use:** a clean test-bed for future DCM **polarized-cortex / protrusion / durotaxis** work: a patch of elevated σ_NPF on one hemisphere should produce a polarized normal-protrusive response there. This is a *design pattern* for scenarios, not a validated protocol.
- **Size effect to reproduce:** patches ≥4 µm gave straight perpendicular growth; 2 µm patches lost directional straightness (Fig 2c,e). If DCM's M1 protrusion is built, a small patch (few faces) should give a less-directed / noisier normal response than a large patch — a qualitative sanity check on the geometry-sensing behavior.

### G2 — What does NOT map (record so it is not attempted)
- Supported-bilayer fabrication, SUV fusion, His-Ni anchoring, pyrene bulk kinetics, TIRF/FRAP, Xenopus-extract PI(4,5)P₂ patterning — all molecular/2D-in-vitro, no DCM analog.
- No confinement (the "confinement" in the DCM open-problems list is *cell-sized spherical* confinement; this paper's "confinement" is *nucleation-area* confinement on a flat membrane — different meaning, not reusable as a DCM confinement BC).

## 5. VALIDATION TARGETS for DCM observables

**Direct DCM observables validated by this paper: essentially none** (no tension, shape index, division force, ring closure, compaction, contact angle, or continuum flow speed is measured). The measured numbers are filament-network geometry (pillar length, actin fluorescence) that live at FF scale. The only cross-scale anchors:

| target | value | for what DCM check |
|---|---|---|
| physiological membrane nucleator surface density | 6 500 – 25 000 molecules/µm² | if M1 σ_NPF field is built, its physiological-baseline value must lie in this band (baseline-rule audit) |
| generation footprint copies membrane patch shape/size | qualitative (Fig 1c, 2c–f) | a patterned-σ_NPF DCM scenario should keep the elevated-generation footprint co-located with the painted patch (no runaway spread) |
| directionality vs patch size | ≥4 µm straight / 2 µm not straight | qualitative sanity for a future DCM protrusion-per-patch scenario (large patch → directed, small → noisier) |
| barbed-end / new-material addition at the membrane, normal to it | qualitative (Fig 1f) | confirms a DCM generation/protrusion term should be **face-normal**, added at the membrane, not in the bulk |

None of these is a quantitative acceptance gate for the current DCM engine. Treat them as *architecture-consistency* checks for a hypothetical M1 module, not as numbers a present-day DCM run must reproduce.

## 6. RISKS / caveats

- **R1 — Regime mismatch (biggest).** In-vitro **purified proteins / crude egg extract on a flat 2D supported bilayer**, room-temperature, no turgor, no cytoplasmic viscosity, no closed volume, no myosin contraction (formin case is explicitly *static*, unlike the contractile intracellular network, p.5). DCM is a living-MCF7-like **closed 3D pressurized viscous cell**. The paper's networks are free-growing (unloaded, unbounded z), so their length/density scalings carry NO information about a *tensioned, pressure-borne* cortex. **Do not** import any length/density number as a DCM cortex property.
- **R2 — No force law; density→output is non-monotonic.** Pillar length and actin density respond to NPF density in *opposite, range-dependent* ways (low→med: density↑ length~flat; med→high: length↑ density~flat, Fig 3c,d), and the paper itself invokes competing pushing-vs-tethering (ref 26) without resolving it. Any σ_NPF→γ or σ_NPF→protrusion-force coefficient fitted from this data would be a **magic number tuned to in-vitro geometry** → violates the no-magic-number rule. Adopt σ_NPF only as a *presence/areal-density field*, get the force magnitude from DCM's own mechanistic actomyosin/pressure machinery, not from this paper.
- **R3 — Cell type / species.** Mouse N-WASP VVCA + porcine/rabbit actin + Xenopus egg extract. Not MCF7. The 6 500–25 000 molecules/µm² band is a *general* membrane-NPF-density range cited across cell types — acceptable as an order-of-magnitude physiological anchor, but there is **zero MCF7-specific cortex-nucleator density** here.
- **R4 — Physiological-baseline compliance.** If M1/σ_NPF is ever built, it must be initialized at a physiological areal density (in-band, 6 500–25 000 molecules/µm²) from the start, and the underlying cortex must remain pressure-pre-tensioned (γ=ΔP·R/2 on a turgor-loaded shell) — this paper's un-tensioned free-growth baseline is exactly the "convenient unphysical zero" the baseline rule forbids; use its *density*, never its *mechanical baseline*.
- **R5 — Scope discipline.** The paper's rich content (branch geometry, fascin bundling, PI(4,5)P₂ cascade, lateral NPF diffusion, turnover) is genuinely valuable but belongs to the **FF filament-resolved engine**, not DCM. Pulling it into DCM would mean re-introducing filament-scale detail the DCM continuum deliberately coarse-grains — a scope error. Keep DCM's take to the single lever M1 (surface-localized, normal-directed, density-weighted generation) plus the σ_NPF/a_lipid parameters.

---

### One-line verdict
For the DCM engine this paper contributes one conceptual lever — make cortex/protrusion generation a **membrane-surface-localized, face-normal, NPF-density-weighted source field** — plus a physiological anchor density (6 500–25 000 molecules/µm²) and the lipid area 0.64 nm²; it offers no equation, no cortex-tension number, and no aggregate/division/confinement mechanism. Its real payload is for FF, not DCM.
