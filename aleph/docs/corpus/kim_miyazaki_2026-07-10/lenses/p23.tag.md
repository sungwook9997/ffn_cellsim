## Coverage vs existing gates

- **No overlap with any existing gate.** Our actin/cortex gates concern emergent single-cell mechanics (VG-H3-KU35 cortical tension; VG-U3-cortex-multifilament topology z, N_filaments; VG-U3-lamellipodia-separation Arp2/3 branching w~2). This paper reports neither tension, nor topology, nor branch angle/velocity — it reports how much fluorescent actin accumulates under a light dose on a bilayer. Different observable class entirely.
- **Conceptual (not numerical) touch-point** to `VG-U3-lamellipodia-separation` / `VG-U3-cortex-multifilament`: this is exactly the Arp2/3-branched, CP-limited, profilin-suppressed, NPF-nucleated network biology that FF models. But the paper supplies **no** rate constants, branch angle, capping rate, or nucleation rate — those live in the cited primaries. So it CONFIRMS none of our bands and CHALLENGES none.
- Nothing to TIGHTEN.

## New gate candidates

**None proposed.** Rationale (conservative, per philosophy):
- The two candidate "laws" (density ∝ light intensity; thickness ∝ illumination duration) are (a) monotonic qualitative trends without a closed form, and (b) parameterised by apparatus-specific light dose in arb. fluorescence units — not measurable at our single-cell mechanistic scale and not expressible as a defensible pre-registered band. Making one a gate would require inventing a band, which the rules forbid.
- Even the "network density set by NPF surface density" idea is the companion Nat Commun paper's result, not this protocol's, and would be sourced from there.

If anything, this is a **SourceEvidence pointer** (method/scenario reference for the Miyazaki optogenetic SLB actin-reconstitution system), carrying **no citable KB-x.y constant** — PI-gated, low priority.
