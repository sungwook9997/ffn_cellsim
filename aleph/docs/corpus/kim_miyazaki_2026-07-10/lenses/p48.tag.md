# TAG-validation lens — p48

**Kim & Kim 2025, "The mechanism of dynamic steady states in lamellipodia," PLOS Comput Biol 21(10):e1013572** — DOI 10.1371/journal.pcbi.1013572. Taeyoon-Kim lab (Purdue), the canonical fine-grained cytoskeleton line the FF engine mirrors. Open code: github.com/ktyman2/Lamellipodia.

## Paper kind

**simulation** (agent-based, filament-resolved Brownian dynamics — model output, not measurement).

This is decisive for how its numbers may be used: **every value in the Quantitative-Results table is a MODEL OUTPUT and is comparison-only — never a truth source and never a gate band.** The paper is, however, the FF engine's *exact paradigm* (Cytosim-class BD: F-actin bend/stretch/torsion segments, Arp2/3 70° angle-harmonic branch, ACP crosslinks, NMII via parallel-cluster-model with catch-bond unbinding, Bell-model adhesion clutch, buckling-driven severing). Its value to us is threefold: (1) a near-drop-in **reference implementation** to cross-check FF behavior against; (2) it *re-cites* genuinely experimental literature anchors (NMII 120 nm/s from Johnson 2019 / Cuda 1997; Arp2/3 70° from Amann-Pollard 2001) that CAN be registered — attributed to those underlying refs, not to this simulation; (3) it independently reproduces a **qualitative law** (motor-density → flow rises while traction *saturates/plateaus*) that CONFIRMS an existing KB finding.

## Coverage vs existing gates

- **VG-U2-biphasic-traction** (KB-2.8; observable = mean traction vs substrate stiffness; note already records *"biphasic is saturating not peaked"*). → **CONFIRMS.** Fig 5G shows total traction force *rising then plateauing* with motor density (FA-unbinding-limited), and Fig 6G shows force rising with FA-region then dropping — i.e. saturating/monotone-then-limited, not a sharp peak. This is an *independent agent-based reproduction* of our KB-2.8 "saturating" finding. Does not tighten a band (different abscissa: motor/FA density, not substrate stiffness) but reinforces the mechanism. No band change.
- **VG-U3-lamellipodia-separation** (Phase-2 entry gate, blocked; per-instance {r_LE, p, w_L, n_barbed, v_p}, Mueller-2017 Hill / Bieling-2016 branching). → **RELATED, not covered.** This paper is the retrograde-flow dynamics of exactly that lamellipodial compartment; when FF's lamellipodium separation lands, this paper is the reference for the flow observable. No numeric band imposed here.
- **VG-U3-gactin-pool** (monomer conservation, Phase-2). → **RELATED.** Paper runs a closed monomer pool (~10% free floor, recycled) — same conservation concept; its "periodic density band" is an acknowledged closed-pool artifact, a cautionary note for our pool implementation.
- **VG-U2-traction-magnitude** (FA 1–10 nN, cell total 10–100 nN). → **NOT comparable.** Paper's ~0.2–0.3 nN is total over a 5×2.5 µm sub-cellular patch, not a whole-cell number; do not confront the band with it.
- **VG-U2-catch-slip-bond** / **MC-U2-fa-motor-clutch** (Bell-Evans off-rate). → **SAME physics.** Paper's Eq S10 FA unbinding is a Bell slip-bond (k = k⁰ exp(λ F/kBT)) — identical form to our clutch off-rate; a structural cross-check, no new datum.
- **MC-U4-junction-cadherin** / catch-bond — the NMII here uses a PCM *catch*-bond assumption; tangential, not the cadherin junction.

Nothing in this paper lands *inside* an existing gate band to tighten or challenge it. The only direct interaction is the **CONFIRM** on VG-U2-biphasic-traction.

## New gate candidates

All flagged **candidate — PI-gate review**. Bands, where given, must be anchored to *experimental* retrograde-flow literature (Ponti 2004, Gardel 2008, Alexandrova 2008), NOT to this simulation's output; the paper only tells us the FF engine should *land in* the physiological window.

1. **VG-FF-retrograde-flow-emergence** (candidate). Observable: steady-state actin retrograde flow speed emerging from an FF lamellipodium/cortex with front polymerization + rear NMII contraction + FA clutch. Engine: **FF**. Band: **~20–80 nm/s** (physiological, experimentally anchored — this paper's ~20–30 nm/s sits at the low end and is *consistent*). Rationale: retrograde flow is a genuine emergent, measurable observable at our single-cell scale and directly ties to the FF crawl work (memory: crawl 28–45 nm/s). Source: Fig 2E,F + refs 50–53. *Do not adopt 20–30 nm/s as the band — that is model output; anchor to experimental flow measurements.*

2. **VG-FF-flow-vs-friction-monotonicity** (candidate, behavioral). Observable: sign of ∂(flow)/∂(FA friction) and ∂(traction)/∂(motor density). Engine: **FF**. Expected (qualitative contract, no magic number): flow **decreases** monotonically with FA-region size/friction; traction **rises then saturates** with motor density (force-limited by clutch unbinding). This is a mechanism-shape cross-check, defensible as a contract because it is a sign/monotonicity statement, not a fitted band. Source: Figs 5,6. Overlaps VG-U2-biphasic-traction's spirit — may fold in there rather than stand alone.

(Arp2/3 70° and NMII 120 nm/s are *input constants*, not emergent observables → registered as Params below, not gates.)

## Oracle candidates

- **Angle-dependent severing law (Eq S9): k_sev = k0,sev · exp(λ_sev · θ).** Empirical (adapted from Kim ref 61, reproducing McCullough 2011 severing-angle distribution) — *not* a first-principles closed form. Low-priority candidate as an FF buckling-severing **cross-check** if/when FF implements angle-dependent severing; not oracle-grade truth.
- **Cylinder drag (Eq S2): ζ = 3πμ r_c (3 + 2 r₀/r_c)/5** (Clift-Grace-Weber). A standard hydrodynamic closed form — usable as a drag-coefficient sanity cross-check for FF segment drag. Minor.
- **Bell FA unbinding (Eq S10)** — already covered by our Bell-Evans oracle; no new oracle.
- **NMII force-velocity via parallel-cluster-model** — our motor oracle is Hill (1938); PCM (Erdmann-Schwarz) is a *different* mechanistic model, not a closed form. Not an oracle; noted as an alternative motor mechanism the FF NMII could be validated against on-demand.
