## Coverage vs existing gates

- **Talin / filamin unfolding forces (>4 pN, 300 pN, ΔL 3.2→6.2 nm)** — these are single force-sensor protein-unfolding events. Our nearest existing contract is `VG-U2-catch-slip-bond` (integrin-FN lifetime-vs-force, peak ~30 pN) and the Bell-Evans oracle. Talin/filamin domain unfolding is a *different observable* (protein-internal unfolding, not bond off-rate) and is **below OUR coarse-graining floor** — we do not resolve intramolecular SMD. Not covered, and not proposable as a gate at our scale.
- **Actin+ACP network stress ~10–40 Pa (Fig. 1b)** — conceptually adjacent to `VG-U1-G0-linear` (G0 ~30–100 Pa reference collagen) and `VG-U1-strain-stiffening`. But the number is read off a plot axis of a *different* Brownian-dynamics reconstitution (Kim ref 4), at a ms window, with no stated strain amplitude or crosslink density → not a usable band. It **weakly, qualitatively CONFIRMS** that crosslinked semiflexible networks sit in the tens-of-Pa modulus regime our U1 gates already encode; it does not tighten or challenge them.
- **Strain-stiffening bending→stretching transition** (Fig. 3 three-regime stiffness curve) — qualitatively the same physics as `VG-U1-strain-stiffening` (exponent [1.5,2.5]). Review is schematic (no exponent, no critical strain) → **CONFIRMS the conceptual picture, adds no band.**
- **Cortical/junction tension, spheroid compaction (Fig. 4, ref 128 actomyosin 3D compaction)** — touches `VG-H3-*`, `MC-U4-junction-cadherin`, DCM necrosis/collective gates conceptually but supplies zero tension values, rates, or geometry.
- **~10 s motor processivity / nN chromatin force** — no existing gate; both are below/outside our current observable set and given only as order-of-magnitude discussion points.

**Net: every quantitative item is already conceptually covered or lies below our scale. Zero new coverage.**

## Oracle candidates

**None.** The only force expressions in the paper are schematic annotations of someone else's model (Fig. 1c, Kim ref 110): nodal Stokes drag `F_D,i = −C_c·v_i` and a quasi-static integrin-node force balance `F_SF + F_E + F_FA + F_L + F_D = 0`. These are generic (linear drag = our BAOAB drag; force balance = our residual gate `VG-U3-force-balance`) with **no constants given**, so they add no closed-form cross-check we don't already have. No Helfrich/Young-Laplace/Bell-Evans/Hill-type law is derived here.
