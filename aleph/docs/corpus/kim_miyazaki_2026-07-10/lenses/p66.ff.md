# FF lens — p66

**Paper:** Hassan, Biel, Umulis, Kim (2019), *Interplay Between the Persistent Random Walk and the Contact Inhibition of Locomotion Leads to Collective Cell Behaviors*, Bull. Math. Biol. 81:3301–3321. DOI 10.1007/s11538-019-00585-1.

**One-line verdict for FF:** This is a *lumped, over-damped, 2-point agent-per-cell* model of 2D mesenchymal migration — the exact abstraction level the FF engine exists to replace. Its traction is a constant-torque Lagrange construct, not a mechanistic clutch/myosin. Direct mechanism/parameter transfer to FF is **near-zero** (and the mechanistic constants κ, α, β, η, γ, M live in Online Table 1, an ESM not in the PDF, so cannot be KB-registered anyway). What it *does* give FF is (a) **behavioral validation targets** for emergent single-cell crawl (PRW MSD ballistic→diffusive crossover, speed magnitude) that our FF crawl (28–45 nm/s) should sit near, and (b) **conceptual protocol ideas** — lamellipodium finite-lifetime turnover and "FA only forms under an active protrusion" — that FF already realizes more mechanistically. Report at OUR native single-cell fine-grained scale, keep it as an *oracle*, not a source of physics.

## 1. MECHANISMS to adopt

Honest triage — most of this paper is at the wrong altitude for FF. The salvageable levers:

- **Clutch / lamellipodium module — finite-lifetime protrusion turnover as an EMERGENT-crawl driver (conceptual confirmation, not new physics).** The paper's core PRW mechanism is: a lamellipodial "active section" lives for a finite duration `T_F` (canonical 6 min), lays down traction-bearing FAs only under itself, then deactivates and a *new random-direction* section activates. This finite-lifetime + directional-reset loop is precisely what FF already produces mechanistically via clutch turnover (motor-clutch engage/disengage + Bell-Evans integrin catch-bond off-rate). **Do NOT adopt their `T_F`-timer as a mechanism** — that is a lumped proxy for clutch/actin-turnover kinetics FF resolves from first principles. The value: it confirms our FF crawl-emergence storyline (protrusion + clutch turnover → persistent-then-diffusive walk) is the mainstream mechanism, and gives the *effective lamellipodium lifetime* FF should emit as a derived observable (~minutes) rather than set.
  - *Why it doesn't beat current FF:* it is strictly coarser. FF's clutch already delivers the same behavior with force-dependent off-rates and real retrograde flow. Use as cross-check only.

- **CIL as substrate-resource partitioning (conceptual, for the multi-cell/DCM boundary — NOT single-cell FF).** Their CIL emerges with *no explicit repulsion or cadherin* — purely because each substrate point is claimed by the single nearest cell-point, starving a contacting cell's protrusion of FAs so its section deactivates. For FF this is out-of-scope at single-cell scale, but it flags a design idea if FF ever runs two adherent cells sharing an ECM: **traction competition for shared ECM anchor points** could produce contact-inhibited protrusion *mechanistically* (integrin binding-site occupancy) without a lumped repulsion. Park for a future multi-cell FF scenario; do not build now.

- **"Sensitivity to contact" = contact-triggered protrusion turnover (conceptual only).** Higher sensitivity → a small drop in engaged substrate points deactivates the protrusion. In FF this maps to nothing new — it is just clutch disengagement when engaged-clutch count falls (load redistribution → Bell-Evans avalanche). Note it as the *emergent* behavior FF should exhibit, not a parameter to add.

**Net:** zero new fine-grained mechanism to port. This paper is a behavioral oracle, not a physics donor.

## 2. FORCE LAWS / KINETIC SCHEMES to port

None are mechanistically portable — but transcribed for completeness and to show *why* each is below FF fidelity:

- **Front–rear Hookean spring (Eq. 3):** `F_I^FR = −κ (|r_I^FR| − r₀^FR) · r̂_I^FR`. A single lumped 2-node cell body spring. FF has no analog need — the cell is an explicit filament network + membrane + cortex. **Do not port.**
- **Traction force `F_c,i` (propulsion):** equal-magnitude, *non-centripetal tensile* force tangent to a circle of radius ½·(mean cell-point→FA distance); magnitude re-solved every step from the coupled torque/force/kinematic system (Eqs. 1,2,4). It is a **Lagrange-multiplier construct with no force–extension constant** — the antithesis of the FF motor-clutch, where traction is the summed force of explicit integrin clutches loaded by myosin-driven retrograde flow. **Do not port**; it is exactly the lumped mechanism the PI hard-rule forbids.
- **Over-damped drag laws:** internal `−α(v_F−v_R)`, cell–cell friction `−Σ_J β(v_I−v_J)` within `r_crit`, substrate/medium `−η v`, rotational `−γ ω`. FF uses BAOAB with per-node drag derived from cytoplasm viscosity (η=65.9 Pa·s) — mechanistically grounded, unlike these fitted lumped coefficients. **Do not port.** (The `β` cell–cell effective-friction term is worth noting only because it echoes the project's "cadherin contact ≈ viscous friction" framing — a DCM-side note, not FF.)
- **Constant generated torque `M` (M_F ≫ M_R):** each cell-point injects a fixed torque, justified by the roughly constant strain energy a cell delivers to a substrate (Gardel 2010). This is a *macroscopic energy-injection* proxy for the entire actomyosin engine. FF generates this emergently from Hill-law myosin minifilaments. **Do not port** — but the Gardel constant-strain-energy observation is a useful *sanity band* for FF: total traction strain energy per cell should be roughly conserved across protrusion cycles.
- **Kinematic constraint (Eq. 4):** `v = Δ × ω` — a geometric device to force the traction to be tensile. No FF analog; FF traction sign is emergent from clutch loading. **Do not port.**
- **MSD & slope (Eqs. 6–7):** `MSD(τ)=(1/N)Σ_I⟨‖r_I(t+τ)−r_I(t)‖²⟩`, `s = d ln MSD / d ln τ`. **PORT AS AN ANALYSIS METRIC** (not physics) — this is the standard crawl-persistence diagnostic FF should compute on its rear/centroid track: s→2 ballistic (short lag), s→1 diffusive (long lag).
- **Nematic order tensor / Saupe scalar (Eqs. 8–9):** `Q=(1/2N_C)Σ(3u⊗u−I)`, `h=(1/2N_C)Σ[3(u·n)²−1]`. Multi-cell only; keep filed for a future FF monolayer analysis, not single-cell.

## 4. NETWORK ARCHITECTURE / PROTOCOL to reproduce

Not a reconstitution/rheology assay — a 2D rigid-substrate motility model. Nothing here is an FF network-architecture donor. Two protocol elements are worth filing as *future FF crawl-analysis conventions*:

- **Single-cell PRW protocol (usable as an FF crawl-analysis harness):** track the cell over long time (30–60 h equivalent), compute MSD(τ) over a τ-window out to ~400 min, report the log-slope curve and its ballistic→diffusive crossover, plus the redirection-angle distribution between successive displacement vectors. **This is the right way to characterize FF's emergent crawl persistence** — adopt the *analysis*, run it on the native full-compartment adherent FF cell (`--from-resting --cortex-fil 38000 --microtubules`), not their 2-point cell.
- **Substrate as a discrete anchor lattice:** their FAs form only where a substrate lattice point lies under an active protrusion. FF's ECM/collagen anchors already play this role mechanistically (integrin clutches bind discrete ECM sites). No change needed; note the parallel.

Multi-cell density/nematic protocols (Figs 5–7) are DCM-monolayer territory, explicitly out of FF single-cell scope.

## 6. RISKS / caveats

- **Altitude mismatch (primary risk).** The whole model is lumped/over-damped/2-point — importing any of its physics (constant torque, kinematic-constraint traction, fitted α/β/η/γ) would directly violate the fine-grained-mechanistic hard rule. Treat as oracle, never as mechanism. The dossier's own FF flag ("Low direct, moderate conceptual") is correct.
- **No thermal / Langevin dynamics.** Stochasticity is only random section activation. FF's BAOAB thermal + Bell-Evans stochastic unbinding is a different (and more physical) noise source; their PRW statistics are not a strict target for FF's noise structure.
- **Rigid substrate, no FA maturation, no catch bonds.** They explicitly drop FA maturation (justified only by substrate rigidity) and use no Bell/catch bond. FF must keep its integrin catch-bond + FA-maturation machinery ON; do not let this paper's simplifications leak in as "acceptable."
- **Species/isoform & 2D vs 3D.** Fibroblast-like mesenchymal, 2D assay; MCF7 native FF is 3D with turgor/cortex. Speed magnitudes (10–17 nm/s) are *order-of-magnitude* comparators, not calibration data — never fit to them (no-fitting rule).
- **Parameter table is in the ESM.** Even if one wanted the mechanistic constants they are not in this PDF; do not fabricate or infer κ/α/β/η/γ/M values into the KB.
- **Native-scale rule intact.** Everything actionable here (MSD/slope/redirection analysis on the emergent crawl) must be run on the native full-compartment adherent FF cell (`--from-resting --cortex-fil 38000 --microtubules`), never a coarse or stripped config — the paper offers analysis conventions, and those conventions apply to the native cell only.
