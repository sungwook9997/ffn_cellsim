# FF lens — p09

**Paper:** Jo, Yim, … Taeyoon Kim, Pilnam Kim (2025) "Reciprocal folding dynamics in cellular networks at the stroma–basement membrane interface." *Acta Biomaterialia* 201:360–371. DOI 10.1016/j.actbio.2025.05.069.

**Bottom line for FF:** This is a tissue-scale continuum (Ansys linear-elastostatics) + in-vitro reconstitution paper. It contains **no microscopic force law** (no Bell/catch bond, no Hill f-v, no bending/stretch potential, no rate constants). Its FF value is therefore **NOT parameter anchoring** — it is a clean, well-imaged **mechanistic phenotype** that FF's fine-grained traction/protrusion/clutch machinery should *emergently reproduce* on a stiff substrate: the "zipper-like" polarized-traction cell (F-actin at pseudopod tips, phospho-MLC2 between tips, paxillin FA anchoring to a stiff membrane), blebbistatin-abolished contraction, proteolytic-path migration, and collagen-fiber alignment → mechanical coupling. Treat as a **validation narrative + scenario spec**, not a rate-constant source. Relevance to FF is real but indirect (score ~2/5).

## 1. MECHANISMS to adopt

None of these supply a new *microscopic* mechanism (the paper has none). They are **emergent-phenotype targets** and one **scenario geometry** that FF's existing modules should generate. Where FF already has the machinery, the paper is the acceptance phenotype.

1. **Clutch + myosin (traction polarization) — "zipper" spatial organization as an emergent target.**
   - Observation (Fig 3C, 3E): a migrating stromal cell on a stiff eBM shows **F-actin concentrated at pseudopod tips**, **pMLC2 (active myosin-II) concentrated *between* the tips (cell body/inter-tip region)**, and **paxillin focal adhesions anchoring the tips to the stiff membrane**. Traction (PIV) points **perpendicular to the fold**, magnitude 0.13–6.62 µm surface displacement (Fig 5E).
   - Why it matters for FF: this is a spatial **separation of protrusion (barbed-end/Arp2/3 at tips) from contraction (myosin minifilaments in the body)** — exactly the front-protrusion / rear-contraction organization FF's crawl story ([[project-ff-crawl-mechanism]]) needs. FF currently gets crawl to *emerge* from protrusion + clutch-turnover, but has no independent validation that the *spatial* actin/myosin/FA layout matches a real polarized cell. This paper is that spatial acceptance test: FA (clutch density / paxillin) enriched at leading tips, myosin engaged behind them.
   - Adopt as: an FF observable — measure clutch (integrin/paxillin-proxy) areal density at protrusion tips vs body, and myosin-minifilament engaged-fraction at tips vs body, and require tip-FA-enriched / body-myosin-enriched (matches Fig 3C sign).

2. **Myosin-II is the necessary contraction driver (blebbistatin gate).**
   - Observation (Fig 3D): **blebbistatin 10 µM abolishes fold *formation* but does not disrupt an already-formed fold.** I.e. active myosin-II is required to *generate* the traction, but the anchored/adhered state is myosin-independent once set.
   - FF use: a **null-control validation** for FF's Stam–Hocky minifilament module — with myosin off (duty ratio→0 or minifilaments removed), FF traction/protrusion-driven substrate deformation must vanish, while a pre-established adhered configuration (from `--from-resting`) must persist. This directly exercises the FF crawl/traction chain and is a mechanism-clean pass/fail (no fitting).

3. **Proteolytic-path / stromal-fluidity migration — as a caveat, NOT a module to build now.**
   - Observation (Fig 3A/B, S6): cells migrate by locally, **irreversibly** remodeling (proteolysing) the collagen ECM, leaving a "proteolytic path"; MMP inhibition removes the condensate boundary. Condensate size ~70 µm is set by the proteolytic path.
   - FF relevance: FF's collagen-Mikado ECM ([[project-ff-active-movement-pieces]]) is currently **elastic/reversible**. This paper shows a real stromal cell's migration is partly **plastic** (protease-cut fibers don't spring back). This is a *future* fine-grained lever (an explicit crosslink/fiber-scission channel, MMP-like bond removal on the ECM crosslinker), but building it now is out of scope — flag it. Do NOT lump it as a plastic-strain proxy; if built, it must be an explicit per-bond scission event on ECM crosslinkers.

4. **Durotaxis / mechanical coupling via fiber alignment — emergent, already in FF's reach.**
   - Observation (Fig 4C): collagen fibers **align first (red)**, cell protrusion follows (blue) → cells couple through the aligned/bundled fiber matrix ("mechanical coupling", r<90 µm). Bundled fibers facilitate migration via durotaxis.
   - FF use: FF already has explicit filament ECM + crosslinkers; fiber alignment→guided protrusion should EMERGE. This is a two-cell (or cell + pre-aligned fiber-bundle) FF scenario validation, not a new mechanism.

## 2. FORCE LAWS / KINETIC SCHEMES to port

**None portable to FF's fine-grained runtime.** The paper's only "force law" is a coarse-grained boundary condition and two image-analysis definitions. Transcribed for completeness, but explicitly **not** for FF's mechanistic runtime (they are lumped — porting them would violate the fine-grained-over-lumped hard rule):

- **Contractility as boundary stress (FEM only, do NOT adopt):** constant contractile stress `P_Cell` applied as a Neumann traction on condensate faces; linear-elastic Hooke restoring stress `σ = C(E,ν):ε`, `∇·σ + f = 0` (quasi-static). This is exactly the *lumped* contractility FF is built to replace with explicit myosin minifilaments — it is an anti-pattern reference, not a port target.

- **(Eq. 1) Alignment %** `= (1 − D_{A−B}/180)·100%`, `D_{A−B}` = orientation difference (deg) between two features. **Portable as an FF *analysis* metric** (not a force law): quantify filament-vs-protrusion, protrusion-vs-substrate-fold, or fiber-vs-fiber co-orientation in FF outputs to compare against the >90% in-vitro alignment.

- **(Eq. 2) FA polarity** `= (PAX_whole − PAX_DAPI)/PAX_DAPI` = peripheral focal-adhesion enrichment relative to cell center. **Portable as an FF *analysis* metric**: replace paxillin intensity with FF's engaged-clutch count integrated over periphery vs nuclear-region ROI → a polarity scalar to compare tip-vs-body FA enrichment (§1.1) and to test the polarity↔elongation correlation (§5).

No off-rate, duty cycle, force-velocity, branching-angle distribution, or unbinding spectrum is present in this paper.

## 4. NETWORK ARCHITECTURE / PROTOCOL to reproduce (as an FF scenario)

The single-cell-relevant, FF-buildable scenario: **one polarized fibroblast crawling/traction-pulling on a thin STIFF substrate (eBM), with a soft fibrous ECM behind it.**

- **Substrate:** a stiff quasi-2D sheet the cell adheres to via clutches (the eBM analog). In FF terms: a rigid or high-stiffness membrane/plate the integrin clutches bind to (FF already has membrane containment + clutch machinery). The stiffness *contrast* (stiff sheet, soft fiber ECM) is the key ingredient — reproduce the contrast, not the kPa numbers.
- **Cell:** full-compartment native FF cortex cell (`--cortex-fil 38000 --from-resting --microtubules`, Nc≈266k) with a **polarized protrusion front** (Arp2/3 lamellipodium + bundled filopodium tips) and **myosin minifilaments enriched in the body/rear** — the zipper layout of §1.1. NO stripped/coarse config (native-scale hard rule).
- **ECM behind:** FF collagen-Mikado fiber network ([[project-ff-active-movement-pieces]]), initially isotropic, physiological density.
- **Protocol / observables to record:**
  1. Let protrusion + clutch turnover drive traction (existing FF crawl mechanism).
  2. Measure **clutch/FA polarity** (Eq. 2 analog) tip-vs-body → expect tip-enriched.
  3. Measure **myosin engaged-fraction** tip-vs-body → expect body/inter-tip-enriched.
  4. Measure **traction direction** relative to the protrusion axis (PIV analog) and **fiber alignment** ahead of the cell (should align *before* protrusion advances — Fig 4C ordering).
  5. **Blebbistatin null run:** myosin off → traction/substrate-pull must vanish; a pre-adhered (`--from-resting`) state must persist (Fig 3D).
- **Two-cell extension (optional, if FF multi-cell is available):** two fibroblasts <90 µm apart on the fiber ECM → expect fiber alignment → mechanical coupling → co-migration (Fig 4). Single-cell FF is the primary deliverable; this is a stretch goal and edges toward DCM/aggregate scale.

This scenario mainly re-uses FF's existing crawl/traction/ECM assets — its value is providing an *externally-measured phenotype* (polarity signs, alignment %, blebbistatin gate) to validate them against, addressing the open "crawl / traction-driven self-organization" problem with a real dataset.

## 5. VALIDATION TARGETS for FF observables

Only the phenotype-level, single-cell-relevant numbers (tissue-network numbers like joining/branching angles are out of single-cell FF scope):

- **Traction polarity sign (Fig 3C):** FA/clutch enrichment **higher at protrusion tips than body**; active-myosin enrichment **higher in body/inter-tip than tips.** (Qualitative-sign pass/fail, both significant, p<0.05.)
- **Traction direction (Fig 5E):** highest surface displacement **perpendicular to the migration/fold axis** during onset; displacement amplitude **0.13–6.62 µm**.
- **Myosin-dependence (Fig 3D / blebbistatin):** traction generation **requires** myosin; pre-established adhesion is **myosin-independent**. → FF myosin-off run: substrate pull → 0; adhered state persists.
- **Fiber-alignment ordering (Fig 4C):** collagen fibers align **before** cell protrusion advances (causal ordering, not just co-occurrence).
- **Alignment magnitude (Fig 4F, 5B):** filament / protrusion / substrate-fold co-orientation **>90%** (≈93.5% early, →97.8% at 24 h). Use Eq. 1 metric on FF outputs.
- **FA-polarity ↔ elongation (Fig 5C):** focal-adhesion polarity **inversely correlated with cell circularity** — more elongated (lower circularity) cells pull harder / are more polarized. FF test: correlate clutch-polarity scalar (Eq. 2 analog) with cell aspect-ratio across seeds.
- **Whole-cell modulus cross-check (loose):** FF's emergent cell stiffness should sit in the 3T3 regime **~8–14 kPa** (AFM-cited) — a sanity band, not a fit target.

## 6. RISKS / caveats

1. **Regime mismatch — tissue continuum vs single-cell fine-grained.** The paper's *quantitative* physics is coarse-grained linear elastostatics with contractility as a boundary stress. Its numbers (756 kPa, 354 Pa, 200 kPa, 1.91 µm) are **tissue/condensate-level and lumped** — importing any as an FF molecular constant would violate the fine-grained + no-magic-number rules. Use ONLY the qualitative phenotype and the analysis-metric definitions.
2. **In-vitro reconstituted fibroblast-on-collagen vs living cortex.** 3T3/CCD18Co/NOF fibroblasts on engineered collagen ≠ MCF7 cortex (FF's baseline). Isoform/species/cell-type differ; the polarity *signs* and myosin-dependence should transfer (conserved actomyosin), the *magnitudes* should not be assumed to.
3. **2D-sheet assay vs 3D cell.** Traction is measured on a quasi-2D stiff sheet interface. FF runs a full 3D compartmented cell; reproduce the *interface* interaction (adhere to a stiff plate, ECM behind) without flattening the cell — keep the native full-compartment build.
4. **Plastic/proteolytic ECM is real but unbuilt in FF.** The proteolytic-path migration (§1.3) means the real cell's substrate is *irreversibly* remodeled. FF's current ECM is elastic. If FF results diverge from the paper's migration persistence, the missing plastic/scission channel — not an FF bug — is a likely cause. Flag to PI as a future explicit-scission crosslinker channel; do not lump it as a plastic-strain proxy.
5. **Physiological-baseline discipline.** Any FF-on-stiff-substrate scenario must still start from the physiological operating point (`--from-resting`, turgor/ΔP on, cytoplasm η, gravity, membrane, nucleus, microtubules ON) — the stiff-substrate BC is an *addition*, not a license to strip compartments.
6. **Contractility magnitude trap.** The 200 kPa "contractile stress" is a condensate-level areal stress and is **~10³× above** single-cell cortical-tension force scales — do NOT let it contaminate FF's γ (~0.15 N/m interphase) reasoning or the myosin force-scale ([[project-gamma-floor-likely-deficit]]). Different observable, different scale.
7. **Two-cell coupling edges into DCM/aggregate scale.** Multi-cell mechanical coupling (§4 stretch goal, 90 µm threshold) is arguably aggregate-scale; keep the primary FF deliverable single-cell to respect the OUR-scale constraint.
