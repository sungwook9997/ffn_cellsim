# TAG-validation lens — p56

Chiba, Miyazaki & Ishiwata (2014) *Quantitative Analysis of the Lamellarity of Giant Liposomes Prepared by the Inverted Emulsion Method*, Biophys. J. 107(2):346–354. DOI 10.1016/j.bpj.2014.05.039.

## Paper kind

**method / experimental** (in-vitro reconstitution + quantitative fluorescence microscopy). The paper's deliverable is an imaging assay that quantifies how many bilayers a giant liposome has, plus a single closed-form fluorescence-vs-radius *optics* fit. There is **no simulation, no dynamics integrator, and no mechanical force/rate law**. The only "model" (Eq. 1) is a geometric-optics fluorescence-intensity curve — not a mechanobiology oracle. Consequently its numbers may be used only as **method/reference provenance** (unilamellarity justification for a single-bilayer membrane assumption); its two fit constants are microscope-optics parameters, not physiological constants, and are **not overlay targets** for DCM/FF observables.

## Coverage vs existing gates

- **VG-H8-membrane-tension** (MC-H8-membrane-surface): the closest contact point. That gate targets tether force f_t (5–40 pN), apparent tension T (3e-5–3e-4 N/m), bending modulus κ_m (0.4–1.2e-19 J), and K_A (~0.24 N/m). **This paper measures NONE of these** — no tension, no modulus, no bending rigidity, no tether pull. It only certifies that the *membrane is a single bilayer* and gives a *cited* ~5.2 nm bilayer thickness. So it neither confirms, tightens, nor challenges any band in VG-H8; it is at most background provenance ("inverted-emulsion GUVs are >90% unilamellar → a single-bilayer membrane surface is the right idealization").
- No existing gate concerns lamellarity, fabrication yield, or microscope optics, so σ=0.913 µm and δ=0.143 µm map to **nothing** in the gate set (correctly — they are instrument constants, not observables of our cell).
- Actin-encapsulation / depletion-bundling (Fig 3) touches FF's filament domain thematically but provides **no persistence length, no bundling energy, no rate constant** — nothing comparable to any FF oracle or gate.

Net: **0 gates covered, 0 confirmed, 0 tightened, 0 challenged.**

## New gate candidates

**None.** No observable here is (a) measurable at our single-cell mechanistic scale AND (b) a defensible force/rate/modulus contract. Lamellarity fraction and imaging σ are not physics observables of the simulated cell; proposing a gate on them would violate the "real, measurable observable with a defensible band at OUR scale" rule. The unilamellarity result is an *assumption-justifier*, not a gate.

## Param candidates

**None registrable as new physiological Parameters.** For the record, the only quantity with any physical-constant character is:

- Lipid **bilayer thickness ≈ 5.2 nm** — but it is *cited* (Song 1996 / ref 45), not measured in this paper, and no current Parameter row needs it (membrane is modeled as a surface with tension/κ_m, not resolved bilayer thickness). If a bilayer-thickness constant is ever wanted, source it from the primary reference, not from p56. **Do not register σ=0.913 µm or δ=0.143 µm** — microscope-optics constants, explicitly system-dependent/sample-independent (Parameter-table note in dossier), and would be magic numbers in our context.

No conflict with or supersession of any existing Parameter row (K_A, gamma_cortex, ell_p, etc. are untouched by this paper).

## Oracle candidates

**None.** Eq. 1 (apparent equatorial fluorescence I(r) with a Gaussian out-of-focus kernel) is a **microscopy forward-optics model**, not a mechanobiology closed form. It is unrelated to our analytic oracles (Helfrich κL/2R² & κq⁴, Young–Laplace ΔP·R/2, Bell–Evans, Hill, Kelvin–Voigt). Implementing it would only predict a fluorescence image, which the simulator does not produce. Not an oracle candidate.
