# DCM lens — p36

**Paper:** Jung, Fillenwarth, Matsuda, Li, Inoue, Kim — "Collective and contractile filament motions in the myosin motility assay." *Soft Matter* 2020, 16, 1548–1559. DOI 10.1039/c9sm02082a.

**Bottom line for DCM:** This is a **filament-resolved** (cylindrical-segment F-actin + explicit PCM myosin + Bell's-law ACP cross-linkers) Brownian-dynamics model of an in-vitro **2D motility assay** — it is FF physics, not DCM surface-mesh continuum physics. There is **no membrane, no cortex-as-surface, no turgor/osmotic pressure, no nucleus, no cell-cell junction**. So the direct "port an equation to the mesh" yield is **low**. Its real DCM value is *upstream justification and calibration of the lumped cortical-tension abstraction* γ: it quantifies how a contractile actomyosin network's macroscopic tension and its arrest ("frozen" network) depend on cross-link density and turnover — exactly the microscopic story DCM collapses into a single γ. Treat it as a **DCM sub-grid closure / validation-of-abstraction reference**, not a new mesh force. Relevance ≈ 1.5/5.

---

## 1. MECHANISMS to adopt (honest, DCM-scale)

DCM represents the whole actomyosin cortex as a lumped surface tension γ on the triangulated membrane. This paper's mechanisms live one level below that abstraction. Only two are worth touching at DCM scale, and both are **calibration/closure**, not new mesh forces.

- **(M1) Cortical-tension γ as an emergent, turnover-gated network tension — NOT a free constant.**
  DCM currently sets γ either as a literature scalar (interphase 0.15, mitotic 1.5 mN/m) or via the pressure-borne identity γ = ΔP·R/2. The paper shows the network's macroscopic **tensile force is (a) maximized at *intermediate* cross-link density R_ACP and *intermediate* zero-force off-rate k⁰_{u,ACP}**, (b) **buckling-limited** (too many rigid links → filaments buckle, no tension), and (c) **relaxes from a peak to a nonzero asymptote** set by cross-link turnover. **Why it beats DCM-now:** it tells you γ is *not* monotone in "how much cross-linker / myosin you add" — there is a percolation-gated optimum. Any future DCM effort to *generate* γ from a sub-grid cortical model (the standing open problem "cortical-tension γ generation") must reproduce this intermediate-optimum, or it is unphysical. Use as an **acceptance oracle** on a sub-grid γ generator, not as a runtime force.

- **(M2) "Frozen network" ↔ compaction-RATE saturation analogy for the aggregate-σ open problem.**
  The paper reports the network reaches a **network-level frozen steady state while remaining dynamic at the filament level**, and that adding permanent/slow-turnover cross-links (low k⁰_{u,ACP}) *arrests* motion (gliding speed → 0 at R_ACP≈0.03, C_A=60 µM). This is a clean microscopic instance of the DCM finding that **aggregate-σ compaction reaches a mechanical end-state fast then stalls, and real 24–48 h compaction needs cross-link/junction *turnover-driven rearrangement* (T1), not more static tension** (see `project-dcm-compaction-turgor-blocked`). **Why it helps:** it independently supports the DCM decision that the compaction *rate* limiter is **bond turnover / rearrangement**, not tension magnitude — a static high-tension / low-turnover network *freezes*, it does not keep compacting. Use as corroborating literature for the T1-rearrangement escalation direction (the paper's cross-link `k⁰_{u,ACP}` turnover ↔ DCM cadherin junction off-rate). This is an *argument*, not code.

Everything else in the paper (gliding speed vs motor density/length, bundle↔ring morphology, PCM walking rates) is **FF-scope** and should not be pulled into DCM.

## 2. EQUATIONS to port to the surface-mesh / continuum level

**None of the paper's six numbered equations port cleanly to the DCM mesh.** Eqs 1–2 are velocity autocorrelations of filament endpoints; Eqs 3–4 are filament persistence-length / tangent correlations; Eq 6 is filament–filament volume exclusion. All require explicit filaments DCM does not carry. For completeness and to flag *why* they don't map:

- **Eq. 3 (persistence length)** `l_p = k_{b,A}·r_{0,A}/(k_B T)` — filament bending → **no DCM analog** (DCM bending is membrane κ_m on faces, a different object). Do NOT reuse κ_m ↔ k_{b,A}.
- **Eq. 5 (heterogeneity Q_A)** `Q_A = (1/2N_G)[Σ_i std_j(ρ_A^{ij}) + Σ_j std_i(ρ_A^{ij})]` over an N_G×N_G grid of segment counts — this **is portable as a DIAGNOSTIC**, not a force. Map ρ_A^{ij} → number of surface nodes (or cells, in an N≈400 aggregate) in grid cell (i,j); Q_A then measures **spatial heterogeneity of a compacting aggregate** (clustering vs uniform). Useful as a DCM aggregate-compaction observable to detect non-uniform collapse. N_G=20 for a 10 µm box → pick N_G so grid pitch ≳ cell diameter for the N≈400 aggregate.
- **Eq. 6 (soft harmonic repulsion)** `U_r = ½κ_{r,A}(r₁₂ − r_{c,A})²` for r₁₂<r_{c,A} — same functional form DCM already uses for **node-face penalty contact** (Ericson); no new physics, just confirms the soft-harmonic contact choice is standard. `κ_{r,A}` ↔ DCM contact penalty stiffness; `r_{c,A}` ↔ contact cutoff. Not a change, a consistency check.
- **Bell's law (unnumbered)** `k_u = k⁰_u · exp(F·λ / k_B T)` — DCM's cadherin junction already uses a catch-bond (Rakshit KU-4.2), which is *more* mechanistic than this slip-only Bell form. **Do not downgrade** the DCM junction to Bell; note only that the paper's cross-link turnover parameter k⁰_{u,ACP} is the conceptual twin of the DCM junction zero-force off-rate.

**Net: port Eq. 5 as a heterogeneity diagnostic only. No force equation ports.**

## 4. GEOMETRY / PROTOCOL to reproduce

The paper's geometry is a **10 × 10 × 0.1 µm quasi-2D slab with x,y periodic BCs and surface-immobilized motors** — a motility-assay, fundamentally not a closed-surface cell. It is **not** a DCM scenario. Nothing here maps to confinement, aspiration, AFM, division, or durotaxis at the single-cell mesh level.

The one transferable *protocol idea* is analytical, not geometric:
- **Turnover-controlled arrest sweep.** The paper sweeps cross-link turnover k⁰_{u,ACP} (0 = permanent → 10× reference) and shows tension peaks-then-relaxes with a nonzero asymptote set by turnover. **DCM analog protocol:** in the N≈400 aggregate, sweep the **cadherin junction off-rate / T1-rearrangement rate** and measure compaction trajectory and final packing — expecting the DCM twin of "permanent links freeze, intermediate turnover keeps rearranging/compacting." This directly instruments the standing DCM open problem (compaction RATE). This is a *DCM scenario the paper motivates*, not one it provides geometry for.

## 5. VALIDATION TARGETS for DCM observables

Almost all quantitative targets are FF observables (gliding speed, autocorrelation decay, bundle/ring size). For **DCM** the usable targets are indirect and qualitative:

- **Intermediate-optimum shape of network tension.** A DCM sub-grid γ-generator (if/when built) must yield **tension maximal at intermediate cross-link density and intermediate turnover**, buckling-limited at high connectivity — reproduce the *shape*, not a number.
- **Percolation-gated arrest.** Contractile network tension/motion collapses once connectivity crosses ≈3% cross-links (network-spanning). A DCM cortex closure that keeps rising monotonically with cross-linker density would FAIL this oracle.
- **Peak-then-nonzero-asymptote tensile relaxation.** Tension relaxes from a transient peak to a turnover-set steady value — a DCM aggregate under sustained σ with junction turnover should show the analogous relax-to-plateau (not relax-to-zero, not monotone rise).
- **Frozen-network end-state.** Network freezes globally while dynamic locally — DCM aggregate-σ compaction likewise reaches a mechanical end-state (`project-dcm-ipc-large-dt`: ~100 s mechanical saturation) — this paper is independent corroboration that *static* tension saturates.
- **Heterogeneity metric Q_A** as a comparable observable if the Eq-5 diagnostic is adopted for aggregate uniformity.

No DCM run should be scored against the 140 nm/s speed, the C_A/κ_{r,A} morphology map, or ring radii — those are FF.

## 6. RISKS / caveats

- **Regime mismatch (severe).** In-vitro **purified F-actin + purified myosin on a glass surface, quasi-2D, no cell**. DCM is a living MCF7-scale 3D closed cell. There is no membrane, cortex-as-surface, turgor, osmotic pressure, nucleus, or cadherin junction in the paper. **Do not import any parameter as a DCM physiological setpoint** — that would violate the physiological-baseline rule (e.g. an assay cross-link off-rate is not a cadherin k_off; ACP density is not cortical cross-link density in a cell).
- **Level-of-description mismatch.** The paper is *sub-grid* to DCM. Its lessons enter DCM only as (a) acceptance oracles on a future sub-grid cortex model, or (b) qualitative corroboration of the turnover-limited-compaction direction. Porting Eqs 1–4/6 as DCM mesh forces is a category error (filament objects DCM does not carry).
- **Missing constants.** The quantitative force-law constants (k_{s,A}, k_{b,A}, drag, dt, r_{c,A}, k_B T) are in **ESI Tables S1/S2, absent from the provided PDF** — do not cite exact stiffness/timestep numbers; harvest ESI first if ever needed (FF, not DCM).
- **Slip-bond ACP vs DCM catch-bond junction.** The paper's cross-linkers unbind via **slip-only Bell's law**; DCM's cadherin uses the fuller **catch-bond (Rakshit KU-4.2)**. Adopting the paper's Bell form for the DCM junction would be a *downgrade* violating the "fine-grained mechanistic over lumped/proxy" hard rule. Keep DCM's catch-bond; use the paper only for the turnover-rate *intuition*.
- **2D vs 3D.** Percolation threshold (~3%) and the bundle/ring morphology are 2D-slab-specific; the *existence* of an intermediate-connectivity optimum generalizes, but the numeric threshold does not transfer to a 3D cortex/aggregate.

**How to use without violating baseline:** treat this paper as literature *support* for two DCM design choices already in flight — (1) γ is an emergent intermediate-optimum quantity, not a free monotone knob; (2) compaction rate is turnover/rearrangement-limited, not tension-magnitude-limited — and as a source of **oracle shapes** (intermediate optimum, percolation arrest, relax-to-plateau) for any future sub-grid cortex generator. Set no DCM physiological parameter from it.
