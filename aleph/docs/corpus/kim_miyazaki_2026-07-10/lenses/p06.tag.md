# TAG-validation lens — p06

**Paper:** Li, Kim & Szymanski (2019), "Multi-scale regulation of cell branching: Modeling morphogenesis," *Developmental Biology* 451:40–52. DOI 10.1016/j.ydbio.2018.12.004.

## Paper kind

**review** (modeling survey). No original data, no original model. It surveys others' finite-element cell-wall models (Yanagisawa 2015; Krupinski 2016) and cortical-microtubule-array self-organization simulations. Consequence for use: nothing here is a first-party experimental measurement, an analytic closed-form, or a simulation output *we* could treat as a truth source. Every number is a **secondary, cited plant-cell-wall value** — usable at most as SourceEvidence context, never as a validation-gate band for our MCF7/animal single-cell engines. The one FE-model output it reproduces (Yanagisawa 2015, Fig. 4c) is *simulation* (comparison-only, never a truth source) and it is plant-regime.

**Regime mismatch (decisive):** all quantities are plant cell-wall mechanics — turgor 0.5–1 **MPa**, wall stress 5–12.6 **MPa**, cellulose-fiber anisotropy, microtubule persistence length ~mm. Our single-cell targets are animal cortex tension (mN/m), cytoplasm viscosity (10–70 Pa·s), FA/junction forces (nN), turgor ~40 Pa. These differ by 10³–10⁶× and by mechanism (fiber-reinforced pressurized wall vs actomyosin cortex). No overlap in observable *or* band.

## Coverage vs existing gates

None of items 1–12 map onto an existing gate's observable **in-regime**. Nearest conceptual neighbors, and why they do NOT match:

- **VG-H8-membrane-tension / VG-H3-* (cortex tension, mN/m):** our cell's surface tension is actomyosin cortical tension 0.35–0.65 mN/m at turgor ~40 Pa. The paper's "wall stress ~10 MPa under 0.5–1 MPa turgor" is a stiff cellulose *wall*, a different object 10⁵–10⁶× stiffer. No confirm/tighten/challenge relationship — different physics.
- **Young-Laplace oracle (ΔP·R/2, `oracles/young_dupre.py` / surface_tension_bridge):** the paper's σ_wall ~ Π·R/t is the *thin-shell pressure-vessel* cousin of Young-Laplace but with a finite wall thickness t (membrane-stress form). It is directionally consistent with our oracle family but adds nothing — our DCM surface is a zero-thickness tension membrane (γ = ΔP·R/2), and this paper gives no numeric that would tighten that oracle at our scale.
- **VG-U5-* spheroid / collective gates:** the ~120° branch spacing and 14 h growth are plant single-cell morphogenesis, unrelated to MCF7 spheroid necrosis/expansion observables.
- **VG-U3-cortex-multifilament / microtubule content:** our MT module (if built) concerns animal cytoplasmic MTs; the plant *cortical* MT-array zippering/catastrophe rules are a different organization problem and the L_p ~mm here is a generic cited value, not a measurement.

Verdict: **0 gates confirmed, 0 tightened, 0 challenged.**

## New gate candidates

**None proposed.** No observable here is measurable at our single-cell animal scale with a defensible in-regime band. Proposing a plant-wall stress/strain gate would be inventing an out-of-scope contract — explicitly declined per the philosophy (a gate must be a real observable with a defensible band at OUR scale). If the project ever opens a plant/turgor-shell or cortical-MT-array line (currently out of scope), items 3–5 (FE stress 5–12.6 MPa, strain 0.023–0.039, wall material 3–5.3 nm/cycle) could seed *simulation-comparison* references then — flagged for a future PI scope decision, not now.

## Param candidates

**None to register** for the active KB. All numeric params are plant-regime and would conflict in *meaning* (not value) with our animal params:
- Turgor 0.5–1 MPa vs our physiological baseline ΔP ~40 Pa (osmotic). Do NOT register — would pollute the turgor parameter with a plant value 10⁴× off.
- σ_wall ~10 MPa vs cortex tension 0.35–0.65 mN/m — different observable entirely.
- E0 (matrix modulus), k1 (fiber modulus), κ (fiber dispersion): **symbols only, no numeric values given in this review** — even if wanted, they are not extractable here (they live in Yanagisawa 2015 primary).
- MT persistence length ~mm: generic cited textbook value; if an animal-MT module ever needs L_p, cite the primary (Howard&Hyman 2003 / Gittes 1993), not this review.

## Oracle candidates

**One weak, already-covered candidate — do not implement.** The paper states (verbally, order-of-magnitude) the thin-wall pressure-vessel scaling **σ_wall ~ Π·R/t** (in-plane wall stress ≈ 10× turgor because the wall is thin relative to radius). This is the finite-thickness membrane-stress form of the Young-Laplace / Laplace-law family we already encode as an oracle (ΔP·R/2 for a zero-thickness tension surface; `oracles/young_dupre.py`, `spheroid/surface_tension_bridge.py`). It offers no new closed form and no calibration numeric — our DCM surface is a tension membrane, not a thick elastic wall, so the t-dependent form is not the right oracle for our geometry. **No new oracle.**

(Conceptual, non-oracle note for DCM: the Yanagisawa **load → relax → remesh → reset-material** iterated growth loop under turgor is structurally analogous to DCM's surface-mesh-under-pressure + remesh approach — a transferable *modeling pattern*, not a validation artifact.)
