# TAG-validation lens — p33

**Paper:** Miyazaki, Kinosita, Shiroguchi (2013), "Accurate polarity control and parallel alignment of actin filaments for myosin-powered transport systems," *RSC Advances* 3, 8728–8733. DOI 10.1039/c3ra41112e.

## Paper kind

**experimental** — in-vitro reconstitution + nanofabrication method paper (fluorescence microscopy + myosin-V bead motility assay). No governing equations, no simulation, no closed-form law. Under our philosophy its numbers are usable only as **overlay / on-demand cross-check targets**, never fit targets, and only where the observable maps to a single-cell DCM/FF gate. Most of its headline numbers are **nanofabrication/device metrics** (array alignment quality, polarity accuracy, anchoring density) with **no DCM/FF gate homolog** — they describe an engineered isopolar array on glass, not cell mechanics.

Critically, the two quantities most relevant to us (actin persistence length; actin diameter) are **cited from prior literature (refs 15–19), not measured in this paper** — so this paper is at best a secondary/duplicate citation for values we already source, not a new primary measurement.

## Coverage vs existing gates

- **Actin persistence length l_p 15–20 µm (item 1)** — Adjacent to `PARAM-ell_p` (default 17 µm, range 9–22 µm), but that row is explicitly **Type-I collagen** persistence length (KU-1.1, ECM fiber mechanics), not actin. There is **no actin-l_p parameter row** in the current KB. The FF cortex/filopodium bending uses κ = k_B T · l_p implicitly; a dedicated actin-l_p param would be the right home. This paper is a **secondary (cited) source that CONFIRMS the widely-used 15–20 µm range** — it does not tighten it (it inherits refs 16–19). Note the collagen `PARAM-ell_p` numeric overlap (17 µm) is coincidental; do NOT conflate the two.
- **Myosin-V bead velocity 0.62 µm/s (item 3)** — Nearest existing knob is `PARAM-v_unloaded` (default 100 nm/s, range 10–100 nm/s), which is **actin retrograde-flow / motor-clutch unloaded velocity (KU-2.4/3.12)**, a different physical quantity (retrograde flow, not a single processive myosin-V bead on a track). 0.62 µm/s = 620 nm/s is ~6× that band but they are **not the same observable** → this neither confirms nor challenges `v_unloaded`. Our myosin force-generation model is the **Stam-Hocky bipolar NMII minifilament + Hill force-velocity oracle** (per architectural principle), not a myosin-V single-molecule processive motor; myosin V is the wrong isoform for cortical contraction. So item 3 is at most a **soft motor-velocity sanity reference**, not a gate target.
- **Alignment σ, polarity accuracy, densities** — **No existing gate; none proposed.** These are properties of an engineered glass array, not emergent single-cell observables. They fail the "measurable at OUR single-cell scale + defensible as a contract" test.
- **F_stall_motor / Hill** — paper gives **no stall force, no duty ratio, no force-velocity curve** → nothing for the Hill oracle.

## New gate candidates

**None recommended.** No observable in this paper maps to a defensible single-cell DCM or FF validation contract:
- The reconstitution/device metrics (σ=10.8°, 99.6% polarity, densities) are not cell-mechanics observables at our scale.
- l_p and diameter are cited constants (belong in Parameters, not gates), and are already covered in spirit by FF filament mechanics.
- Myosin-V velocity is the wrong motor isoform for our NMII contractility model and is single-molecule (a reconstitution cross-check at best, per "oracles are cross-checks not truth").

3. *(reference only, not for registration)* myosin-V unloaded processive speed 0.62 µm/s and myosin-XI 60–100 µm/s — keep as citation-backed sanity references in notes; not our NMII isoform, do not register as motor params.

## Oracle candidates

**None.** The paper contains no closed-form law, force-velocity relation, bond-kinetics equation, or constitutive model. The only implicit relation is the trivial geometric "line density ∝ area density," which is not an analytic cross-check for our engines.
