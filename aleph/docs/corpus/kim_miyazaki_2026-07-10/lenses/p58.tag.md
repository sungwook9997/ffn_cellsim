## Paper kind

**experimental** (in-vitro reconstitution) **+ simulation** (phase-field active-fluid model).
The registrable, validation-grade numbers are **measurements** (confocal + PIV on Xenopus egg-extract actomyosin in microwells) → usable as **overlay / on-demand cross-check** targets, never fit. The accompanying continuum model (Eqs 1–3, control params Pe, k_p^surf) is **simulation output** → comparison-only, and its nondimensional parameters are **model-internal, NOT physiological setpoints** (do not register as constants).

Scale mismatch caveat that governs everything below: this is a **bulk cell-sized reconstituted contractile gel** (Xenopus extract, cluster D_c≈100–140 µm), **not** our single MCF-7 cell (R=7.5 µm) and **not** a surface-mesh cortex. Per the validation philosophy (lab reconstitution = on-demand cross-check, oracles = truth), these numbers enter as **FF emergent-behavior sanity cross-checks flagged for PI-gate review**, not as new single-cell contracts.

## Coverage vs existing gates

No existing gate covers **myosin-driven bulk contractile advection / actin flow under confinement**. Nearest neighbors:

- **PARAM-v_unloaded = 100 nm/s (0.1 µm/s)** retrograde-flow / motor unloaded velocity (KU-2.4/3.12). This paper's **v=1.2 µm/s is a different observable** — emergent *network advection* of a contracting bulk gel, not single-motor unloaded velocity — and is ~12× faster. **Not a conflict**; do not overwrite. It is a useful upper-envelope cross-check that a native FF actomyosin network under contraction can produce ~µm/s collective flow.
- **VG-U3-cortex-multifilament / MC-U3-cell-integration** — our FF actomyosin cortex. The advection speed and periodic-wave behavior here are candidate *emergent* cross-checks for that module, but at bulk-gel scale, not the single-cell contract band.
- **VG-U5-wetting-dewetting / VG-U4-contact-angle** — confinement-shape→morphology transfer is thematically adjacent (geometry sets shape) but those are cell-substrate/junction contracts, not a confined-gel roundness law.
- Roundness-vs-W/D, cluster-area∝volume, quartic parameter: **no coverage**, and they are **system-specific to this reconstitution geometry** (microwell segment). Not portable to our single-cell scale as contracts.

Net: **CONFIRMS none, TIGHTENS none, CHALLENGES none** of the existing bands. Purely additive, and additive as cross-checks rather than contracts.

## New gate candidates (all: candidate, PI-gate review)

**CAND-VG-FF-myosin-advection-speed** *(strongest, most defensible)*
- Observable: peak inward actin advection speed in a myosin-contracting FF actomyosin network.
- Band/value: **~1.2 µm/s, order-of-magnitude cross-check (0.5–2 µm/s)** — treat as overlay envelope, not a tight contract.
- Condition: contractile actomyosin network, confined; myosin II active. Reconstitution (Xenopus), so cross-check only.
- Engine: **FF**.
- Source: v=1.20±0.41 µm/s, p.3/Fig 1c.
- Rationale/limits: physiologically sensible for a contracting network (cortical flows are 0.01–1 µm/s); good FF sanity number. Do NOT band tightly — reconstitution µm/s ≠ MCF-7 cortical setpoint.

**CAND-VG-FF-boundary-shape-transfer** *(qualitative / emergent)*
- Observable: a confined contractile actomyosin gel inherits its confining-boundary asymmetry (roundness R decreases as boundary asymmetry W/D increases), with cluster *area* set by confinement *volume* not shape.
- Value: **directional/monotonic** (R: ~1.0→~0.25 as W/D 0→1); area∝volume (n.s. across shapes at equal A_μ). No tight band — a sign/monotonicity contract.
- Condition: confined actomyosin, semiclosed geometry.
- Engine: **FF** (or DCM confined-aggregate as qualitative analog).
- Source: Fig 1i–l, Fig 2c,d.
- Rationale/limits: a mechanism-level emergent cross-check (contraction sets size, polymerization sets shape). Defensible only as a *qualitative* PI-reviewed sanity gate, not a numeric contract at our scale.

Not proposed as gates (too system-specific / not measurable at our single-cell scale): quartic parameter 0.069→0.088, 83% central positioning, 9.5× confinement ratio.

## Oracle candidates

**None qualifying as an analytic ground-truth oracle.** The paper's governing equations (Eq 1 force balance, Eq 2 advection–diffusion mass conservation with k_p source / k_d ρ sink, Eq 3 phase field) are a **continuum simulation model**, not a closed-form law, and their outputs are simulation → comparison-only (never a truth source, per philosophy). The roundness-vs-W/D and area-vs-volume relations are empirical, no closed form.

One conceptual cross-check worth noting (not an implementable oracle): the **Péclet-number scaling** Pe=(ζΔμ)₀/(Dγ) as the dimensionless group separating advection-dominated (size↓) from diffusion/polymerization-dominated (shape-preserving) regimes — a useful *dimensional-analysis sanity frame* for interpreting FF myosin-vs-turnover balance, but the numeric Pe values are model-internal and cannot be a band.
