# FF lens — p38

**Paper:** Yamazaki, Miyata, Morigaki, Miyazaki (2024) "Controlling Physical and Biochemical Parameters of Actin Nucleation Using a Patterned Model Lipid Membrane." *Nano Lett.* 24(5), 1825–1834. DOI 10.1021/acs.nanolett.3c02742.

**One-line for FF:** A membrane-templated in-vitro reconstitution that cleanly separates the two nucleator archetypes FF already models — Arp2/3+VVCA → dense branched pillar growing **perpendicular** (barbed-end-out, polymerization pushing) vs mDia1(FH1-FH2)+fascin → **parallel-bundled vortex growing horizontally** — and gives concrete, DOI-verified NPF/nucleator **surface densities (10 000–30 000 molecules/µm²)** and reagent stoichiometries. It carries **no force laws and no rate constants** for motor/bond kinetics: its value to FF is (a) a nucleation-site *seeding density* and *geometry* protocol, (b) two crisp architecture-validation scenarios, and (c) the barbed-end-at-membrane polarity rule for protrusion. It is NOT a source of γ-generation, contraction, or unbinding-spectrum physics.

Bottom line: **moderate FF relevance** — strong for two validation scenarios + a defensible nucleation-density anchor, weak/none for force laws, motors, crosslinker kinetics, or cortex-tension.

## 1. MECHANISMS to adopt

### 1.1 Nucleation-site **surface-density** as an explicit FF input (not lumped) — the strongest transfer
FF currently seeds nucleators; this paper pins the areal density to a measured, cell-matched number.
- **FF module:** Arp2/3 branch nucleator seeding + formin (mDia1) seeding on the membrane/cortex inner leaflet.
- **Change:** set the **surface density of active NPF/nucleator sites** on the membrane patch to **~10 000–30 000 molecules/µm²** (VVCA at 0.66% and 2% Ni-lipid), which the authors note sits in the *cellular* NPF range **6 500–25 000 molecules/µm²** (refs 40–42). Use the low end (~6 500–10 000/µm²) as the physiological-baseline seeding for a lamellipodial patch; do not exceed ~30 000/µm² without justification.
- **Why it beats current FF:** today FF nucleation-site counts are effectively convention/tuned. This gives a **literature-anchored, cell-matched areal density** so the number of Arp2/3 branch origins per µm² of protrusion is set from data, not chosen — directly serving the "no magic numbers / physiological operating point from t=0" rule.

### 1.2 Barbed-end-at-membrane polarity = polymerization pushing (protrusion mechanism confirmation)
- **FF module:** filament growth / protrusion (the crawl & filopodium/lamellipodium work).
- **Change:** enforce that **newly polymerized, uncapped barbed ends localize at the membrane and point toward it**; monomer addition there produces the pushing force that extends the network perpendicular to the membrane (two-color pulse-chase, Fig 1f: new actin incorporates *only at the pillar base*).
- **Why:** this is an independent, direct experimental confirmation of the Brownian-ratchet protrusion polarity FF already uses (barbed-end growth kernel, KB-3.6). It validates the **sign and geometry** of FF's protrusion force — useful as a sanity check for the crawl/protrusion module rather than a new mechanism.

### 1.3 Nucleator **type → architecture** dichotomy as a mechanistic switch, not a lumped mode
- **FF modules:** Arp2/3 (angle-harmonic 70°±35° branch) vs formin (mDia1 processive barbed-end elongation) + fascin bundling crosslinker.
- **Change:** the *same* membrane geometry produces qualitatively different networks purely from which nucleator/crosslinker is present:
  - **Arp2/3 + VVCA** (branched) → **dense, isotropic-in-plane pillar, grows perpendicular** to the membrane.
  - **mDia1(FH1-FH2) + fascin** (processive + bundler) → **vortex-like parallel bundles, grow horizontally along the pattern contour** (filopodia-like).
- **Why it beats a lumped "protrusion mode" flag:** FF's mechanistic rule says architecture must *emerge* from the branch-angle + crosslinker + motor primitives, not be imposed. This paper is the clean experimental target that the emergent architecture must reproduce: swap Arp2/3→formin+fascin in the same seeded geometry and the growth direction must flip perpendicular→horizontal. It is the FF lamellipodium-vs-filopodium test at reconstitution fidelity.

### 1.4 (Optional, lower priority) Fascin as an explicit parallel-bundling crosslinker
- **FF module:** crosslinker set (currently α-actinin, filamin).
- **Change:** add **fascin** as a tight parallel-bundle crosslinker (used at 50 nM here) if FF wants to reproduce the filopodial vortex; fascin enforces small inter-filament spacing / parallel polarity vs α-actinin's looser network.
- **Why:** the horizontal vortex architecture is fascin-dependent; without a parallel bundler FF cannot emerge the filopodium arm. Note: **this paper gives no fascin kinetics** (no k_off, no bond stiffness) — only its presence/concentration. Kinetics must come from another source (e.g. Kim rheology / Courtemanche); do not invent them.

## 2. FORCE LAWS / KINETIC SCHEMES to port

**None.** This is an experimental paper with no governing physics equations, no bond off-rate, no force-velocity, no branching-angle distribution, no duty cycle. The only transcribable quantitative relation is an **empirical calibration fit** (a lab-specific instrument calibration, NOT a physics law — do not port as a mechanism):

- **NPF surface density vs Ni-lipid mole fraction (Fig 3b, empirical linear fit):**
  `FI_VVCA = 157.7·x − 2.32`, R² = 0.88,
  where `x` = Ni-lipid mol% and `FI_VVCA` ∝ anchored His-VVCA surface density.
  **FF use:** only as the bridge from their assay control (Ni-lipid %) to the density numbers in §3. It maps to FF state solely as "set membrane nucleator areal density σ_NPF" — the fit itself has no place in the runtime.

- **Density derivation (stated in text, usable arithmetic):**
  σ_NPF ≈ (Ni-lipid fraction) / a_POPC, with `a_POPC = 0.64 nm²` (König 1997).
  At 2% Ni-lipid: 0.02 / 0.64 nm² ≈ 0.031 nm⁻² = **3.1×10⁴ /µm²**. This is the arithmetic FF should use if converting any assumed membrane lipid composition to a seeding density.

## 4. NETWORK ARCHITECTURE / PROTOCOL to reproduce (FF scenarios)

Two ready-made FF single-cell-scale scenarios (both at ~10 µm patch scale — matches MCF7 R=7.5 µm cortex length scale, so native-scale-compatible):

**Scenario A — "Arp2/3 pillar" (lamellipodium archetype).**
- Geometry: a 10×10 µm (also 2/4/8/16 µm) membrane patch seeded with Arp2/3-NPF sites at σ ≈ 10 000–30 000/µm².
- Mix: 5 µM actin, 50 nM Arp2/3, 15 µM profilin, 25 nM CP.
- Expected FF emergent output: dense **branched network growing perpendicular** to the patch, horizontal cross-section copying the seeded patch shape/size; barbed ends at membrane.
- FF variant of interest: **size sweep** — pillar length & actin density increase with patch size (2→16 µm); patches ≤2 µm lose straight vertical growth (a symmetry-breaking/buckling signal at small system size — relevant to FF's buckling/condensation open problem).

**Scenario B — "formin vortex" (filopodium archetype).**
- Geometry: same 10 µm patch, seeded with mDia1(FH1-FH2) sites.
- Mix: 5 µM actin, 15 µM profilin, 50 nM fascin.
- Expected FF emergent output: **parallel-bundled vortex growing horizontally along the patch contour** (not perpendicular). The perpendicular→horizontal flip vs Scenario A, with everything else fixed, is the discriminating test.

**Shape-invariance control:** equal-area (100 µm²) shapes (circle, polygons, cross, star, concave) give the same pillar length/density → FF's emergent architecture should depend on nucleation **area**, not perimeter shape (Fig 2, Fig S7). Good null test that FF is not spuriously perimeter-sensitive.

**Regime note:** this is a **flat 2D membrane patch**, not a closed cortex. For FF it maps onto a *protrusion patch on the cortex inner surface*, not the whole-cell cortex — keep it as a local-patch scenario inside the full-compartment cell, not a replacement for the native 38 000-filament cortex.

## 6. RISKS / caveats

- **Purified in-vitro, flat 2D, static — not living cortex.** No myosin, no turnover-driven flow, no turgor, no 3D confinement. Adopt *only* the nucleation-density seeding and the two architecture scenarios; do NOT infer cortex tension γ, contraction, or stress relaxation from this paper (it contains none). Keep FF's γ-generation work sourced elsewhere.
- **No force laws / rate constants.** Resist the temptation to treat the empirical fit `FI=157.7x−2.32` or any concentration as a mechanism. They are assay controls. Porting them as physics would violate the "no fitting / no magic numbers" rules.
- **Species/isoform:** mouse N-WASP VVCA and mouse mDia1; Xenopus egg extract; *Nostoc/rabbit* actin typical for such assays. FF targets human MCF7. The **architecture dichotomy is species-robust** (well-established biology) so it transfers; the exact densities are membrane-composition-specific — use the **cell-range 6 500–25 000/µm²** as the physiological anchor rather than the assay-specific 30 000.
- **Patch scale vs whole cortex:** 10 µm patch ≈ cell length-scale, so it's native-scale-compatible as a *local protrusion patch*, but it is a flat membrane, not the closed pressurized shell. Embed Scenario A/B as a patch on the full-compartment cell (membrane+cortex+turgor ON), never as a standalone stripped bare-membrane run — that would violate the native-full-compartment HARD rule.
- **Capping-protein-limited length:** pillar length here is set partly by CP (25 nM) capping barbed ends; the paper gives no k_cap, so FF length reproduction is qualitative (monotone-with-size), not a quantitative gate.
- **Small-patch symmetry breaking (≤2 µm loses straight growth)** is intriguing for FF's buckling/condensation open problem, but the paper offers no mechanism — treat as a *hypothesis to probe in FF*, not a validated target with a threshold to hit.
- **Related-work pointer (not this paper's data):** ref 63 = Muresan/Kim/Murrell 2022 (F-actin architecture constrains myosin thick-filament motion, Taeyoon Kim co-author) — that is the paper to mine for myosin/architecture *force* coupling and crosslinker rheology; p38 itself does not carry it.
