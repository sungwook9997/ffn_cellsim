# DCM lens — p52

**Paper:** Kubota, Ogawa, Miyazaki et al., *Microscopic Temperature Control Reveals Cooperative Regulation of Actin–Myosin Interaction by Drebrin E*, Nano Lett. 2021, 21, 9526–9533. DOI 10.1021/acs.nanolett.1c02955.

**One-line verdict for DCM:** Low direct impact. This is a purified in-vitro HMM/actin gliding assay with an optical heat-pulse method — no continuum, no cell-scale geometry, no surface mesh, no force law. It offers **one usable structural insight for DCM's cortical-tension γ model**: cortical actomyosin activity is a *cooperative* (Hill n up to 4), regulator-gated, temperature-sharpened switch, not a linear duty. Everything else is a candidate literature anchor for the KB, not a mechanism to port. I flag this honestly rather than manufacture applicability.

---

## 1. MECHANISMS to adopt

DCM's cortex module currently sets surface tension γ either as a static physiological value (interphase 0.15 / mitotic 1.5 mN/m) or via the pressure-borne identity γ = ΔP·R/2. There is no explicit *regulator-gated, cooperative* activation of the cortical myosin duty. This paper does not give us a cell-scale force, but it gives one genuinely portable qualitative constraint and two weaker ones:

- **(Primary, weak-moderate) Cortex γ generation → cooperative activation gate on the active-tension term.** The paper's central finding is that actomyosin force output is a **highly cooperative (Hill n → 4.0 at 37 °C) sigmoidal function** of an allosteric side-binding regulator, and that this cooperativity *sharpens with temperature* (n ≈ 1 below 34 °C). DCM's known-open γ-floor problem (memory: `project-gamma-floor-likely-deficit`, active γ floored ~500× under band) has been chased as a force-magnitude / density deficit. This paper argues the *duty is switch-like and cooperative near the physiological setpoint*, not a smooth linear ramp — so a cortex activation multiplier `a(regulator, T)` should be a **Hill sigmoid, not a linear scaling**. WHY it beats now: DCM's active-tension activation is effectively a linear/constant factor; a Hill gate reproduces the observed all-or-none suppression/recovery and would let a small change in a regulator variable flip the cortex between the interphase 0.15 and mitotic 1.5 mN/m regimes — matching the ~10× physiological γ swing DCM must span. **Caveat (hard-rule):** the Hill parameters here are for drebrin E on gliding *velocity*, not for cortical *tension* of a living MCF7 cell. Adopt only the **functional FORM** (cooperative sigmoid), not the numeric n/IC50, unless/until a cortex-specific datum is found. Do not hard-code n=4 as a DCM cortex parameter — that would be a magic number.

- **(Secondary, weak) Regulator stays bound while modulating — no dissociation switch.** Qdot tracking shows drebrin E does NOT detach when inhibition is relieved (Friedman p=0.344); regulation is by allosteric **filament-structure change (helical half-pitch 36→40 nm)**, tropomyosin-like, propagating cooperatively along the filament (≤2 helical repeats per bound regulator). Design lesson for any future DCM cortex-regulator variable: model activation as a *bound-state conformational modulation of duty*, not a bind/unbind occupancy latch. This directly echoes the project's own hard rule against binary latches (`feedback-junction-switch-fine-grained`) — the same anti-lumping logic applies to cortex activation.

- **(Tertiary, informational) Temperature as a first-class cortex modulator.** Heating raises actin–myosin affinity and the number of force-generating heads (paper cites muscle-physiology refs 19–21), roughly doubling glide speed 7–8 → 15 µm/s across 28→37 °C. DCM initializes at the physiological operating point (37 °C) by rule, so temperature is normally frozen. This is only relevant if DCM ever runs a thermal/confinement-heating scenario (cf. Oyama 2015 bleb/neurite heat, cited ref); otherwise it just confirms the physiological-baseline temperature is where cooperativity is maximal — i.e. DCM is right to sit at 37 °C.

## 2. EQUATIONS to port to the surface-mesh/continuum level

Only one equation has a plausible DCM mapping; the rest are geometric/apparatus-specific (temperature-gradient calibration Eq 1, per-event glide velocity Eq 2, endpoint-averaged temperature Eq 3) and do NOT map to a surface mesh.

**Hill dose–response (paper Eq 4)** — port the FORM as a cortex-activation gate:
```
v = 1 − C^n (1 − b) / (k^n + C^n)          (paper Eq 4, inhibitory)
```
Symbol map for an *inhibitory* cortex regulator (structure only, values NOT adopted):
- `C` → concentration/occupancy of a cortical actomyosin regulator variable carried per cortex node/face (DCM state: a scalar field on the surface mesh, like the existing per-node γ).
- `n` → Hill cooperativity (paper: 1 low-T → 4.0 at 37 °C). **Do not fix numerically** — derive or surface to PI.
- `k` → IC50 (paper: few nM → >30 nM). Regulator-specific; not a DCM cortex datum.
- `b` → residual (saturating) activity baseline.
- `v` → normalized activity multiplier applied to the cortex **active surface-tension term** γ_active, i.e. `γ_active(node) = γ_max · a(node)` with `a` a Hill gate on the regulator. For an *activating* regulator, use the ascending complement `a = C^n/(k^n+C^n)`.

**Equilibrium occupancy (paper Eq 5)** — port as the binding-state law feeding the Hill gate:
```
p = [R] / (Kd + [R])                        (paper Eq 5)
```
Map: `[R]` = free regulator concentration, `Kd` = dissociation constant (temperature-dependent, see §3), `p` = fractional saturation of cortex actin sites. In a fine-grained DCM cortex-regulator model, `p` (not `[R]`) would be the natural argument of the activation multiplier so that the regulator effect saturates physically — consistent with DCM's mechanistic-over-lumped rule.

No bending, area-elasticity (K_A), viscosity (η), or pressure (Π) equation is provided by this paper. The paper's `T(x) = −3.1 ln(x/x0) + 42.9` (Eq 1) is an apparatus calibration, not a physical law — do NOT port.

## 4. GEOMETRY / PROTOCOL to reproduce

Nothing from this paper is a DCM scenario. The experimental geometry is a 2D glass-surface gliding assay with a radial optical heat gradient — no cell, no membrane, no enclosed volume, no confinement, no aspiration, no division, no aggregate. There is no cell-sized boundary condition to reproduce.

The only reusable *protocol idea*, and only weakly: a **switch-and-recover activation protocol** — apply a step change to a cortex activation input, watch tension turn on with a ~5 s recovery time constant, then remove it and confirm reversibility (paper Fig 2C: three reversible heat pulses; Fig S3: τ = 5.4 s recovery). If DCM ever validates a dynamic cortex-γ activation gate, this reversible step-response with a single-exponential recovery is a clean qualitative test template. Not a physical geometry.

## 5. VALIDATION TARGETS for DCM observables

Direct DCM observables (cortical tension, shape index, division force, ring closure, compaction rate, contact angle, cortical flow speed): **none** — the paper reports none of these.

Indirect / cross-domain targets (register in KB, mostly FF-side):
- **Cortical actomyosin duty is cooperative** — a DCM cortex activation gate, if built, should be a *sigmoid that steepens toward the physiological setpoint*, not linear. Qualitative shape target (decay→sigmoidal), not a number.
- **~10× activity swing from a small regulator/setpoint change** — mirrors the interphase-0.15 ↔ mitotic-1.5 mN/m γ span DCM must reproduce; supports modeling that span as a switch, not a continuum ramp.
- **HMM/actin glide 7–15 µm/s over 28–37 °C** — sanity reference for FF myosin minifilament sliding speed (not DCM).
- **Activation recovery τ ≈ 5.4 s** — timescale sanity for any dynamic cortex activation relaxation.

These are shape/scale sanity checks, not quantitative DCM gates.

## 6. RISKS / caveats

- **Cell-type mismatch (major).** Drebrin E is a **neuronal** actin side-binding protein; DCM targets MCF7-like epithelial/cancer cells. There is no MCF7 drebrin datum. Importing drebrin's Kd/Hill numbers as MCF7 cortex parameters would violate the physiological-baseline and no-magic-number rules. Adopt only the *generic mechanistic form* (cooperative bound-state allosteric activation), and only if a cortex-appropriate regulator (e.g. tropomyosin, which the authors explicitly analogize to) is anchored with its own data.
- **In-vitro purified vs living cortex (major).** Purified HMM on glass + short 1–5 µm free filaments in 2D ≠ a crosslinked, pressurized, membrane-coupled 3D cortical shell. Gliding *velocity* is not cortical *tension*; the mapping velocity→γ is not established by this paper. Treat the Hill-form transfer as a hypothesis to be validated at native scale, never as a fitted law.
- **No continuum / no force law (structural).** The paper has zero interaction potentials, no bending/area/volume law, no motor force-velocity law, no bond kinetics. There is nothing to parity-check a Warp kernel against. Any DCM use is inspiration for model *structure*, not a portable equation with parameters.
- **Temperature is normally frozen in DCM.** DCM initializes at 37 °C by hard rule, so the paper's headline temperature-sweep result is off-regime — useful only to confirm the physiological setpoint sits at maximal cooperativity, or if a thermal/heat-scenario is ever run.
- **Do not fit.** Even the qualitative "sigmoid steepens at 37 °C" must not be tuned to make a γ gate pass; if a cooperative cortex gate is built, its n must be derived or PI-surfaced, per the no-gate-loosening / no-magic-number rules.

**Adaptation without violating physiological-baseline:** if this ever feeds DCM, do it as an *additive, default-off cortex-regulator module* whose production config is turned ON only at a cortex-appropriate regulator's real physiological value — not with drebrin's neuronal numbers. Until such a datum exists, this paper's DCM contribution is a single design principle ("cortex activation is a cooperative bound-state switch, model it as a Hill gate on occupancy") plus KB literature anchors.
