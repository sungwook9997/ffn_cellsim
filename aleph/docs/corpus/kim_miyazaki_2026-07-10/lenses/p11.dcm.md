# DCM lens — p11

**Paper:** Miyazaki & Harada, "Go-and-Back method: Effective estimation of the hidden motion of proteins from single-molecule time series," *J. Chem. Phys.* 134, 135104 (2011), DOI 10.1063/1.3574396.

**Bottom line up front.** This is a *statistical-inference / path-optimization* paper for single-molecule biophysics: given a noisy probe trajectory `y(t)`, recover the most-probable hidden protein trajectory `x̂(t)` by maximizing an Onsager–Machlup path probability (minimizing an action `S`), solved cheaply via a forward-x / backward-v perturbation scheme. It contains **no surface-mesh cell mechanics** — no cortex tension, turgor, membrane area-elasticity, bending, nucleus, cadherin junction, contact, division, confinement, or aggregate physics. There is **nothing to adopt as a DCM force or module**, and **no DCM parameter, geometry, protocol, or validation target** is derivable from it. Its only contact with our stack is the *shared substrate physics* (overdamped Langevin with fluctuation–dissipation noise `2γk_BT`) — which is already fully covered by the BAOAB/Leimkuhler–Matthews integrator, and even that touches the FF/integrator layer, not DCM force laws. The honest DCM relevance is near-zero; below I record the one speculative, non-mechanism method-transfer for completeness so the finding is auditable, then close out the six sections as N/A with the reasons.

---

## 1. MECHANISMS to adopt

**None applicable.** The paper contributes an inference algorithm, not a mechanistic force law. Nothing here replaces or improves any DCM module (membrane K_A / κ_m, cortex γ, cytoplasm η / Π₀, nucleus E_nuc, node-face contact, cadherin catch-bond junction, aggregate σ, remesh, T1). The paper's own Models A/B are a 1-D double-well (two-state protein switching) and a tilted washboard (F₁-ATPase stepping) — molecular-scale, not cell-scale, and not a mechanism DCM lacks.

**One speculative method-transfer (NOT a mechanism, flagged as out-of-scope):** the Go-and-Back / dominant-reaction-path (DRP) machinery is a *rare-event transition-path* optimizer. In principle it could post-process a stochastic DCM trajectory to extract the most-probable pathway of a rare shape transition (e.g. the loose→compact aggregate T1 rearrangement, a bleb nucleation, or a division/cytokinetic pinch-off) from noisy node coordinates, at ~10³× lower cost than gradient descent on the action. This is an *analysis* tool over DCM output, not a change to DCM physics, and it would require (a) a DCM configuration-space action, (b) the small-friction-ratio assumption `γ/Γ ≪ 1` to hold between a "hidden" slow collective coordinate and a "visible" fast one — neither is set up in our engine. It does **not** advance the DCM forward simulator and should not be scheduled as engine work; note only.

## 2. EQUATIONS to port

**None.** The governing equations are inference/action functionals in an abstract 1-D reaction coordinate, with symbols that have no DCM surface-mesh counterpart:

- Onsager–Machlup action, Eq. (5): `S = (1/4γ)∫[γẋ + V'_eff + U_x]² dt − (k_BT/2γ)∫[V''_eff + U_xx] dt + (probe terms)`. This penalizes deviation of a *trajectory* from deterministic drift; it is a path-space objective, not a nodal force. `γ` here is a scalar protein friction, not our per-node drag; there is no face, no volume constraint, no `K_A`/`κ_m`/`γ_cortex`/`Π`.
- The reduced pair Eqs. (11)–(12) `γẋ = −G_x + v`, `γv̇ = G_xx v − k_BT G_xxx` and the Go-and-Back iterates Eqs. (17)–(18) are the *optimizer*, mapped to nothing in DCM state.

The only equation with a familiar shape is the discrete thermal fluctuation SD `√(2 k_B T Δt / γ)` (Ref. 58) — the standard fluctuation–dissipation result our BAOAB integrator already implements. No porting needed; it is not new and it is not DCM-specific.

## 6. RISKS / caveats

- **Regime mismatch is total for DCM purposes.** This is purified single-molecule statistical inference (F₁-ATPase-like probe assays), 1-D, dimensionless, equilibrium double-well or NESS washboard — not a living-cell continuum, not 3-D, not MCF7, not a surface mesh. There is no way to "adapt" a mechanism from it because it contains none.
- **Do not manufacture applicability.** The temptation is to over-read the shared Langevin/fluctuation–dissipation substrate as DCM-relevant. It is not: that substrate lives in the integrator (BAOAB / Leimkuhler–Matthews), already ratified, and the paper adds *inference*, not a forward force law. Importing the action/DRP formalism into DCM would violate no physiological-baseline rule per se, but it is out-of-scope engine work with no mechanistic payoff and should be surfaced-not-built.
- **KB hygiene.** All numbers are toy-model units; registering any as a physical DCM parameter would inject a non-physical baseline (violating the physiological-operating-point rule) and a citation with no cell-mechanics content. File this paper, if at all, as a *methods / statistical-inference* reference in the Miyazaki corpus (path-integral / hidden-trajectory estimation), never as a DCM source.
