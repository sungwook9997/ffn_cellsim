# FF lens — p46

**Paper:** Gupta et al. 2021, *The nature of cell division forces in epithelial monolayers*, JCB 220(8):e202011106 (DOI 10.1083/jcb.202011106). Chaudhuri lab (experimental) + T. Kim (coarse-grained membrane BD sim + FEM).

**Verdict up front (honest):** This is a TFM/MSM cell-biology paper whose *computational core is a DCM* (triangulated-membrane Brownian-dynamics shell), not an FF paper. Myosin II, kinesin-5, anillin appear only as *pharmacological knobs*, never as fine-grained filament/motor/clutch mechanisms. There are **no** filament-scale force laws, off-rates, duty ratios, or branching distributions to port. FF impact is therefore **LOW–PARTIAL** and lives in exactly one place: it defines a *new FF scenario* — the **cytokinetic actomyosin contractile ring** as an emergent fine-grained structure — plus hard **validation numbers** that ring must reproduce, and one drag-model idea relevant to the FF grid-drag open problem. Nothing here supersedes an existing FF force law.

---

## 1. MECHANISMS to adopt

### 1.1 (NEW SCENARIO) Cytokinetic contractile ring as an emergent FF actomyosin band — build, don't prescribe
- **FF modules:** filament (bundled actin, equatorial), myosin (Stam–Hocky bipolar minifilament), crosslinker (α-actinin/**anillin-as-scaffold**), membrane containment, turgor/volume.
- **What the paper establishes mechanistically (the thing FF should reproduce from first principles):** *cytokinetic ring contraction — not interpolar-spindle elongation — is the force generator* that drives ~60% axial cell elongation against confining neighbors. Blebbistatin (myosin II), BTO-1 (Plk1), and anillin-siRNA each abolish ~60% of the elongation and impair chromosome segregation; kinesin-5 perturbation and spindle laser-ablation do NOT (Fig. 6–7).
- **Why it beats the current FF picture:** FF today has cortex / lamellipodium / filopodium but **no equatorial constriction module**. The paper's BD sim *prescribes* the ring kinematically (522 nodes pushed inward at constant v_c). FF's mandate (fine-grained over lumped) says: build the ring as a real equatorial bundle of actin + Stam–Hocky myosin minifilaments + anillin crosslinkers, and let the constriction *velocity and force emerge* from Hill force–velocity + duty ratio + Bell–Evans crosslink turnover under the cortex's own turgor. Then this paper is the acceptance oracle (§5). This is the mechanistic version of the paper's proxy.
- **Anillin → crosslinker channel, not a knob.** Fig. 7 m,n: ring-contraction extent scales with elongation, Pearson **r = 0.6** (n=40); graded anillin knockdown gives graded contraction. In FF, anillin is a ring-specific **crosslinker/scaffold** that sets bundle connectivity — model it with the existing crosslinker machinery (explicit bond, Bell–Evans off-rate, Ferrer stiffness), and the "graded knockdown → graded contraction" curve becomes a crosslinker-density sweep. This is a concrete use of the FF crosslinker module in a new geometry.

### 1.2 (CANDIDATE) Node/segment drag proportional to local area — relevant to the FF grid-drag open problem
- **Paper's convention (Eq. 3):** `ζ_i = λ · A_i` — drag coefficient on node *i* is proportional to the instantaneous area A_i of its surrounding polygon, λ constant.
- **Why FF should note it:** FF's crawl-speed / stress-relaxation slowdown is a *grid-drag* pathology — total drag Σγ ∝ N_c (particle count), so the native 266k-particle cortex is ~500× too draggy (memory: project-ff-crawl-mechanism). The paper's `ζ ∝ local area` is the *physically-motivated* alternative to per-particle constant drag: drag should track the **membrane/solvent contact area a segment sweeps**, not the number of discretization beads. This does NOT by itself fix the FF problem (it is still a mesh-density-coupled drag), but it is a documented, dimensionally-clean template for making FF's hydrodynamic drag *refinement-invariant* — worth an explicit sanity-gate comparison, not adoption as-is.

### 1.3 (CONSTRAINT, mostly DCM but FF-adjacent) near-volume-conservation couples contraction → elongation
- Cell volume drops only **~5%** across cytokinesis (Fig. 7a, n=22), yet ring constriction yields **~60%** axial elongation; a **~40%** volume loss would be needed to abolish elongation (Fig. 7d,e). Mechanistically: an incompressible bag, pinched at the equator, must extrude axially. For FF this is a *reminder* that the cortex+turgor incompressibility (already in FF via enclosed-volume / turgor) is what converts equatorial myosin contraction into axial protrusion — the elongation is not a separate active push. It validates keeping turgor ON at the physiological setpoint (CLAUDE.md hard rule) rather than adding an ad-hoc axial force.

## 2. FORCE LAWS / KINETIC SCHEMES to port

**None at filament scale.** The paper's governing equations (Eqs. 1–8) are all **membrane/DCM** shell physics, not FF:
- Overdamped Langevin `F_i − ζ_i ṙ_i + F_i^T = 0` (Eq. 1) — same BAOAB-limit family FF already uses; no new content.
- FDT `⟨F_i^T F_j^T⟩ = (2 k_B T ζ_i δ_ij / Δt) δ` (Eq. 2) — standard.
- Drag `ζ_i = λ A_i` (Eq. 3) — the one idea flagged in §1.2.
- Extensional dead-band spring (Eq. 5), dihedral bending `U_b = κ_b[1−cos(θ−θ_0)]` (Eq. 6), global volume `U_v = κ_v(V−V0)²/2V0` (Eq. 7), area `U_a = κ_a(A−A0)²/2A0` (Eq. 8) — **all membrane-surface potentials; these belong to the DCM lens, not FF.**

The **cytokinetic driver is kinematic** (prescribed constant-velocity inward displacement of 522 equatorial nodes until distance-to-axis < 0.5 µm) — explicitly *not* a force law. FF's job is to *replace* this prescription with emergent myosin force; the paper gives the target behavior, not the scheme.

So: nothing to transcribe into an FF force law. The FF contribution is the *scenario spec* (§4) and the *oracle numbers* (§5).

Do not back-solve a per-head force from them (that would be fitting; violates the no-magic-number rule).

## 4. NETWORK ARCHITECTURE / PROTOCOL to reproduce (FF scenario spec)

**Scenario "FF-CYTOKINESIS-RING": emergent actomyosin furrow at native scale.**
- **Geometry:** take the native FF cortex (`--cortex-fil 38000 --from-resting --microtubules`, MCF7 R=7.5 µm, Nc≈266k) at its physiological baseline (turgor ~40 Pa, cytoplasm η≈65 Pa·s ON). Do NOT strip compartments (native-scale hard rule).
- **Ring seeding:** define an **equatorial band ~1 µm wide** and enrich it with (i) circumferentially-bundled actin filaments (existing filopodium/bundle architecture, oriented tangentially), (ii) **Stam–Hocky bipolar myosin minifilaments** at ring-appropriate density, (iii) **anillin crosslinkers** (crosslinker module, Bell–Evans off-rate, Ferrer stiffness) setting bundle connectivity.
- **Drive:** myosin walks toward barbed ends (Hill 1938 force–velocity, duty ratio) → circumferential contraction; crosslinker turnover (Bell–Evans k_off = k_off0·exp(F/F_b)) allows the ring to shorten. The **furrow radius, constriction velocity, and net force are read-outs, not inputs.** Stop criterion: furrow radius < 0.5 µm.
- **Confinement / incompressibility:** turgor + enclosed-volume constraint ON (physiological) so equatorial pinch → axial elongation emerges (the ~5%-volume / ~60%-elongation coupling).
- **Perturbation arm (matches paper's causal tests):**
  - myosin OFF (blebbistatin analog: set myosin engagement → 0) must abolish ~60% of elongation and stall constriction (Fig. 7f).
  - anillin/crosslinker density sweep (siANLN analog) must give graded constriction, with contraction-extent vs elongation correlating (target r≈0.6).
  - kinesin-5 / spindle is *irrelevant* — FF has no spindle; the paper confirms elongation is spindle-independent, so no microtubule-motor push is needed.
- **Density note:** paper is MDCK/MCF10A on ~1 kPa gel; the FF run stays single-cell fine-grained (no monolayer). The monolayer-confinement is replaced by FF's own membrane+turgor; the *elongation-against-confinement* is the observable, not a required neighbor mesh.

## 6. RISKS / caveats

- **Regime mismatch — this is a DCM paper.** The computational model is a membrane shell (SimuCell3D-style), not a filament network. Do not import its potentials (Eqs. 5–8) into FF — they are DCM's. The FF value is the *scenario + oracle*, not the physics.
- **Force numbers are whole-structure, inferred, not measured directly.** 300–500 nN comes from **FEM back-fitting** of adjacent-cell deformation (SSIM), and the actual tractions read basally are only 5–20 Pa (the paper explicitly notes TFM under-reports because division is at the mid-plane). So treat 300–500 nN as an *order-of-magnitude* acceptance band, not a precise fit target. **Do not tune FF myosin density to hit exactly 400 nN** — that is fitting; report where emergent FF lands and surface a discrepancy to PI.
- **MDCK/MCF10A + monolayer confinement ≠ FF single MCF7.** R=10 µm (MDCK) vs FF's 7.5 µm (MCF7); elongation is *against neighbors* in a monolayer, which FF replaces with turgor+membrane confinement. The ~60%-elongation number is monolayer-specific geometry; the *transferable* claim is "near-volume-conserving equatorial contraction → large axial extension," not the exact 60%. Keep FF at its own physiological R and confinement; use the coupling *shape*, not the magnitude, when geometry differs.
- **v_c is prescribed in the paper, must be emergent in FF.** The paper's constant-velocity kinematic ring is exactly the lumped shortcut FF must avoid. If an FF build ever prescribes constriction velocity, it has violated the fine-grained rule — the whole point is that v_c and force fall out of Hill + duty + turnover.
- **Anillin r=0.6 is a soft correlation, not a mechanism.** Use it as a directional check (more crosslinker → more contraction), not a quantitative gate.
- **No filament kinetics to lean on.** This paper cannot supply k_off0, F_b, stall, ℓ_p, branching, or duty ratios — those must still come from the FF canon (Bell–Evans, Hill, Stam–Hocky, Ferrer, KB-3.x). Nothing here supersedes an existing FF parameter.
