# TAG-validation lens — p07

**Paper:** Wisdom, Indana, Chou, Desai, **Taeyoon Kim**, Chaudhuri (2020) — "Covalent cross-linking of basement membrane-like matrices physically restricts invasive protrusions in breast cancer cells." *Matrix Biology* 85–86, 94–111. DOI 10.1016/j.matbio.2019.05.006.

## Paper kind

**Hybrid: experimental (in-vitro biomaterial rheology + MDA-MB-231 morphology) + simulation (Taeyoon-Kim agent-based Brownian-dynamics cross-linked fiber network).** No closed-form theory.

Consequence for use:
- The **matrix-mechanics measurements** (Young's modulus, creep-recovery permanent strain, loss tangent) are **experimental → OVERLAY / on-demand cross-check targets**, never fit.
- The **Kim-model curves** (creep permanent-strain vs k0,u/λu; invasion max/permanent strain) are **simulation → COMPARISON ONLY**, never a truth source. Notably this is the *same class of model* (Bell-slip-bond cross-linked fiber network) as our FF ECM engine, so the model outputs are best read as "what a sibling agent-based model produces," i.e. a qualitative sibling-code cross-check, not a KB anchor.
- The **Bell's-law off-rate** is a re-use of an existing analytic form (already our Bell-Evans oracle), not a new law.

Primary classification for the schema: **experimental** (the validation-grade anchors we would actually consume are in-vitro measurements; simulation component flagged throughout).

## Coverage vs existing gates

- **Matrix stiffness (data 1,4,7)** partially overlaps **VG-U1-G0-linear** (`G0` band ~30–100 Pa, applied [15,200] Pa; status=passing). rBM ~100 Pa sits at the top of that band → **CONFIRMS** the collagen-like reference. IPN G′ ~600–1000 Pa / E~2 kPa is *above* our collagen band but is a fundamentally different material (Matrigel+alginate IPN, tumor-like) → **out of scope**, not a challenge to VG-U1-G0. No tighten warranted.
- **Bell force-dependent off-rate (data 8,9; Eq.1)** — the FF crosslinker uses Bell-Evans (see MC-U2 caveat "Off-rate is Bell-Evans force-dependent"); the analytic form is already our Bell-Evans oracle. The paper **CONFIRMS the oracle form** applied to ECM crosslinkers. **VG-U2-catch-slip-bond** covers *integrin* catch-slip (lifetime peak ~30 pN) — a different bond; this paper's crosslinker is a pure **slip** bond, not catch. No overlap of band.
- **Traction/protrusion force (data 14)** vs **VG-U2-traction-magnitude** (FA 1–10 nN; cell total 10–100 nN). A single modeled protrusion ≈1 nN is consistent with a per-FA/per-protrusion force within the FA 1–10 nN band → **CONFIRMS** order-of-magnitude, but it's a model input not a measurement, so weak.
- **Mesh/topology (data 15,16)** vs **VG-U1-mikado-topology** (ξ~1–5 µm; mean segment length) and PARAM-mesh_size_xi. 140 nm spacing + ~4 µm fibers are model choices consistent with our ξ range; no contract change.
- **Matrix viscoplasticity / permanent strain (data 2,3,5,6,10) — NOT COVERED by any existing gate.** Our FF ECM contract (MC-U1-ecm-network) validates G0, σ(r) decay, and strain-stiffening — all *elastic/instantaneous* observables. There is **no gate on creep-recovery permanent (plastic) strain**, and no gate on the **stiffness↔plasticity decoupling** that is this paper's central mechanistic claim. This is the genuine gap. → new candidate below.

## New gate candidates (all: candidate, PI-gate review — bands staged, not authored)

1. **VG-U1-matrix-plasticity (proposed)** — *ECM viscoplastic permanent strain from creep-recovery.*
   - Observable: permanent (unrecovered) shear strain after 100 Pa creep for 1 h then unload to 0 Pa, on the FF cross-linked Mikado network, as a function of crosslinker off-rate / crosslink stability.
   - Band/value: emergent monotone rise with crosslinker off-rate; anchor endpoints from Fig. 6d model + Fig. 2e/4f expt — **low-plasticity (stable/covalent crosslinks) permanent strain <10%; high-plasticity (fast off-rate) permanent strain ~70–80%.**
   - Engine: **FF** (Mikado + Bell-slip crosslinkers; the creep-shear protocol is directly portable).
   - Rationale/quote: "permanent strain ~80% vs ~10%" (Fig 2e, pg 3); "permanent strain rises with k0,u ~0→~70%" (Fig 6d, pg 8). This is an **emergent** observable (no imposed viscoplastic law) — exactly the mechanistic style our KB favors.
   - Caveat: absolute band depends on crosslinker density/kinetics; stage as a *shape* contract (monotone, endpoints) not a single hard number.

2. **VG-U1-plasticity-stiffness-decoupling (proposed)** — *stiffness invariance under crosslinking.*
   - Observable: initial shear modulus G0 vs crosslinker stability, alongside gate 1.
   - Band/value: **G0 roughly CONSTANT (±~30%) while permanent strain drops ≥5×** as crosslinks are stabilized (expt "ns" for both rBM ~100 Pa and IPN ~2 kPa; model initial modulus ~500–750 Pa flat across k0,u/λu).
   - Engine: **FF**.
   - Rationale/quote: "reduces plasticity without significantly changing stiffness" (abstract); Fig 2b, 4c (ns); Fig 6d,f (flat modulus). A strong falsifiable mechanistic contract at our scale.

Both are FF-scale, measurable, and defensible; neither invents a convenient band (endpoints are lit/model-anchored, shape-based). **PI authors the final bands.**

## Oracle candidates

- **Bell's law, Eq. 1** `ku = k0,u·exp(λu·|F_s|/kBT)` (tensile) / `ku = k0,u` (compressive). This is the **already-implemented Bell-Evans oracle** applied to a crosslinker slip bond. **CONFIRMS** the existing oracle form; the compression→baseline / tension→elevated asymmetry is a useful implementation note for FF crosslinkers (slip only under tension). **Not a new oracle.**
- `E = 2(1+ν)G* , ν=0.5 → E = 3G*` (incompressible Young↔shear). Trivial elasticity identity; usable as a units helper when comparing our G0 to reported Young's moduli, but too elementary to warrant an oracle module.
- Strain metric `ε = (d − r0,m)/(2 r0,m)` — model-specific geometry readout, not a general law.

**Net new oracles: 0.**
