# TAG-validation lens — p70

**Paper:** Sakamoto, Tanabe, Hiraiwa … Miyazaki (2020) *Tug-of-war between actomyosin-driven antagonistic forces determines the positioning symmetry in cell-sized confinement*, Nat Commun 11:3063. DOI 10.1038/s41467-020-16677-9.

## Paper kind

**experimental** (primary) — in-vitro reconstitution: metaphase *Xenopus laevis* egg cytoplasmic extract encapsulated in cell-sized water-in-oil droplets (16–330 µm), microtubules removed (nocodazole), F-actin imaged (TMR-LifeAct), quasi-2D confinement, UV laser ablation + PIV. Secondary layers: **analytic** (a 1D-percolation bridge-maturation law + an active-gel wave-period law → closed-form transition-radius Eq. 3) and **simulation** (stochastic cluster-motion model, comparison-only).

Consequence for use: the measured numbers are **overlay / on-demand cross-check** targets, and — critically — they are at **egg-extract-reconstitution scale + context, NOT MCF7 single-cell scale**. The confinement/positioning phenomenon (cluster centering vs edging in a 16–330 µm droplet) is not a single-cell observable of our DCM/FF cell. The analytic laws are **candidate oracles** (cross-check only, never truth). Simulation outputs are never a truth source.

Analytic laws (closed-form):
- τ = 1/(k_off + C0·k_on) — crosslinker turnover.
- τp = 2^N·τ, N ≃ R/L — bridge maturation (super-exponential in R).
- Rc/L = log₂(T/τ), Dc = 2Rc — timescale-crossing transition radius (Eq. 3).
- p(D) = 1 − [1 − (½)^{D/2L}]^{T(D)/τ} — edge-positioning probability (Eq. 2).

- **Wave period / velocity / cluster-size / D_c laws (items 9–14):** **NOT covered** by any existing gate. These are droplet-confinement collective observables, not single-MCF7-cell observables. Closest gates (VG-U5-*, MC-U5-collective) are spheroid-scale but chemically/geometrically unrelated (Xenopus extract droplet ≠ MCF7 spheroid). No overlap — but also not a natural fit for our single-cell contract set.
- **Force percolation:** no existing gate encodes a network force-percolation threshold. Conceptually relevant to FF (Cytosim-style crosslinked filaments), but currently un-gated.

Net: **0 existing gates confirmed/tightened/challenged as gates; 1 existing Parameter challenged** (α-actinin off-rate).

## Oracle candidates

1. **Crosslinker turnover time** τ = 1/(k_off + C0·k_on) — clean closed form. **Good analytic cross-check** for FF crosslinker binding/unbinding kinetics: given registered k_off, k_on, C0, the emergent mean bond lifetime should reproduce τ. Low implementation cost; complements the existing Bell-Evans oracle. *Candidate — PI-gate review.*
2. **Bridge maturation time** τp = 2^N·τ (N ≃ R/L) — mean first-passage time for all N in-series sites to be simultaneously occupied; **super-exponential in system size**. Interesting cross-connection: our DCM cadherin-cluster maturation work (`project-dcm-cadherin-cluster-redesign`) independently found a *super-exponential* T(n_b) maturation law — this percolation result is an analytic analog worth noting to PI, though it is a confinement-geometry law, not a direct single-cell oracle.
3. **Transition-radius crossing** Rc/L = log₂(T/τ) and edge-probability p(D) (Eq. 2) — elegant but **confinement-geometry-specific** (tug-of-war between waves and bridges in a bounded droplet). Not a single-cell oracle; register as literature reference only, do not implement.
