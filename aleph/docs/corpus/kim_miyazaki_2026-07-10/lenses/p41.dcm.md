# DCM lens — p41

**Paper:** Ding, Chou, Deguchi, Kim. "Impacts of structural properties of myosin II filaments on force generation." eLife 2025;14:RP105236. DOI 10.7554/eLife.105236.

**One-line verdict for DCM:** This is a *fine-grained* agent-based actomyosin paper (Kim lab `ThickFilament`; explicit F-actin beams + bipolar myosin minifilaments + ACP cross-links, overdamped Langevin). It is a near-direct sibling of the **FF** engine, NOT of DCM. DCM represents actomyosin as a *lumped cortical surface tension* γ on a triangulated shell — there is no explicit filament, motor, or cross-linker to receive most of this paper's mechanism. The paper offers DCM **one genuine lever**: a mechanistic *scaling law and efficiency ceiling* for how emergent contractile force depends on myosin number/density/localization, which is a sanity anchor for DCM's open **γ-generation** problem — a cross-check oracle, not a runtime input. Relevance is LOW-but-nonzero; I flag honestly rather than over-mine.

---

## 1. MECHANISMS to adopt (DCM module → specific change)

DCM does not simulate filaments, so almost nothing ports as a *runtime mechanism*. What is usable:

- **Cortical-tension γ generation (open problem) — impose a myosin-density scaling and an efficiency ceiling.**
  DCM currently treats interphase γ≈0.15 mN/m and mitotic γ≈1.5 mN/m as set/pressure-borne values (γ=ΔP·R/2). The known open gap (`project-gamma-floor-*` memory) is that active-γ generation from myosin is force-magnitude-floored ~400–570× under band. This paper says *why a lumped "sum of motors" over-predicts*: cooperative overlap is gated by intervening cross-linkers (ACPs), so bundle force saturates at an **efficiency η = Ftot/F_M^max that falls as ~1/N_M and plateaus at ~0.08** (Eq 3, Fig 4A). Concretely: if DCM ever derives an active γ from a per-area myosin count N_M and single-motor capacity, it must apply an efficiency envelope η(N_M) rather than a linear sum — a floored, sub-linear generation law is *expected physics here*, not a bug. This reframes the γ-floor deficit as partly an *architecture/efficiency* effect, not purely missing force magnitude.
  **Why it beats what DCM does now:** DCM's γ is a free set-point; this gives a mechanistic upper bound (η≤~0.25 thin, →0.08 dense) that a from-first-principles γ estimator must respect — prevents "tune myosin density to hit the band" (a magic-number trap).

- **Bundle-vs-network anisotropy of tension generation.**
  Ordered/aligned actomyosin (bundle) gives Ftot ∝ N_M (slope 1); randomly-oriented network gives **Ftot ∝ N_M^0.65 ≈ √N_M** (Eq 8, Fig 7B). The DCM cortex is an *isotropic quasi-2D network* on the surface, so its active tension should scale ~√(myosin density), NOT linearly. If DCM adds a myosin-modulated γ (e.g. mitotic ramp), the ramp vs myosin recruitment should be sub-linear (√-like), not proportional. This is the single most DCM-relevant scaling statement in the paper.

- **(Not adoptable) contractile-unit partitioning, catch-bond PCM walking, two-spring crossbridge, excluded volume** — these live below DCM's representation level. Route them to the FF lens. Grafting explicit filaments into DCM would violate the DCM design charter (surface-mesh continuum), so do NOT.

## 2. EQUATIONS to port to the surface-mesh / continuum level

Only the *macroscopic scaling/efficiency* relations lift to DCM's level. The Langevin/beam equations (Eqs 9–15) are FF-level and are intentionally NOT ported.

- **Efficiency law (Eq 3):** `η = Ftot / F_M^max`, with empirical envelope `η ∝ 1/N_M` (sparse) → plateau `η ≈ 0.08` (dense).
  DCM mapping: if an active cortical tension is derived as γ_active = η(ρ_M)·γ_capacity(ρ_M), where ρ_M = myosin surface density (per face or per unit area of the mesh), then γ_capacity ∝ ρ_M·f_stall·ℓ (the naive sum) is throttled by η. Use as an **upper-bound check** on any γ-generation model, not as a live term.

- **Network force scaling (from Eq 8 / Fig 7B):** `Ftot ∝ N_M^0.65`.
  DCM mapping: cortex is network-like → active surface tension increment Δγ ∝ ρ_M^0.65 (per-area), not ρ_M^1. Symbol map: N_M → ρ_M·A_face·(cortex thickness proxy); Ftot → line tension γ × cross-section. Dimensionally, γ [N/m] should track √(areal myosin) rather than linearly.

- **Single-motor capacity (Eq 2 pieces):** `F_M,z ≈ F_st·N_h·N_a` with `F_st ≈ 5.7 pN/head`, `N_h = 8 heads/arm`. Motor stall per arm ≈ 45.6 pN.
  DCM mapping: sets the *per-minifilament* force quantum feeding any first-principles γ estimator; DCM would multiply by an areal minifilament density and the η envelope. This is the only place DCM touches a concrete pN number from this paper.

- **Eqs 1, 4–7 (Ftot bounds, Ξ contractile-unit count, L_c critical-overlap, F_est):** filament-geometry specific (arm spacing L_sp, bare zone L_bz, N_a). No surface-mesh analog. **Do not port to DCM.**

## 4. GEOMETRY / PROTOCOL to reproduce (as DCM scenarios)

The paper's setups are filament-box scenarios (two clamped antiparallel actins; 2N_F² bundle; 20×20×0.1 µm network) — none maps to a DCM cell-shape scenario. There is **no confinement, aspiration, AFM, division, aggregate, durotaxis, or contact-angle protocol** in this paper for DCM to reproduce. The only transferable *protocol idea*:

- **Isometric tension measurement by cross-section summation** (their "cross-sections every 200 nm, sum z-component of spring forces"). DCM already measures cortical tension via the virial/section method; this paper's isometric clamp + section-averaging at steady state is a methodological consistency check for DCM's γ read-out (measure at plateau, average over sections). Not new to DCM, but confirms the protocol.

DCM should NOT build a filament-box scenario — that is an FF scenario.

## 5. VALIDATION TARGETS for DCM observables

Direct DCM observables (tension γ, shape index, division force, ring closure, compaction, contact angle, flow speed) are **not measured in this paper** — it reports bundle/network forces in nN for explicit filament assemblies. So the only DCM-checkable statements are the *scaling/efficiency laws*, used as guardrails on a future γ-generation model:

- Active-tension efficiency must not exceed η ≈ 0.25 (thin) and should approach ≈0.08 (dense cortex) — any DCM γ-from-myosin model predicting η→1 (linear sum) is wrong.
- Cortex (network-like) active tension should scale ~ρ_M^0.65, not linearly — if DCM ties mitotic γ ramp (0.15→1.5 mN/m, a 10×) to myosin recruitment, the recruitment fold must be ~10^(1/0.65) ≈ 32× (super-linear recruitment for linear-looking tension), a concrete sanity relation.
- Per-head stall 5.7 pN and unloaded v 140 nm/s bound any cortical flow speed DCM might emit.

These are *cross-check oracles* (FF/TAG-validation home), not DCM production targets. There is nothing here to plug into the compaction-rate, division, or confinement open problems.

## 6. RISKS / caveats (regime mismatch + adaptation)

- **Representation mismatch (dominant risk).** This is a sub-continuum, filament-explicit model. DCM is a surface-mesh continuum. Porting any of its per-filament mechanism into DCM would violate the DCM charter and the fine-grained-vs-lumped rule *in the wrong direction* (DCM is deliberately the lumped surface engine; FF is the fine-grained one). Keep the mining to *scaling laws that constrain γ*, and send everything else to `p41.ff.md`.
- **Disorganized reconstituted bundles/networks, not a living cortex.** The paper's ACPs are α-actinin-like, *permanent* (never unbind), and the actin has no turnover in the force runs. The MCF7 cortex has dynamic cross-linkers, turnover, and membrane coupling — so the η≈0.08 ceiling is a *lower-bound-flavored* reference, not a hard MCF7 number. Adapt by treating it as an order-of-magnitude guardrail, not a fitted constant (no gate built on 0.08 exactly).
- **No pressure, no shell, no volume constraint.** The paper is force-only in open boxes; there is no turgor Π₀, membrane K_A, or enclosed volume. It says nothing about DCM's physiological-baseline stack, so it cannot be used to set any baseline value — respect the physiological-baseline rule by leaving DCM's γ, Π₀, η, K_A untouched.
- **Species/isoform breadth.** Non-muscle myosin II generic; not MCF7-specific. Do not KB-register as MCF7 tension provenance.
- **No magic-number leakage.** The η envelope and 0.65 exponent are *descriptive constraints*, not tunable knobs to make a DCM gate pass — use them to *reject* over-optimistic γ models, never to back-solve a myosin density that hits the tension band.

**Bottom line:** For DCM, p41 contributes one honest thing — a mechanistic *efficiency ceiling and √-density scaling* that any future first-principles cortical-γ generator must obey — plus per-head force/velocity cross-check numbers. Everything else is FF territory. Score accordingly low.
