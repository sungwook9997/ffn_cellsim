# FF lens — p06

**Paper:** Li, Kim & Szymanski (2019), "Multi-scale regulation of cell branching: Modeling morphogenesis," *Developmental Biology* 451:40–52, DOI 10.1016/j.ydbio.2018.12.004.
**Type:** REVIEW (no original data/model). Subject = **plant** cell branching (trichomes, root hairs, moss protonema, pavement cells): turgor-driven cell-wall expansion + **cortical microtubule (MT) array self-organization**. Co-authored by Taeyoon Kim, but this is his plant-morphogenesis review, NOT one of his agent-based actin-network Brownian-dynamics papers.

**Bottom line for FF: essentially not applicable.** FF is a fine-grained actomyosin filament engine (Cytosim physics — actin bending beams, α-actinin/filamin Bell-Evans crosslinkers, Stam–Hocky myosin minifilaments, Arp2/3 branch angle, clutches, turgor). This paper has **no actin fine-grained mechanics, no myosin force-velocity, no crosslinker Bell/catch kinetics, no adhesion clutches, and no transcribed equations or force laws**. Its cytoskeletal content is (a) plant cortical **microtubule** array self-organization (zippering/catastrophe/katanin severing) and (b) ARP2/3 as a *genetic regulator* of branch initiation — not as the angle-harmonic branch nucleator FF implements. All quantitative numbers are plant-cell-wall regime (MPa turgor/stress, mm-scale MT persistence length), off by orders of magnitude from the MCF7 animal-cortex regime FF targets (mN/m cortical tension, ~65 Pa·s cytoplasm). Included below is the small set of items that *could* touch FF if a microtubule cortical-array or turgor-shell line is ever opened; none supersede a current FF value.

---

## 1. MECHANISMS to adopt

**Verdict: none directly adoptable into the FF actomyosin core.** The paper offers no actin/myosin/crosslinker/clutch mechanism.

Conceptual-only, and only if an FF **microtubule module** is later prioritized (FF currently has an MT compartment `--microtubules` in the native cell as bending beams, so this is the one contact point):

- **MT dynamic instability as a three-state channel (growth / pause / shrinkage) with treadmilling** (Allard 2010b, Shaw 2003a, cited). FF's MTs are static/quasi-static bending beams; if MT turnover ever matters (e.g. for cortex–MT coupling or microtubule-organizing-center dynamics) this is the mechanistic upgrade — but it is a *plant cortical-array* scheme and the primary rate constants live in the cited papers, not here. **Low priority; out of current FF scope.**
- **Angle-dependent MT–MT collision outcome** (zippering/bundling below a threshold encounter angle vs collision-induced catastrophe above it; crossover → preferred katanin severing site). This is a genuine fine-grained interaction rule, but it governs **2D cortical MT array ordering**, a phenomenon FF does not simulate. Not a lever for cortical-tension γ, contraction, or crawl.
- **NOT adoptable:** ARP2/3 here is a *signaling/genetic* node (SPK1→WAVE-SCAR→ARP2/3 patterning branch sites), giving no branch-angle physics that would improve FF's existing 70°±35° angle-harmonic Arp2/3.

Why nothing beats the current FF mechanism: the paper operates in a plant turgor-shell + microtubule-patterning regime that FF does not model, and transcribes no force law that could replace an FF proxy.

## 2. FORCE LAWS / KINETIC SCHEMES to port

**None.** The review transcribes **zero closed-form equations** — no Langevin/BD update, no bending/stretch potential, no motor force–velocity, no Bell/catch off-rate, no branching-angle distribution function. The only quasi-formal statements are verbal:

- **Thin-wall pressure-vessel scaling (implied, not written):** σ_wall ≈ Π·R/t. For plant epidermis Π ≈ 0.5–1 MPa, t thin ⇒ σ_wall ≈ ~10 MPa. FF/DCM already use the analytic Laplace form ΔP·R/2 for the animal cortex; this adds nothing new and the numbers are the wrong regime.
- **Iterated growth loop (algorithmic):** shape_{n+1} = Remesh(Relax(shape_n under turgor)); reset material properties each cycle. This is a *plant plastic-growth* emulation — conceptually adjacent to DCM's turgor-shell + remesh, **not FF**.

Nothing to map onto FF filament/motor/crosslinker state.

## 4. NETWORK ARCHITECTURE / PROTOCOL to reproduce

**No FF-usable scenario.** The reconstitution/assay geometries here are (a) axisymmetric viscoelastic **cell-wall shell** under turgor (Yanagisawa 2015 trichome; Krupinski 2016 root hair) and (b) **2D/3D cortical MT-array** self-organization domains (2D square, 3D cylinder x,y∈[−4,4] z∈[0,50] µm, triangulated confocal meshes, ellipsoid). None is an actomyosin reconstitution, motility assay, or confinement relevant to FF's single-cell cortex/lamellipodium/filopodium. The turgor-shell + remesh growth loop is a **DCM** conceptual analogy at most, not FF.

## 5. VALIDATION TARGETS for FF observables

**None.** No G′(ω)/G″(ω), no stress-relaxation modes/times, no network contraction rate/tension, no motor velocity-vs-load, no actin buckling threshold, no mesh size, no scaling exponents. Nothing to add to the FF validation suite.

## 6. RISKS / caveats

- **Regime mismatch is total, not partial.** Plant turgor (0.5–1 MPa) and wall stress (~10 MPa) are ~10⁴× the MCF7 animal-cortex regime FF initializes at (turgor ~40 Pa, cortical tension ~0.15 mN/m). Importing any number here would violate the physiological-baseline HARD rule.
- **Cytoskeleton mismatch.** The paper's active cytoskeleton is the **microtubule cortical array**; FF's core is the **actomyosin** network. Even the MT dynamic-instability and zippering rules govern 2D cortical-array *ordering*, a process FF does not simulate — adopting them would add a whole new module for no current open-problem payoff (γ-floor, contraction, crawl-speed, buckling all remain untouched).
- **ARP2/3 category error to avoid.** Here ARP2/3 is a genetic/signaling regulator (SPK1→WAVE-SCAR→ARP2/3 patterning); do **not** conflate with FF's mechanistic Arp2/3 branch nucleator. No branch-angle or nucleation-rate physics is transcribed.
- **It's a review, not a source.** Any value one might want (E0, k1, κ, MT rate constants) resides in the cited primary papers (Yanagisawa 2015, Allard 2010a/b, Eren 2010, Krupinski 2016), not here — so even the sparse conceptual items cannot be parameterized from this PDF.
- **How to "adapt without breaking rules":** the honest adaptation is to **not** port anything into FF. If the project ever opens a microtubule-cortical-array or plant/turgor-shell line, treat this review only as a map to the primary modeling papers, and re-anchor every constant to a measured animal/MCF7 value before use.
