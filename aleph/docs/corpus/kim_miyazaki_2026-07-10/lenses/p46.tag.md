## Paper kind

**experimental** (primary) **+ simulation** (supporting) — a measurement-driven TFM/MSM biophysics study whose mechanistic conclusion is backed by (a) FEM force estimation and (b) a coarse-grained triangulated-membrane BD simulation.

How the numbers may be used, by provenance:
- **Measured (TFM/MSM/confocal/imaging)** → OVERLAY / cross-check targets, never fit: cell-volume change, elongation strains, boundary curvature, traction anisotropy.
- **FEM-inferred** (300–500 nN division force) → model-derived; comparison only, treat as order-of-magnitude, not a contract band.
- **BD membrane sim** (5% vol loss ⇒ ~60% elongation; 40% to abolish; area +22%) → **simulation output**; per philosophy this is *comparison-only, never a truth source* — but because it is our own DCM architecture (Kim), it is a high-value **DCM-to-DCM reproduction** cross-check.

This paper contributes **no closed-form law** → **no new analytic oracle** (governing Eqs. 1–8 are just the standard overdamped-Langevin + harmonic volume/area/bending/extensional DCM potentials we already implement).

## Coverage vs existing gates

The KB currently has **NO cell-division / cytokinesis gate** and no division-force contract. Nearest neighbors:

- **VG-U4-monolayer-stress** (`372…d48e…`, band midline ~1–2 kPa): different observable (edge-buildup steady-state monolayer stress), **not** the transient per-division Δstress here. Item 18 (Δ traction 5–20 Pa per division stage) is a *differential single-event* signal — does not overlap. → not covered.
- **VG-H3-composite-tension / VG-H3-KU35** (cortical tension 0.35–0.65 mN/m): resting cortex tension, unrelated to division-driven elongation force. → not covered.
- **VG-H8-membrane-tension** (`372…e7b6…`): membrane tether/apparent tension; the paper's **membrane area-reservoir** (+22%, item 4) is conceptually adjacent (Gauthier 2011 / Raucher-Sheetz reservoir) but the observable is different (area-expansion capacity vs tether force). → informs, not covered.
- Volume conservation (item 1) — DCM has a global volume-conservation potential (`U_v`, in MC-U3 / DCM engine) but **no gate on physiological volume change during division**. → not covered.
- **Oracles** (Helfrich, Young-Laplace, Bell-Evans, Hill, Kelvin-Voigt): none is a division/cytokinesis law. → no overlap.

**Verdict:** everything division-specific is a **NEW candidate**; nothing existing is confirmed, tightened, or challenged by this paper. `n_tighten = 0`.

## New gate candidates (all "candidate — PI-gate review"; PI authors gates)

**G1 — VG-DCM-cytokinesis-volume-conservation** (strongest, experimental overlay)
- Observable: fractional cell-volume change from early anaphase to end of cytokinesis, DCM cell.
- Band/value: **≈ −5% (accept [−7%, −3%])**, near-volume-conservation. Source item 1 (measured, n=22).
- Condition: MDCK/epithelial, mid-plane cytokinesis. Engine: **DCM**.
- Contract role: OVERLAY-only — driving equatorial ring constriction must NOT require a large volume drop; if our DCM sheds >~10% volume during a division event, the volume constraint is mis-set. Directly measurable at our single-cell scale.
- Quote (p6/16): "cell volume … drops by only ~5% across cytokinesis (Fig. 7 a, n=22)."

**G2 — VG-DCM-ring-constriction-elongation-coupling** (simulation cross-check, DCM-to-DCM)
- Observable: axial elongation = (max membrane extent along axis)/(initial diameter) − 1, produced by prescribed equatorial-band inward constriction under volume+area conservation.
- Band/value: **~60% elongation at ~5% volume loss; elongation collapses only as volume loss → ~40%** (items 2,3). Also the κ_a-dependence (item 5): κ_a=10⁻⁴ N/m → steep, 10⁻⁶ N/m → ~linear elongation-vs-vol (item 4/S4e).
- Condition: R=10 µm sphere, 522-node 1-µm equatorial band, stop at 0.5 µm from axis. Engine: **DCM**.
- Contract role: **simulation-comparison, NOT a truth source** — this is Kim's own DCM-lineage output, so it is a same-architecture reproduction test (our DCM should reproduce the monotone elongation-vs-volume-loss curve and the ~40% abolition threshold), NOT an experimental fit target. Flag prominently.
- Quote (p6): "constriction of the cytokinetic ring with only a ~5% loss in volume was sufficient to drive ~60% elongation … ~40% volume loss would be required to abolish elongation."

**G3 — VG-DCM-division-elongation-force** (model-inferred, weak-contract)
- Observable: peak outward force a dividing cell exerts on its neighbors during elongation.
- Value: **300–500 nN** (FEM best-SSIM, item 12). Note this is >>10³× the basal TFM reading (5–20 Pa) because division is mid-plane; the paper explicitly says TFM under-reports.
- Engine: DCM (as an emergent adjacent-cell push, multi-cell). Contract role: **order-of-magnitude sanity only** — it is FEM-inferred, not directly measured, and requires a multi-cell configuration; stage as a soft reference, not a tight band.
- Quote (p4): "forces on the order of 300–500 nN best reproduced the experimentally observed deformation."

**G4 — VG-DCM-division-shape-anisotropy** (experimental overlay, multi-cell)
- Observable: (a) axial vs perpendicular strain anisotropy — perpendicular stress **<25%** of axial during elongation, ~75% in rounding/spreading (items 15,16); (b) adjacent-cell-boundary curvature increase **0.035 → 0.092 µm⁻¹** (item 13).
- Engine: DCM multi-cell. Contract role: OVERLAY — a qualitative-plus-magnitude signature (anisotropic push along the division axis + neighbor-boundary bowing). Measurable but needs neighbors; stage as Phase-2+ multi-cell DCM.
- Quote (p3): "stress perpendicular to the division axis was less than 25% of that along the axis."

> Skipped as gate candidates (not defensible/at-scale as contracts): major/minor axis absolute lengths (items 6,7 — cell-type geometry, not a physics band), drug-perturbation deltas (items 10,11 — pharmacological, mechanism-agnostic), item 17 (cited prior-lit AFM, not this paper's datum — already the domain of existing rounding literature), item 18 basal Δ-traction values (mid-plane under-reporting makes the basal number a lower bound, not a clean band).

## Oracle candidates

**None.** The paper's governing equations (Eqs. 1–8) are the standard overdamped-Langevin BD update plus harmonic global-volume `U_v=κ_v(V−V₀)²/2V₀`, global-area `U_a=κ_a(A−A₀)²/2A₀`, dihedral bending `U_b=κ_b[1−cos(θ−θ₀)]`, and dead-band extensional spring — i.e. exactly the DCM potentials we already run, not a new closed-form cross-check law. The 5%-vol⇒60%-elong result is an emergent sim output, not an analytic law, so it stages as a **simulation cross-check (G2)**, not an oracle.
