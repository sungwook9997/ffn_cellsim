# TAG-validation lens — p11

**Paper:** Miyazaki & Harada (2011), "Go-and-Back method: Effective estimation of the hidden motion of proteins from single-molecule time series," *J. Chem. Phys.* **134**, 135104. DOI 10.1063/1.3574396.

## Paper kind

**method** (statistical-inference / numerical algorithm). Secondary tag: analytic (it derives closed-form ODEs and a timescale estimate) and simulation (all worked numbers come from synthetic toy trajectories, not measurement).

This is a hidden-trajectory *estimation* method: given an observed probe-particle time series y(t) and a **known** model + parameters, recover the most-probable hidden protein reaction coordinate x̂(t) as the Dominant Reaction Path / MAP estimator of an Onsager–Machlup action. It is NOT a forward physics model of any cytoskeletal/ECM/junction mechanism, and it does NOT report an experimental cell-mechanics observable. Per the validation philosophy, its numbers are (a) synthetic simulation output (comparison-only, never a truth source) or (b) dimensionless model-unit method-benchmark quantities — neither is an overlay/cross-check target for DCM or FF.

## Coverage vs existing gates

No overlap with any existing gate. The current gates cover cortical tension (VG-H3-KU35, VG-H7-gate-a/b), composite/compartment tension (VG-H3-composite, VG-H8/H9/H10), ECM network mechanics (VG-U1-*), FA motor-clutch (VG-U2-*), junction (VG-U4-*), collective/spheroid (VG-U5-*). This paper touches **none** of these observables.

The only conceptual contact is the **substrate stochastic dynamics**: the paper's coupled overdamped Langevin equations with fluctuation–dissipation noise variance 2γk_BT are exactly the regime our BAOAB / Leimkuhler–Matthews integrator targets. But the paper contributes an *inverse/inference* algorithm on top of that dynamics, not a new force law or a new expected band — so there is nothing to confirm, tighten, or challenge.

## Oracle candidates

**None registrable as a cross-check oracle for DCM/FF.** The paper's closed forms are inference machinery (Onsager–Machlup action S, Euler–Lagrange DRP, Go-and-Back forward-x / backward-v ODEs, τ_dim≈γ/G_xx), not analytic ground-truth laws for a forward observable we simulate (unlike Helfrich κL/2R², Young-Laplace ΔP·R/2, Bell-Evans, Hill).

One minor, already-owned identity worth noting: the discrete-time thermal SD **√(2k_BT Δt/γ)** (Ref. 58) is the standard overdamped-Langevin fluctuation–dissipation result our integrator already encodes; it could serve as a trivial BAOAB noise-variance sanity check, but it is textbook FDT, not novel to this paper, and no new oracle config is warranted.
