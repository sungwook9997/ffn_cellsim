# TAG-validation lens — p17

**Paper:** Prigent Garcia et al. 2026, *Kinetic Control of Out-Of-Equilibrium Dynamics in the RhoA Signaling Cascade Shapes Actomyosin Contractility* (bioRxiv, DOI 10.64898/2026.02.08.704688). Robin (Sorbonne) × Taeyoon Kim (Purdue) collaboration.

## Paper kind

**Primary: experimental** (in-vivo single-molecule near-TIRF/HILO microscopy of endogenous CRISPR reporters in the 2-cell *C. elegans* embryo, interphase AB cell). The novel validation-grade data are measured binding/unbinding kinetics and cascade delays.

Two secondary modes matter for us:
- **analytic** — a weakly-activated-cascade ODE reduces to a first-order (RC / leaky-integrator) law with a closed-form high-pass attenuation `√(1+ω²/k_off²)` and an exponential step-relaxation solution. These are the paper's most reusable content for `ffn_cellsim` → **oracle candidates**.
- **simulation** — an agent-based Brownian-dynamics actomyosin meshwork (the Jung/Murrell/Kim model; Bell's-law cross-linkers + parallel-cluster-model motors). This is the **same physics class as FF** but is model output → comparison/cross-check only, never a truth source.

**Consequence for use:** experimental numbers are **overlay/cross-check only** and carry a hard **cross-species caveat** — *C. elegans* embryonic cortex, NOT MCF7 single cell. They may not become hard bands at our scale. The analytic laws are engine-agnostic and are the defensible additions.

## Coverage vs existing gates

**No existing gate covers cortical myosin turnover kinetics, pulsed contraction, or the out-of-equilibrium signal-processing law.** Nearest neighbours and why they do NOT match:

- `PARAM-F_stall_motor` (2 pN), `PARAM-k_on_clutch` (1 s⁻¹), `PARAM-v_unloaded` (100 nm/s), `PARAM-N_clutch`: these are the **FA motor-clutch / retrograde-flow** parameters (substrate traction, KU-2.4/2.18). The paper's `k_off`/`k_app` are **cortical actomyosin turnover** (myosin minifilament binding/unbinding to the cortex network), a distinct observable — no overlap, no tighten.
- `VG-H3-KU35-cortex-tension` / `VG-H7-gate-a/b` (γ_active 0.35–0.65 mN/m): these gate the **magnitude** of cortical tension. This paper gives the **temporal/kinetic** structure (turnover rates, delay) that generates the tension pulse, not the tension band. Complementary, not overlapping. (Potentially useful context for the long-standing ~1000× active-γ floor: turnover `k_off ~0.03–0.10 s⁻¹` sets a myosin residence/duty timescale the FF cortex should reproduce — but this is a mechanistic cross-check, not a fix, and stays a candidate.)
- `MC-U2-fa-motor-clutch` uses **Bell-Evans** off-rate; the paper's cross-linker off-rate is also **Bell's law** (same Bell 1978 family) → consistent with our architecture, not new. Its motor model is the **Parallel Cluster Model** (Erdmann–Schwarz), which is a *different* mechanistic choice from our **Hill (1938)** force-velocity oracle — noted under Duplicates.

**Verdict: 0 existing gates to CONFIRM/TIGHTEN/CHALLENGE directly.** All contributions are NEW candidates.

## New gate candidates (all: candidate — PI-gate review; new gates are PI-authored)

1. **`VG-FF-myosin-turnover-relaxation` (FF, oracle-cross-check).**
   *Observable:* cortical-myosin bound-count N(t) after a step change in binding rate relaxes as a first-order process to `N_eq=k_app/k_off` with time constant `1/k_off`.
   *Band/value:* exponential form `N(t)=N_eq²+(N_eq¹−N_eq²)e^{−k_off t}` (Eq. 7); reproduce within a tolerance on the fitted `k_off` (e.g. ±20%). This is an **analytic ground-truth cross-check** of FF's myosin bind/unbind kinetics (preferred class per philosophy), NOT a fit to embryo data.
   *Condition:* FF cortex patch, myosin minifilament turnover on, isothermal. *Source:* Eqs. 5–7, p.24–25.

2. **`VG-FF-turnover-highpass` (oracle, analytic).**
   *Observable:* under sinusoidal modulation of the myosin binding rate at angular frequency ω, the output amplitude attenuates/phase-lags by `√(1+ω²/k_off²)`.
   *Band/value:* the closed-form attenuation curve (Eq. 4) — a linear-response cross-check joining Kelvin-Voigt as an analytic oracle. *Condition:* small-signal (weakly-activated) regime. *Source:* Eq. 4 / Supp. Note 1, p.11.

3. **`VG-FF-delay-tunes-force` (FF, emergent — QUALITATIVE only).**
   *Observable:* net contractile force of an actomyosin patch as a function of the formin→myosin activation delay d_M is **non-monotonic with an interior maximum** (filaments must cross-link before motors engage, else premature collapse).
   *Band/value:* qualitative — interior peak (paper: peak near d_M≈5 s over 0/5/10 s sweep). Do **NOT** adopt the ~38 nN magnitude as a band (simulation output, different model/geometry). Flag as a candidate emergent-mechanics reproduction target, not a numeric contract. *Source:* Fig 5D, p.17.

I deliberately do **not** propose a hard-band gate on the ~4.5 s cascade delay or on `k_off` magnitude: those are *C. elegans* embryo measurements, cross-species, and would be a convenience band at our MCF7 single-cell scale.

## Oracle candidates

1. **First-order turnover / leaky-integrator (RC) oracle.** `dN/dt = k_app − k_off·N` ⇒ steady state `[Myo⁰]=k_app/k_off`, step solution `N(t)=N_eq²+(N_eq¹−N_eq²)e^{−k_off t}` (Eqs. 3,5,7). Implementable as a closed-form analytic cross-check for FF's myosin (and any bind/unbind) population dynamics — directly analogous to how Bell-Evans and Hill are used as oracles. Ground-truth is the ODE, not the embryo.
2. **High-pass attenuation oracle.** `A_out/A_in = 1/√(1+ω²/k_off²)` with phase lag `arctan(ω/k_off)` (Eq. 4). A linear-response oracle for turnover-filtered forcing; pairs naturally with a Kelvin-Voigt-style config. Sanity-gate the low-ω (near-equilibrium, passes) and high-ω (far-from-equilibrium, attenuated) limits.

Both are cheap to implement and belong in `validation/oracles/` as analytic cross-checks; recommend staging one YAML (`turnover_kinetics.yaml`) carrying k_app/k_off and the two closed forms.

## Duplicates / notes

- **Bell's law cross-linker off-rate** — already our architecture (Bell-Evans, `MC-U2-fa-motor-clutch`; Bell 1978 family). Not new; consistency confirmation only.
- **Parallel Cluster Model motor (Erdmann–Schwarz)** — a *different* mechanistic motor choice from our **Hill (1938)** force-velocity oracle and Stam-Hocky minifilament. Note as an alternative multi-head detachment model the KB could later cross-reference; NOT a duplicate of Hill, NOT proposed for adoption here.
- **Agent-based model = FF physics class** (Langevin cylindrical segments, harmonic bend+stretch, excluded-volume repulsion) — this is the closest external analogue to FF and a good qualitative validation partner, but every number from it is simulation output (comparison only).
- **Preprint, not peer-reviewed** — cite with that caveat; verdict N/A (not yet in `source_audit`). If any datum is registered, mark provenance = preprint.
- No matching KU/KB claim found for cortical myosin turnover kinetics or pulsed-contraction period in the current state snapshot → genuinely new territory, but cross-species overlay-only.
