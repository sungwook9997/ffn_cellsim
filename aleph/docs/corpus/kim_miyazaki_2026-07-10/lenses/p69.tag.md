# TAG-validation lens — p69

**Paper:** Jung, Murrell & Kim (2015) "F-actin cross-linking enhances the stability of force generation in disordered actomyosin networks," *Comp. Part. Mech.* 2(4):317–327. DOI 10.1007/s40571-015-0052-9.
**Lab:** Taeyoon Kim (Purdue) — agent-based actomyosin Brownian/Langevin dynamics; Murrell (Yale) in-vitro cortex collaborator.

## Paper kind

**simulation** (3D agent-based / coarse-grained Brownian-Langevin model output). This is the decisive classification: essentially every quantitative result (σ_max, sustainability S, σ_max∝R_M^0.6, E′/E″ spectra, critical frequencies, force distributions) is **model output, not a laboratory measurement**. Under the project philosophy these numbers are **comparison-only, never a truth source and never a fit target**. They may be used to (a) sanity-check FF cortex-contractility emergent behavior against a peer model of the SAME mechanism, and (b) source *upstream* single-molecule constants that the model itself imports from experiment (Ferrer 2008 filamin-A off-rate; myosin stall/velocity from Veigel/Uemura).

Consequence: this paper generates **zero new hard ValidationGate contracts** (a gate band must trace to experiment or analytic ground truth, not to a sibling simulation). It generates **parameter-provenance candidates** and **qualitative FF cross-check targets** flagged "candidate, PI-gate review."

## Coverage vs existing gates

- **PARAM-F_stall_motor (default 2 pN, KU-2.4/2.18)** — item 3 gives f_M^stall ≈ 5.7 pN (per non-muscle myosin II motor, calibrated to single-molecule). This is ~2.8× the current default and matches the widely-cited ~5–6 pN skeletal/NMII per-motor stall. **CHALLENGES/updates the default provenance**, but as a *per-motor-TF* value in a sibling simulation — flag as a param cross-check, not a silent overwrite. Existing PARAM is per-single-motor and marked "Relation pending"; note the reconciliation need (single head vs 64-head TF vs per-arm).
- **PARAM-v_unloaded (default 100 nm/s, KU-2.4/3.12)** — item 4 gives ~140 nm/s unloaded. Within order; **CONFIRMS band, mild upward nudge**. Cross-check only.
- **VG-U2-catch-slip-bond (lifetime vs force, KB-2.5)** — the paper's myosin arms are **catch-bonds** (k_w, k_u ↓ with force; Fig S1) and ACPs are **Bell slip-bonds** (Eq.1). Mechanistically the same class our Bell-Evans/Hill oracles cover; **CONFIRMS** the mechanism choice (force-dependent off-rate, not Metropolis) but adds no new band (integrin-FN peak ~30 pN is a different bond).
- **VG-H3-KU35-cortex-tension / VG-H7-gate-a/b (γ 0.35–0.65 mN/m)** — the paper's σ_max~600 Pa is a *reconstituted network areal stress in Pa over a 0.5 µm slab*, NOT a cortical line tension in mN/m for MCF7, and it is simulation output. **Not a coverage match; do not convert.** At best a qualitative "an actomyosin network of this composition generates O(100–1000 Pa) contractile stress" sanity reference for FF, explicitly non-authoritative.
- **VG-U3-cortex-multifilament / MC-U3-cell-integration (KU-3.18/3.19/3.20)** — the paper IS an FF-class cortex model (explicit filaments + Bell-Evans crosslinks + bipolar myosin). Its emergent σ_max∝R_M^0.6 and ACP-clutch stabilization are the **closest published analog to FF cortex-contractility emergent behavior** — candidate qualitative cross-checks for that contract, not new gates.
- **Ferrer 2008 already in KB** — the α-actinin k_off0 was re-anchored to 0.066 s⁻¹ (Ferrer 2008) per the 2026-06-02 audit. This paper cites the **filamin-A** value from the same paper (0.115 s⁻¹, λ=1.04×10⁻¹⁰ m). Same source, **different crosslinker species** — complementary, not a conflict.

## Oracle candidates

- **No new analytic ground-truth oracle.** The paper's laws (σ_max∝R_M^0.6, E′∝f^0.09, critical-frequency shifts) are **fitted empirical exponents of a simulation**, not closed-form theory — they cannot serve as analytic cross-checks (that would be validating one simulation against another's fit).
- **Reinforces existing oracle mechanisms**: Eq.1 (Bell slip-bond off-rate k = k⁰·exp(λF/k_BT)) is exactly the **Bell-Evans oracle** form already in the stack; the myosin catch-bond walking/stall is the **Hill force-velocity** class. This paper is a good *provenance/params* feed for those oracles, not a new oracle. The σ=Eε substrate stress-sensor (Eq.2) is a measurement protocol, not an oracle.
