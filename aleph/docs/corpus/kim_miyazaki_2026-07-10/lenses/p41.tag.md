# TAG-validation lens — p41

**Paper**: Ding, Chou, Deguchi, Kim — "Impacts of structural properties of myosin II filaments on force generation." eLife 2025;14:RP105236. DOI 10.7554/eLife.105236.

## Paper kind

**simulation** (agent-based / Brownian-dynamics, Taeyoon Kim lab `ThickFilament` code). The headline quantities (Ftot vs N_F/N_M/f/L_M, efficiency η, network vs bundle scaling exponents) are **model outputs** — under our philosophy they are **comparison-only, never a truth source**. A handful of embedded constants are *cited* experimental/literature anchors (single-head stall F_st≈5.7 pN, unloaded v≈140 nm/s, actin L_p=9 µm, NMII minifilament geometry) — those may be used as FF cross-check anchors, not fit targets. The paper's closed-form **estimator** equations (Eqs 1, 5–7) are analytic and are **candidate cross-check oracles** (but they are the authors' own reduced approximations of their model, not first-principles physics like Young-Laplace — treat as tier-2 oracles).

Engine relevance: **FF = high** (near-direct architectural sibling — explicit F-actin beams, harmonic excluded volume, overdamped Langevin+Euler, cylinder-drag, bipolar myosin minifilament with N_h heads/arm, PCM catch-bond walking/unbinding, permanent ACP cross-links). **DCM = low/indirect** (DCM uses a lumped cortical tension γ; this paper offers at most a scaling sanity anchor for how a lumped myosin term should grow with motor number/density).

- **VG-H3-KU35-cortex-tension / VG-H7-gate-a/b** (γ cortical tension, DCM/cortex) — lumped γ; this paper does NOT give a continuum γ. Its η-ceiling (~0.08) and Ftot∝N_M scaling could serve as an *indirect sanity anchor* for how the lumped active-γ should scale with myosin number, but NOT a gate. (Relevant to the long-running "~1000× active-γ floor" investigation as a scaling cross-check only.)
- **MC-U2-fa-motor-clutch** carries a Hill/PCM motor context but its gates (traction magnitude, biphasic, catch-slip) are FA-substrate observables, not intracellular actomyosin bundle force.

Net: the paper's core observables (cooperative-overlap force bound, contractile-unit emergence, bundle-vs-network scaling) are **uncovered** by any current gate.

## New gate candidates (all "candidate — PI-gate review", FF engine)

1. **proposed_vg_id VG-FF-cooperative-overlap-bound** — *observable*: total tension of a minimal two-antiparallel-filament, two-motor clamped system, with/without an ACP between the motors. *band*: **Ftot(no ACP) → 2× Ftot(single motor), i.e. → F_M^max; Ftot(ACP between) → 1× single-motor, i.e. → F_M^max/2**; equivalently 0.5 ≤ Ftot/F_M^max ≤ 1 (Eq 1). *condition*: 2 antiparallel F-actin clamped, N_a=24-equiv motor, isometric. *engine*: FF. *why defensible*: dimensionless ratio bound, mechanistic (buckling counterbalance), directly measurable in FF as an emergent contractile-unit test — a strong architectural cross-check that FF's myosin minifilament + crosslinker reproduce cooperative partitioning. *source*: Eq 1, Fig 2A, pp4–5.
2. **proposed_vg_id VG-FF-bundle-network-scaling** — *observable*: log-log slope of steady-state Ftot vs motor number N_M. *band*: **bundle slope ≈1.0 (±0.15); 2D network slope ≈0.65 (±0.15, i.e. ~√N_M)**; efficiency η falls with slope ≈−1 (low N_M) → plateau ≈0.08 (high N_M, bundle). *condition*: disorganized bundle (2N_F², R_ACP=0.04, R_M=0.005) vs thin 2D network. *engine*: FF. *caveat*: these are **simulation-derived exponents from a sibling model → comparison-only cross-check**, NOT a truth oracle; band is generous and the gate passes if FF reproduces the *qualitative* bundle>network exponent ordering. *source*: Fig 4A / Fig 7B, pp7,11.
3. **proposed_vg_id VG-FF-contractile-unit-Lc** (weaker, optional) — *observable*: whether cooperative overlap (thus contractile-unit force) turns on when motor overlap L_ov ≥ L_c = 2 L_sp(N_a/4−1). *band*: geometric identity (Eq 6) — pass if FF's emergent overlap threshold matches L_c within ~1 L_sp. *engine*: FF. *source*: Eqs 5–6, p8. (This is really an oracle cross-check; see below.)

## Oracle candidates

- **Cooperative-overlap force bound (Eq 1)**: `0.5·F_M^max ≤ Ftot ≤ F_M^max`. Closed-form, dimensionless — implementable as an FF acceptance oracle for the two-motor contractile-unit test. **Tier-2** (author's model reduction, but mechanistically sound).
- **Critical-overlap geometry (Eq 6)**: `L_c = 2·L_sp·(N_a/4 − 1)`. Pure geometric closed form — a clean deterministic oracle for the onset of full cooperativity given motor architecture (N_a, L_sp). Recommended if FF implements variable-architecture minifilaments.
- **Analytic force estimator (Eqs 4–5,7)**: `Ξ = max_i[1 + Σ_k ζ_ik]`, `ζ` per Eq 5, `F_est = F_st·N_h·N_a·Ξ/2`. A structural estimator that maps motor spatial configuration → bundle force. Could serve as a coarse analytic cross-check of FF bundle force, but requires computing Ξ from FF motor positions — more of an analysis routine than a standing oracle. **Tier-2, optional.**
- The scaling laws (Ftot∝N_M bundle, ∝N_M^0.65 network; η∝1/N_M plateau 0.08) are **empirical simulation fits, NOT analytic** → comparison targets only, do not implement as truth oracles.
