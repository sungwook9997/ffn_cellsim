# TAG-validation lens — p64

**Paper:** Bidone, Kim, Deriu, Morbiducci, Kamm (2015) — *Multiscale impact of nucleotides and cations on the conformational equilibrium, elasticity and rheology of actin filaments and crosslinked networks.* Biomech Model Mechanobiol 14:1143–1155. DOI 10.1007/s10237-015-0660-6.

## Paper kind

**simulation** (multiscale computational — atomistic MD → ENM/RTB normal-mode analysis → Brownian-dynamics agent-based crosslinked-actin network). Every quantitative output (filament diameter, subdomain distances, lₚ, kₛ, network stress, p-values) is a **model output**, not an in-vitro measurement. Consequence for our KB: these numbers are **comparison-only cross-checks, never a truth source and never a fit target**. The one genuinely *experimental* datum imported into the paper is **Kang et al. 2012** lₚ = 2.1→12.7 µm (fed in as input, not measured here).

Crucially, this is the **Taeyoon Kim lab Brownian-dynamics engine** (Kim 2009a/b, Kim 2014) — the direct conceptual ancestor of our **FF** fine-grained crosslinked-actin runtime (cylindrical actin segments + harmonic stretch/bend + excluded volume + two-arm ACP crosslinkers + Langevin overdamped). So the paper is best used as an **engine-level cross-check / parameter donor for FF**, exactly the "on-demand cross-check" tier the philosophy allows — not as an oracle (no closed form) and not as experimental overlay (it is simulation).

## Coverage vs existing gates

- **VG-U1-strain-stiffening** (`372120daec5d81489a91d139ae8b5d8d`, status=passing): observable = strain-stiffening exponent, band magnitude **[1.5, 2.5]** on the stiffening branch over γ∈[0.30, 0.65]. This gate is for the **ECM collagen Mikado** network, NOT actin. p64's **critical strain ~0.5** (item 6) sits **inside** the gate's γ window and is qualitatively consistent, so it **CONFIRMS** the onset-strain choice — but it does **not** measure our exponent and it is a *different network* (crosslinked actin, not collagen). **Does not tighten or challenge**; it is a same-family corroboration of the stiffening-window contract. No overlap on magnitude.
- **VG-U1-G0-linear** (`372120daec5d8131b2b4c8518da5f412`): G₀ for collagen (~30–100 Pa). p64's actin-network stress (200–2500 Pa) is a *different material* and *finite-strain* (not linear G₀) — **not covered, not comparable**.
- **VG-H3 / cortex-tension gates** (KU-3.5, 0.35–0.65 mN/m): cortex *tension*, an emergent whole-cell observable — p64's network shear rheology is a constituent property, not this observable. No overlap.
- **Parameters `PARAM-ell_p` (17 µm, collagen)** and **`PARAM-kappa_fiber` (7e-26 N·m²)**: these are **collagen ECM fiber** persistence/bending, NOT actin. p64's lₚ (3.5–17 µm) is **F-actin** and belongs to a *separate* actin-filament parameter family we do **not** currently register. No conflict — different molecule.
- **No existing gate or parameter covers crosslinked-F-actin network rheology, actin lₚ/kₛ, or ACP crosslinker stiffness.** This is a genuine gap for the FF engine.

## New gate candidates (all **candidate — PI-gate review**; simulation-tier cross-check, not truth)

1. **`VG-FF-actin-strain-stiffening` (candidate).** Observable: shear stress–strain σ(γ) of an FF crosslinked-actin network; strain-stiffening onset. Band: **critical strain ≈ 0.5** (order-of-magnitude, γ_c ~0.3–0.6). Condition: 20 µM actin, R_ACP=0.01, compliant ACP, shear rate 0.1 s⁻¹, 3 µm box. Engine: **FF**. Source: Fig. 5, p8. Rationale: FF *is* this engine class — reproducing σ(γ) with γ_c~0.5 is a legitimate engine cross-check. NOTE: magnitude (σ~200 Pa @60%) is **comparison-only** since it is simulation, not an experimental band.
2. **`VG-FF-crosslinker-stiffness-gating` (candidate, qualitative law).** Observable: sensitivity of network strain-stiffening to filament rigidity as a function of ACP stiffness. Contract: with **compliant ACP (0.002 N/m)** network rheology is **insensitive** to modest lₚ change (curves overlap, ns); with **rigid ACP (0.2 N/m)** it becomes **sensitive** (curves separate; ~3× stress spread over lₚ 2.1→12.7 µm). Engine: **FF**. Source: Fig. 5a–d, p8. Rationale: a mechanistic, sign-sense validation ("stiff crosslinker transmits filament rigidity into the network; compliant crosslinker screens it") — defensible as an emergent-behavior contract without inventing a numeric band.
3. **`VG-FF-actin-persistence-length` (candidate, cross-check only).** Observable: F-actin persistence length from FF filament bending modes. Value: model **~3.5–17 µm** depending on cation/nucleotide (baseline ~9–17 µm lit; this MD gives a low ~3.5–4 µm). Engine: **FF / analytic** (lₚ=κ/k_BT). Rationale: useful sanity target BUT p64's baseline (3.5–4 µm) is **below** the accepted experimental ~9–17 µm actin lₚ — flag as a **discrepancy to resolve, not a band to adopt**. Prefer an experimental anchor (Gittes 1993 / Kang 2012) over this simulation value.

## Oracle candidates

- **None net-new as a closed form.** p64 has **no** new analytic law beyond textbook relations we already treat as oracles: (i) **lₚ = κ/(k_BT)** (persistence↔bending — already implicit in `PARAM-kappa_fiber` derivation and the fiber oracle); (ii) harmonic stretch V=½kₛ(ℓ−ℓ₀)² and bend V=½k_f(θ−θ₀)² (standard FF potentials). The RTB mode→rigidity mapping lives in the (absent) Supplementary Info and is a *method*, not a portable closed form.
- The strain-stiffening σ(γ) divergence above γ_c~0.5 is a **simulation curve, not an analytic oracle** — it cannot serve as ground truth (philosophy: analytic/mechanistic cross-checks are primary; this is neither).

## Duplicates / notes

- **Same-lab lineage, likely partial KB overlap.** The Kim-lab BD engine and its actin/ACP constants already inform our FF design (see MEMORY: FF is GPU-native Warp Cytosim/Kim-style). The 140 nm/7 nm segment geometry, R_ACP=0.01, 20 µM, and the two ACP-stiffness classes are **Kim 2009a** conventions — check whether Kim 2009a is already a SourceEvidence row before adding p64; if so, p64 is a *secondary* corroboration, not a new source.
- **Taeyoon Kim cross-ref:** the project already validated `DCM⊗ECM vs Kim: 1/r RECOVERED` (single-cell fiber-ECM matches Taeyoon-Kim). p64 is the *actin-network-rheology* arm of the same lab — complementary, not duplicate.
- **Flag the lₚ discrepancy:** p64's baseline actin lₚ ~3.5–4 µm is **below** the canonical ~9–17 µm; this is an MD-force-field/short-filament artifact acknowledged in its own Discussion (low end of Kang's 3–12 µm). Do **not** register 3.5 µm as an actin lₚ band — use the experimental Kang/Gittes anchor.
- **Overall:** high FF *relevance*, but the paper is **simulation**, so it delivers **parameter donors + a qualitative cross-check law + one experimental anchor (Kang 2012)** — no experimental overlay curve and no new oracle. Net-new gate potential is real but must be **PI-authored** and kept at comparison/cross-check tier.
