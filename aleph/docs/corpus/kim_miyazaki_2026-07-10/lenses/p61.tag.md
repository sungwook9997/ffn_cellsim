# TAG-validation lens — p61

**Paper**: Linsmeier, Banerjee, Oakes, Jung, Kim, Murrell (2016) — *Disordered actomyosin networks are sufficient to produce cooperative and telescopic contractility*. Nat. Commun. 7:12615, DOI 10.1038/ncomms12615.
**Lab**: Taeyoon Kim (agent-based sim, W.J.+T.K.) + Murrell (Yale, in-vitro) + Banerjee (continuum active-gel). A Kim-lab corpus paper → directly in the FF fine-grained actomyosin domain.

## Paper kind

**experimental** (primary), with an embedded **analytic** continuum active-gel sub-model and a **simulation** (agent-based Brownian dynamics) sub-model. Three data streams, three use-classes:

- **In-vitro reconstitution measurements** (PIV strain/velocity fields, Hill fit, telescopic law, filamin effect) → **experimental → OVERLAY / cross-check target**, never a fit target. Note these are a *2D reconstituted biomimetic cortex* (purified F-actin + NMM II on a lipid bilayer, methylcellulose-crowded, no crosslinker) — NOT a whole MCF7 cell. Numbers are condition-specific to this prep.
- **Continuum active-gel model** (σₐ(r,t)=σ₀(1−e^(−t/τₐ)), radial-symmetric equilibrium) → **analytic → candidate oracle** for the telescopic scaling *form* (but has free E/σₐ; scaling-law cross-check, not parameter-free ground truth).
- **Agent-based sim** (Jung-Murrell-Kim lineage; F-actin segments + bipolar myosin, load-dependent kinetics) → **simulation → comparison only, never a truth source**. Its geometry constants are usable as FF parameterization, but its *outputs* (cooperativity, telescopy, bending-invariance) are model reproductions, not validation truth.

## Coverage vs existing gates

The KB has **no cortex-contractility / cooperativity / telescopic gate at all**. The closest existing gates are magnitude-of-tension gates, not contraction-dynamics gates:

- **VG-H3-KU35-cortex-tension**, **VG-H7-gate-a**, **VG-H7-gate-b** — these test steady-state active *cortical tension* γ (band 0.35–0.65 mN/m), i.e. the ~1000× active-γ floor problem. This paper measures **strain / strain-rate / contraction-velocity fields of a reconstituted 2D network**, a different observable class (kinematics of contraction, not a whole-cell tension magnitude). **No overlap** — the paper neither confirms nor challenges these bands (different system: no turgor, no membrane, no substrate FA, 2D purified network). It is a *mechanistic sibling* to the γ-floor work (the cooperative/percolation view of how myosin density sets contractility could inform the γ-floor "generation vs transmission" question) but not a band on the same gate.
- **VG-U3-cortex-tension-spreading** (KB-3.5) — qualitative "lower myosin → altered spreading". Paper's cooperativity result is adjacent (myosin density → contraction) but a different, quantitative observable; not covered.
- **VG-U3-cortex-multifilament** (KB-3.18/3.19/3.20, Phase-2 entry gate) — the FF disordered multi-filament cortex this paper's physics maps onto. This paper supplies *what to validate that cortex against* (cooperativity + telescopy) once it runs contractility, but doesn't change the gate's own band.
- **PARAM-F_stall_motor**, **PARAM-v_unloaded**, **PARAM-gamma_cortex** — myosin/cortex params; this paper adds network-geometry and composition ratios, not stall force or unloaded velocity, so no supersession.

**Conclusion**: everything of value here is NEW-gate / cross-check material for a not-yet-built FF cortex-contractility validation, plus FF network-geometry params. Nothing tightens or challenges a current band.

## New gate candidates (all CANDIDATE — PI-authored gate review required)

All are for the **FF** engine (fine-grained actomyosin) as **cross-check / overlay** against a reconstituted 2D network — explicitly *not* the physiological single-cell cortex band, and *not* to be fit.

1. **VG-FF-contractility-cooperativity (candidate)** — *observable*: max network strain εmax vs myosin thick-filament areal density ρ, in a disordered 2D actomyosin network. *Band (form, defensible)*: a **sharp percolation-like transition** — εmax<0.5 for ρ below a critical density, εmax>1 above it; strain rate LINEAR in ρ. *The threshold ρc≈0.56 µm⁻² and nH≈11 are the specific-prep OVERLAY values* (1.3 µM actin, 24 nM NMM II, methylcellulose-crowded), NOT a portable band — nH=11 is a Hill FIT and must never be treated as a contract band to hit. *Engine*: FF. *Source*: Fig 3c, p2–3. *Caveat*: register the qualitative "below/above-threshold binary + linear strain-rate" as the contract; carry ρc/nH as overlay only.

2. **VG-FF-telescopic-contraction (candidate)** — *observable*: as activation-region radius ξ grows, spatially-averaged **εmax and dε/dt stay ~constant while boundary contraction velocity increases with ξ**. *Band (form)*: monotone increase of \|v(r=ξ)\| with ξ (S/M/L ~0.025→0.07 µm/s as overlay), with εmax and dε/dt flat within scatter. *Engine*: FF (agent-based cortex); continuum active-gel is the analytic cross-check. *Source*: Fig 4c–e, p3. This is the strongest, most portable contract (a scaling *law*, not a magnitude).

3. **VG-FF-strain-kinetics-phases (candidate, weaker)** — *observable*: ε(t) shows three phases (lag → linear accumulation → plateau) consistent with saturating active-stress buildup σₐ∝(1−e^(−t/τₐ)). *Band*: presence of lag+linear+plateau; ∇·v reaches a minimum ~−7×10⁻³ s⁻¹ at peak contractility (overlay). *Engine*: FF, cross-checked by the analytic buildup law. *Source*: Fig 2c, Fig 3a. Weaker because timescales are prep-specific.

4. **VG-FF-bending-invariance (candidate, sanity/robustness)** — *observable*: telescopic contraction is RETAINED as F-actin persistence length rises 20 µm → 2.6 mm (filament buckling suppressed). *Band*: telescopy qualitatively survives; strain magnitude decreases modestly with lp. *Engine*: FF. *Source*: Fig 6, p7. **Note this is a SIMULATION result** (agent-based) — use as a mechanism-robustness sanity check, not an experimental truth. Good FF self-consistency gate: FF should reproduce "bending not required for telescopy."

## Oracle candidates

- **Active-stress buildup law** σₐ(r,t) = σ₀(r)·(1 − e^(−t/τₐ)) (Methods, p8). Closed-form, implementable as an analytic cross-check for FF contractility kinetics (the P1→P2→P3 ε(t) shape). **Candidate oracle, with caveat**: σ₀ and τₐ are free/fit parameters, so this is a *functional-form* cross-check (does FF's ε(t) follow a saturating exponential with a single τₐ?), not a parameter-free ground truth like Young-Laplace or Helfrich. Lower-tier than the existing analytic oracles.
- **Telescopic velocity scaling from the continuum active-gel** (radial-symmetric equilibrium, Supp Notes 1–2). Predicts boundary velocity ∝ ξ with slope set by E/σₐ, while εmax, dε/dt stay flat. Usable as an analytic cross-check for gate candidate #2, again as a scaling *form* (free E/σₐ). Full E, η, ν, σ₀, τₐ, ζ values are in Supplementary Table 1 (not in dossier) — fetch SI before implementing.
- No parameter-free universal law (no analog of κL/2R² or ΔP·R/2) is offered.
