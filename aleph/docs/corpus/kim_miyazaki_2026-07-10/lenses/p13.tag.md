## Paper kind

**experimental** (primary) **+ simulation** (confirmatory Kim-lab Brownian-dynamics fiber network).

Rationale: the load-bearing claims — negative normal stress ≈ shear stress in col1/IBC networks, TACS-3 radial alignment, swirling — are **measured** (shear rheology on real acIPN gels + human tissue; confocal + FIDIC on live spheroids; intravital 4T1). A confirmatory **agent-based BD fiber sim** (annular geometry, dipole shear) reproduces them and supplies the only *quantitative* exponents (radial n=1.2 / tangential n=2.2). Usage rules therefore split:
- **Experimental numbers** (tissue moduli, plasticity, negative-normal-stress sign/magnitude) → **overlay / cross-check targets only, never fit**.
- **Sim numbers** (power-law exponents 1.2/2.2, ~50% fiber stretch) → **comparison only, never a truth source** (this is a model output, same class as our own FF/DCM output).
- **Fiber-model parameter table (Suppl. Table 2)** → **model values, NOT empirical constants**; register as a ModelContract *reference* for the FF collagen-ECM side, not as sourced physical parameters.
- ⚠️ Preprint, NOT peer-reviewed → any KB SourceEvidence row gets a `CHECK` verdict flag until published.

## Coverage vs existing gates

**CONFIRMS + TIGHTENS — `VG-U1-force-propagation`** (σ(r) decay exponent n; current band "fibrous regime closer to 1/r than 1/r³"; status=passing, PASS-as-documented r⁻¹ for 2D sub-isostatic Mikado z~3.4).
- This paper is a **direct independent confirmation from a Taeyoon Kim BD fiber network** (the same physics class we cross-check against). The **radial** deformation exponent **n = 1.2** (Fig. 4p) sits essentially at 1/r — squarely inside "closer to 1/r than 1/r³" — and the **tangential** exponent **n = 2.2** shows the *anisotropy*: the same network gives ~1/r along the tension (radial) direction and ~1/r² transverse. Our gate is a scalar "closer to 1/r"; this paper motivates **tightening the band to an anisotropic pair** (radial n≈1–1.3 vs tangential n≈2–2.4). Flagged as a **tighten candidate, PI-gate review** — do not silently narrow; note both are *sim* exponents (comparison, not truth) so the tighten is "our FF collagen annulus should reproduce radial≈1/r, tangential≈1/r²," not a fit target.
- The **alginate-only control (row 8): radial decays as fast as tangential when the fibrous col1 is removed** is the mechanistic control for this gate — it says the ~1/r long-range propagation is a *fibrous-network* property, exactly the KB-1.10/1.30 basis of VG-U1-force-propagation. Strong corroboration.

**ADJACENT / partial — `VG-U1-strain-stiffening` (passing) & `MC-U1-ecm-network`.** Negative normal stress is the same semiflexible-network nonlinearity family (fibers stretch under shear → contractile inward pull, Janmey 2007). Not the same observable as the strain-stiffening exponent, but same contract's physics. No band change.

**NOT covered (no existing gate):**
- **Negative normal stress** (σ_N ≈ −σ_shear in a fibrous col1 network; sign flips to positive in non-fibrous/healthy) — we have **no gate** for the shear→normal-stress coupling. New candidate below.
- **Swirling / tangential-dominant interfacial motion** and **collective radial alignment** — cell-collective-on-fibrous-ECM observable; DCM does not currently model surrounding fibrous ECM, so no gate maps. Motivates a future DCM-aggregate-on-FF-ECM scenario, not a Phase-1 gate.
- Tissue moduli (1.4/4.7 kPa) and plasticity (15/32%) — bulk-tissue, not our single-cell scale; **overlay context only**, not a cell-scale contract.

## New gate candidates (all: candidate, PI-gate review)

**PROPOSED VG-U1-negative-normal-stress** (engine: **FF**, collagen/Mikado network; cross-check vs Janmey-class analytic + this sim)
- **Observable:** ratio of first normal-stress difference to shear stress, σ_N/σ_shear, in a sheared semiflexible fibrous (collagen) network at moderate strain.
- **Band/value:** **σ_N < 0 (contractile) with |σ_N|/σ_shear = O(1)** at ~20% shear strain for a fibrous col1-like network; **σ_N ≥ 0 for a non-fibrous / flexible control** (sign-reversal is the discriminating contract). Tolerance: report ratio in [0.3, 1.5] magnitude, sign strictly negative.
- **Condition:** parallel-plate shear, 20% strain amplitude, crosslinked WLC fiber network (our FF collagen build).
- **Why defensible:** it is a *sign* + *order-of-magnitude* contract emerging from fiber mechanics, not a tuned number; measurable directly in FF as a virial normal-stress component. Cross-checks the FF ECM against a known fibrous-network hallmark.
- **Source:** Fig. 4c–i, pg 8–9. ⚠️ magnitude is sim/rheology (overlay); the *sign-reversal fibrous-vs-flexible* is the robust, contract-worthy part.

**PROPOSED VG-U1-annular-shear-anisotropy** (engine: **FF**; scenario gate)
- **Observable:** in an annular collagen network sheared by dipole tangential forcing at the inner boundary, the radial-vs-tangential deformation power-law exponents.
- **Band/value:** **radial n ≈ 1.0–1.3, tangential n ≈ 2.0–2.4** (Δn ≳ 0.8 separation), i.e. radial contractile deformation propagates farther than tangential.
- **Condition:** annulus (inner ~50 µm / outer ~200 µm surrogate), dipole shear (not stochastic/uniform — only dipole reproduced it).
- **Why defensible / caveat:** this is a **sim-reproduction target**, not an experimental truth — mark "comparison only." Could alternatively be folded into VG-U1-force-propagation as its anisotropic extension. **Prefer folding over a standalone gate** unless FF builds the annular TACS-3 scenario.

*(No new DCM gate proposed — DCM lacks the surrounding fibrous ECM; the swirling/BM-insulator biology is conceptually rich but has no measurable single-cell-scale band here.)*

## Oracle candidates

**Bell's-law slip-bond off-rate (Eq. 9)** — `k₋ = k⁰₋·exp(λ|F_s|/k_BT)` for arm under tension, `= k⁰₋` under compression.
- This is **exactly our existing Bell-Evans off-rate oracle**. The paper supplies a concrete parameter pair (k⁰₋=1e-6 s⁻¹, λ=0.4 nm) usable as a **cross-check config** for the FF crosslinker unbinding kernel. Confirms our mechanistic choice (Bell-Evans, not Metropolis). **Duplicate of existing oracle** — register the (k⁰, λ) pair as a cross-check datum only.

**Negative normal stress (Janmey 2007, ref 24)** — *not a closed form the paper derives*; it is the emergent network property. Candidate for a **semi-analytic cross-check** ("sheared semiflexible network → σ_N<0, |σ_N|~σ_shear") but there is no clean scalar law to implement as an oracle. Treat as a qualitative sign/scale cross-check (see VG candidate), not a formula oracle.

**Overdamped Langevin + fluctuation-dissipation + cylinder drag (Eqs. 3–6)** — standard BD; matches our FF Brownian-dynamics core. No new oracle, but a **methods cross-check** that our FF integrator/drag (Clift cylinder drag, Eq. 5) is in the same family. Δt=4×10⁻⁴ s, explicit Euler is a *coarser* choice than our BAOAB — comparison only.
