# IF cage (nucleus↔cortex load path) — design + first-landing finding (2026-07-16)

**Goal:** the intermediate-filament cage is the DOMINANT nucleus↔cortex coupling that should transmit
cortex/AFM deformation to the nucleus (the internal-displacement diagnosis proved the nucleus is decoupled;
the FSI fluid is too weak). Design agent-verified; `ff/intermediate_filaments.py` + `network_warp.py`
`if_cage=None` channel (additive, bit-identical OFF).

## Design (radial-spoke cage, LINEAR-first)
The old plumbing's TANGENTIAL perinuclear shell does NOT couple nucleus↔cortex (decoration). The load path =
RADIAL SPOKES (bead chains from just outside R_nuc to just inside R_cortex along Fibonacci directions) +
**LINC** (spoke inner bead ↔ nearest nucleus surface bead, nesprin/SUN) + **cortex anchor** (spoke outer bead
↔ nearest cortex node, plectin/desmosome) + tangential **crosslinks** (plectin). LINEAR-first: every bond
Hookean (k_bb=E_if·A_if/l_seg=942 pN/µm, KB-anchored G1 gate) → rides `link_spring_kernel`, no new kernel;
IF beads a TAIL block (not in enet — reshape would kill extensibility). Nonlinear 3-regime strain-stiffening
(F_yield/λ1/λ2/k_stiff) deferred behind PI KB registration — not faked.

## ⚠ First-landing finding (NATIVE, NF=70686, n_fil=7600 dense) — it does NOT flatten the nucleus
NATIVE `--if-cage`: nucleus RAW |Δ| **0.1 nm, flatten 0.0%** (still frozen), BUT **F_plate 9106 → 53140 pN
(~6×)**. So the cage is built + wired + exerting force, but:
1. **Cortex over-stiffened ~6×** — contradicts Guo 2013 (IF contributes little to cortical stiffness). The
   design gate's explicit HALT signal ("if F_plate jumps → over-stiff anchors/k_bb → HALT, re-examine the KB
   anchor, do NOT tune down").
2. **Force does not reach the nucleus.** Root causes: (a) polar radial spokes are under AXIAL compression and
   BUCKLE (IF has no bending stiffness, Lp~l_seg) → transmit ~0; (b) at strain 0.10 the plate (6.93 µm) does
   NOT reach the nucleus (R_nuc 5.1 µm, 1.8 µm gap) and the cortex R_eq barely changes (7.695→7.695) → the
   equatorial spokes barely stretch → little tensile transmission.

**Honest status: the linear radial-spoke cage is NOT a success — it over-stiffens the cortex and does not
flatten the nucleus.** This is a design finding to iterate + surface to PI, NOT a knob to tune.

## Open design questions → PI
- **Over-stiff cortex (F_plate 6×):** the KB-anchored k_bb=942 pN/µm × dense anchors (n_fil, anchor count =
  PI magic numbers) is load-bearing at the cortex. Needs a PI-authored IF/anchor DENSITY anchor (the honest
  analog of the cortex 100/µm²) — don't tune down to pass.
- **Buckling transmission:** radial spokes buckle under axial compression. Options to surface: (a) a small IF
  bending term; (b) test at HIGHER strain (>0.34, where the plate reaches the nucleus — the regime where real
  AFM flattens the nucleus); (c) a different cage topology; (d) accept that at gentle strain the nucleus
  genuinely barely deforms (the plate doesn't reach it) — a small response may be physically correct.
- Nonlinear strain-stiffening (F_yield/λ1/λ2) + k_LINC + n_fil — PI KB registration (HALT items).
