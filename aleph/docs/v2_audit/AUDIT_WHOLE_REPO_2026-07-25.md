## 7. Honest quality assessment

### As engineering and as science, the genuine strengths — named as practices, with artifacts

**Self-refutation by measurement, repeatedly, against your own interest.** This is the strongest thing here and it is rare. The 857× AFM "too stiff" finding traced through large-strain-σ-over-small-strain-Hertz → under-relaxation → press speed, with the earlier verdict "K_vol=736 kPa is correct, do not retune" **explicitly retracted as "wrong — that canonizes the bug."** BDF2 implemented specifically to test the over-damping hypothesis, refuting it, and concluding the biology-time gap is fundamental rather than banking the 25× operator-split speedup (correctly diagnosed as "it prescribes drift, bypassing force balance"). SC2 deflated-PCG committed as "iteration win REAL but wall BREAK-EVEN (honest negative)." Treadmill front-bias and clutch-density hypotheses REJECTED with the dead-end flags left in the code, off by default. The Salbreux myosin density recorded as a CONFIRMED MISATTRIBUTION and re-anchored to Nie 2015 **even though re-anchoring deepens the unfavourable floor.** GATE A concluding that the residual is discrete-myosin local non-equilibrium requiring ~7× heads = unphysical — i.e. "the solver is not the problem; the modelling question is."

**Naming your own outcome-tuning in writing.** `_historical/DCM_SPREADING_HONEST_CORRECTION_2026-07-14.md`: "back-rationalised 15 nN via a ~3 FA per coarse node argument **invented AFTER seeing which value passes G5**" — reported as a FAIL, decomposed, then fixed by a grounded total-traction distribution rather than a re-tune, and the fix demonstrated grid-invariant across subdiv 2/3/4. And an inline comment at the exact kernel that produced a retracted result: "that was a 10⁶× unit bug … **the crush it caused faked the earlier 'compaction'**."

**Required-no-default parameter guards.** `ac/motor/params_i0b3.yaml`: every contested magnitude is `value: null` + `evidence_status: "GAP — PI"` + owner + `expiry_trigger` + `demonstrated_by`, with `native_run_blocked_on: [F_stall_head, N_side, v0, kappa_hill, k_xb]` blocking the magnitude gate by construction; `claim_a`/`claim_b` naming the exact competing `ff/` module and constant; a documented three-way muscle-vs-non-muscle κ conflict recorded rather than resolved; `k_xb` flagged "⚠ MASTER force knob" with the legacy 1 pN/µm surrogate called out as unphysical; and the policy line "NEVER tuned to lift gamma."

**Oracle quarantine that actually holds, and an external oracle that did real work.** Zero `validation.oracles` imports in any engine package. And the Cytosim parity check found a fidelity gap the paper-literal reading missed — the interior-triple bending sum under-counts by (n−2)/(n−1) — which was then **fixed in the kernel** (`_per_triple_alpha`, default ON) with the correction proven to preserve force = −∇E.

**Citation auditing that found and removed real fabrications, with a propagated numeric consequence.** Three hallucinated sources identified, removed from SourceEvidence, and the α-actinin `k_off0` re-anchored 0.4 → **0.066 s⁻¹** (Ferrer 2008) in the live config as a result. Very few research codebases have ever done this.

**Gates that encode project rules as machine logic, with the right polarity.** `verify_runs`: disk overrides the claim, downgrade-only, and honestly-declared `NEEDS_REGEN`/`RETRACT` stays green so nobody is incentivised to over-declare; the PI-forbidden A/A0 metric set (basal-contact / footprint / convex-hull) → automatic RETRACT. Pure stdlib, no token, no network, blocking in CI. The design is right; only the manifests are stale.

**Adversarial self-audit as a habit, not an event.** Four `AUDIT_*` docs in one day with severity-ranked tables and recorded *failed* refutation attempts (including checked-and-correct conventions, so they are not re-litigated); a 22-agent adversarial review of S2 that fixed 6 bugs and disclosed the residual artifact in the REPORT. And the sharpest instance: **the project found most of what this audit found and wrote it down first** — the harvest-glob blindness, the stray zip, the duplicate keys, the ×14.6 Hertz miss, the self-inconsistent `density_per_fil`, the N=2000 static-demo disavowal, the retracted footprint metric. Two of this audit's findings were fixed *while it was running* (`0e1f52f7`, `cc204043`).

### Systemic weaknesses — as patterns

**1. Documentation outruns evidence, and the charter mandates it.** `CLAUDE.md:306` makes `STRUCTURE.md`/`README.md`/briefs read-only during unit work; `:277` makes briefs immutable. The three highest-severity doc-drift findings are therefore the *designed behaviour* of the governance model, not neglect — which is why per-file hygiene will not fix them.

**2. The enforcement layer points at the past.** Every automated gate defends finished work: 25/31 result claims are June DCM parity vs HOOMD fixtures, 42/42 param rows are retired v1 oracle configs, the Warp-only contract test sees only `ac/`, CI runs a pre-I0-A configuration. **And neither manifest can be incomplete** — a manifest can be wrong about what it lists but never wrong about what it omits — which is the structural reason both froze in June while the gates stayed green. The most active engine has the least coverage.

**3. Breadth accumulated faster than validation, and the ratio is visible in the file counts.** `scripts/` is 396 files / 84k LOC — more than ff+dcm+common+validation combined ✔ — and is simultaneously the production surface, the gate surface, and the archive-dependency surface, versioned as none of them. Ten `ff/` and 27 `dcm/` modules have no test at all, including the poroelastic Biot core `ac/fluid` builds on and the self-described "missing DCM spreading physics."

**4. Three engines with overlapping physics — resolved better in code than in docs.** The code is clean (`ac→ff` is real kernel reuse with a written per-file harvest plan; `ac→dcm` is zero). The docs are not: two of three engine SoT files claim to be going-forward, README asserts a `dcm→ac` dependency that does not exist, and the same physics carries 2–3 unmarked implementations (two `xl_turnover_kernel`s with different force laws, three EV implementations, four myosin force scales, three `K_vol` values, three turgor values).

**5. Results quoted from unconverged, coarse, or *known-invalidated* runs.** This is the pattern that matters most, because the invalidation is not hidden — `bc5ff3b0`'s own message names under-percolation as "the upstream root of … no myosin transmission," and the γ-floor measures exactly myosin transmission, yet the floor is still on the public site as a measured shortfall. Same shape smaller: the σ-compaction taken at a dt the repo itself measured as a blow-up dt; the superseded 1.62–1.68 spreading number left in a doc after the same-day fix commit.

**6. One gate written so a broken build cannot fail it.** `engine_reval` P3.1 tests giant ≫ 2nd rather than absolute connectivity, and its note declares 84% connectivity / 9,154 fragments "the validated native-cortex connectivity." That is the only failure mode in this audit that is worse than an un-gated result, because it manufactures confidence.

**7. Audit-doc sprawl makes the true state expensive to recover.** It took seven mapped dimensions, seven adversarial verification passes, and this synthesis to answer "what is proven" — a question a maintained 2-page `STATE.md` would answer in ninety seconds.

### The one habit to keep, and the one to stop

**Keep: measuring your own hypothesis until it dies, and writing the death certificate in the code and the commit message.** The AFM retraction, the BDF2 refutation, the honest-negative PCG, the misattribution that deepened the unfavourable result, the "faked the earlier compaction" inline comment, GATE A's "the solver is not the problem." This is what makes the rest of the repo trustworthy, and it is the practice most research codebases never acquire.

**Stop: letting a superseding finding land without retiring the results it invalidates.** The percolation fix reached one of two cortex builders and nothing was re-run or re-bannered. The 2026-07-07 linear-FV ratification never reached CLAUDE.md. The 40 Pa re-anchor never reached `mcf7_baseline.yaml`. The 2026-07-22 architecture decision never reached `AGENTS.md`. The `0e1f52f7` harvest fix left duplicate rows behind. **Every one of R1–R7 above is an instance of the same omission.** The cheap discipline that closes all of them: *when a finding supersedes something, the same commit banners the artifacts it invalidates and edits the one public sentence that quotes them.*
