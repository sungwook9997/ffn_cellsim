## Open items (PI to triage)

1. **KU-2.5 experimental F\* gap (info-only).** KU-2.18 illustrative parameters give F\* = 6.99 pN; experimental is 30 pN. Refit deferred to Phase 2 per D2. v1 sanity gate convention preserved (`catch_peak_KU_2_5_experimental_gap_logged`).
2. **KU-2.4 biphasic verdict still on literature band.** The week-3 HOOMD substrate-stiffness sweep is the canonical mechanistic test; `test_ku24_biphasic_production` currently `skip()`s with an explanation. Owner: Sub session week 3 dispatch.
3. **Spring-CFL-limited dt = 6.5 ns.** At k_int_bare = 1 pN/nm and γ_int from a 5 nm bead, the BAOAB CFL dt is much smaller than the bond-event-rate CFL dt (27 ms). Full-cell HOOMD runs need ~1.5e8 steps per τ_F\* — wall-time bottleneck for week-3 production. Possible mitigations to discuss with PI: stiffer integrin substrate bead (smaller γ ratio), or implicit BAOAB for integrins only.
4. **Pre-allocation vs snapshot-rebuild for integrin bonds.** Current `IntegrinBondUpdater` does a full snapshot rebuild every batch (~100 steps). At 43 FAs × 50 integrins the per-batch cost is ~tens of µs (negligible vs 100 BAOAB steps); for 10× larger cells the cost grows linearly and may dominate. Alternative: pre-allocate all `N_FA · N_total` bonds with engaged/unbound typeids and toggle in `cpu_local_snapshot` (avoids set_snapshot). Surface in week-3 perf review.
