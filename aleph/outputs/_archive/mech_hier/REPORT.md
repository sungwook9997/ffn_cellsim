## S1 status (native, A5000, NF=70686)

- **Loading, not layer, sets the modulus:** naive loading = 888 kPa (AFM-overshoot artifact) →
  physiological loading (biphasic drainage + turnover + η_bulk=65.9 + rigid plate) = **4.1 kPa**
  (~215× drop, same bare cortex).
- **Bare actin cortex ≈ 4 kPa is physical;** the ~17× gap to the MCF7 whole-cell band (249 Pa) is the
  softening layers (membrane/turgor/nucleus) — the bottom-up decomposition target.
- **S1 gate = constitutive-class self-consistency** (not the MCF7 band). Converged native (15000 steps,
  rigid plate): **PASS as a clean LINEAR-SHELL** — shell r²=**0.992**, E_fit=**3624 Pa** (properly
  relaxed). The drained bare cortex is a pressurized thin shell (F∝δ), not a Hertzian solid.
- **Filament count = physical density** (E_fit rises 1.5→4.1 kPa with NF; native = physiological anchor),
  not a numerical-convergence knob.

## S2 status (localized load — native, A5000, NF=70686) ✅ both gates PASS

- **Sign reversal PASS:** a localized cap force (cortex-surface only) → inward force makes a **dimple**,
  outward force makes a **bulge** (converged, 15000 steps/force, drift-free). Force–deflection at ±2000 pN:
  inward **−2.12 µm**, outward **+3.09 µm**.
- **Compression STIFFENS, tension ≈ linear:** inward deflection grows sub-linearly (r=0.60 — turgor
  reaction rises as the dimple deepens); outward is ≈linear (r≈1.03). The outward bulge is 45% larger than
  the inward dimple at the same force — a real pressurized-shell asymmetry.
- **Localization PASS:** load-induced Δr is confined to the load pole (θ≤20° |Δr|=2.78 µm) and decays to
  ~0 by θ≈35° (equator 0.001 µm) → **loc_ratio 2695 ≫ 3**. Textbook localized boundary layer.
- **Deflection SATURATES** to elastic equilibrium at the mechanical timescale (~20 µs; does NOT creep).
- **Hardened by a 22-agent adversarial review** (6 bugs fixed: patch force dim=Nc, centroid-relative
  metrics, physical dt_s); parity 7/7 held. *Strain-scene caveat:* the areal-strain colour scale is
  inflated by a few degenerate rest-triangulation faces at the dimple (real strain ~σ/E ~0.2); the field
  is qualitatively correct (concentration at the pole), magnitude scale artifact-limited (driver fix
  landed for S3).

## S3 status (nucleus layered compression — native, A5000, NF=70686) ✅ PASS; PI decision A implemented

- **Soft nucleus, MODEST contribution (ROBUST):** the physiological MCF7 nucleus (E=399 Pa) is **softer
  than the bare cortex (~3624 Pa)**, so cortex+nucleus compression is only **+12% over cortex-only at
  ε=0.45** — the nucleus is the compliant inclusion, not a stiff dominator (contradicts "nucleus
  dominates"). This holds for BOTH the compressible (+11%) and incompressible (+12%) nucleus → robust.
- **PI decision A — nucleus INCOMPRESSIBILITY (ν=0.499) implemented:** a nucleoplasm bulk-modulus
  pressure so the nucleus flattens at constant volume. Native re-run: volume conserved EXACTLY (1.000);
  the nucleus now **BULGES past contact** (R_nuc_eq 5.25→**5.40** at ε=0.45, vs the compressible flat
  5.26). The fidelity fix works — it corrects the *shape*, and confirms the modest *magnitude* (nucleus
  is shear-limited soft + its bulge is unconfined).
- **Contact onset detectable:** the force ratio ACCELERATES 2.5× past ε≈1−R_nuc/R0≈0.30. G1 (accel≥1.5×)
  + G2 (net monotonic stiffener) PASS. See plan Findings 13–15.
- **GPU-main turgor-volume port:** the enclosed-volume was on CPU (scipy ConvexHull per refresh, GPU
  util ~0%) → GPU divergence kernels + periodic re-hull. Native 15000-step runs **~10 min → ~1 min**, F
  within 0.2% of CPU. Benefits every native run (S1/S2/S3 forward).
- Viz `s3_nucleus_incompressible_3d.html` + `deform_media/s3_nucleus_incompressible.{gif,mp4}`: cortex
  flattens to a drum, the incompressible nucleus squeezed + bulged.
