# TAG-validation lens — p35

**Paper:** Li, Biel, Lomada, Yu, Kim — "Buckling-induced F-actin fragmentation modulates the contraction of active cytoskeletal networks" — Soft Matter 2017, 13, 3213–3220 (DOI 10.1039/c6sm02703b). Taeyoon-Kim agent-based Brownian-dynamics lineage.

## Paper kind

**simulation** (agent-based / coarse-grained Brownian dynamics; model output, NOT lab measurement, NOT closed-form theory). Consequence for our KB: its numbers are **comparison-only** — they are FF-parity / FF-cross-comparison targets, **never a truth source and never an overlay fit-target**. Crucially this is the *same modeling lineage FF is built on* (explicit F-actin bending-beam segments, ACP cross-linkers with force-dependent Bell-like unbinding, myosin thick filaments = 8 arms × 8 heads bipolar minifilament, LJ-style volume exclusion, Langevin/Euler BD). So it is best used as a **mechanism source (Eq. 1 severing law) + qualitative phase-behavior parity check for the FF engine**, not as a quantitative experimental contract. The one genuine closed-form it contributes (Eq. 1, curvature-dependent severing rate) is an **analytic mechanism candidate** worth registering as an FF force/kinetics law.

Governing forms:
- **Eq. 1** — curvature-dependent severing rate: `k_s,A = k⁰_s,A · exp(θ_s,A / λ_s,A)`, θ_s,A = sum of two adjacent bending angles at a point. Angle-activated (buckling ↑θ ↑rate), NOT a Bell *force* law.
- **Eq. 2** — contraction extent: `ξ(t) = 1 − [Σ_i π R_i(t)²] / [Σ_i π R_i(0)²]` over N foci; steady state at ξ̇ < 0.1·ξ̇(0); average rate = ξ_ss / t_ss.

- **VG-H7-gate-b (`379120daec5d81bb91a1df65d3b3e12a`)** — "relaxed-M-SHAKE buckling/condensation transmission lever" for γ_active. ## New gate candidates

All are **candidate, PI-gate review**, **FF engine**, and must be labelled **simulation-parity (comparison-only), not experimental**. They are qualitative/scaling contracts (defensible because they are shape/ordering laws, not invented numeric bands). None should be presented as an experimental fit target.

1. **`VG-FF-contraction-nonmonotonic-kappa` (candidate, PI review)** — Observable: steady-state contraction ξ_ss vs F-actin bending rigidity κ_b,A, with curvature-severing ON. Contract (shape): ξ_ss is **non-monotonic with an interior maximum** (peak at intermediate κ_b,A), not monotone-decreasing. Engine: FF. Source: Fig. 3D, p.4–5. Rationale: this is the paper's central, falsifiable qualitative result and a direct FF-parity check once FF has severing.

2. **`VG-FF-buckling-suppression-Racp` (candidate, PI review)** — Observable: contraction extent vs cross-linker density R_ACP (no severing). Contract: large contraction requires **intermediate R_ACP**; above R_ACP ≈ 0.32 (molar ratio to actin) buckling is suppressed and ξ_ss collapses. Engine: FF. Source: Fig. 2C, text p.3/6. Caveat: 0.32 is a model threshold at the paper's density/geometry — register as an **ordering/existence contract (interior optimum + upper cutoff), not the literal 0.32 number**.

3. **`VG-FF-large-contraction-threshold` (candidate, PI review)** — Observable: ξ_ss over (R_M, R_ACP). Contract: ξ_ss > 0.5 only when R_M ≥ a critical minimum AND R_ACP in an intermediate window (the "contraction island"). Engine: FF. Source: Fig. 2C.

4. **`VG-FF-critical-kappa-scaling` (candidate, PI review)** — Observable: the buckling-cutoff bending rigidity κ_b,A*. Contract (scaling): κ_b,A* **∝ R_ACP / R_M and independent of k⁰_s,A**. Engine: FF. Source: Fig. 6B, p.7. Rationale: a clean dimensionless scaling law — good parity target because it constrains functional dependence, not a magic number.

Deliberately **NOT proposed**: severing-frequency 20–7000 and focus-count 1–11 absolute numbers (grid/parameter-specific model outputs, would be magic numbers as contracts); the 0.58 µm segment L_p (coarse-grained, not physiological); 100–500 s timescale (setup-dependent).

## Oracle candidates

1. **Eq. 1 — curvature-dependent (buckling-activated) severing rate** `k_s,A = k⁰_s,A · exp(θ_s,A/λ_s,A)`. This is the highest-value import: a concrete closed-form **kinetics law FF currently lacks** (buckling → severing → connectivity loss → foci). Register as an **FF mechanism / analytic cross-check** in the same category as Bell-Evans off-rate and Hill force-velocity (mechanistic runtime law + oracle for its own rate). Underlying validation vs in-vitro is in Jung/Murrell/Kim 2016 (ref 15) + ESI — pull those for the acceptance band.
2. **Eq. 2 — contraction-extent metric** `ξ(t) = 1 − ΣπR_i(t)² / ΣπR_i(0)²` with the ξ̇<0.1·ξ̇(0) steady-state and ξ_ss/t_ss rate definitions. Not a physics oracle but a **measurement-protocol standard** — adopt as the FF/DCM contraction observable definition so contraction gates are computed consistently (foci-area reduction, not a bespoke metric).
