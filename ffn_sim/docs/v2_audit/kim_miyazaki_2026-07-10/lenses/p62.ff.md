# FF lens — p62

**Paper:** Kubota, Miyazaki, Ogawa, Shimozawa, Kinosita, Ishiwata (2018), *Processive Nanostepping of Formin mDia1 Loosely Coupled with Actin Polymerization*, Nano Lett. 18, 6617–6624. DOI 10.1021/acs.nanolett.8b03277.

**One-line for FF:** Direct single-molecule, nanometer-resolution measurement of mDia1 barbed-end **stepping** — unitary step 2.8 nm (fwd) / −2.4 nm (back) ≈ one actin subunit, forward-step rate 0.92 s⁻¹ @ 40 nM and 1.4 s⁻¹ @ 75 nM G-actin (association, concentration-dependent), backward-step rate 0.56 / 0.62 s⁻¹ (dissociation, concentration-independent), under 4–6 pN load — plus the discovery that stepping is **loosely** coupled (double/triple-subunit steps occur). This is a direct kinetics + step-size dataset for FF's formin `barbed_end_growth_kernel` (KB-3.6 ratchet), and a validation oracle for elongation-rate provenance. It is NOT a force–velocity law (tension held ~constant).

---

## 1. MECHANISMS to adopt

**FF module: formin (mDia1) barbed-end elongation ratchet — `barbed_end_growth_kernel` / KB-3.6.**

1. **Discretize formin elongation into explicit ~2.7 nm subunit steps, not a continuous smooth growth velocity.** Current FF grows the barbed end at a continuous rate (`v0≈0.623 µm/s` measured piece-1, [[project-ff-active-movement-pieces]]). This paper shows elongation is fundamentally a **discrete Poisson stepping process**: forward association steps of +2.7 nm at rate `k_on_eff([G-actin])` and backward dissociation steps of −2.7 nm at rate `k_off_eff`. Adopting an explicit stochastic subunit-addition/removal channel (add/remove one 2.7 nm bead-rod increment per Poisson event) is strictly *more mechanistic* than a lumped drift velocity — it recovers the mean growth as an emergent average (`v = 2.7 nm × (k_fwd − k_back)`) AND automatically reproduces elongation *fluctuation/noise* (diffusion in length), which a deterministic v0 cannot. This matches the FF hard rule: fine-grained mechanistic over lumped.

2. **Split the growth rate into a concentration-dependent association channel and a concentration-independent dissociation channel.** The paper cleanly separates: forward rate scales with [G-actin] (0.92→1.4 s⁻¹ as 40→75 nM), backward rate does not (0.56→0.62 s⁻¹). FF should carry the barbed-end elongation as `k_on·[G_actin] − k_off` (two-rate polymerization), so that G-actin depletion/reservoir coupling (FF already has a cytoplasm reservoir, [[project-ff-afm-overshoot-was-artifact]]) modulates growth *mechanistically* through the on-rate, and a genuine **critical concentration** emerges where `k_on·c_c = k_off` (net growth = 0). This is the correct place to couple filament growth to the monomer pool.

3. **Loose coupling → allow multi-subunit (2× / 3×) step events.** The headline finding: mDia1 does not translocate at *every* monomer event; it can stay put through one association and then take a double (5.4 nm) or triple (8.1 nm) step. For FF this is a *refinement*, not a required core mechanism: at our cortical/filopodial scale the emergent mean growth is what matters and single-subunit stepping already captures it. Adopt loose coupling only if/when FF models formin cooperation with capping protein (CP) or barbed-end competition — the paper's mechanistic payoff of loose coupling is that the forefront subunit is transiently *free* (intermediate states `<n+1>*`, `<n+1>**`), permitting other barbed-end proteins to bind. Flag as an OPTIONAL channel; do not add magic intermediate-state rates without a datum for their kinetics (none given here).

**Why it beats current FF:** current formin growth is a single lumped drift velocity with no monomer-pool coupling and no intrinsic length noise. The two-rate discrete-stepping scheme is mechanistic, reservoir-coupled, gives an emergent critical concentration, and is anchored to direct single-molecule measurements.

## 2. FORCE LAWS / KINETIC SCHEMES to port

This is a kinetics paper; there is **no force field**. The portable content is the stepping/dwell-time kinetic scheme.

**(A) Two-channel barbed-end stepping (port as the formin growth kernel):**
```
forward (association) event:  L_barbed += δ,   rate k_fwd = k_on · [G_actin]
backward (dissociation) event: L_barbed -= δ,   rate k_back = k_off   (independent of [G_actin])
δ = 2.7 nm  (one actin subunit / half-helical-repeat 36 nm ÷ 13 subunits)
```
Map to FF state: `L_barbed` = barbed-end bead position along filament axis; each event adds/removes one 2.7 nm bead-rod segment (or advances a sub-segment accumulator until a full FF bead length is reached, since FF beads are coarser than 2.7 nm). `[G_actin]` = local monomer concentration from FF cytoplasm reservoir. Poisson-sample events per dt (`P_event = 1 − exp(−k·dt)`), consistent with FF's stochastic binding-updater pattern (myosin/xlink).

**Emergent mean elongation velocity:**
```
v_elong = δ · (k_fwd − k_back) = 2.7 nm · (k_on·c − k_off)
```
Sanity check against the data: at 75 nM, v ≈ 2.7 nm × (1.4 − 0.62) s⁻¹ ≈ 2.1 nm/s ≈ 0.8 subunit/s. (Low because [G-actin] is near critical concentration; physiological cytosolic G-actin is ~µM, giving far higher rates — see Risks. Also note this is the *centroid* of two FH2 halves, so true subunit addition rate = 2× the observed step rate.)

**(B) Dwell-time distribution (validation / calibration oracle, eq 1):**
```
f(t) = a[ A·e^{−kt} + B·k t·e^{−kt} + C·k² t²·e^{−kt} ]
```
Sum of 1-, 2-, 3-sequential first-order (Gamma order 1/2/3) reactions sharing rate `k`; A,B,C = area fractions of the single/double/triple step-size Gaussians. Use to validate that an FF formin-stepping simulation reproduces the measured dwell-time statistics (not just the mean).

**(C) Multi-step detection-probability model (eqs 2–5) — an ANALYSIS tool, port as a measurement-protocol check, not physics:**
```
P(t) = e^{−kt}                          # survival: not yet stepped by t
p_single = e^{−kT}                      # T = temporal resolution
p_double = kT·e^{−kT}
p_triple = 1 − e^{−kT} − kT·e^{−kT}
p_single + p_double + p_triple = 1
```
Relevance to FF: when we *measure* stepping/growth statistics from an FF run at finite sampling interval T, apparent multi-steps arise from merged events at rate `k`. These formulas tell FF's measurement layer how much of an observed "multi-step" tail is a sampling artifact vs genuine loose coupling — a measurement-protocol-consistency guard (a Sanity-Gate item), not a runtime force law.

## 4. NETWORK ARCHITECTURE / PROTOCOL to reproduce

**Single-formin, single-filament reconstitution — a clean FF micro-benchmark scenario (not a cortex scenario):**

- One actin filament, barbed end capped by one mDia1 (FH1FH2), pointed end fixed (biotin-avidin analog = a pinned FF node).
- Constant tension 4–6 pN applied along the filament axis (FF: apply a fixed pulling force to the barbed-end bead).
- Monomer pool at fixed [G-actin] = 40 or 75 nM (FF reservoir held constant).
- T = 26 °C, ATP-G-actin, Mg²⁺/K⁺ buffer ionic conditions (informs FF's binding-rate temperature/ionic context; not directly a mechanical input).
- **Observable to extract:** barbed-end position vs time → step-find → step-size histogram + dwell-time histogram.

This is a **piece-1 (ratchet) validation harness** for FF: run a single FF filament with the two-channel stepping kernel at 40 and 75 nM and confirm the emergent step-size distribution (peak at δ) and dwell-time rate (`k_fwd`) match. It is a *dev-scale single-filament sanity test*, explicitly non-authoritative for cortex conclusions (native-scale rule) — its role is to calibrate the formin kernel before it is deployed in the native 38000-filament cortex.

## 5. VALIDATION TARGETS for FF observables

FF formin-stepping kernel should reproduce, at 4–6 pN load and 26 °C:

1. **Unitary step size:** forward +2.8 nm, backward −2.4 nm (≈ ±2.7 nm subunit). FF single-filament run → step histogram peak at |δ| ≈ 2.7 nm.
2. **Forward-step rate (concentration-dependent):** k = 0.92 s⁻¹ @ 40 nM, 1.4 s⁻¹ @ 75 nM. FF must show k_fwd increasing with reservoir [G-actin].
3. **Backward-step rate (concentration-independent):** k = 0.56 s⁻¹ @ 40 nM, 0.62 s⁻¹ @ 75 nM — FF k_back must be ~flat in [G-actin].
4. **Mean elongation velocity:** v = δ(k_fwd − k_back) ≈ 0.36 subunit/s @ 40 nM, ~0.8 subunit/s @ 75 nM (centroid); ×2 for true subunit addition.
5. **Critical concentration:** net growth → 0 below ~64 nM (barbed-only somewhat lower). FF's two-rate scheme should cross zero net growth in this range if calibrated to these k's.
6. **Multi-step fractions (loose-coupling, optional target):** at 75 nM forward, ~45 % single / 46 % double / 9.3 % triple; these EXCEED the merged-artifact prediction from eqs 3–5 at T = 0.003 s and 0.1 s — i.e. genuine. If FF implements loose coupling, reproduce this excess-over-artifact.
7. **Dwell-time distribution shape:** monotonic decay fit by eq 1 (Gamma-mixture) — FF dwell histogram should match, not just the mean.

## 6. RISKS / caveats

1. **Purified in-vitro, near-critical-concentration regime.** Rates (0.92–1.4 s⁻¹) are deliberately *slowed* by 40–75 nM G-actin (near c_c). Physiological cytosolic G-actin is ~1–100 µM → elongation is 10–100× faster (tens of subunits/s). **Port the two-channel STRUCTURE and the step size δ = 2.7 nm; do NOT hard-set the production growth rate to 0.92–1.4 s⁻¹.** Anchor the physiological on-rate to a µM-regime formin-elongation source and surface to PI (no fitting, no magic number).

2. **Temperature 26 °C, not 37 °C.** Rate constants carry an Arrhenius correction to body temperature (~1.5–2× faster). Treat the numbers as 26 °C calibration points; the *ratio* k_fwd:k_back and the concentration scaling are more transferable than absolute values.

3. **Force held ~constant (4–6 pN) — this is NOT a force–velocity law.** mDia1 elongation IS force- and torque-dependent (refs 6,8–11; Yu 2017 disputes saturation; immobilization-method-dependent). FF still needs a separate load–velocity / force-modulation law for the formin ratchet under cortical tension — this paper only fixes the low-load stepping kinetics and cannot supply the force dependence. Do not infer force-independence in FF from this single 4–6 pN window.

4. **Centroid-of-two-FH2-halves detection.** The measured 2.8 nm step is the *centroid* of two FH2 halves; a true single-half translocation is 5.4 nm and true subunit addition rate is **2×** the observed step rate. When mapping to a per-subunit FF addition event, account for this factor-of-2 (the observed "double step" 5.4 nm = one full half-translocation). Getting this wrong double-counts growth.

5. **No profilin in the assay.** Physiological formin elongation is profilin-accelerated (>10×, refs 3–8). The paper argues profilin decreases dwell time (raises k) without changing step choice. FF's physiological-baseline rule means profilin-enhanced kinetics should be the production operating point — these bare-formin rates are a *floor*, not the physiological setpoint. Surface to PI when setting the production formin growth rate.

6. **FF bead granularity vs 2.7 nm subunit.** FF cortex beads are much coarser than a 2.7 nm actin subunit (mesoscale rod segments). Implement stepping as a sub-bead length accumulator (`ΔL += δ` per event; emit a real FF segment when accumulator ≥ FF bead length) so the discrete kinetics drive an emergent, coarse-grained growth without inflating particle count — keeps native-scale feasibility.

7. **Single-cell scope OK.** This is single-filament/single-molecule; it stays cleanly at FF's fine-grained single-cell scale. No 2D-assay-vs-3D or spheroid-vs-single-cell confound. Loose coupling's cellular payoff (CP cooperation) is out of current FF scope — keep it flagged, not built, until a barbed-end-competition scenario is on the roadmap.
