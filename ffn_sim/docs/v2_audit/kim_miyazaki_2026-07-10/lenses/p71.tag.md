# TAG-validation lens — p71

**Paper:** Muresan, Sun, … Taeyoon Kim, Murrell (2022) *F-actin architecture determines constraints on myosin thick filament motion*, Nat. Commun. 13:7008. DOI 10.1038/s41467-022-34715-6.

---

## Paper kind

**experimental (primary) + simulation (secondary).** Two coupled bodies of data:

1. **In-vitro reconstitution** (Murrell lab) — thin quasi-2D "cortex-like" actomyosin networks nucleated from a supported lipid bilayer by formin mDia1 (linear/bundled) vs Arp2/3 (branched), + skeletal-muscle myosin-II thick filaments. Strain (PIV), thick-filament tracking (MSD/van Hove/NGP), two-point microrheology (C_rr), laser-ablation Kelvin-Voigt, micro-rheology G*. → **overlay / cross-check targets, NEVER fit.**
2. **Agent-based Langevin Brownian-dynamics simulation** (Taeyoon Kim lab; Figs 8, S14, Table S1) — cylindrical-segment filaments/motors/Arp2/3 with bending+stretch+torsion + repulsive **volume-exclusion (VE)**, PBC box 4×4×1.5 µm, 64-arm motors, force-dependent unbinding. → **comparison only, never a truth source** (this is essentially the physics FF re-implements in Warp; use for behavioral cross-comparison, not as a validation oracle).

**Important scale/condition caveats that gate how the numbers may be used:**
- **Reconstitution, not a cell.** pH 8.5 (raised to suppress nucleation), Egg-PC bilayer, methylcellulose depletant, **skeletal-muscle** myosin-II (SKMMII, ~20 molecules/thick filament) — the paper explicitly contrasts this with cellular **non-muscle NMIIA** (~300 molecules). So myosin geometry/kinetics here are **not** the MCF7 physiological operating point.
- **Native filament scale.** Pore ~0.5 µm, thick-filament L_M ~1.3 µm are native-scale; the FF ×40-mesoscale caveat applies to any direct geometric comparison.
- Consequently every quantitative datum below is an **overlay/cross-check candidate**, and the strongest contributions are *emergent-behavioral contrasts* (architecture → contractility; steric caging) rather than a single physiological band.

## New gate candidates (all: candidate — PI-gate review; engine = FF; overlay/cross-check, not fit)

> These are *emergent-behavioral contrast* gates, well-suited to FF because FF re-implements exactly the Taeyoon-Kim BD physics (segment bending/stretch/torsion + repulsive VE + force-dependent motor unbinding). They are contracts on **the sign/qualitative regime of an emergent observable**, which is defensible even though absolute magnitudes are reconstitution-specific.

1. **FF-p71-1 — architecture-gated contractility contrast.**
   - Observable: max mean network strain ⟨ε⟩_max = ⟨∇·x_c⟩ of an FF actomyosin network under identical myosin load, comparing a **linear/bundled** (formin-like) vs **branched** (Arp2/3-like) architecture.
   - Band/value: linear network **contracts**, ⟨ε⟩_max ~ O(1) (paper 0.8–1.3); branched network **non-contractile**, ⟨ε⟩_max ≤ ~0.15 (order-of-magnitude ≥5–10× separation, paper p=8×10⁻⁵). Gate on the **contrast ratio / sign**, not the absolute reconstitution value.
   - Condition: quasi-2D cortex-like network, identical total actin + identical myosin density, only nucleator architecture varied.
   - Source: Fig 2e / p3 (#1–2, #4).

2. **FF-p71-2 — connectivity-gated 1/r stress transmission (actomyosin).**
   - Observable: two-point displacement correlation C_rr(r) decay exponent α (C_rr ~ 1/rᵅ), r = 3–20 µm.
   - Band/value: **linear/connected** network α ≈ 0.8–1.15 (continuum-like, ≈1/r); **branched** network **no assignable 1/r** (suppressed). Gate on presence/absence of the 1/r regime as a function of connectivity.
   - Engine: FF (analysis mirrors two-point microrheology Eqs 10–11). Note independent parallel to **VG-U1-force-propagation** (ECM) — same 1/r-vs-connectivity physics in a different material.
   - Source: Fig 4e,f / p5 (#9–11).

3. **FF-p71-3 — myosin steric-caging (volume-exclusion) MSD signature.**
   - Observable: ensemble translational MSD exponent α of tracked myosin thick filaments; and the **VE-toggle** effect (MSD with VE < MSD without VE at fixed R_Arp2/3).
   - Band/value: **linear** network α > 1 (super-diffusive/directed); **branched/dense** network α < 1 (sub-diffusive/caged); turning **volume-exclusion off** in FF must **raise** MSD (VE is the dominant caging cause). Gate on the sign of the α shift with architecture and the direction of the VE effect.
   - Engine: FF (this is the paper's own agent-based result, Fig 8c — a *behavioral cross-comparison* between FF and the Taeyoon-Kim BD sim, not a truth import).
   - Source: Fig 5h / p6 (#12–13); Fig 8c / p9 (#23–24).

4. **FF-p71-4 — cofilin de-branching restores motility (optional / lower priority).**
   - Observable: myosin MSD exponent and speed before/after de-branching a dense Arp2/3 network.
   - Band/value: de-branching raises MSD exponent ~0.35 → ~0.77 and speed ~7× (τ<15 s). Gate on directional restoration.
   - Condition: 74 nM Arp2/3 + 790 nM cofilin. Requires an FF de-branching/severing mechanism (may be out of current FF scope → flag as future).
   - Source: Fig 6f–h / p7 (#14–15).

*(Deliberately NOT proposed as gates: τ_v = 22/50 s, network elasticity 1–10 Pa, pore-size µm² — these are single reconstitution-specific magnitudes with no physiological-cell contract band; register as overlay params instead.)*

## Oracle candidates

- **Kelvin-Voigt retraction — ALREADY AN ORACLE.** Eq 12 `ε(t)=(σ/E_v)(1−e^{−t/τ_v})`, τ_v=η_v/E_v is our existing Kelvin-Voigt cross-check. This paper **confirms the form and its use as a post-ablation extraction protocol** — no new oracle, but a good worked reconstitution example and a measurement-protocol reference.
- **Measurement-protocol oracles (not truth oracles, but useful FF analysis cross-checks):** anomalous-diffusion MSD `4D_T t^α` (Eq 2) + rotational `2D_θ t^α` (Eq 3), non-Gaussian parameter `NGP(τ)=3⟨r⁴⟩/(5⟨r²⟩²)−1` (Eq 6, 2-D form), two-point correlation C_rr (Eqs 10–11), nematic order `⟨n⟩=2⟨cos²θ−½⟩` (Eq 1), GSER `|G*(ω)|≈k_BT/(πa⟨Δr²⟩Γ[1+α])` (Eq 9). These are standard **analysis estimators**, candidates to implement as FF post-processing so FF myosin-tracking output can be compared to the paper's curves in the same units. Recommend implementing NGP + C_rr as FF diagnostics (supports gates FF-p71-2/3).
- **No new closed-form *physical* law** to adopt as a ground-truth oracle — the paper's core result (steric caging) is emergent, not a closed form. The Taeyoon-Kim BD equations (bending/stretch/torsion + VE + Bell-type unbinding) are in the SI (not in this PDF) and are *the same class of physics FF already implements*, so they are a **parity/behavioral reference, not an oracle**.
