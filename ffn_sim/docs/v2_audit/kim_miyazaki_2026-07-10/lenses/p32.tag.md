## Coverage vs existing gates

Almost everything maps onto gates/contracts **already staged** in the KB — this review supplies literature *corroboration*, not new observables. Key mappings:

- **#1 F-actin L_p ~9 µm, #2 d 5–9 nm** → FF filament parameters. Compare to `PARAM-ell_p` (17 µm, but that row is *collagen* type-I persistence length, KU-1.1 — a **different filament**, do not conflate). No dedicated actin-L_p Parameter row exists; #1/#2 are a clean **param candidate** (see below), CONFIRMS the mesoscale actin semiflexibility assumption (L_p ≈ contour length).
- **#7 chromatin↔lamin ~3 µm crossover, #8 lamin ~2× area, #19 nucleus poroelasticity** → `VG-H9-nucleus` (E_nuc 1–10 kPa, ratio 1.4–5×, arrest at ~10% cross-section). The ~3 µm crossover and the lamin-A load-bearing/strain-stiffening mechanism CONFIRM the two-element (chromatin + lamin-A shell) design in MC-H9-nucleus; they do **not** tighten E_nuc (no modulus number given). #19's rate-dependence is a *poroelastic* nucleus signature not currently in any band.
- **#9/#10 superelastic dome σ ~1–3 mN/m, ε up to 1000%** → this is a whole-cell/epithelial cortex-tension observable. Partial overlap with `VG-H3-KU35-cortex-tension` / `VG-H3-composite-tension` band **0.35–0.65 mN/m** — but that band is the *resting single-cell MCF7 cortical tension*, whereas #10 is the *tensional-plateau surface tension of a pressurized multicellular epithelial dome* (Latorre MDCK). **Different scale and different state** → does NOT challenge the 0.35–0.65 band; it is a distinct superelasticity observable with no current gate. NEW candidate, DCM, flagged for PI review (multicellular, may be out of single-cell scope).
- **#11 K′ ~1→100 Pa vs prestress, #12 fibroblast stiffening 0.3→0.9 kPa** → strain-stiffening. Related to `VG-U1-strain-stiffening` (exponent [1.5,2.5]) but that gate is the **ECM Mikado network**, not the actin cortex. The *cortex/whole-cell* strain-stiffening these describe has no gate. #11 (prestress-set onset, myosin:actin dependence) is a good FF mechanistic cross-check; NEW candidate.
- **#13 0.1–10 s, #14 ~0.05 s crossover, #15 β 0.18–0.31** → `VG-H10-cytoplasm` (η 10–70 Pa·s, G 30–80 Pa, **Dp 40–60 µm²/s**, mesh ξ~14 nm). The poroelastic *timescales* (#13/#14) and the poroelastic→power-law *crossover* are the temporal signature of the same Dp the H10 gate already targets; they CONFIRM/complement it and could be added as a time-domain sub-band. #15's β exponents are vimentin-variant-specific (not MCF7) → overlay only.
- **#16 fascin G″-minimum at crosslinker off-rate, low/high-ω slopes 0.06/0.5** → FF viscoelastic signature: G″ minimum located at the ACP unbinding rate is a mechanistic prediction the FF engine (Bell-Evans crosslinker off-rate) should reproduce. No current gate covers frequency-domain G′/G″ of the cortex. Strong NEW mechanistic-cross-check candidate.
- **#17/#18 whole-cell moduli ~10²–10⁴ Pa** → generic whole-cell rheology; overlaps the general modulus scale but these are chondrosarcoma/fibroblast, not MCF7 → overlay only, no gate action.
- **#20 ~1000 subvolumes** → this is the Kim-lab BD model's *coarse-graining choice*, notably the same order as our ratified ~1000-effective-filament mesoscale. Confirms precedent, not a validation datum.

**Net:** 0 gates challenged; 0 bands tightened numerically (review gives no MCF7-specific number sharper than an existing band). Several existing gates receive literature corroboration.

## New gate candidates

All **candidate, PI-gate review** — staged, not authored. Conservative: only observables that are measurable at our single-cell scale with a defensible secondary band.

1. **proposed_vg_id VG-FF-cortex-strain-stiffening** — observable: differential (tangent) modulus of the actomyosin cortex/whole cell rises with imposed strain/prestress. band: K′ climbs ~1→~100 Pa as prestress increases above a critical σ_crit (myosin-prestress-set onset), myosin:actin dependence 0.02/0.005/0.001. engine: **FF** (mechanistic) with whole-cell overlay. condition: crosslinked actin ± myosin, bulk-rheometer analog. source: p.6 Fig.3(d) →[115] "K′ increases … above a critical prestress". *Distinct from ECM VG-U1-strain-stiffening.* Note: value is a shape/onset cross-check, not a hard band — flag as overlay.

2. **proposed_vg_id VG-FF-crosslinker-Gsecond-minimum** — observable: frequency-swept cortex G″ has a **local minimum at the crosslinker (ACP) unbinding rate**, with power-law slopes ~0.06 (low ω) / ~0.5 (high ω). engine: **FF** (this is a direct emergent prediction of the Bell-Evans force-dependent off-rate the engine already uses). condition: fascin/α-actinin-crosslinked actin. source: p.8 Fig.5(f) →[96]. This is the single most mechanistically valuable new candidate — it ties a measurable rheological feature to the crosslinker off-rate the FF runtime computes.

3. **proposed_vg_id VG-H10-poroelastic-timescale** (sub-band under existing VG-H10-cytoplasm) — observable: force/stress relaxation is **exponential (poroelastic) for t≲0.05 s then power-law (viscoelastic)**; water-flow relaxation window 0.1–10 s. engine: DCM/H10. condition: AFM/optical-tweezer relaxation. source: p.9 Fig.6(d) →[101]; p.12 §4.4 →[98]. Complements the existing Dp 40–60 µm²/s band with its time-domain signature; the crossover time links to Dp via D_p = E_solid·ξ²/η_fluid (see oracle).

4. **proposed_vg_id VG-H9-nucleus-poroelastic-rate** (sub-band under VG-H9-nucleus) — observable: isolated-nucleus indentation **peak force increases with loading rate** (1→16 µm/s). engine: DCM/H9. source: Fig.6(e) →[159]. Adds a rate-dependence check to the nucleus gate (currently static E_nuc only). Flag: HEK-293 not MCF7 → overlay.

5. *(low priority, likely out of single-cell scope)* **VG-DCM-superelastic-plateau** — epithelial-dome surface tension plateau σ ~1–3 mN/m at areal strains up to ~1000%. engine: DCM multicellular. source: Fig.3(e-f) →[8]. Flag: this is a *pressurized multicellular dome* (Latorre MDCK), not a single MCF7 cell — surface to PI on whether it is in scope before staging.

## Oracle candidates

- **Poroelastic diffusion coefficient (relational law)** — the review states D_p ∝ (Young's modulus of the solid part) × (pore size)² / (fluid viscosity), i.e. **D_p = E_solid·ξ²/η_fluid** (p.12 §4.4, →[98]). This is a closed-form relation we could implement as an **analytic cross-check** for the H10 cytoplasm module: given E_solid, mesh ξ (~14 nm) and η, predict Dp and the relaxation crossover time τ ≈ L²/D_p. Candidate oracle `poroelastic_Dp.py`. Caveat: the review states it relationally (∝), not with a numeric prefactor — implement the scaling and cross-check the existing Dp 40–60 µm²/s band, do not treat as ground truth.
- **Young–Laplace (named, already an oracle)** — the review confirms micropipette+Young-Laplace ΔP·R/2 as the standard cortical-tension estimator (p.4 §3); this **matches our existing Young-Laplace oracle** — no new work, corroboration only.
- No new *fundamental* closed-form (Helfrich/Bell-Evans/Hill/Kelvin-Voigt) is introduced; the review names Maxwell/Kelvin-Voigt/SLS lumped models qualitatively (Fig.4), which our Kelvin-Voigt oracle already covers.
