**experimental + analytic** (hybrid). The core is an **in-vitro single-molecule reconstitution** (dual-trap optical-tweezers "actin dumbbell," individual mDia1-capped actin filament) — an in-vitro **overlay/cross-check** target, NOT a fit target, and NOT native-cortex physiology (mDia1 FH1-FH2 fragment, 26 °C, purified rabbit-muscle actin). ## Coverage vs existing gates

**No existing gate covers force-dependent formin/barbed-end polymerization.** Scan of `validation_gate`:

- The closest force-law oracles in the KB (per CLAUDE.md/task brief) are **Hill force-velocity** (myosin **motor** stall, not polymerization) and **Bell-Evans off-rate** (bond rupture, not filament elongation). Neither is the formin elongation law. **VG-U2-catch-slip-bond** (integrin lifetime-vs-force, peak ~30 pN) and **MC-U2-fa-motor-clutch** use Bell-Evans for *adhesion bond rupture* — different observable, different force scale.
- **VG-U3-lamellipodia-separation** references "Mueller 2017 Hill (w~2); Bieling 2016 branching" for barbed-end/branching — the nearest FF actin-growth gate, but it is a Phase-2 *dataclass-separation* entry gate with no force-velocity band, and it is Arp2/3-branching-centric, not formin-processive-capping. **Does not cover** this observable.
- **VG-U3-gactin-pool** covers monomer-pool conservation (c_G), relevant as a *coupling* to any elongation kernel but not the elongation rate itself.

Conclusion: this paper's force-dependent formin elongation is a **genuine gap** → new candidates below. It does not confirm/tighten/challenge any *existing band* (no formin band exists to challenge).

## New gate candidates *(all "candidate, PI-gate review" — new gates are PI-authored)*

**GATE-FF-formin-force-velocity (candidate)** — FF engine.
- Observable: FF single mDia1-capped filament barbed-end elongation rate vs applied tension f, at fixed G-actin, no profilin.
- Band/value: monotonic **acceleration** with tension; concretely **3 µM ADP-G-actin: 3.4 → 10.9 subs/s (ratio ~3.2×, tol ±25%) for f: 0.13 → ~4 pN** (item 8). Cross-check curve shape against Eq. 1 with d=5.5 nm.
- Rationale for band: direct single-filament measurement at OUR scale (one filament = FF native object); ratio is robust across the paper's replicates; overlay-only, never fit.
- Source: Fig. 3B/6B, pg 8-10.

**GATE-FF-formin-open-state-saturation (candidate)** — FF/oracle.
- Observable: fraction of time FH2 is polymerization-competent (open-state probability) vs tension.
- Value: **p_open → ~1 above ~3 pN** (item 7); equivalently elongation-rate-vs-force curve **saturates by ~3 pN** (instantaneous 38.7 nm/s at 3.0 pN ≈ 39.8 at 4.1 pN, item 13).
- Rationale: a clean saturation landmark; testable as an emergent property of a Bell-gated formin kernel.
- Source: Fig. 3E,F + Fig. S2, pg 6-7, 18.

**GATE-FF-formin-profilin-force-switch (candidate, lower priority)** — FF engine.
- Observable: net elongation polarity (sign) under excess profilin, single vs high tension.
- Value: **net −1.8 subs/s (depolymerizing) → +40.2 subs/s (elongating)** at 3 µM ADP-GA + 3 µM profilin, low→high tension (item 11) — an all-or-none force switch.
- Rationale: strong mechanistic emergent target IF FF implements a profilin biphasic gate; band is a *sign reversal* (robust), magnitude tol wide (±30%). Flag: requires profilin module — likely Phase-2/not-yet-built; stage as future contract.
- Source: Fig. 5B, pg 8.

All three are FF single-filament observables (native scale = one filament), defensible as contracts. Do NOT invent an absolute-magnitude band beyond the paper's measured values; keep tolerances wide and mark in-vitro/26 °C/fragment.

## Oracle candidates

**ORACLE-formin-force-velocity (candidate, analytic).** Eq. 1 is a closed-form steady-state law:

```
v_elong(f, C_A) = kon·C_A·[1 − p_c⁰·exp(−f·d / k_B·T)] − koff·p_c⁰·exp(−f·d / k_B·T)
```

with d = 5.5 nm, T = 299 K. This is a strong analytic cross-check candidate — same status class as the existing **Hill force-velocity** and **Bell-Evans** oracles, but for **processive-formin barbed-end elongation** (a gap in the oracle set). Implementable as a small pure-function oracle (`ffn_sim/validation/oracles/actin/formin_force_velocity.py` + a `configs/` YAML carrying kon/koff/p_c⁰/d per nucleotide). Predicts both (a) elongation-vs-force curves and (b) the tension-induced critical-concentration drop (C_A* where v=0 shifts with f). Note: it is a fitted-then-validated law (globally fit to low+high-tension data, then *predicted* the force curves), so treat as a mechanistic cross-check oracle, not ground truth — consistent with "oracles are cross-checks, not truth."

Secondary: **F = 6πηrv** (Stokes drag, trap calibration) and the Boltzmann/Bell factor `exp(−fd/kT)` are already generic; only the formin-specific parameterization is new.
