# FF lens — p53

**Paper:** Costache et al. 2022, "Rapid assembly of a polar network architecture drives efficient actomyosin contractility," Cell Reports 39(9):110868. DOI 10.1016/j.celrep.2022.110868.
**Why this paper matters for FF:** the modeling core (Fig 6 / Fig S7 / Table S4) is the **Taeyoon Kim agent-based Brownian-dynamics actomyosin engine** — a Cytosim-class fine-grained filament/motor/crosslinker simulator that is the *direct sibling* of the FF engine, published with a **complete, self-consistent parameter table** and two mechanistic results FF can reproduce: (1) a formin-built **barbed-end-out polar network ("actin aster")** transmits myosin force farther and avoids collapse into disconnected aggregates; (2) network polarity, not just density, sets contractile efficiency. It also supplies formin barbed-end elongation kinetics and a filament-length law FF's growth kernel can adopt. Regime caveat throughout: *C. elegans* embryonic cortex + in-vitro-anchored constants; we adapt, not copy, to the MCF7 native cortex.

## 1. MECHANISMS to adopt

### M1 — Formin barbed-end elongation as a *processive tip-tracking ratchet with a finite, depletable barbed-end pool* (FF module: `formin` / `barbed_end_growth_kernel`)
Current FF formin is a "processive barbed-end elongation ratchet" (KB-3.6) with a fixed v0. This paper adds two mechanistic upgrades that beat the current lumped picture:
- **(a) Free barbed ends are the limiting, rapidly-depleted resource; formins are in excess.** During a contraction pulse the barbed-end pool half-life is ~3 s (`t½ = ln2/(k_on·[F])`, k_on=0.029 nM⁻¹s⁻¹, [F]≈10 nM). This makes elongation *self-limiting* and produces a "first-come-first-served" kinetic sequence (elongating formins engage ~3.3 s **before** the bulk recruited pool saturates). FF currently has no barbed-end accounting — growth is per-filament unconditional. Adopting a shared, finite barbed-end pool per activation zone makes filament elongation a *competitive* process (mechanistic, emergent length ceiling) rather than an imposed rate.
- **(b) Filament length is set by a race between two exponential clocks** — formin detachment vs. anchoring-monomer turnover — giving an emergent exponential length distribution centered at ~6 µm (see force law L2). This supersedes FF's *imposed* exponential(1–10 µm) length draw with a **mechanistically generated** distribution: length falls out of v_elong, formin k_off, and actin turnover, not a config parameter. Directly satisfies the "no magic number" rule — the current exponential draw is a phenomenological input, this makes it derivable.

### M2 — Network POLARITY (barbed-ends-out aster) as a first-class force-transmission mechanism (FF module: `filament` architecture / cortex + `myosin` placement)
Key result (Fig 6): a **barbed-end-out** radial arrangement transmits myosin-generated tension farther across the network and prevents collapse into disconnected aggregates; the **inverted** (pointed-ends-out) arrangement gives *higher local actin/motor contraction* but *lower summed filament tension* (0.76 → 0.37 nN when inverted) because myosin polarity-sorts to the center instead of pulling the network. **Implication for FF:** cortical-tension γ generation and network-scale stress transmission depend on *filament polarity orientation*, not only density/crosslink/motor count. FF currently seeds cortex filaments with (I believe) unbiased/random polarity. Adding a **polarity field** (barbed-end orientation) as an initial condition and an observable is a genuinely new mechanistic lever for the open γ-floor and stress-relaxation problems — polar-aster geometry may raise transmitted tension without adding motor force.

### M3 — Pulsed activation protocol (RhoA excitable input) driving co-localized formin+myosin bursts (FF module: scenario driver / `myosin` activation gating)
The engine implements RhoA as **spatially localized, temporally pulsed** activation: subdomain-local barbed-end polymerization enhancement (ρf=10 for τf=10 s) followed, with a **delay d_M=5 s**, by myosin activation for τM=15 s; minifilaments self-assemble → contract → disassemble; pulse period 30 s. This is a concrete, mechanistic protocol for driving FF's cortex out of steady state to *measure* contraction, flow, and tension generation — superior to a spatially uniform "myosin on" switch because it reproduces the real cortex's transient excitable dynamics and lets buckling/condensation symmetry-breaking emerge locally.

### M4 — Motor minifilament as an *arm-cluster* (Na arms × Nh heads) with parallel-cluster-model kinetics (FF module: `myosin`)
The Kim motor is a bipolar backbone with **Na=8 arms, each Nh=4 heads (32 heads/minifilament)**; each arm binds F-actin at a constant rate and walks toward the barbed end or detaches at **force-dependent rates from the Parallel Cluster Model (Erdmann & Schwarz 2012; Erdmann 2013)**. This is consistent with FF's Stam–Hocky multi-head bipolar minifilament + Hill force-velocity, and offers a cross-check/alternative kinetic scheme (PCM) for the head ensemble. Adopt the **structural stoichiometry (8 arms × 4 heads)** and backbone/arm stiffness split (below) if not already matched; PCM is a validation cross-check for our Hill law, not a replacement (mechanistic parity check).

## 2. FORCE LAWS / KINETIC SCHEMES to port

### L1 — ACP (crosslinker) force-dependent unbinding, Bell's law (Bell 1978), FF module `crosslinker` (α-actinin/filamin off-rate)
```
k_u(F) = k_u0 · exp(F · x_u / (k_B T))
k_u0 = 0.115 s⁻¹        (zero-force ACP unbinding rate; Ferrer 2008)
x_u  = 1.04×10⁻¹⁰ m     (Bell length / force sensitivity; Ferrer 2008)
k_B T = 4.142×10⁻²¹ J   (T ≈ 300 K)
```
Symbol map to FF: `F` = instantaneous tensile force on the crosslink bond (Warp bond force); `k_u0` = `k_off0`; `x_u/(k_B T)` = FF's `1/F_b` (so F_b = k_B T / x_u = 4.142e-21 / 1.04e-10 = **3.98×10⁻¹¹ N ≈ 40 pN**). **This is the SAME Ferrer 2008 anchor already in the FF/DCM KB** (the α-actinin k_off0 re-anchoring note in CLAUDE.md cites Ferrer 2008; the KB records α-actinin k_off0 = 0.066 s⁻¹). ⚠️ **Reconcile:** this paper uses 0.115 s⁻¹, the KB uses 0.066 s⁻¹ (re-anchored 2026-06-02 audit). Do NOT overwrite the KB value — flag the discrepancy to PI (both cite Ferrer 2008; likely different bond-condition/temperature or a different reported constant). Use the KB's 0.066 s⁻¹ as authoritative for FF runtime; record 0.115 s⁻¹ as this paper's usage.

### L2 — Emergent filament-length law (competition of two exponential clocks), FF module `barbed_end_growth_kernel` + turnover
```
L_filament = V_elongation × τ_filament
τ_filament = 1 / (1/τ_actin + 1/τ_formin)         (min of two independent exponentials)
V_elongation = formin tip speed (see P-table)
1/τ_actin  = actin-monomer turnover rate = 0.05–0.15 s⁻¹
1/τ_formin = formin off-rate = 0.11 s⁻¹ (bulk, smPReSS)
→ ⟨L⟩ ≈ 6 µm (2-cell C. elegans cortex)
```
Symbol map: `V_elongation` = FF barbed-end growth velocity v0; `τ_formin` = 1/k_off(formin); `τ_actin` = 1/(cortex actin turnover). Port as: filament stops growing when EITHER the formin detaches (Poisson at k_off_formin) OR the anchoring monomer turns over (Poisson at k_turnover) — whichever fires first. The exponential length distribution then **emerges** instead of being drawn.

### L3 — Formin orientation persistence (barbed-end trajectory as persistent random walk), FF module: filament tip trajectory / polarity seeding
```
θ_{n+1} = θ_n + X · arccos( exp( −R / (2·P_L) ) ),   X ~ N(0,1)
R   = step length = 1.23 ± 0.30 µm
P_L = persistence length = 15 µm  (≈ actin ℓ_p; Howard 2001)
```
This reproduces the observed outward (radial) polarity **from kinetics alone**. Symbol map: `P_L` ↔ FF actin persistence length ℓ_p (FF uses ℓ_p≈17 µm; this paper 15 µm — consistent within lit spread). Use to seed a **polar barbed-end orientation field** in a pulse zone (M2) and to validate that FF's bending-beam κ produces the correct tip-path persistence.

### L4 — Barbed-end saturation self-consistency (bimolecular binding), scenario check
```
t½(barbed end) = ln2 / (k_on · [F])
k_on = 0.029 nM⁻¹ s⁻¹    (formin→barbed-end binding rate)
[F]  = 10 nM             (local formin concentration at pulse)
→ t½ ≈ 3 s
```
Use as a mechanistic check on FF's formin-nucleation/binding rate: if FF assigns formin binding, its effective barbed-end capture half-life should be O(seconds), not instantaneous.

### L5 — RhoA pulse waveform (scenario input), FF module: myosin/formin activation gating
```
RhoA(t) ∝ sin⁶(π t / 30)     (period 30 s, sharp pulses)
```
Drives per-subdomain activation timing (M3). Map to FF's activation-zone scheduler.

## 4. NETWORK ARCHITECTURE / PROTOCOL to reproduce (FF scenario)

**Scenario "P53-pulsed-cortex":**
- **Geometry:** thin cortex slab **20 µm × 20 µm × 0.1 µm**, **periodic BC in x,y**, repulsive z-walls (confinement). (For FF native validation, adapt onto the MCF7 spherical cortex surface manifold rather than a flat slab — the slab is the in-vitro-style test; the native run must stay on the full cortex.)
- **Composition:** actin C_A=200 µM, myosin:actin=0.08, ACP:actin=0.01; self-assembled thin network at t=0; initial ⟨L_f⟩≈1 µm, growing to ~6–10 µm in pulse zones.
- **Pulse driver:** 4×4 = 16 subdomains; every 30 s one random subdomain activates. In it: barbed-end polymerization ×ρf=10 for τf=10 s (filaments elongate ~10 µm); myosin activates after d_M=5 s for τM=15 s (minifilaments self-assemble → contract → disassemble).
- **Sweep parameter:** fast-elongating filament fraction 0% → 2% (the paper's primary knob).
- **Polarity conditions (the decisive experiment):** (i) barbed-ends-out (control, formin-built aster); (ii) pointed-ends-out (inverted). Compare force transmission.

This is a ready-made FF stress-generation / contraction scenario. It exercises exactly FF's open problems: γ generation, motor-driven flow, network contraction & stress transmission, buckling/condensation.

## 6. RISKS / caveats

- **Regime: purified/embryonic vs MCF7 cortex.** All constants are *C. elegans* embryonic cortex or in-vitro reconstitution. Species/isoform: formin is CYK-1 (diaphanous), not mDia1; myosin is NMY-2, not human NMIIA. Use kinetic *forms* and orders-of-magnitude; keep FF's MCF7-anchored values where they exist. Do not KB-register these as MCF7 parameters.
- **Viscosity mismatch (hard-rule critical):** paper µ=0.86 Pa·s is a thin-slab/medium value. FF's physiological baseline is **~65 Pa·s MCF7 cytoplasm** (CLAUDE.md HARD rule). **Do not adopt 0.86 Pa·s** — it would violate the physiological-baseline rule and give wrong dynamics. Keep 65 Pa·s.
- **Coarse-grain convention:** 140-nm actin segments and the cortex-slab geometry are Kim-model coarse-graining, comparable to FF's ratified ×40 mesoscale. Consistent, but be explicit that FF's native validation must be on the **full 38000-filament cortex** (`--cortex-fil 38000 --from-resting --microtubules`), not a 20-µm slab — the slab is a dev/reconstitution test only, findings must be reconfirmed native (2026-07-09 HARD rule).
- **2D-slab vs 3D spherical cortex:** the periodic-slab is planar; MCF7 cortex is a closed curved shell. Polarity ("barbed-ends-out") must be re-expressed as *outward-normal* vs *tangential* on the manifold. The "aster" is a local pulse feature; map it onto surface patches, not a global radial field.
- **ACP k_off0 discrepancy (0.115 vs KB 0.066, both Ferrer 2008):** do not silently overwrite the KB. Surface to PI; likely different reported constant/condition.
- **Formin v0 concentration dependence:** 1.1–1.3 µm/s is at high physiological profilin-actin; the same formin is 20× slower at 1.5 µM actin. Any v0 change must be set at the physiological actin concentration and justified, not tuned to pass a gate (no-fitting rule).
- **PCM vs Hill:** the Kim motor uses the Parallel Cluster Model, FF uses Hill (1938) F-V. These are alternative kinetic schemes; use PCM as a cross-check oracle, not a runtime replacement (mechanistic-fidelity rule prefers the model we've committed to unless PCM is demonstrably finer-grained per-head).
- **Contraction/force numbers are model outputs, not measurements.** Fig 6B/C/E/F/G are the Kim *simulation's* results (an oracle sibling), not experimental data. Treat them as an inter-engine parity target (FF vs Kim engine), NOT as ground-truth experimental validation — the experimental validations are the kinetics (v0, k_off, length, delays, flow).
