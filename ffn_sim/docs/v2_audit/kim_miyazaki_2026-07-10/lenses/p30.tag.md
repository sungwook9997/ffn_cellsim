# TAG-validation lens — p30

**Paper:** Sakamoto, Miyazaki & Maeda, *State transitions of a confined actomyosin system controlled through contractility and polymerization rate*, Phys. Rev. Research **5**, 013208 (2023). DOI 10.1103/PhysRevResearch.5.013208.

## Paper kind

**Mixed experimental + simulation** → classified **experimental** for the primary
validation-grade numbers, with an explicit carve-out: the paper's model outputs are
**simulation-only** and may be used for *comparison, never as a truth source*.

- The **measured** observables (wave-onset CalA threshold, wave period T≈1.5 min, PIV
  inward-flow speed ~0.7 µm/s, perturbation-map 1/T boxplots) are in-vitro reconstitution
  data → **overlay / cross-check targets, never fit** (project hard rule).
- The **Pe thresholds** (flow at Pe=38, waves at Pe=115), the αp<0.5 wave criterion, the
  ksurf_p reentrant-flow phase diagram, and the rotational-wave boundary are outputs of
  the authors' continuum active-fluid + phase-field PDE model → **model comparison only**;
  they are dimensionless and model-specific, so they are NOT registrable constants and
  NOT oracle ground truth.
- The constitutive laws (σ_act=(ζµ)₀·ρ/(ρ₀+ρ), Newtonian σ_vis, friction −γv, volume-
  conserving phase field) are the authors' modeling *choices*, not closed-form ground
  truth → not oracle candidates.

Critical scale/system caveat that governs everything below: the data are from **M-phase
Xenopus laevis egg-extract cytoplasm encapsulated in 200–300 µm water-in-oil droplets**
(quasi-2D, h=60 µm, 20 °C). This is a **cell-sized-confinement reconstitution**, not an
MCF-7 single cell. Length scale (200–300 µm droplet), protein source (frog egg extract),
and pattern class (bulk inward flow / ring waves at the droplet's midplane) are all far
from our MCF-7 single-cell operating point. Transferability to OUR gates is therefore
**weak**; anything proposed below is a candidate for a *future confined-cortex / DCM
active-fluid* module and is explicitly PI-gate-review, overlay-only, low priority.

## Coverage vs existing gates

Nothing in the current KB covers actomyosin **pattern selection** (steady inward flow vs
periodic ring waves vs rotational waves), a cortical **flow speed**, or an **actin-wave
period**. The nearest existing rows and why they do NOT overlap:

- **VG-H3-KU35-cortex-tension / VG-H7-gate-a/b** (cortical tension γ_total, band
  0.35–0.65 mN/m): concern *steady-state tension magnitude*, a scalar. p30 gives no
  cortical-tension value (γ is left literal). No confirm/tighten/challenge.
- **VG-U3-cortex-tension-spreading** (spreading vs γ): different observable (area), and
  p30 is a non-adherent confined droplet with no substrate. No overlap.
- **VG-U3-cortex-multifilament / MC-U3-cell-integration**: our cortex is filament-resolved;
  p30 is coarse-grained continuum, so it cannot be a parameter source for FF, only a
  qualitative emergent-pattern benchmark.
- **Young-Laplace / Helfrich / Bell-Evans / Hill oracles**: p30 introduces no closed-form
  law of that ground-truth character (its transition thresholds are numerical model
  outputs). No oracle overlap.

**Verdict: no existing gate is confirmed, tightened, or challenged.** All p30 content is
*new observable class*, but at a **different system and 20–40× larger length scale** than
our MCF-7 single cell, so it enters only as candidate/overlay, PI-gated.

## New gate candidates

All flagged **candidate — PI-gate review, OVERLAY-ONLY, low priority**. These are only
actionable if/when a DCM *confined-active-cortex / cytoplasmic-flow-pattern* module is
built; they are not contracts for the current MCF-7 single-cell stack. Bands are quoted
as the paper measured them — NOT invented to be convenient, and NOT to be fit.

1. **proposed_vg_id = VG-DCM-actomyosin-pattern-selection** (candidate)
   - Observable: qualitative self-organized pattern (steady inward flow ↔ periodic ring
     wave) selected by contractility vs polymerization rate.
   - Expected: **steady flow at low contractility; periodic waves emerge above the
     contractility threshold**, with the two independent knobs being myosin contractility
     σ_act and F-actin polymerization rate kp (waves = upper-left, flow = lower-right of
     the (σ_act, kp) plane). Engine: **DCM** (active-fluid cortex), comparison to
     simulation Pe=38 (flow) / Pe=115 (waves) as a *dimensionless model cross-check only*.
   - Source: Fig.1–3; "increasing contractility drives steady-flow → periodic-wave
     transition… the two control axes are σ_act and kp" (§I.A–I.C, pp2–6).
   - Caveat: pattern class, not a scalar band; Xenopus-extract droplet, not MCF-7.

2. **proposed_vg_id = VG-DCM-actin-wave-period** (candidate, overlay)
   - Observable: dominant period of the ring-shaped actin wave (Fourier of angle-averaged
     midplane intensity).
   - Value/band: **T ≈ 1.5 min** (≈90 s), 1/T ~0.8–1.0 min⁻¹ at high CalA; period is set
     by polymerization kinetics, not contractility. Engine: **DCM**.
   - Source: "T≈1.5 min… period set by polymerization" (§I.A p2; Fig.1h).
   - Caveat: droplet 200–300 µm; wave period is expected to scale with system size and
     polymerization/turnover rate, so the *value* is not portable to a ~7.5 µm MCF-7
     cortex — treat as **cross-check on the physics (period ∝ 1/kp, ⊥ to σ)**, not the
     number.

3. **proposed_vg_id = VG-DCM-cortical-flow-speed** (candidate, overlay, weakest)
   - Observable: steady inward actomyosin flow speed (PIV) in a confined contractile gel.
   - Value: **~0.7 µm s⁻¹** (Fig.1c scale bar), droplet-scale bulk flow.
   - Caveat: this is a **read-off from a scale bar**, not a reported measurement with error,
     and it is droplet-scale (200–300 µm) bulk flow — an order of magnitude above our
     MCF-7 cortical/retrograde flow (~28–45 nm/s in the FF crawl work). **Do not adopt as
     a single-cell band.** Register at most as a droplet-scale sanity anchor. Recommend
     **not** promoting.

## Param candidates

**None registrable.** Every dimensional physical constant (η, η_b, γ, D, (ζµ)₀, ρ₀,
V_tar) is left as a literal symbol; the numeric values in the paper are either
dimensionless model knobs (Pe, ksurf_p, αp, ε_ρ, α₀, Dφ, Γφ, β, λ=1/3), numerical grid
settings (Δx=0.1, Δt=1e-5), or reagent concentrations for the extract assay (CalA,
nocodazole 100 µM, TMR-Lifeact 1 µM, CK666 1 mM, eggPC 1 mM, VVCA MW 39,500 Da) — none of
which map to a physical constant in our Parameter table. **No new Parameter rows, no
supersessions, no conflicts** with existing PARAM-* rows.

- λ = 1 + η_b/η = 1/3 (⇒ η_b/η = −2/3) is a modeling assumption, not a measured
  material property; do not register.

## Oracle candidates

**None.** The paper contributes no ground-truth closed-form of the Helfrich / Young-Laplace
/ Bell-Evans / Hill class. Its constitutive relations are modeling choices:
- σ_act = (ζµ)₀·ρ/(ρ₀+ρ) — a chosen Michaelis-Menten saturation, not a universal law.
- Pe = (ζµ)₀/(Dγ) — a definition of the control parameter, not a predictive oracle.
- Flow/wave/rotation transition thresholds — numerical outputs of *their* PDE solver
  (finite-difference + spectral), inherently model- and discretization-dependent.

The **framing** (a single dimensionless contractility Péclet number controlling
flow↔wave, and the phase-field volume-conserving droplet paralleling DCM's
enclosed-volume/turgor treatment) is architecturally useful to DCM as *design guidance*,
but it is a comparison model, not an analytic cross-check to implement.
