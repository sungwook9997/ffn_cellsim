# FF lens — p02

**Paper:** Kim, Hwang & Kamm (2011), *Dynamic Role of Cross-Linking Proteins in Actin Rheology*, Biophys. J. 101(7):1597–1603. DOI 10.1016/j.bpj.2011.08.033.

**One line for FF:** This is the direct methodological ancestor of the FF Cytosim-style crosslinker layer (Taeyoon Kim lineage — the same BD agent line the FF engine's ACP kinetics descend from). It hands us a *complete, literature-anchored, fine-grained* cross-linker force + kinetics model — Bell-Evans unbinding **and** multi-step domain unfolding on a WLC arm — plus the decisive mechanistic verdict: **at physiological strain rates (γ̇_eff ≈ 0.1 s⁻¹) crosslinker UNBINDING, not unfolding, governs network rheology** (strain-stiffening, stress relaxation, plasticity). That answers a live FF design question — *does FF need to model α-actinin/filamin domain unfolding?* — with **no, not for the interphase cortex; the Bell slip-bond off-rate is the right and sufficient mechanism.**

---

## 1. MECHANISMS to adopt

### M1 — Crosslinker force-dependent UNBINDING is the rheology driver (keep the Bell-Evans off-rate; it is the mechanism, unfolding is secondary)
FF already has Bell-Evans off-rate on α-actinin/filamin bonds (`k_off = k_off0·exp(F/F_b)`). This paper **validates that choice as the load-bearing mechanism** and quantifies it: across strain-stiffening, stress relaxation, and plastic deformation, unbinding (not unfolding, not the actin filaments themselves) sets the emergent constitutive response. Concretely — unbinding is what caps stress at experimental ~10–100 Pa, produces stress relaxation to a finite plateau, and produces irreversible plastic reorganization. **Why it beats current FF:** it tells us the crosslinker off-rate is not a nicety but *the* rate-limiter for cortex viscoelasticity / stress relaxation — one of FF's named open problems ("network contraction & stress relaxation", "cortical-tension γ generation"). FF's cortex stress-relaxation timescale is currently emergent-but-unvalidated; this paper gives the mechanism + numbers to check it against.

### M2 — Rebinding sets the finite stress plateau (unbinding WITHOUT rebinding relaxes to ~0; WITH rebinding → finite ~1 Pa plateau)
Current FF crosslinker turnover: bonds break (Bell), and new bonds form by a binding rate. This paper shows the **balance** matters mechanistically: rebinding "dampens full relaxation of stress" and is what makes the relaxed state resemble real filamin networks (Tseng 2004). **Adopt:** ensure FF's crosslinker *rebinding* channel is on at physiological rate in the cortex baseline (not off-by-default) — otherwise the cortex will over-relax (stress → 0), which is unphysical. This is a direct instance of the physiological-baseline HARD rule: the rebinding channel must be ON at its real rate from t=0.

### M3 — Load-bearing "supportive framework" reorganization (emergent, ~25% of the network bears ~85% of the high-force ruptures)
Highly loaded crosslinkers (>20 pN) unbind predominantly from a small **supportive framework** (25% of the network carrying most load) and rebind to a **different** filament after a diagonal displacement (turnover→1 above 20 pN). This is an *emergent* stress-relief cascade, not a lumped rule. **Why it matters for FF:** it is exactly the kind of emergent symmetry-breaking / network-reorganization FF should reproduce natively (FF's "buckling/condensation symmetry-breaking" and "confinement-driven self-organization" open problems). It also gives a concrete falsifiable FF observable: measure the fraction of crosslinkers carrying most of the load and the >20 pN rebind-to-different-filament turnover — FF should reproduce ~25%/~85% without tuning.

### M4 — Domain unfolding is a SECONDARY channel FF can defer (or add as an optional catch-like buffer), NOT the primary rheology mechanism
The paper models each crosslinker arm as a **series WLC that can unfold up to N_uf times** (filamin has many Ig domains; saw-tooth 100–200 pN at ~30 nm intervals). Verdict: unfolding *only delays* stress rise; it never changes topology, so it cannot relax stress or produce plasticity. **FF decision:** for the interphase MCF7 cortex at γ̇_eff ≈ 0.1 s⁻¹, **do NOT invest in multi-step unfolding** — the Bell unbinding off-rate is sufficient and correct. Keep unfolding as an *optional* module only if FF later probes high-strain-rate transients (γ̇_eff > 1 s⁻¹) where unfolding starts to dominate (Fig. 3A crossover). This is a fidelity-vs-cost call the HARD "mechanistic over lumped" rule permits: the paper *proves* unfolding is negligible in our regime, so omitting it is not a lumping shortcut, it is a validated scale separation.

### M5 — First-order irreversible bond formation during polymerization + orthogonal V-shaped crosslinks (network-generation protocol)
The network is grown by BD with **first-order irreversible bond formation**, ACPs are **filamin-like V-shaped forming orthogonal cross-links** during polymerization, then coarse-grained. FF's cortex builder can adopt this generation protocol as a validated way to seed a mechanically-representative crosslinked network geometry (relevant to FF's "confinement-driven self-organization" and to reproducible network builds).

## 2. FORCE LAWS / KINETIC SCHEMES to port

### Eq. (1) — Crosslinker-arm force–extension (WLC extended + linear compression)
Per arm, current length `r₁₂`, equilibrium `r₀`:
```
if r₁₂ ≥ r₀ (extension):
  F_s = -(k_B T / p) · [ (2·l₀,i − r₁₂ − r₀)(r₁₂ − r₀) / (4·l₀,i²·(1 − r₁₂/l₀,i)²·(1 − r₀/l₀,i)²)  +  (r₁₂ − r₀)/l₀,i ]
if r₁₂ < r₀ (compression):
  F_s = -κ_s,ACP · (r₁₂ − r₀)
```
Marko–Siggia-type WLC restoring force; `p` = arm persistence length, `l₀,i` = contour length of the i-th unfolded state.
**Map to FF:** `r₁₂` = distance between the two FF crosslinker-arm endpoints (particle positions on the two bonded filament segments); `r₀` = crosslinker rest length; `κ_s,ACP` = compressive link stiffness. If FF keeps unfolding OFF (M4), set `l₀,i = l₀,0` constant (single WLC arm, no saw-tooth) — this is the minimal port. If FF's crosslinker is currently a simple harmonic bond, **upgrade the extension side to this WLC form** for large-strain fidelity (harmonic over-stiffens near contour length).

### Eq. (3) — Bell-Evans UNBINDING off-rate (this is the core FF port)
```
k_ub = k⁰_ub · exp(λ_ub·|F_s| / (k_B T))   if r₁₂ ≥ r₀   (loaded)
k_ub = k⁰_ub                                if r₁₂ < r₀   (compressed → force-free rate)
```
**Map to FF:** identical in form to FF's existing `k_off = k_off0·exp(F/F_b)`. Equivalence: `k⁰_ub ≡ k_off0`, and `F_b ≡ k_B T / λ_ub` (Bell force scale). With `λ_ub = 1.04×10⁻¹⁰ m` and `k_B T = 4.11×10⁻²¹ J` (300 K): **`F_b = k_B T / λ_ub ≈ 39.5 pN`.** Key modeling detail to copy: **when the arm is compressed (`r₁₂ < r₀`), the rate reverts to the force-free `k⁰_ub`** — no acceleration under compression (slip bond only responds to tension). FF should gate the exponential on tensile load, not on |force| indiscriminately.

### Eq. (2) — Bell-Evans UNFOLDING rate (OPTIONAL, only if M4 unfolding module is built)
```
k_uf = k⁰_uf · exp(λ_uf·|F_s| / (k_B T))   if r₁₂ ≥ r₀
k_uf = k⁰_uf                                if r₁₂ < r₀
```
Each unfolding increments `i → i+1`, raising max extension `l₀,i → l₀,i + 30 nm`; refolding decrements `i` when `r₁₂` drops below the recorded `ruf,i`. Relaxation/extension detection window = 10 BD steps (=61.7 ns); ≥8 of 10 shrinking ⇒ relaxation. **Only port if FF probes high-strain-rate transients.**

### Eq. (5) — Crosslinker cross-link count decay under constant strain (validation kinetic)
```
N_ACP(t) = N⁰_ACP · exp(−2·k⁰_ub·t)
```
Factor 2 = either of the two arms can unbind. Scheme `[actin-ACP] → (k⁰_ub) [actin] + [ACP]`. **FF use:** analytic acceptance oracle — hold an FF cortex patch at fixed strain (low stress, τ<5 Pa so force-effect negligible) and check the intact-crosslinker count decays as `exp(−2·k⁰_ub·t)`. Sanity-gate FF's crosslinker kinetics.

### Eqs. (6)–(7) — Stress-relaxation constitutive law (emergent, for validation)
```
dτ/dt = (a·τ − b)·dN_ACP/dt
τ(t) = b/a + (τ₀ − b/a)·exp[ a·N⁰_ACP·(exp(−2·k⁰_ub·t) − 1) ]
```
Fits: `a = 0.006`, `b = 0` (no rebinding → τ→0), `b = 1.8 Pa` (with rebinding → finite plateau ~1 Pa observed). **FF use:** target curve for FF cortex stress-relaxation validation. FF should reproduce the finite-plateau-with-rebinding vs relax-to-zero-without-rebinding dichotomy.

### Eqs. (9)–(10) — Plastic (irreversible) strain recovery after release
```
dγ/dt = c·exp(−11.5·γ)     ⇒     γ(t) = ln[ exp(11.5·γ₀) + 11.5·c·t ] / 11.5
```
`γ₀ = 1.0`; fits `c = 0.11, 0.015, 0.0005` for γ̇_eff = 10, 1, 0.1 s⁻¹; restoring force `F_r ~ exp(11.5·γ)`. **FF use:** shear an FF cortex patch to γ=1, release, and check residual plastic strain + the power exponent x (γ~t^x, x=0–0.4, ≈0.17 at γ̇_eff=0.1) emerges from crosslinker unbinding — a native-scale FF plasticity test.

## 4. NETWORK ARCHITECTURE / PROTOCOL to reproduce (FF scenarios)

**S1 — Bulk shear rheology in a box (the core reconstitution scenario).** Cubic domain, width 2.8 µm; bottom face fixed, top face sheared. Actin `C_A = 12.1 µM`, `L_f = 1.5 ± 0.65 µm`, filamin crosslinks at `R = 0.020`, orthogonal V-shaped, first-order irreversible formation. This is an FF *validation* scenario (purified network, not the cortex) — small enough to run native, and it maps FF's emergent τ(γ) to a measured constitutive law.
- **Protocol A (strain-stiffening):** constant shear-rate ramp, γ̇_eff = 0.1 s⁻¹ (physiological). Measure τ(γ) and K = dτ/dγ.
- **Protocol B (stress relaxation):** pre-shear to τ₀ = 5 Pa, hold γ constant 5 s. Measure τ(t) with rebinding ON vs OFF.
- **Protocol C (plastic deformation):** shear to γ = 1.0 at γ̇_eff = 0.1/1/10 s⁻¹, release top boundary free, measure residual γ(t).
- **Protocol D (oscillatory, hard):** G'(ω), G''(ω) at 10 Hz, prestress 10 Pa. NOTE the paper's caveat: the time-rescaling scaling is NOT valid for G'/G'' (thermal fluctuation/diffusion not accelerated), and stochastic-unbinding sampling limits it to 10–10³ Hz. FF, being GPU-native and able to run real (non-rescaled) kinetics, can potentially do this *better* than the 2011 model — a differentiator, not just a reproduction.

**Time-rescaling trick (methods lever, use with care):** to reach experimental (seconds) timescales from a ns BD step, they rescale zero-force rates `k⁰ → n·k⁰` and time `t → t/n`, holding the product `k⁰·t_d` fixed; runs with equal `k⁰·t_d` collapse to one universal curve (Fig. 2). γ̇_eff = γ̇/n. **FF caution:** FF runs native kinetics on GPU and generally should NOT need this rescale for the cortex (physiological-baseline rule wants real rates). Use it only as an analytic cross-check / to reach long-time asymptotes cheaply in a *validation* scenario, never in a production cortex run — and never for G'/G'' (see Protocol D caveat).

## 6. RISKS / caveats

**R1 — Isoform bookkeeping (filamin vs α-actinin). CRITICAL.** `k⁰_ub,r = 0.115 s⁻¹` and `r₀ = 105 nm`, `p = 0.33 nm` are **filamin-A** specific (long V-shaped, many Ig domains). FF's cortex crosslinker set includes α-actinin (`k_off0 = 0.066 s⁻¹`, shorter ~35 nm rod, from the same Ferrer 2008 paper). **Do not overwrite α-actinin params with filamin values.** Register filamin as a distinct crosslinker species with these params; keep α-actinin as-is. The genuinely portable, isoform-robust datum is the Bell compliance `λ_ub = 1.04×10⁻¹⁰ m` → force scale ≈ 39.5 pN.

**R2 — Purified in-vitro network ≠ living cortex.** The model is a reconstituted actin+filamin network with **no myosin motors, no membrane, no turgor** (explicitly listed as limitations). The rheology numbers (~1–100 Pa, K~γ³) are the *passive crosslinked-network* response. In the FF cortex, motors add prestress and *enhance* elasticity; the paper explicitly predicts the crosslinker-governed strain-stiffening / relaxation / plasticity behaviors "would appear even with contractile motors," with the difference that stress becomes *internal* (motor-generated) rather than external-shear. **Adapt:** use S1–S3 as *validation-scenario* targets on a purified FF network (motors off, membrane off) — this is a legitimate stripped configuration **because it is a validation cross-check against an in-vitro paper, not a production cortex run.** The native-scale/full-compartment HARD rule governs *cortex conclusions*; matching an in-vitro rheology paper legitimately reproduces the in-vitro (purified) geometry. Any conclusion about the *living MCF7 cortex* must still be reconfirmed on the native full-compartment cell with motors + membrane + turgor ON at physiological values.

**R3 — Coarse-graining + time-rescaling are BD-model crutches FF should mostly avoid.** The `k⁰·t_d` time-rescaling reaches seconds by inflating rate coefficients; it is invalid for G'/G'' and mixes real and accelerated dynamics. FF (GPU-native, real kinetics, differentiable) should run the true `k⁰_ub = 0.115 s⁻¹` rate directly wherever wall-time permits — this is a fidelity *upgrade* over the 2011 model, consistent with the "mechanistic over proxy" rule. Only borrow the rescale for cheap long-time analytic cross-checks in validation, never in production.

**R4 — Small domain / no thick bundles / limited frequency band.** The 2.8 µm box cannot represent bundles with mesh size >10 µm, and the frequency range (10–10³ Hz) barely overlaps bulk rheometers. FF's filopodium/bundle modules operate in exactly the bundled regime this paper cannot reach — so **do not extrapolate these orthogonal-crosslink rheology numbers to FF bundled structures.** Bundle rheology needs a bundle-specific source.

**R5 — WLC arm persistence p=0.33 nm is filamin-arm-specific, NOT an actin backbone datum.** Guard against the unit/semantic trap: actin filament ℓ_p ≈ 17 µm (FF bending), crosslinker-arm p = 0.33 nm (Eq. 1 restoring force of the linker). These are different physical quantities on different FF state variables (filament angular bending vs crosslinker bond force-extension). Keep them in separate config namespaces.

**R6 — Unfolding deferral is validated, but regime-bounded.** M4 (skip unfolding) is justified *only for γ̇_eff < 1 s⁻¹*. If FF later models fast mechanical transients (AFM indentation ramps, rapid Piezo-scale loading, shock), unfolding re-enters at γ̇_eff > 1 s⁻¹ and the multi-step Eq. 2 module (k⁰_uf,r = 3.0×10⁻⁵ s⁻¹, λ_uf = 6×10⁻¹⁰ m, 30 nm saw-tooth) must be built. Flag this as a conditional, not a permanent, omission.
