## Paper kind

**Mixed: experimental + simulation** (report as `experimental` for grading — its measurements are the validation-grade data; the modeling half is `simulation` and is COMPARISON-ONLY, never a truth source).

- **Experimental (in-vitro):** AFM cell modulus (Hertz), CLAN incidence vs substrate stiffness / enrichment, CLAN persistence kinetics, hydrogel stiffness. → overlay / cross-check targets, never fit.
- **Simulation (agent-based, Kim engine):** network-stiffness anisotropy (shear/tension/compression) and the nucleus-compression stress ladder with LINC coupling. These are *another model's outputs* — usable only as a same-family code cross-check / portable protocol, NOT as a truth band. No analytic closed form is offered.
- **NOT analytic:** no new closed-form law → no oracle candidate.

**Off-target caveat (dominant):** cell type is **GTM3L transformed glaucomatous trabecular-meshwork**, not MCF7. All numbers are TM-context. Nothing here is an MCF7 datum; treat as mechanistic/cross-species corroboration only.

## New gate candidates (all **candidate, PI-gate review** — most are simulation-cross-checks, weaker than analytic/lab)

1. **`VG-FF-actin-network-anisotropy` (candidate, soft cross-check)**
   - Observable: differential stiffness of a self-assembled cross-linked actin+ACP network under shear vs tensile vs compressive strain, virial boundary stress.
   - Value: **tension : shear : compression ≈ 2.4 : 1.0 : 0.5** (ordering + ~2–5× tension dominance), at \|ε\|=0.05, ε̇=0.001 s⁻¹, C_A=100 µM, R_ACP=0.1.
   - Engine: **FF**. 
   - Caveat: the target is *another simulation's output* (Kim), not analytic ground truth or lab — so this can only ever be a same-family code cross-check, and the absolute Pa values depend on κ_s/κ_b/κ_r which are NOT in this PDF (supplement/refs 33–36). Register the **ordering/ratio** as the defensible content, not the 1e4-Pa magnitudes. Do NOT elevate to a truth contract.
   - Source: Fig 6B, p8.

2. **`VG-DCM-linc-nucleus-stiffening` (candidate, mechanistic cross-check)**
   - Observable: increase in nucleus compressive resistance when a peri-nuclear actin network is physically coupled (LINC-like tethers, ~20% of filaments) vs uncoupled/absent.
   - Value: fold-change **× ~2.7** (linked) and **× ~1.4** (unlinked, no tether) relative to bare nucleus, under ε=−0.5 compression.
   - Engine: **DCM** (meshed nucleus with V + A conservation + node contact + actin tether) — directly our DCM nucleus idiom.
   - Caveat: simulation-derived fold-change, off-target cell; use as a qualitative "LINC coupling stiffens the nucleus 2–3×" cross-check for MC-H9-nucleus + LINC coupling, not a numeric contract band.
   - Source: Fig 6D, p8.

3. **AFM CLAN± cell-modulus contrast — do NOT propose as a gate.** GTM3L (~4 kPa CLAN+ / ~0.3 kPa CLAN−) is off-target cell type; keep as an overlay data point only if/when a TM line is ever modeled. No MCF7 relevance.

## Oracle candidates

**None.** No closed-form/analytic law is given. The stress readout is a virial-style boundary-reaction stress σ = ΣF_component / A_boundary — the same method our existing rheology gates already use, not a new analytic oracle.

## Duplicates / notes
