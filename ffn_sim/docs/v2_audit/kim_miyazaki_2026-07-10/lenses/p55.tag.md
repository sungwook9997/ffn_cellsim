# TAG-validation lens — p55

**Paper:** Oyama, Arai, Isaka et al. (Ishiwata/Miyazaki labs), *Directional Bleb Formation in Spherical Cells under Temperature Gradient*, Biophys J 109(2):355–364 (2015). DOI 10.1016/j.bpj.2015.06.016.

## Paper kind

**experimental** (live single-cell biophysics — local IR-laser thermal perturbation + fluorescence thermometry + pharmacology on spherical HeLa cells). No agent-based/continuum simulation, no integrator/timestep, no fitted force laws or rate constants. The only closed forms are apparatus calibrations (dye fluorescence-vs-T quadratics) and a steady-state heat-conduction fit (ln-law for a cylindrical source) — these are thermometry, **not cell-mechanics laws**.

Consequence for use: all cell-mechanics numbers are **overlay / cross-check targets, never fit targets**. The physical driver (an intracellular temperature gradient) is **not in the DCM/FF parameter set**, so the paper informs *phenomenology and magnitudes* of blebbing, not new force constants. To use it, DCM would impose the observed cortical asymmetry directly (a spatially varying γ field and/or a local cortex-rupture event) and check whether the *emergent* bleb geometry matches.

## Coverage vs existing gates

- **No existing gate covers blebbing or cortex–membrane detachment.** The KB gates are cortex *tension magnitude* (VG-H3-KU35, VG-H7-gate-a/b — all `failing`/`blocked` on the γ-floor), membrane tether tension (VG-H8, not-started), ECM, FA, junction, necrosis. Blebbing (loss of cortex–membrane cohesion under a local tension/pressure imbalance) is an **uncovered observable** that sits squarely in DCM's core physics (membrane + cortex γ + turgor ΔP + node-face contact already modelled).
- **Related, not covered:** the paper's governing qualitative law — cell sphericity set by inward cortical tension vs outward hydrostatic pressure, a *local* imbalance → immediate bleb — is exactly the **Young-Laplace balance (γ·2/R vs ΔP)** the DCM already encodes and the KB already has as an oracle (`young_dupre.py` / surface-tension bridge). The paper **CONFIRMS** the qualitative force balance; it neither tightens nor challenges any numeric band (it reports no γ or ΔP value).
- **PARAM-gamma_cortex** already carries a note "Blebbistatin test variant = 10× drop." Row 10/13 here (blebbistatin abolishes then blue-light restores blebbing) **qualitatively confirms** that myosin-II sets the active cortical tension that this parameter represents — no numeric change.

## New gate candidates (all *candidate, PI-gate review* — staged, not authored)

1. **VG-DCM-directional-bleb-extension** *(candidate)*
   - Observable: single directional bleb extension length on a rounded cell (R≈10 µm) when a cortical-tension asymmetry is imposed (elevated γ and/or a local cortex-rupture patch on one hemisphere).
   - Band/value (OVERLAY-ONLY): extension **7–8 µm** for a strong asymmetry (anchor 8.1 ± 3.0 µm rounded interphase, row 1; 7.5 ± 5.3 metaphase); expect **sub-µm** for a flat/adherent cell (row 7, 0.5 µm) — sphericity is a precondition.
   - Engine: **DCM**. Source: Fig. S3B, p357/SI-7.
   - Caveat: temperature is not modelled; the asymmetry is imposed, so this validates the *mechanical response magnitude*, not the thermosensing. Do not fit; overlay only.

2. **VG-DCM-bleb-directionality** *(candidate)*
   - Observable: angle between the emergent bleb axis and the imposed cortical-asymmetry axis.
   - Band: **≤ ~10°** (anchor 8.4 ± 5.2°, row 4) — i.e. the bleb is strongly co-axial with the weakest-cortex direction.
   - Engine: DCM. Source: Fig. S2B/SI-6. Clean geometric contract, robust to the temperature caveat (pure directionality).

3. **VG-FF-cortex-symmetry-break** *(candidate, FF)*
   - Observable: sign of cortex F-actin / myosin density change under an imposed local contractility/severing perturbation.
   - Value (qualitative sign contract): density **decreases** at the high-activity ("warm") pole and **accumulates** at the opposite pole, moving in-phase with the membrane, preceding detachment/rupture (rows 8–9).
   - Engine: **FF** (native actomyosin cortex + myosin minifilaments + Bell-Evans crosslinkers; myosin-II as network-disassembly agent, refs 41–44). Source: Fig. 3B, 4B–E.
   - Note: a *sign/mechanism* gate, not a numeric band — flag for PI on whether a directional density-asymmetry sign qualifies as a contract.

*(Not proposed as gates: the 1.3 °C threshold and ~3 s onset latency — the first is a thermal quantity outside the model; the second is drag-limited/timescale-fragile in our engines per the standing "no rate/timescale citation from mechanical end-states" caution.)*

## Param candidates

**None registrable.** The paper reports no mechanical moduli, off-rates, motor stall forces, γ or ΔP values, or persistence lengths. Its numeric table is drug concentrations (LatB 500 nM, CytoD 10 µM, blebbistatin 100 µM, Y-27632 10 µM, ML-7 20–50 µM, jasplakinolide 50 nM, nocodazole 10 µM), dye concentrations, and laser/thermometry apparatus constants — none are `ffn_cellsim` runtime parameters. The blebbistatin/Y-27632 suppression **supports** the existing PARAM-gamma_cortex blebbistatin-variant note (myosin-II & ROCK set active γ) but adds no number.

## Oracle candidates

**None new.** The relevant closed form — the Young-Laplace cortical-tension-vs-turgor balance (γ·2/R vs ΔP) that governs bleb nucleation — is already an implemented oracle (`young_dupre.py`, surface-tension bridge). The paper's own equations are thermometry calibrations (dye quadratics) and a cylindrical steady-state heat-conduction ln-law `T(x₁)=T(x₂) − Q/(2πlκ)·ln(x₁/x₂)` — a heat-transport oracle, **not cell mechanics**, so out of scope for this KB. (It also explicitly *rejected* the spherical 1/x form for the source geometry.)
