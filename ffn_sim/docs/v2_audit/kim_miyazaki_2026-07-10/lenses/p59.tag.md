## Paper kind

**experimental** (bottom-up in-vitro reconstitution: purified rabbit skeletal-muscle actin + myosin/α-actinin encapsulated in cell-sized water-in-oil droplets; fluorescence microscopy + bulk biochemistry). No simulation, no integrator. There is **no computational model output** here.

Consequences for use under project philosophy:
- All measured numbers are **OVERLAY / on-demand cross-check** targets — never fit targets, and this is a *reconstituted purified-protein* system (10 µM rabbit actin, 25 °C), **not** an MCF7 or any cell, so it is a mechanistic-benchmark cross-check for the **FF** engine (emergent actomyosin self-organization), one physical scale removed from our single-cell production target.
- The paper also states two **closed-form scaling relations** (effective-concentration duty-ratio law; constant-volume contraction invariant; elastic-rod confinement criterion) that qualify as **candidate analytic oracles / conservation cross-checks** — these are the highest-value items here under our "oracles/mechanistic-analytic first" rule.

## New gate candidates (all **candidate, PI-gate review**; primary engine = FF emergent, DCM-division secondary)

These are proposed as *mechanistic emergent benchmarks* for the FF actin/myosin/crosslinker engine (Cytosim-style bending beams + duty-ratio myosin + Bell-Evans crosslinkers), reproducing this reconstitution as an out-of-cell cross-check. Bands are the paper's measured values with stated tolerance; **do not treat as MCF7 production gates** — they validate the FF *mechanism*, not the cell.

1. **VG-FF-confinement-equatorial-ring** — a semiflexible actin filament network confined in a sphere of radius R self-organizes into a **single equatorial ring** iff R_confine ≲ Lₚ; ring formation probability → 0 at critical diameter **R* ≈ 15 µm ≈ Lₚ** (spherical). Band: R* = 15 ± 3 µm at Lₚ ≈ 5–18 µm; ring at equatorial great circle (θ-histogram peaked at equator). Engine: FF. Source: Fig 2d,e (p3).
2. **VG-FF-ring-droplet-ratio** — assembled ring diameter / confinement diameter **R_ring/R_droplet = 0.96 ± 0.05** (hugs inner periphery), robust to actin concentration. Engine: FF. Source: Fig 2c (p3). *(Tight, well-bounded, geometry-only — the most defensible contract here.)*
3. **VG-FF-contraction-rate-per-perimeter** — fast-phase ring constriction velocity per unit initial perimeter **≈ 0.39 µm·min⁻¹** (accept 0.31–0.39 spanning main/SI), at constant ring volume, phalloidin-insensitive (no depolymerization). Engine: FF (DCM furrow-module cross-check — authors note this matches cytokinetic rings *in cells*). Source: Fig 4f (p5,p8).
4. **VG-FF-critical-contractile-perimeter** — minimum contractable perimeter **P* ≈ 4.5 µm ≈ mean filament length (5.4 µm)**; contraction velocity ∝ (perimeter − P*) linear. This is the mechanistic statement "effective contractile unit = one filament length." Engine: FF. Source: Fig 4f (p8). *(Candidate analytic-linear-law cross-check — see Oracles.)*

Deliberately **not** proposing gates on: ring-formation probability magnitude (74.3%, condition-specific, protocol-bound), ATPase 4.82/7.89 µM·s⁻¹ (bulk biochemistry of rabbit HMM, not a cell observable), the 44 % complete-contraction fraction (highly assay-specific). These are overlay data, not defensible standing contracts.

## Oracle candidates

Three closed-form relations here are candidate analytic cross-checks (preferred over experimental overlays per philosophy):

1. **Duty-ratio effective-concentration law** — `[myosin]_eff = [myosin]_bulk × (duty ratio)²`. A clean, parameter-free **motor-model check** for the FF myosin implementation: predicts the ~100× (= (0.5/0.05)²) myoV↔myoII offset. Implement as an oracle asserting that FF's two-heads-bound translocation probability scales as duty². High value — directly tests the mechanistic motor law, not a fitted number.
2. **Constant-volume contraction invariant** — `V ≡ π R_ring · (π d²/4) = const` during constriction. A **conservation cross-check** (in the family of our existing conservation invariants): as an FF ring contracts, actin mass conserved ⇒ R_ring↓ while bundle width d↑ at constant V. Cheap, sign-definite, engine-agnostic invariant.
3. **Semiflexible elastic-rod confinement criterion** — bending energy `E ≈ (κ/2)∫(1/R)²ds`, κ = k_B T·Lₚ, minimized on the largest-radius great circle ⇒ equatorial localization iff R_confine ≲ Lₚ. This is the analytic ground truth behind gate candidate #1 and pairs naturally with the existing Helfrich/κ-bending oracle family. Implementable as an energy-minimization cross-check for a single confined semiflexible filament.
4. (weaker) **contraction velocity ∝ (perimeter − P*)** linear law with intercept P* = filament length — a linear-fit cross-check, less fundamental than 1–3.
