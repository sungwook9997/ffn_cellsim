## 1. Mechanisms to adopt  (moderate–high for DCM⊗ECM)
- **Fibrillar-collagen viscoelastic stress relaxation as the DCM boundary-ECM constitutive law.** Kim's agent-based collagen model (fibrils + cross-linkers + bundlers, Langevin/Euler BD, Bell's-law crosslinker unbinding) causally shows that shorter fibrils, tighter/smaller-angle bundling, and lower connectivity/heterogeneity → *faster* stress relaxation at *unchanged* stiffness. DCM couples cells to an ECM field; this gives the microstructure→viscoelasticity mapping to make the DCM ECM viscoelastic (τ½, loss tangent) rather than purely elastic. Extends the existing DCM⊗ECM 1/r work ([[project-dcm-ecm-kim-1r-gap]]).
- **Viscoelasticity (not stiffness) as the mechanotransduction cue.** Faster stress relaxation → integrin-β1 → Tensin-1 → RhoA → YAP. For DCM adhesion/mechanosensing this argues the cell reads matrix *relaxation rate*; a DCM durotaxis/mechanosensing scenario should sweep viscoelasticity at fixed stiffness (they did: 0.8/2/5 kPa stiffness alone does NOT drive the phenotype).

## 2. Equations / laws to port
- Over-damped Langevin + Euler update (Eq.1) of fibril/crosslinker/bundler cylindrical segments — same integrator family DCM's ECM sub-model can use.
- Harmonic stretch/bend potentials for fibril (κ_s,f, κ_b,f), crosslinker (κ_s,xl, κ_b,xl), bundler (κ_s,bu + TWO bend angles κ_b,bu,1/2) — the bundler with two enforced angles is the mechanistic way to make collagen bundles (vs a lumped stiffness).
- **Bell's law** `k_off = k_off0·exp(F·x_b/k_BT)` for cross-linker unbinding = microscopic origin of ECM stress relaxation.
- Observable: σ(t) at fixed γ=20% → τ½ (relaxation half-time). Lower τ½ = higher viscoelasticity.
