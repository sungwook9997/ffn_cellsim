# TAG-validation lens — p09

**Paper:** Jo, Yim, … T. Kim, P. Kim (2025), *Reciprocal folding dynamics in cellular networks at the stroma–basement membrane interface*, Acta Biomaterialia 201:360–371. DOI 10.1016/j.actbio.2025.05.069.

## Paper kind

**experimental + simulation (mixed), classified `experimental` for KB use.**
Two methodologies: (1) primary in-vitro reconstitution (engineered nm-thick collagen-I BM + fibroblast-laden collagen stroma + epithelium; IF/PIV/FN-FRET/tensile testing) and (2) a **continuum Ansys linear-elastostatic FEM** (two 70-µm hemispherical contractile condensates under a 2-µm BM). The **measured moduli** (E_BM, E_Str, E_Cell) and **geometric/kinematic observables** (fold angles, coupling distances, alignment %) are experimental → usable only as **overlay / on-demand cross-check**, never fit targets. The **FEM outputs** (+1.91 µm evagination, phase boundary, E_Cell≈14 kPa optimum) are **simulation output → comparison only, NEVER a truth source**. There is **no closed-form law written out** in the paper (linear elastostatics solved numerically; two image-analysis definitions only).

**Scale mismatch is the dominant fact for this analysis:** the paper is a **tissue-scale, multilayer continuum-elasticity + morphogenesis** study. Our KB validates **single-cell** DCM (surface-mesh cell) and FF (fine-grained filaments). Almost nothing here is a measurable observable at our single-cell scale, so this paper is a **weak gate source and a weak parameter source** — its value is (a) one collagen-stroma modulus cross-check, and (b) a mechanistic narrative for FF traction/ECM-remodeling. Treat as SourceEvidence-grade, not contract-grade.

## Coverage vs existing gates

- **E_Str = 354 Pa (collagen-I 2 mg/ml + fibroblasts) — CROSS-CHECK / CONFIRM of `VG-U1-G0-linear`.** Our gate bands linear shear modulus G0 at ~30–100 Pa (applied [15,200] Pa) for reference collagen. Converting this Young's modulus for a near-incompressible gel (G≈E/3) gives **G≈118 Pa**, inside our applied band. This **confirms, does not tighten**, our collagen-network stiffness gate. Caveat: it is a *cell-laden* stroma Young's modulus by macroscopic tensile test, not a cell-free shear modulus — so it is a soft, indirect cross-check only, not a band change.
- **E_Cell = 8.6 kPa (3T3 fibroblast AFM)** — loosely adjacent to `VG-H9-nucleus` (E_nuc 1–10 kPa) and the H.10 cytoplasm bands, but this is a **whole-cell fibroblast** modulus (not MCF7, not a compartment) → NOT a coverage match, NOT registerable against those gates. Overlay context only.
- **P_Cell = 200 kPa imposed stress** — NOT covered and NOT registerable. This is a coarse-grained FEM boundary stress, not a measured single-cell traction. Our traction gates (`VG-U2-traction-magnitude` FA 1–10 nN / cell 10–100 nN; `VG-U2-biphasic-traction`) are force-based single-cell observables; 200 kPa condensate-face stress is ~1–2 orders above physiological single-cell traction stress and is an FEM modeling convenience — **do not import as a parameter or band.**
- **Fold angles (16.2° / 100.2°), coupling distances (~90 µm), alignment % (97.8/93.5), fold wavelengths** — NONE covered; all are **tissue-network / multicellular morphogenesis** observables with no analog at our single-cell DCM/FF scale. Not registerable as gates here.
- **PIV displacement 0.13–6.62 µm** — traction-adjacent but it is a bulk matrix-displacement field around a multicell condensate, not a calibrated single-cell traction; no defensible single-cell band. Not a gate.
- **+1.91 µm evagination / phase boundary** — simulation output, comparison-only; cannot become a gate (would violate "simulation is never a truth source").

**Net: 0 new gates defensible at our single-cell scale. 0 tightenings. 1 soft confirm cross-check (E_Str→G0).**

## Oracle candidates

**None directly implementable.** The paper's physics is film-on-substrate elastic instability (stiff thin BM over soft stroma → evagination/invagination governed by stiffness contrast), but **no closed-form law is written out** — the phase boundary is a numerical Ansys sweep. The underlying analytic scaling (Biot / Cerda–Mahadevan wrinkling, λ ~ h·(E_film/E_sub)^{1/3}, and the buckling-vs-compaction sign set by stiffness ratio) is *implied* but not stated, is **tissue/continuum-scale**, and does not map onto our single-cell DCM/FF oracles (Helfrich, Young-Laplace, Bell-Evans, Hill, Kelvin-Voigt). Not an oracle candidate for our engines. The two equations the paper *does* define (alignment% = (1−D/180)·100; FA polarity = (PAX_whole−PAX_DAPI)/PAX_DAPI) are image-analysis metrics, not physics oracles.
