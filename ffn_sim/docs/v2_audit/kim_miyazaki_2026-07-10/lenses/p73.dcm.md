## 1. Mechanisms to adopt
- **Cortex density as the upstream control of myosin engagement.** DCM currently models the cortex as a single surface-tension field γ. This paper shows myosin-II filament *engagement* is an all-or-none steric function of local network density (switch at ~2.5× density / ~1.5× mesh-size change). For DCM this justifies making the *active* part of γ a function of a cortical-density state variable rather than a free scalar — i.e. γ_active = f(ρ_cortex) with a threshold, feeding the long-standing γ-floor discussion ([[project-gamma-floor-layered-resolution]]).
- **Density-gradient-driven cortical flow.** In a network with a density gradient, penetrated myosin drives unidirectional actin flow toward the densest region, requiring only the gradient + connectivity (no patterned motor activity). This is a candidate emergent-flow mechanism for DCM cortical-flow / contractile-ring positioning: a surface density field advected by a myosin stress ∝ ∇ρ.

## 2. Equations / laws to port (to the surface/continuum level)
- Disassembly (turnover) scaling `Ḋ ∝ ρ_actin^(-2) · ρ_cofilin,bound` — if DCM ever carries a cortex-turnover field, this sets the density dependence of cortex renewal (relevant to the turnover lever already ruled through in the γ-floor work).
- Steric-exclusion threshold: myosin engages only when mesh spacing ≳ filament length (~110–160 nm vs 1.32 µm filament) → a density gate on the myosin contribution to γ.

## 4. Geometry / protocol to reproduce
- Density-gradient cortex → directed flow toward dense region (a DCM cortical-flow scenario, membrane-templated).
- 3D shape control (cups/domes/rings/pillars up to 35 µm) via patterned nucleation — a reference for DCM cortex-thickness/shape actuation, though DCM is a thin-shell model.

## 5. Validation targets for DCM observables
- Directed cortical-flow onset requires density gradient + connectivity (no patterned activity) — a qualitative DCM cortical-flow test.
- Actin sheet stays stationary ≥24 min without myosin — a DCM "cortex at rest under turgor, no active flow" baseline check.

## 6. Risks / caveats
- In-vitro reconstituted branched (Arp2/3) network on a flat SLB — NOT the living MCF7 cortex; densities and geometry differ. Use as the *microscopic basis / scaling* for the density→engagement mapping, not as a direct γ value. Respect physiological-baseline: DCM cortex must stay at its pressure-borne γ = ΔP·R/2 operating point; this paper informs the density-dependence of the *active add-on*, not the baseline.
- Relevance is moderate (DCM is a continuum surface model, no explicit mesh); score ≈ 1.5/5.
