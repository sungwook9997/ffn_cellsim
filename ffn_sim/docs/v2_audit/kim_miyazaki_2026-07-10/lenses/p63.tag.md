# TAG-validation lens — p63

**Paper:** Kim, T. (2015) "Determinants of contractile forces generated in disorganized actomyosin bundles." *Biomech Model Mechanobiol* 14:345–355. DOI 10.1007/s10237-014-0608-2.

## Paper kind

**simulation** (agent-based 3D Brownian-dynamics / over-damped Langevin of an explicit actin+ACP+myosin bundle). This is a *model output*, not an in-vitro/in-vivo measurement and not a closed-form theory paper. Per our validation philosophy this means its numbers are **comparison-only cross-checks — never a truth source, never a fit target, and never KB-registered as an oracle value** (see the "Oracle is cross-check, not truth" hard rule).

Two important nuances that raise its usefulness above a generic third-party sim:
1. It is the **same engine lineage as FF** (Taeyoon Kim = the Cytosim-class fine-grained actomyosin physics FF re-implements: semi-flexible beam filaments + Bell force-dependent ACP cross-linkers + bipolar myosin minifilaments with force–velocity/stall + excluded volume). Its outputs are therefore an unusually apt *engine-to-engine cross-check* for an FF actomyosin-bundle test — the "on-demand oracle cross-check" tier, explicitly the lower-priority tier vs analytic ground truth.
2. It contains **one genuine analytic law** (Eq. 7, Euler buckling) and **two literature-sourced parameter anchors** (Ferrer 2008 ACP off-rate constants; motor bare-zone geometry) that live above the simulation tier.

## Coverage vs existing gates

**No existing gate covers single-bundle actomyosin contractility.** Our gate set is DCM/spheroid + ECM Mikado + FA clutch + junction + whole-cell cortical-γ. The closest touches:

- **VG-H3-KU35-cortex-tension / VG-H7-gate-a/b (cortical γ floor).** These lump the entire actomyosin cortex into a surface tension γ (0.35–0.65 mN/m) via method-of-planes. Kim's paper is the *mechanistic layer beneath* that lump — it says how bundle-scale tension emerges from R_M, R_ACP, ACP off-rate and buckling. It does **not** cover the same observable (bundle nN tension vs areal mN/m γ are different quantities at different scales) so it neither confirms, tightens, nor challenges these gates. **Relevance:** it is a candidate *diagnostic* for the long-standing "~1000× active-γ floor" investigation — Kim's χ≈0.005-without-ACP → 0.15-with-ACP result is directly the "cross-bridge / crosslink efficiency" lever family that MEMORY flags as the leading γ-floor candidate. Worth noting to PI as mechanistic context, not as a gate.
- **VG-U2-biphasic-traction (KB-2.8, "biphasic is saturating not peaked").** Kim's substrate-rigidity result (bundle tension rises with E, plateaus above ~300 Pa; "stress ∝ E below 1000 Pa, flat above", Borau 2012) is the **same saturating (not peaked) shape** our KB already recorded. This is a qualitative CONFIRM of the KB-2.8 finding from an independent engine — but it is a different observable (bundle tension vs clutch traction) so I would not attach it to VG-U2 as a band; log as corroborating context.
- **PARAM-F_stall_motor (2 pN), PARAM-v_unloaded (100 nm/s).** See Param candidates — Kim's 4.5 pN / 550 nm/s are per-head-ensemble PCM outputs at a different coarse-graining, not a supersession.

Net: **0 existing gates confirmed/tightened/challenged in the strict band sense.** Everything Kim adds is *new territory* (an FF-scale actomyosin bundle test we do not yet have) plus qualitative corroboration.

## New gate candidates

All are **comparison-only, PI-gate review** — the reference is a same-lineage simulation, so any gate built on it is an engine-to-engine cross-check (lower tier than analytic/experimental), never a hard contract band. Stage, do not author.

1. **`VG-FF-bundle-clutch-efficiency` (candidate, PI-gate review).**
   Observable: contractile efficiency χ = F_max / Σf_M^stall of an FF clamped actomyosin bundle, with vs without passive cross-linkers.
   Band/value: χ ≈ 0.005 (crosslinker-free) rising to ≈ 0.15 with ACPs at reference density — i.e. an **~30–40× amplification**, cross-check the *ratio* (robust) more than the absolute.
   Condition: clamped bundle, R_M=0.025, R_ACP=0.032, E_sub=5000 Pa, ⟨L_f⟩≈6.5 µm.
   Engine: **FF**. Source: Fig 2b / p350 / Discussion p9.

2. **`VG-FF-bundle-Fmax-scaling` (candidate, PI-gate review).**
   Observable: exponent of F_max vs motor molar ratio R_M in an FF bundle.
   Band/value: F_max ∝ R_M^1 without ACPs; **F_max ∝ R_M^0.6 with ACPs** (exponent ~0.6 ± tolerance TBD by PI). The *change of exponent* on adding crosslinkers is the discriminating signature.
   Condition: R_ACP=0.032, low-R_M regime. Engine: **FF**. Source: Fig 2b/4a, p6.

3. **`VG-FF-bundle-sustainability` (candidate, PI-gate review).**
   Observable: sustainability S=⟨F⟩/F_max of bundle tension over ~100 s.
   Band/value: motors-only → tension collapses in <5 s (S≈0); with sufficient ACP (R_ACP≥~0.01 at R_M=0.025) → S≈1 sustained ~100 s. A **qualitative bistable** contract (unsustained vs sustained), not a tight number.
   Condition: as above. Engine: **FF**. Source: Fig 2a/3a, p5–6.

(A fourth candidate — parallel vs anti-parallel ACP efficiency η≈0.61 vs 0.15 — is a strong mechanistic cross-check but geometry-specific; hold as context, not a staged gate.)

## Oracle candidates

- **Euler buckling of a filament segment (Eq. 7): F_buckle = π²·κ_b,A·r0,A / L_seg².**
  This is a genuine **closed-form analytic cross-check** (standard Euler beam critical load; here κ_b,A·r0,A plays the role of bending rigidity EI, L_seg the cross-link spacing). It is a *candidate FF oracle* — FF already has beam bending; this gives the critical compressive load at which a segment between two cross-links buckles, which Kim identifies as the **tension/compression symmetry-breaking mechanism** (buckling on the compressive stroke → net contraction). Recommend implementing as an on-demand analytic oracle `filament_segment_buckling(kappa_b, r0, L_seg)` alongside the existing Helfrich/Young-Laplace/Bell-Evans/Hill oracles, used to cross-check that FF's emergent buckling threshold matches π²EI/L² for a segment. This is the highest-tier contribution of the paper (analytic ground truth, not simulation output).
- No other closed form; F=E·A·ε (substrate readout) and the PCM/Bell rate laws are standard and already covered by Bell-Evans + Hill oracles.
