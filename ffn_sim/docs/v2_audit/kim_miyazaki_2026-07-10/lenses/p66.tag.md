**Paper:** Hassan, Biel, Umulis & Kim (2019), *Interplay Between the Persistent Random Walk and the Contact Inhibition of Locomotion Leads to Collective Cell Behaviors*, Bull. Math. Biol. 81:3301–3321. DOI 10.1007/s11538-019-00585-1. Lab: Taeyoon Kim (Purdue Weldon BME).

## Paper kind

**simulation** (agent-based model *output*, not measurement, not closed-form theory).

The paper is an over-damped, agent-based mechanical model on a rigid 2D substrate. Each cell is a lumped 2-point "machine" (front + rear cell-point joined by a Hookean spring), traction is a **constant-torque, non-centripetal tensile construct** fixed as a Lagrange-multiplier-like unknown by the coupled `K u = f` system — exactly the kind of lumped abstraction the FF engine replaces with mechanistic clutches/Hill motors. Under the project's philosophy this classifies the paper's numbers as **comparison-only / behavioral cross-check, NEVER a truth source and NEVER a fit target**. It is also NOT an analytic-oracle source: it provides no closed-form law we can implement as ground truth (its "laws" are emergent measurements from the sim).

Consequence for use: every quantitative item below is an *emergent behavioral target of the same qualitative form we would expect FF/DCM to reproduce* — usable as a loose sanity/plausibility overlay, not as a contract band. Mechanistic constants (κ, α, β, η, γ, M_F, M_R, radii, r₀, lattice spacing) live in **Online Table 1 (ESM), which is NOT in this PDF** → nothing registrable as a Parameter from this file.

Not covered; candidate below (behavioral, low priority).
- **Collective / jamming / nematic order (items 9–15):** partially adjacent to **VG-U5-velocity-correlation** (ξ_v ~50–200 µm) and **VG-U3-shape-index-p0** (jammed/unjammed at p0=3.81), and to **MC-U5-collective**. Item 9 could inform, but does not challenge, VG-U3-shape-index-p0 (that gate is a geometric jamming criterion, this is a density-driven kinetic one; complementary, not conflicting).
- **VG-U1-force-propagation** (1/r) and other mechanistic gates: unrelated.

Net: this paper CONFIRMS nothing already banded and CHALLENGES nothing. It surfaces **new emergent-behavior observables** the KB does not currently gate.

## New gate candidates

All are **behavioral cross-checks, simulation-sourced → low authority; flag "candidate, PI-gate review".** Bands are deliberately wide plausibility brackets from a lumped model, NOT contracts. Only propose if/when FF-crawl or a DCM monolayer run is being validated for emergent motility.

1. **`VG-FF-crawl-MSD-crossover` (candidate, PI-gate review)** — Engine FF.
   Observable: log-slope s = d ln MSD/d ln τ of a single migrating cell.
   Band/value: s ≈ 2 at short lag → s → 1 at long lag (a monotone persistent→diffusive crossover, 1 ≤ s ≤ 2), crossover present. *Qualitative form only.*
   Rationale: this is the generic PRW signature (Fürth/Ornstein–Uhlenbeck), independently expected — the paper is corroboration, not the source of truth. **Prefer an analytic PRW-MSD oracle (see Oracle candidates) as the real ground truth; use this paper only as a "yes, an agent-based cell reproduces it too" overlay.**
   Source: Fig 3b, p.8–9.

2. **`VG-FF-crawl-speed-order` (candidate, PI-gate review)** — Engine FF.
   Observable: emergent single-cell mesenchymal crawl speed.
   Band/value: order-of-magnitude bracket **~30–60 µm/hr (≈ 8–17 nm/s)** for a fibroblast-like cell (NO cadherin), as an *order-of-magnitude plausibility overlay only* — do NOT adopt as a tight contract (it is a lumped-model output and cell-line/condition dependent). Cross-check against the FF crawl datum (28–45 nm/s).
   Source: Fig 3f, p.10.

3. **`VG-DCM-jamming-density` (candidate, PI-gate review)** — Engine DCM (monolayer/confluent).
   Observable: PRW→CIL/jamming transition density.
   Band/value: transition at **~2300–2400 cells/mm²** with the *sign* of the effect (MSD slope drops sharply above it). **Flag: this is fibroblast-like, rigid-substrate, no-cadherin — NOT MCF-7; do not import the number as an MCF-7 contract.** Register only as a qualitative "density-driven kinetic jamming exists and appears at O(10³) cells/mm²" cross-check if/when a DCM monolayer run is done. Complements, does not supersede, VG-U3-shape-index-p0.
   Source: Fig 5c / Fig 6a, p.12–13.

4. **`VG-DCM-nematic-order-hp-hv` (candidate, weak, PI-gate review)** — Engine DCM.
   Observable: nematic order parameters — polarity h_p vs velocity h_v.
   Band/value: both rise to an asymptote after ~20 h with the robust inequality **h_p > h_v** (polarity ordering leads velocity ordering). Register the *inequality/form* as the checkable claim, not the magnitudes (h_p~0.8, h_v~0.4–0.6 are model-knob-dependent).
   Source: Fig 7, p.15–16.

I do NOT propose gates for redirection-angle distributions or trajectory extent — no defensible single-cell band.

## Oracle candidates

**None from this paper directly** (it derives no closed form). BUT the paper's central single-cell result (item 1–3) points at a genuine analytic oracle the KB does not yet hold:

- **PRW / Ornstein–Uhlenbeck MSD (Fürth formula):** `MSD(τ) = 2 D [ τ − P (1 − e^{−τ/P}) ]` in 1D (×2 per dimension), with persistence time P and diffusion D — closed-form, gives s→2 (ballistic) as τ→0 and s→1 (diffusive) as τ→∞. This IS implementable as an analytic cross-check for any FF/DCM emergent-migration run. **Source it to Fürth 1920 / Uhlenbeck-Ornstein 1930 / Selmeczi 2005, NOT to this paper** (Hassan 2019 only demonstrates a mechanistic model reproduces it). Flag as candidate oracle, PI-gate review — it would be the ground truth that VG-FF-crawl-MSD-crossover checks against, keeping the paper strictly as overlay.
