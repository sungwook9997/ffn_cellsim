## Paper kind

**`simulation`** (dominant validation-grade data) **+ experimental overlay sub-component.**

This is a hybrid: (a) an *experimental* half — isolated single ventral stress fibers from A7r5 cells de-roofed by hypotonic+detergent, stretched/compressed by a functionalized glass needle, imaged (Fig. 1, S1); and (b) a *simulation* half — the **canonical Taeyoon Kim agent-based Brownian-dynamics actomyosin-network model** (explicit actin beams + ACP Bell-crosslinkers + multi-arm myosin PCM catch-bond motors, overdamped Langevin/Euler). **Almost every number in the Quantitative-Results table (Figs. 2–5, S3–S8) is a MODEL OUTPUT**, and the experimental Fig. 1 curves are qualitative rate-dependent buckling with apparent-length values.

**Usage ruling (hard):**
- The Fig. 2–5 scalings (K_r, ΔL_B, t_r, F_B,ss vs κ_b, ε̇_c, k₂₀, R_M) are **simulation output → comparison-only, never a truth source or fit target.** Because this IS a sibling agent-based cytoskeletal model to our FF engine, these are **model-to-model cross-checks**, not ground truth.
- The Fig. 1 experimental buckling/recovery behavior is **overlay-only** (qualitative: fast compression → buckle→recover; slow → no buckling).
- The parameter values (Table S1) are **candidate FF constants**, most **cited from Kim's own prior network papers [43–48]** — same class as our reconstitution/oracle inputs, so register with provenance and treat conservatively (per project rule "never KB-register oracle values" — these are Kim-model *inputs*, flag as such).

## Coverage vs existing gates

**No existing gate covers the actomyosin-bundle / stress-fiber contractile-unit domain.** Our closest contracts are cell-scale cortex tension (VG-H3-KU35, VG-H7-gate-a/b) and FA motor-clutch (MC-U2). The FF bundle-buckling physics here is **uncovered** — it is exactly the FF engine's target regime but no ValidationGate yet exists for single-bundle contraction/buckling.

Parameter-level touch-points (context/conflict, not gate coverage):

- **PARAM-F_stall_motor (default 2 pN, KU-2.4/2.18)** vs paper **F_s≈24 pN per minifilament** (item 19). **Level mismatch, not a contradiction:** ours reads as per-motor/per-head convenience default; the paper's 24 pN is a **32-head minifilament** (6·N_h, N_h=4). Consistent with ~0.75–6 pN/head. → **flag: our F_stall_motor default likely under-scoped for a minifilament; PI-review the level convention.**
- **PARAM-v_unloaded (default 100 nm/s, range 10–100, KU-2.4/3.12)** vs paper **v₀≈56 nm/s (model)** and **~200 nm/s (in-cell smooth-muscle, Milo/Phillips ref 58)** (item 18). Paper value sits inside/near our band; the 200 nm/s in-cell figure would **push the band upper edge up**. → context note, not a tighten.
- **VG-U2-catch-slip-bond** ("lifetime peak ~30 pN, integrin-FN"): the paper's 30-pN *myosin* slip cutoff (item 24) is a **different bond** (actomyosin, not integrin-FN) — coincidental number; do NOT merge.
- **PARAM-ell_p (17 µm, collagen) / PARAM-kappa_fiber (7e-26 N·m²)**: these are *collagen ECM* fibers. Actin L_p=9 µm / κ_b,A=2.64×10⁻¹⁹ N·m (item 21) is an **actin-specific** constant we do NOT hold — new param, distinct module (FF actin, not ECM). Note κ_b,A **units N·m** (bending-modulus-per-hinge convention) vs our kappa_fiber **N·m²** — do not conflate conventions.
- **Bell-Evans oracle** (already in our oracle set): the ACP off-rate k⁰=0.05 s⁻¹, x_u=0.9 nm (items 22–23) are a **concrete Bell parameterization** for a generic ACP — a cross-check input for the existing Bell oracle, NOT a new oracle. Note: CLAUDE.md re-anchored α-actinin k_off0 to **0.066 s⁻¹ (Ferrer 2008)**; this paper's 0.05 s⁻¹ is a *generic model ACP*, close but distinct — do not overwrite the Ferrer-anchored α-actinin value.

## New gate candidates (all **candidate, PI-gate review**)

> All bands below derive from a *sibling simulation*, so each is a **model-to-model cross-check** at the FF bundle scale, not an experimental truth gate. Framed as FF sanity/emergent-behavior gates. Bands quoted are the paper's sim values with generous tolerance; **do not treat as fit targets.**

1. **proposed_vg_id VG-FF-bundle-rate-buckling** — *rate-dependent buckling threshold.*
   Observable: does an anti-parallel actomyosin contractile unit **buckle** on compression?
   Band/law: **buckles iff compression rate exceeds unloaded motor walking speed; no buckling in the slow limit** (qualitative threshold; paper: no buckling at \|ε̇_c\|=0.01 s⁻¹, buckling ≥0.05 s⁻¹ for v₀≈56 nm/s). Engine: **FF**. Source: items 1,3,13, p18/Fig.4A. *This is the paper's central, experimentally-grounded, turnover-independent finding — the most defensible candidate (the qualitative law is corroborated by Fig.1 experiment).*

2. **proposed_vg_id VG-FF-bundle-recovery-motorspeed** — *recovery time is set by motor walk speed, NOT motor density.*
   Observable: bundle straightening recovery time t_r and shortening rate \|dL_B/dt\| vs (k₂₀ walking rate) and (R_M motor density).
   Band/law: **t_r decreases with motor speed (k₂₀ 4→16 ⇒ t_r ~32→4.5 s) but is ≈flat vs motor density (R_M 0.002→0.01 ⇒ t_r ~14→12.5 s)**. Engine: **FF**. Source: items 14–16, Fig.5B/E. *A clean emergent-behavior discriminator for the FF motor model; ±30–50% tolerance.*

3. **proposed_vg_id VG-FF-bundle-plateau-force-density** — *steady-state contractile force scales with motor density.*
   Observable: post-recovery plateau tensile force F_B,ss vs R_M.
   Band/law: **F_B,ss rises monotonically ∝ motor density (R_M 0.002/0.004/0.008/0.01 ⇒ ~0.3/0.82/1.38/1.62 nN)**. Engine: **FF**. Source: item 17, Fig.5F. *Complements #2: density sets force, speed sets kinetics. Monotonic-trend gate (not a point band).*

*(Deliberately NOT proposing the κ_b,A-sweep curve (items 9–10) or the exact K_r/ΔL_B point values as gates — they are pure sim scans with modeling-choice stiffness (10× reference) and are too parameterization-specific to be a defensible contract.)*

## Oracle candidates

- **Cylinder-segment drag (Eq. S3):** ζ = 3πµ·r_c·(3 + 2r₀/r_c)/5 — closed-form drag for a cylindrical filament segment (length r₀, diameter r_c, viscosity µ). **Candidate analytic cross-check for the FF filament/segment drag coefficient** (Clift-derived, ref [61]). Cheap to implement as an FF oracle; verifies FF per-segment drag against the slender-cylinder limit. *This is the one genuinely new closed-form worth staging.*
- **PCM force-velocity (myosin catch-bond):** described as a 3-state / 5-rate parallel-cluster model, **NOT a closed form** in the paper. We already hold a **Hill force-velocity oracle** — the PCM curve (v₀≈56 nm/s, F_s≈24 pN, Fig.S6) is a *cross-check dataset* for that oracle, not a new oracle.
- **Bell force-dependent unbinding (Eq. S8):** k_u = k⁰·exp(x_u·F/k_BT) — **already an oracle in our set** (Bell-Evans). Paper supplies a concrete ACP parameterization (0.05 s⁻¹, 0.9 nm) as an input, not a new law.
- **Euler beam / filament buckling:** the paper *measures* buckling curvature but gives **no closed-form critical-load expression** — no oracle to extract; the buckling gates above cover it behaviorally.

## Duplicates / notes

- **Not a duplicate KB claim.** No existing KnowledgeClaim/gate covers actomyosin-bundle mechano-adaptation, SF buckling/recovery, or the PCM myosin minifilament at bundle scale. Nearest KB neighbors are cell-scale (KU-3.5 cortex tension) and FA-scale (KU-2.x clutch) — different scale/observable.
- **Lab lineage:** This is the **canonical Taeyoon Kim agent-based code** (refs [43–48]) — the same lineage as our DCM⊗ECM Kim-1/r work ([[project-dcm-ecm-kim-1r-gap]]) and directly the physics the FF (Cytosim-style) engine reimplements. High parameter-transfer value for FF stress-fiber / bundle contraction.
- **Modeling-choice caveat (important when citing):** the reference simulations deliberately used **κ_b,A = 10× the experimental value** (L_p≈90 µm vs true 9 µm) to sharpen the buckling pattern, and used **N_F=50 filaments** (≥ physiological 10–30/cross-section, intentionally overlapping on a 27-nm circle for uniform crosslinking). Any FF cross-check must cite the **experimental** κ_b,A and note the modeling inflation.
- **Turnover-independent finding:** buckling→recovery here occurs with **no protein turnover** (de-roofed SF, fixed filament population) — the adaptation is purely intrinsic filament mechanics + load-free motor walking. This is directly relevant to whether FF needs turnover to reproduce recovery (it does not, per this paper).
- **Experimental L_B values (~24–26 µm, item 2)** are needle-manipulation *apparent* contour lengths of an isolated SF, not the 5-µm model bundle — do not cross-compare the absolute numbers, only the fast-vs-slow *behavior*.
