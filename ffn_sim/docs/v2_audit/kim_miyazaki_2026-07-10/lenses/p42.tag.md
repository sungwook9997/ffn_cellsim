**Paper:** Jung, Murrell, Kim (2016) — *F-Actin Fragmentation Induces Distinct Mechanisms of Stress Relaxation in the Actin Cytoskeleton*, ACS Macro Lett. 5, 641–645. DOI 10.1021/acsmacrolett.6b00232.

---

## Paper kind

**simulation** (primary) — the quantitative curves are all output of a 3D agent-based Brownian-dynamics model (the canonical Taeyoon-Kim F-actin + ACP network, 3 µm cubic PBC domain). Per our philosophy, model output is **comparison-only, never a truth source** — none of its stress values may become a fit target or a lab-anchored band.

Two embedded sub-kinds matter for us:
- **analytic** — eq 2 (Euler buckling force, closed form) is a genuine analytic law → **candidate FF oracle / cross-check**.
- The paper's behavioral claims are validated against **in-vitro sinusoidal-strain reconstitution experiments** (its refs 15–17); those experiments — not this simulation — are what would anchor an overlay. This paper is the *route* to them, not the datum itself.

Engine relevance: **FF strongly, DCM weakly.** The model is architecturally FF's own family (explicit bending-beam filaments + explicit Bell-off-rate ACP cross-links + buckling). Severing is *not* currently in FF's mechanism set; this is the reference to add it. DCM (surface-mesh continuum) sees this physics only as a sub-grid effective viscoelastic law.

## New gate candidates (all PI-gate review — staged, not authored)

**Priority order.** Because the magnitudes are simulation output, the *defensible* gate is the sign/behavior one (which the paper explicitly ties to in-vitro experiment); the magnitude gates are comparison-only and must be labelled so.

1. **`VG-FF-cyclic-relaxation-directionality`** (candidate; FF; **behavior/sign gate — strongest**)
   - Observable: % cyclic stress drop of a cross-linked actin network under large-amplitude (~50%) cyclic shear, bidirectional vs unidirectional, with F-actin severing active.
   - Band/contract: **bidirectional drop > 0 and rising monotonically with amplitude (0→~50–75% across amplitude 0.1→0.5); unidirectional drop ≈ 0 (< ~5%).** The *directionality asymmetry* is the contract, not the exact %.
   - Why defensible: this qualitative law is what the paper matches to in-vitro sinusoidal-strain reconstitution (refs 15–17) — a sign/behavior contract, not a tuned number.
   - Requires: severing mechanism implemented in FF first (see mechanism gap).

2. **`VG-FF-severing-unbinding-orientation`** (candidate; FF; **behavior gate**)
   - Observable: orientation histograms of severed segments vs high-load-ACP unbinding events under cyclic shear.
   - Band/contract: **severing peaks ~135° (compressed, outside framework); high-load (≥8 pN) ACP unbinding peaks ~45° (supportive framework) — orthogonal, ~90° apart, and the two channels do not interfere.** Contract = the orthogonality/independence, not the exact angles.
   - Defensible as an emergent-mechanism sign check; do not band the angles tightly (geometry of the 45° shear framework, robust).

3. **`VG-FF-actin-network-peak-stress`** (candidate; FF; **comparison-only, weak — flag hard**)
   - Observable: peak shear stress of a cross-linked actin network at 50% strain.
   - Value: **~100–150 Pa** (R_ACP 0.032→0.1), spanning >3 orders across the (⟨L_f⟩,R_ACP) sweep.
   - **Caveat: simulation-derived, at a 3 µm coarse PBC cube, NOT a lab datum.** May be used only as a coarse order-of-magnitude comparison for an FF network patch; must never be a fit target or a tight band. Prefer to source the underlying in-vitro rheology (Murrell/Gardel reconstitution) before making this a contract.

n_new_gates = **2** load-bearing candidates (1 + 2) + 1 comparison-only flag (3, weak).

## Oracle candidates

**Euler buckling force — `oracle: filament_buckling` (analytic cross-check, FF).** Strongest KB contribution of this paper.

  F_buckle = π² · κ_b,A · r_0,A / L_c²   (eq 2)   ⇒  F_buckle ∝ L_c⁻²

- Closed-form ground truth for the minimum compressive load that buckles a cross-link-spanning F-actin segment of span L_c. Directly a cross-check for FF filament stability (analogous to the existing Helfrich κq⁴ / Young-Laplace ΔP·R/2 oracles): given a filament segment's bending stiffness and cross-link span, FF's emergent buckling onset should reproduce the π²/L_c² scaling and the 4.1 pN value at L_c=300 nm.
- Standard Euler beam theory (F_cr = π²EI/L²), so it is a *canonical* analytic oracle, not a paper-specific fit — perfectly aligned with "validate FF against analytic ground truth as primary."
- Implementation note: needs κ_b,A and r_0,A from the SI to hard-check the 4.1 pN point; the *scaling* (∝L_c⁻²) is checkable without them.
- The Bell off-rate evaluation (item 9) is a check against the **existing** Bell-Evans oracle, not a new oracle.

n_oracles = **1** (Euler buckling; PI-gate review).
