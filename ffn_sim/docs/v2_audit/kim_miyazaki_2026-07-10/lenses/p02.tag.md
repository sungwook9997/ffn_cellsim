## Paper kind

**simulation** (coarse-grained Brownian/Langevin-dynamics agent model of a reconstituted actin–filamin network; extends Kim/Hwang/Kamm 2009). This is the primary classification and it sets the usage rule: **the paper's constitutive outputs (τ–γ curves, exponents, relaxation plateaus, plastic exponents) are COMPARISON-ONLY — never a truth source and never a fit target.**

Two important carve-outs inside a simulation paper:
- Its **Bell-Evans rate constants are experimentally sourced** — `k⁰_ub,r = 0.115 s⁻¹` from Ferrer et al. 2008 (single-molecule filamin rupture, ref 6), and `k⁰_uf,r = 3.0×10⁻⁵ s⁻¹` + `λ_uf = 6×10⁻¹⁰ m` set from filamin-A AFM + MC (ref 7). These are the genuinely registrable, literature-anchored quantities → **parameter candidates** for the FF crosslinker layer.
- Its **plastic-deformation exponent x ≈ 0.17** is validated against an **in-vitro** measurement (Gardel et al. 2006, ref 5, γ: 0.08→~0.02) → that *experimental* number is an overlay/cross-check target, not the simulation's own 0.17.

Directly relevant engine: **FF** (fine-grained filament + crosslinker; this paper is the methodological ancestor of the FF Cytosim-style crosslinker kinetics). **DCM** relevance is weak/indirect (continuum surface mesh; could inform an effective nonlinear cortex rheology closure someday — low priority).

## New gate candidates (all *candidate, PI-gate review* — staged only)

> Philosophy guardrail: the paper is a **simulation**, so any gate built on its own curve outputs is a *model-vs-model comparison*, never a truth contract. I stage these conservatively; the only near-truth anchors are the experimentally-sourced Bell constants and the Gardel in-vitro plastic exponent.

1. **proposed_vg_id: VG-FF-acp-unbinding-bell** — *(Verification / oracle cross-check, engine FF)*
   - Observable: FF crosslinker off-rate as a function of arm tension.
   - Band/value: `k_ub(F) = k⁰_ub·exp(λ_ub·|F|/k_BT)` with `k⁰_ub = 0.115 s⁻¹`, `λ_ub = 0.104 nm` (filamin/ACP, 300 K) — reproduces the **existing Bell-Evans oracle** form to numerical tolerance.
   - Condition: single ACP arm, slip regime, r₁₂≥r₀. Source: p.1598, Eqs.2–3, Ferrer 2008.
   - Defensible: yes — it is a cross-check of the FF implementation against the analytic Bell law with a lit-anchored `k⁰`. Cleanest candidate.

2. **proposed_vg_id: VG-FF-crosslinker-dominance** — *(mechanistic / simulation-comparison, engine FF)*
   - Observable: ratio of unbinding events to unfolding events vs effective strain rate.
   - Value: **unbinding dominates for γ̇_eff < ~1 s⁻¹** (crossover at ~1 s⁻¹); at the physiological γ̇_eff≈0.1 s⁻¹, unbinding+rebinding governs rheology while domain unfolding merely delays stress rise.
   - Use: decides **whether FF must model Ig-domain unfolding at all** (answer at physiological rates: no). Condition: bulk shear, filamin ACPs. Source: Fig.3A, text.
   - Defensible: qualitative/mechanistic contract; band is a crossover threshold, not a tuned number. Flag *comparison-only* (simulation).

3. **proposed_vg_id: VG-FF-plastic-exponent** *(candidate, LOW priority — overlay)* — plastic-flow exponent `x ≈ 0.17` (γ~t^x) at γ̇_eff=0.1 s⁻¹, **overlay against Gardel 2006 in-vitro** (γ:0.08→0.02). Reconstituted-network scale, not obviously measurable at OUR single-cell scale → stage but flag as possibly out-of-scope for the single-cell gate set; PI to decide.

*(Not proposing gates for the τ=2.5γ^0.4+500γ^4 fit, the ~1 Pa relaxation plateau, or F_r~exp(11.5γ) — these are simulation-fit phenomenology of a bulk box, not defensible contracts at single-cell scale.)*

## Oracle candidates

- **No new fundamental oracle.** The paper's two closed forms — stress relaxation Eq.7 `τ(t)=b/a+(τ₀−b/a)exp[aN⁰(exp(−2k⁰_ub t)−1)]` and plastic strain Eq.10 `γ(t)=ln[exp(11.5γ₀)+11.5ct]/11.5` — are **phenomenological fits to simulation output** (empirical constants a=0.006, b=1.8 Pa, c, 11.5). They are not first-principles laws and **must not be implemented as ground-truth oracles.**
- **Confirms existing oracles:** (a) **Bell-Evans off-rate** oracle — the paper's Eqs.2–3 are exactly the Bell slip form with lit-anchored `k⁰_ub`; use it to *seed/cross-check* the Bell oracle for the ACP species. (b) **WLC/Marko-Siggia** oracle — Eq.1 confirms the WLC arm law and adds a sanctioned multi-step-unfolding extension (saw-tooth peaks 100–200 pN at 30 nm), which could become a filamin-arm force-extension cross-check (simulation/AFM, low priority).
- The **k⁰·t_d time-rescaling invariance** (Fig.2) is a useful *coarse-graining self-consistency check* for FF's own rate-rescaling, but it is a scaling argument, not a validation oracle.
