# DCM lens — p50

**Paper:** Mair et al., "The Arp2/3 complex enhances cell migration on elastic substrates," *MBoC* 34:ar67, 1–10 (2023). DOI 10.1091/mbc.E22-06-0243. (Taeyoon Kim computational + Deok-Ho Kim / Rong Li wet-lab; H4 glioblastoma on polyacrylamide gels.)

**Verdict for DCM: LOW–MODERATE (score ~2/5).** This is a fine-grained actomyosin-network + molecular-clutch **substrate-adhesion / migration** paper — squarely the FF engine's territory, not DCM's surface-mesh cell. There is **no cortex/membrane/nucleus continuum, no closed cell, no aggregate**. BUT it contains **one genuinely transferable mechanistic principle** for DCM: **Parallel Focal Adhesion Compensation (PFAC)** — the emergent load-sharing that lets a bundle of parallel stochastic bonds avoid the all-or-nothing "load-and-fail" collapse of a single bond. That principle maps directly onto DCM's cell–cell **cadherin catch-bond junction cluster** (load-sharing clusters, contact-age maturation) and is relevant to the OPEN **loose→compact aggregate compaction-rate** problem. Everything else (Arp2/3 branching, retrograde flow, lamellipodium width sweep, TFM on gels) is FF/substrate physics with no DCM surface-mesh analogue. Almost all numerical parameters live in Suppl. Tables S1–S4, which are **not in the PDF** — so quantitative porting is blocked pending SI.

## 1. MECHANISMS to adopt

### M1 — Junction cluster **load-sharing / PFAC** as the anti-catastrophic-failure law (PRIMARY DCM lever)
**DCM module:** cell–cell cadherin catch-bond junction interface → `load-sharing cluster` model (the "m→0 only" dissolution rule already in DCM per the S1 redesign).
**Change:** adopt the paper's PFAC finding as the *design contract* for how many parallel catch-bonds a junction cluster must carry, and as the mechanism that sets cluster lifetime. The paper (Fig 4H, PFA model) shows a sharp regime change in a bundle of N parallel stochastic slip-bonds sharing one load:
- **1–6 bonds:** classic Chan–Odde **load-and-fail** — the substrate loads all bonds, one stochastically releases, the surviving bonds see force redistributed → their Bell off-rate climbs super-linearly → **cascading catastrophic failure**, all bonds release together (sawtooth). Time-between-failures actually *decreases* at 3 & 6 bonds vs 1 (machinery split too thin).
- **≥10 bonds:** time-to-failure *increases* significantly; **≥20 bonds** some runs never fail; **30 bonds: failure NEVER occurs** — a surviving bond ramps its force to hold the load while released neighbors re-engage (Fig 5A–C, the "rescue after two adhesions disengage" event).

**Why it beats what DCM does now:** DCM's current cadherin cluster dissolves only when the engaged-bond count m→0, with lifetime treated as *emergent* — but there is no validated statement of **how many parallel bonds a cluster needs to be robust**, nor a mechanistic account of *why* a small cluster collapses catastrophically while a large one is self-healing. This paper supplies exactly that: it is a first-principles demonstration (two independent models) that **cluster robustness has a threshold in parallel-bond count (~10), and self-healing/no-failure emerges by ~20–30**. This is directly usable to (a) set/justify a minimum bond count per DCM junction cluster from mechanism rather than a tuned number, and (b) reframe the compaction-RATE open problem: the memory note flags that aggregate-σ compaction is drag-limited and real 24–48 h compaction "needs cadherin T1 rearrangement escalation, not aggregate-σ." PFAC says the **rate at which a junction can bear and re-form load** is governed by parallel-bond load-sharing and re-engagement, not by a single bond's off-rate — so the rate-limiter DCM should be modeling is the **cluster-level PFAC re-engagement dynamics**, not a lumped junction off-rate.

### M2 — Contact **stress = force/area** as the DCM contact observable (force distribution over area)
**DCM module:** node-face penalty contact (Ericson) + aggregate surface tension σ; cell–cell junction interface.
**Change:** adopt the paper's central observable and its counter-intuitive decomposition (Fig 3C vs 3D): as adhesion area widens, **total transmitted force rises to a plateau while stress (force/area) falls monotonically** (~1700 → ~200 pN/µm²). Instrument DCM's junction/contact interfaces with a per-interface **σ_contact = Σ(bond force) / (contact patch area)** diagnostic, distinct from total interfacial force.
**Why it beats now:** DCM currently reasons about junctions via total interfacial force / aggregate σ. The paper shows that *total force and stress move in opposite directions* when contact area changes — a distinction DCM does not currently track. For confinement / spreading / compaction, the mechanically-relevant quantity for **rupture and remodeling** is the *stress* (localized peaks cause failure), not the total force. Adding the force-vs-stress split gives DCM a physically-grounded criterion for where a junction/contact will fail (high-stress small patches) vs hold (low-stress distributed patches).

### M3 — Catch-slip vs slip robustness (a *don't-bother* result, worth recording)
**DCM module:** Rakshit KU-4.2 cadherin catch-bond.
**Finding to bank:** the authors ran their adhesion model with **catch-slip** bonds (Bangasser 2013 form) vs pure slip and report *conclusions unchanged* — catch-slip raises mean force/lifetime/stress only in the smallest-area (single-bond) case, and even then stays below the large-area values. **Implication for DCM:** the *qualitative* PFAC/robustness conclusions are bond-law-insensitive; DCM should keep its full Rakshit catch-bond (mechanistic rule), but need not fear that the choice of catch- vs slip- flips the load-sharing behavior. This is reassurance, not a change.

## 2. EQUATIONS to port

The main text prints **no differential equations** (deferred to SI + prior Kim-lab papers). Only the following *relations* are stated; port at the junction-cluster / contact level.

**E1 — Bond as linear (Hookean) spring** (their clutch → DCM cadherin bond):
```
f_bond,i = k_bond · x_bond,i
```
Map: `k_bond` → DCM cadherin bond stiffness (Rakshit); `x_bond,i` → stretch of bond i across the cell–cell junction gap. (Paper: clutch spring, k_clutch; equilibrium chain length 50 nm.)

**E2 — Aggregate substrate/interface as one spring** (PFA "substrate domain"):
```
F_interface = k_int · x_int
```
Map: `k_int` → effective compliance of the shared load path (in DCM: the deforming cell surface / node-face contact patch the parallel cadherin bonds pull on); `x_int` → interface displacement. This is the shared elastic element that couples parallel bonds and *creates* the load-sharing (PFAC) — without a shared compliant load, parallel bonds are independent and PFAC vanishes. **Porting note:** DCM must ensure its junction cluster's parallel bonds pull on a **shared deformable patch** (the mesh face/node), else the compensation cannot emerge.

**E3 — Force-dependent (Bell/slip) off-rate** (already in DCM as Rakshit; transcribe for parity):
```
k_off = k_off,0 · exp(F_bond / F_b)
```
Map: `k_off,0` → DCM cadherin zero-force off-rate; `F_b` → Bell force scale; `F_bond` from E1. This is the instability driver: when one bond releases, surviving bonds' F_bond jumps → k_off climbs exponentially → cascade (M1). DCM already carries this via Rakshit; the paper's contribution is the **cluster-level consequence**, not the single-bond law.

**E4 — Motor force–velocity (Chan–Odde linear)** — *NOT for DCM* (FF/actomyosin only), recorded for completeness:
```
v_actin = v_unloaded · (1 − F_motor / F_stall)
```
No DCM surface-mesh analogue (DCM has no explicit retrograde flow). Belongs to FF.

**E5 — Stress definition** (port as M2 diagnostic):
```
σ_contact = ( Σ_i f_bond,i  over the contact/FA patch ) / ( A_patch )
```
Map: numerator = summed cadherin/contact bond forces on a DCM junction patch; `A_patch` = triangulated area of the contact face-set. This is the observable to add to DCM's junction/contact reporter.

**E6 — Bond-budget normalization** (protocol, port to sweeps):
```
A_patch × N_bond^max = const
```
Map: when sweeping DCM junction patch size, hold (patch area × max bonds/patch) constant so total available bonds is matched — clean way to isolate *distribution* effects from *bond-count* effects.

## 4. GEOMETRY / PROTOCOL to reproduce

Only one protocol is DCM-portable, and only as an *abstract* cluster test — the paper's geometry (thin 5×5×0.1 µm lamellipodium slab on a 2D triangulated gel) is **not** a DCM cell and must not be reproduced literally (violates native+full-compartment rule if dressed up as a "cell").

**P1 — Parallel-bond load-sharing test (PFAC), as a DCM junction-cluster unit test.**
Setup: two DCM surface patches (or one cell surface + a rigid counter-face) coupled by **N parallel cadherin catch-bonds sharing one compliant contact patch** (E2 shared spring). Drive with a steady pulling load (myosin-cortex contraction analogue or an imposed separation rate). Sweep **N = 1, 3, 6, 10, 15, 20, 30** bonds with total bond budget held constant (E6). Measure per-bond force, cluster force, time-to-failure, and rescue-event count over ~60 s. **Expected DCM result if the cluster model is correct:** sawtooth load-and-fail at N≤6, crossover to prolonged interaction at N≥10, self-healing (no complete failure) at N≈30. This is a clean, cheap, mechanism-level validation of DCM's existing load-sharing cluster — reproduces the paper's Fig 4/5 in DCM's own bond law.

**P2 — Contact area vs stress sweep (M2 instrumentation check).** On a single DCM cell pressed against a face (node-face contact) or a two-cell junction, vary the contact patch area and confirm **total contact force rises to a plateau while σ_contact = ΣF/A falls** (Fig 3C/D form). Verifies the force-vs-stress split is wired correctly.

*Not portable:* Arp2/3 branch-angle network assembly, retrograde-flow measurement band (y=4.5–5.0 µm), TFM on 90 kPa gel, CK-869 inhibition, migration-speed/directionality tracking — all FF or wet-lab, no DCM surface-mesh scenario.

## 6. RISKS / caveats

- **Wrong adhesion molecule & interface.** The paper's bonds are **integrin–laminin focal-adhesion clutches on a 2D elastic gel**. DCM's junctions are **cell–cell cadherin catch-bonds**. The PFAC *principle* (parallel stochastic bonds sharing a compliant load → threshold robustness + self-healing) is molecule-agnostic and transfers; the **numbers do not**. Adapt by porting only the *mechanism and qualitative regime*, keeping all DCM bond constants at the Rakshit KU-4.2 physiological values. Never overwrite a cadherin parameter with a clutch value.
- **2D substrate, not a 3D closed cell.** The elastic "substrate" is a flat Hookean triangulated mesh at z=0 — geometrically similar to DCM meshing but modeling the *ground*, not a deformable cell with cortex/membrane/nucleus/turgor. Any DCM reproduction must be a **junction-cluster sub-model on the real native full-compartment cell**, never a stripped 2D slab dressed up as a cell (would violate the native+full-compartment HARD rule).
- **Wrong cell type & regime.** H4 glioblastoma, mesenchymal migration on soft brain-stiffness gels — not MCF7, not an aggregate, not compaction. Migration/traction conclusions are out-of-regime for DCM; only the abstract load-sharing mechanism crosses over.
- **No F-actin dynamics; 20 s measurement window.** The agent-based model has no polymerization/turnover and contraction decays as the network compacts, so *all* quantities are read in the first 20 s (quasi-steady). This is a short-time, no-turnover regime — its *rate/timescale* numbers must **not** be cited for DCM's 24–48 h compaction problem (same discipline the memory notes already impose on aggregate-σ rate claims). Use the *mechanism* (PFAC), not any timescale.
- **SI-gated parameters.** k_bond, k_off,0, F_b, k_int, motor F-V constants are all in Suppl. Tables S1–S3, **not in the PDF**. Flag every quantitative port `NO_SI` until the SI is ingested; do not invent values to fill gates.
- **Physiological-baseline compliance.** If P1/P2 are run, they must sit on a DCM cell initialized at its physiological operating point (turgor, cortex γ, cytoplasm η all ON); the parallel-bond test is a *diagnostic on the real baseline*, not a bare spring toy.

---

### One-line bottom line
The only DCM-transferable content is the **PFAC principle** — parallel stochastic catch-bonds sharing one compliant contact load exhibit a **threshold (~10 bonds) beyond which the junction cluster stops failing catastrophically and becomes self-healing (~30)** — which validates and constrains DCM's cadherin load-sharing junction-cluster model and reframes the compaction-rate open problem around cluster-level re-engagement dynamics. Everything else is FF/substrate physics; numbers are SI-gated and heterologous.
