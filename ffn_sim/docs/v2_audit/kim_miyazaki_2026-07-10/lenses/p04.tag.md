# TAG-validation lens — p04

**Paper:** Yu, Li, Murrell, Kim (2018) — *Balance between Force Generation and Relaxation Leads to Pulsed Contraction of Actomyosin Networks*, Biophys. J. 115:2003–2013. DOI 10.1016/j.bpj.2018.10.008.

## Paper kind

**simulation** (agent-based Brownian-dynamics model output; Kim-lab Cytosim-class fine-grained actomyosin network). This is the **FF engine's own model class** — explicit cylindrical F-actin (140 nm segments), myosin thick filaments (4 arms × 8 heads, parallel-cluster-model force-velocity + force-dependent unbinding), ACPs with Bell's-law off-rate, treadmilling turnover, and angle-dependent buckling severing. Consequences for use:

- The paper's **own numbers are comparison-only, never a truth source** (they are model output, not measurement).
- Its **cited in-vivo targets** (pulse size 2–4 µm, duration 40–100 s; from Nishikawa 2017, ref 36) are the only experiment-grade numbers, and those enter as **OVERLAY targets, not fit** — and they belong to the cited paper, not this one.
- Its greatest KB value is as a **sibling/oracle for FF mechanisms** (severing law Eq. 1, PCM motor, treadmilling turnover) and as a qualitative emergent-behavior cross-check (turnover suppresses clustering; intermediate connectivity maximizes contraction; pulsed contraction requires force-generation ⇄ relaxation balance). No new analytic ground-truth oracle.

## Coverage vs existing gates

- **Motor speed ⟨v_M⟩ 0.6–1.8 µm/s** — no dedicated gate, but the **Hill force-velocity oracle** + `PARAM-F_stall_motor` (2 pN) + `PARAM-v_unloaded` (100 nm/s, KU-2.4/3.12) already anchor FF motor kinematics. The paper's ~1 µm/s ensemble speed is a *network-emergent* speed (retrograde-flow / clustering transport), an order of magnitude above the 100 nm/s clutch retrograde-flow anchor, and is **simulation-derived** — so it **CONFIRMS the order of magnitude but does not TIGHTEN** any band; it is a soft FF cross-check, not a contract.
- **Cortex turnover → homogeneity** — directly relevant to `VG-U3-cortex-multifilament` (KB-3.18/3.19/3.20) and to the H.7 γ-floor/`VG-H3-KU35` work, where cortex aggregation-vs-smoothness under turnover is exactly the phenomenon at stake. The paper's qualitative law (higher turnover ⇒ more homogeneous, less clustered) **CONFIRMS/contextualizes** the FF cortex behavior but adds **no quantitative band**.
- **Pulsed contraction / cluster size + lifetime** — **not covered** by any existing gate. Candidate below.
- **Severing (buckling-induced)** — no existing gate or parameter; FF has no severing mechanism registered. Mechanism candidate below (mechanism, not a band).
- **PCM motor + Bell's-law ACP** — already the architectural stance (MC-U2 Bell-Evans off-rate; Hill/PCM motor). This paper is a **DUPLICATE endorsement**, not new coverage.

Nothing here **CHALLENGES** an existing band.

## New gate candidates (all *candidate — PI-gate review*)

1. **proposed_vg_id: VG-FF-pulsed-cluster-size-lifetime** *(candidate, PI review)*
   - observable: emergent pulsed actomyosin cluster diameter and lifetime in an FF cortex/network run at the force-generation ⇄ relaxation balance point (turnover + severing ON).
   - band_or_value (OVERLAY, never fit): cluster diameter **2–4 µm**, lifetime **40–100 s** (in-vivo morphogenesis reference, Nishikawa 2017 via this paper).
   - condition: fine-grained actomyosin network, ⟨L_f⟩≈1 µm, intermediate connectivity, turnover k_t,A ~O(30–70 s⁻¹), angle-dependent severing ON.
   - engine: **FF**.
   - caveat: the numeric target is a **cited experimental reference**, not this paper's measurement, and the emergent pulse is a *narrow-regime* result — register strictly as OVERLAY, never as a pass/fail band to tune toward. Better staged as a qualitative "does pulsed contraction emerge at all" gate than a tight numeric contract.
   - source: dossier text p.5/9; Fig 4/5.

2. **proposed_vg_id: VG-FF-turnover-homogenizes** *(candidate, PI review; qualitative/sanity)*
   - observable: network heterogeneity Q_A (SD of segment-density map) vs actin turnover rate.
   - band_or_value: **monotonic decrease** of Q_A as k_t,A rises (no aggregation at high turnover); sign/trend gate, no magnitude band.
   - condition: FF cortex/network, fixed motor + ACP density, turnover swept.
   - engine: **FF**.
   - rationale: directly de-risks the FF cortex "collapse vs smooth" concern (native cortex stability under treadmilling); a defensible *sign-sense* contract, not a magnitude one.
   - source: Fig 1b.

I do **not** propose gates on ⟨v_M⟩, cluster %-of-actin, or severing localization: the first is a soft order-of-magnitude cross-check already bounded by the Hill oracle, and the latter two are simulation-internal fractions with no measurable in-vivo counterpart at our single-cell scale.

## Oracle candidates

- **None as an analytic ground-truth oracle.** The paper's equations are either (a) *analysis definitions* (Eq. 2/3 motor speed, Eq. 4 g(r) radial distribution, Eq. 5 velocity persistency) — useful as **FF post-processing / diagnostic definitions**, not truth oracles — or (b) *phenomenological rate laws* (Eq. 1 angle-dependent severing k_s,A = k⁰_s,A·exp(θ/λ)) which is a **candidate FF mechanism to port**, not a closed-form ground truth.
- The mechanistic laws it uses (Bell's-law ACP off-rate, PCM/Hill motor force-velocity) are **already our oracles** (Bell-Evans, Hill). This paper endorses them; nothing new to implement.
- **Suggested reuse:** adopt Eq. 4 g(r) and Eq. 5 ⟨cos φ⟩ as FF network-homogeneity / contraction diagnostics (measurement-protocol alignment), and Eq. 1 as an optional FF severing mechanism — both PI-gated, both mechanism/diagnostic additions rather than oracle bands.
