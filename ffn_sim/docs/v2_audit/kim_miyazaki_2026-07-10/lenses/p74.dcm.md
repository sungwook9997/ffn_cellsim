# DCM lens — p74  (Mulla, Jung, Kim, Tans, Koenderink 2022, "Weak catch bonds make strong networks", Nat Mater 21:1019)
*(authored by Lead main-loop; subagent lens lost to session-limit — reconstructed from full dossier)*

## 1. Mechanisms to adopt  (moderate–indirect for DCM)
- **Catch-bond "dissociation on demand" for cadherin junctions.** The paper's central emergent principle: catch bonds unbind from low-stress regions, rebind in high-stress regions, homogenize the per-bond force distribution, and thereby postpone crack initiation — making a *weaker* bond ensemble yield a *stronger, more deformable* network. DCM models cell–cell cohesion as ONE cadherin catch-bond junction interface with load-sharing clusters ([[feedback-cell-cell-contact-junction-mediated]], Rakshit KU-4.2). This paper says: a mobile, load-sharing catch-bond cluster should self-homogenize junction force and resist interfacial rupture better than a static/slip cluster — a direct design principle for the DCM junction and for the aggregate-compaction / T1-rearrangement rework ([[project-dcm-compaction-turgor-blocked]]).
- **Mobility + N≳10 + fast-bind/slow-unbind are prerequisites.** The catch advantage is emergent and *vanishes* if crosslinkers are immobile or N<10. DCM junction clusters must therefore allow bond mobility (rebind at new sites) and a minimum cluster size for the catch benefit to appear — a concrete constraint on the junction model.

## 2. Equations / laws to port
- **Bell–Evans double-exponential off-rate** (Eq.1): `k_off(f) = k_catch0·exp(−f/f_catch) + k_slip0·exp(+f/f_slip)`. This is the correct force-dependent off-rate for a catch bond and maps directly onto the DCM cadherin junction off-rate (currently our junction uses catch-bond kinetics; this is the canonical 2-pathway form).
- **Nearest-neighbour load sharing** (Eq.2): `f_i = (l_i/Σl_i)·σ·N` — force on a junction bond ∝ gap to neighbours. Encodes why homogenizing the force distribution suppresses cracks; usable in the DCM junction cluster as the load-sharing rule.

## 4. Geometry / protocol to reproduce
- Strain-controlled shear to rupture of a load-sharing bond ensemble; measure rupture stress & strain vs bond kinetics (catch vs slip). DCM analog: pull two adhered cells apart, measure interface rupture stress/strain vs junction kinetics.

## 5. Validation targets for DCM observables
- Catch (weak) junction ensemble ruptures at ~2–3× higher stress AND ~2× higher strain than slip — a junction-mechanics target.
- Immobile or small (N<10) clusters lose the advantage — a negative control for the DCM junction implementation.

## 6. Risks / caveats
- The system is actin+α-actinin-4 networks, not cadherin junctions — the transfer is by analogy (the authors explicitly generalize to cell–cell/cell–matrix adhesions). Use the *principle* + Bell-Evans form + load-sharing rule; do not import α-actinin numbers as cadherin parameters. Aligns with the mechanistic-junction hard rule. Score ≈ 2/5 for DCM (principle-level).
