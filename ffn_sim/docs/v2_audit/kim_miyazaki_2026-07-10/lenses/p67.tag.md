# TAG-validation lens — p67

**Paper:** Lee, Pelz, Ferrer, Kim, Lang, Kamm (2009) — *Cytoskeletal Deformation at High Strains and the Role of Cross-link Unfolding or Unbinding.* Cell. Mol. Bioeng. 2(1):28–38. DOI 10.1007/s12195-009-0048-8.

## Paper kind

**experimental** (primary), with an embedded **simulation** cross-check.

The validation-grade numbers come from a single-molecule **optical-tweezers force-spectroscopy (OTFS)** pulling assay on one filamin-A cross-link between two actin filaments — a direct in-vitro measurement, so its numbers are **overlay / cross-check targets, never fit targets** (project hard rule). A companion reconstituted F-actin-network drag assay is also experimental. The third leg is a **3-D Brownian-dynamics agent-based actin network** (Kim/Hwang/Kamm 2009 model) whose outputs (bead force ~150 pN, abrupt drops) are **simulation → comparison only, never a truth source**; its Bell inputs (k_off⁰, x) are borrowed from the experimental family, so they are usable as *parameters* but the sim force curve is not a gate.

This is the **filamin sibling** of **Ferrer 2008** (ref 9), which the KB already cites for the α-actinin `k_off0` re-anchor (0.4→0.066 s⁻¹). Same lab, same OTFS method, same DFS/HS analysis — so this is a familiar, trusted lineage for the corpus.

## Coverage vs existing gates

- **No existing gate covers cross-linker (filamin/ABP) rupture force or force-dependent off-rate.** The closest rows:
  - **VG-U2-catch-slip-bond** (`372120daec5d814ab61fecca9f92d356`) — but that is **integrin–fibronectin** (lifetime peak ~30 pN, catch bond), a *different* molecule and a *catch* (not slip) bond. p67's filamin is a **slip bond**. Not the same observable; no conflict.
  - **VG-U3-cortex-multifilament** (`372120daec5d81f388e5d43867b7539f`, blocked, Phase-2 entry) — its band says "Bell-Evans crosslink dynamics active." p67 supplies the *actual filamin Bell parameters and rupture-force target* that this gate's crosslink dynamics should reproduce. So p67 **feeds** this gate rather than duplicating it.
  - **MC-U3-cell-integration** caveat names KU-3.19 crosslinkers; **MC-U1-ecm-network** uses `PARAM-k_xl` crosslink stiffness. Neither carries a rupture-force / off-rate contract.
- **Oracle side:** the KB has a **Bell-Evans off-rate oracle** (philosophy list) but there is **no filamin-parameterized cross-check config** on disk (oracles dir has ecm/junction/spheroid, no crosslinker-rupture). p67 is a candidate to parameterize that oracle.
- Verdict: p67 is **new coverage for FF cross-linker mechanics**, not a tighten/challenge of any current band. It CONFIRMS the general Bell-Evans slip-bond framing the KB already adopts (MC-U2 caveat: "off-rate is Bell-Evans, NOT Metropolis").

## New gate candidates (all **candidate — PI-gate review**; FF engine)

1. **VG-FF-filamin-rupture-force** (candidate)
   - Observable: peak tensile force to rupture a single filamin-A cross-link between two actin filaments, pulled at 400–2000 pN/s.
   - Band/value: **70 ± 23 pN** (unbinding) as an overlay target; a defensible contract band ≈ **[45, 95] pN** (mean ± ~1σ).
   - Condition: single filamin cross-link, high loading rate, RT, ~axial pull (angle < 45°).
   - Engine: **FF** (fine-grained crosslinker force-extension), cross-check vs **Bell-Evans oracle**.
   - Source: Abstract; Fig. 4; p.33. "unbinding at 70 ± 23 pN."
   - Caveat: overlay-only; this is a reconstitution single-molecule value (oracle-style cross-check), not a cellular observable.

2. **VG-FF-filamin-offrate-angle** (candidate)
   - Observable: rupture force vs inter-filament pulling angle — should stay ~flat then **drop sharply above ~45°** (shear/torsion weakening). α-actinin comparator holds to ~70°.
   - Band/value: monotone-flat below ~45°, significant drop above; qualitative-shape contract (not a single number).
   - Engine: **FF** (emergent geometry of the crosslinker bond).
   - Source: Fig. 5a; p.34/37.
   - Caveat: qualitative-form gate; keep as sanity-check tier, PI to decide if it earns a numeric band.

3. **VG-FF-Ig-unfold-period** (candidate, low priority)
   - Observable: Ig-domain unfolding sawtooth contour-length increment.
   - Band/value: **28 ± 5 nm** (theory ~34 nm).
   - Engine: **FF** only if domain-unfolding is ever modeled (FF currently treats crosslinkers as Bell slip bonds, no internal Ig unfolding). Flag as **out-of-scope for current FF** — record as SourceEvidence, not a gate, unless FF adds unfolding.
   - Source: Fig. 3c; p.32–33.

I do **not** propose a gate on the BD simulation bead force (~150 pN) — it is a simulation output, comparison-only.

## Oracle candidates

- **Bell slip-bond off-rate** `k_off(F) = k_off⁰ · exp(F·x / k_BT)` — the KB already has a Bell-Evans oracle; p67 supplies a **filamin parameterization** (k_off⁰=0.115 s⁻¹, x=0.416 nm) to instantiate a crosslinker cross-check config (none exists on disk today). **Strong candidate** — it is exactly the FF mechanistic rule (Bell, not Metropolis).
- **DFS / HS barrier relation** `ΔG = ½ κ_m x‡²` and the **two-slope ln(loading-rate) law** (rupture-F ∝ ln r, slope change signals an outer-barrier suppression) — a closed-form analytic cross-check for FF crosslinker rupture-force-vs-loading-rate behavior. **Candidate oracle** (dynamic-force-spectroscopy oracle), useful to verify FF reproduces the correct rate-dependence rather than a single rupture force.
- Ig-unfolding contour-length `ΔL_c = N_aa · d_aa` (96 × 3.5 Å ≈ 34 nm) — trivial geometric identity; not worth an oracle unless FF models unfolding.
