# TAG-validation lens — p62

**Paper:** Kubota et al. 2018, *Processive Nanostepping of Formin mDia1 Loosely Coupled with Actin Polymerization*, Nano Letters 18, 6617–6624. DOI 10.1021/acs.nanolett.8b03277.

## Paper kind

**experimental** (single-molecule in-vitro reconstitution; dual-trap optical tweezers "actin dumbbell"). NOT a simulation and NOT an analytic-oracle paper. The only closed-forms are a *statistical detection/dwell-time model* (eqs 1–5: Gamma-order sums + step-merging probabilities), which is an artifact-test estimator, not a physics force law. Consequence per our philosophy: its numbers are **overlay / on-demand cross-check targets for the FF barbed-end-growth kernel**, never fit targets, and it yields **no new analytic oracle**. It supplies mechanistic constraint on formin mDia1 elongation kinetics (step size + stepping rate at controlled load and [G-actin]).

## Coverage vs existing gates

- **No existing gate covers formin/barbed-end elongation stepping.** The closest KB touchpoints:
  - `VG-U3-lamellipodia-separation` (blocked, Phase-2 entry) references *Mueller 2017 Hill (w~2)* and *Bieling 2016 branching* — that is **Arp2/3 branched lamellipodial polymerization**, a different nucleator/mechanism than processive formin capping. No overlap on step size or formin rate.
  - `VG-U3-gactin-pool` (blocked) conserves the shared G-actin pool but sets no elongation-kinetics band.
  - `PARAM-v_unloaded` (10–100 nm/s, KU-2.4/3.12) is **retrograde actin flow**, not barbed-end assembly speed — do not conflate.
  - The FF `barbed_end_growth_kernel` (KB-3.6 ratchet; see memory `project-ff-filament-length-growth`) is the runtime target this data constrains, but it has **no ValidationGate wired to it** in the current state snapshot.
- **Verdict: this observable is NEW.** The paper neither confirms nor challenges any current band; it opens an uncovered corner (single-formin elongation kinetics under load).

## New gate candidates (all: candidate — PI-gate review; FF engine; overlay/cross-check only)

1. **`VG-FF-formin-step-size` (candidate)**
   - Observable: barbed-end advance per formin FH2 translocation event in the FF elongation kernel, resolved as a step-size distribution.
   - Band/value: unitary forward step **≈ +2.7–2.8 nm** (one actin subunit half-repeat), unitary backward **≈ −2.4 nm**; multi-steps at integer multiples (5.4, 8.1 nm ideal) permitted, not forbidden.
   - Condition: single mDia1, low [G-actin] (~40–75 nM, near c_c=64 nM), ~4–6 pN load, 26 °C.
   - Engine: FF (barbed_end_growth_kernel). Measurable at our scale — this IS the native filament length increment.
   - Source: Fig 3b, +2.8/−2.4 nm; corroborated ~2.7 nm subunit (Young 2018 iSCAT).
   - Note: This is really a **structural-invariant sanity gate** (step ≈ actin subunit rise), the strongest, most model-independent piece — a good "does the kernel advance by a physically correct increment" check.

2. **`VG-FF-formin-step-rate` (candidate)**
   - Observable: forward stepping rate constant k vs [G-actin] in the FF kernel.
   - Band/value: **k ≈ 0.9 s⁻¹ at 40 nM → 1.4 s⁻¹ at 75 nM** (concentration-dependent, association-limited); backward k **≈ 0.56–0.62 s⁻¹**, concentration-independent. Effective barbed-end elongation ≈ k·step ≈ 2.5–4 nm/s at these low [G-actin].
   - Condition: as above, ~4–6 pN, 26 °C.
   - Engine: FF. Overlay-only — the *sign-sense* (forward rate scales with [G-actin], backward does not) is the defensible mechanistic contract; the exact 0.92/1.4 numbers are low-[G-actin] anchors, not physiological-[G-actin] extrapolations.
   - Source: Fig 3d.
   - Caveat: assay [G-actin] (40–75 nM) is **near the critical concentration**, far below the physiological cytosolic G-actin pool (~µM). Per the physiological-baseline hard rule, do NOT import 0.92/1.4 s⁻¹ as the production elongation rate — it is a low-conc calibration point for the *concentration dependence*, and the production kernel must run at the physiological G-actin setpoint. Flag for PI.

3. **`VG-FF-formin-loose-coupling` (candidate, weaker / optional)**
   - Observable: fraction of multi-subunit (2×/3×) steps produced by the FF kernel.
   - Band/value: multi-step fraction rises with [G-actin] (fwd 38→46% for 2×; ~2→9% for 3× between 40 and 75 nM) — i.e. stepping is **loosely** coupled, not one-step-per-monomer.
   - Engine: FF. Only propose if the kernel resolves sub-events; otherwise this is a mechanism-refinement note, not a gate. Mark low priority.

## Oracle candidates

**None (no physics oracle).** Eqs 1–5 are a *statistical detection model* — eq 1 is a sum of Gamma-order-1/2/3 dwell distributions f(t)=aA·e^{−kt}+aB·kt·e^{−kt}+aC·k²t²·e^{−kt}; eqs 3–5 are step-merging probabilities p_single=e^{−kT}, p_double=kT·e^{−kT}, p_triple=1−e^{−kT}−kT·e^{−kT} at temporal resolution T. These validate *step-detection*, not cell/filament mechanics, so they are not candidates for the analytic-oracle shelf (Helfrich / Young-Laplace / Bell-Evans / Hill / Kelvin-Voigt). One narrow reuse: if FF ever step-detects a simulated elongation trace, eq 4/5 could be an *analysis-side artifact estimator* — but that is a data-analysis utility, not a validation oracle. The assay's F=6πηrv (Stokes drag) is just trap calibration, already trivially known.

Note: this is **not a force–velocity law** (tension held ~constant at 4–6 pN, elongation treated force-independent), so it does **not** feed the Hill force-velocity oracle. It constrains only the low/zero-load elongation kinetics.
