## Coverage vs existing gates

- **L_p (item 1) — NOT covered for actin.** The KB has `PARAM-ell_p = 17 µm` and `PARAM-kappa_fiber` but both are **type-I collagen** (KU-1.1, ECM fiber), a different molecule. Our FF cortex uses actin filaments and a stated actin persistence-length anchor in `project-ff-filament-length-growth` / KB-3.18; item 1 (5–15 µm) **CONFIRMS the actin-L_p range already in play** and is consistent with the analytic bending oracle κ = k_B·T·L_p. It does not tighten a registered gate because no actin-L_p gate exists — it is a supporting cross-check for the FF bending oracle. No conflict.
- **Contractile ring / cytokinesis (items 2–4) — NOT covered.** No gate in the KB targets cytokinetic-ring self-organization or constriction; the closest contract, `MC-U5-collective`, is spheroid-scale, and the DCM division work (memory: `project-dcm-8h-autonomous-mandate` Phase 5) has no registered ring-formation or constriction gate. Candidate below (PI-gate review).
- **Spindle mechanics (items 5–9) — OUT OF SCOPE, no coverage, do not propose.** We do not model the mitotic spindle; these are active-nematic-material references with no single-cell-cortex observable. The Poisson ratio ~2.0 and the elastic/viscous/active-elastic crossover are interesting cross-checks for a viscoelastic material model but map to no current DCM/FF observable at our scale. Note only.
- **Cardiac sarcomere / SPOC / Frank–Starling (items 10–12, 14) — OUT OF SCOPE.** Striated-muscle observables, not MCF7 mechanobiology. No coverage, no candidate.
- **Cytoplasm/membrane viscoelasticity (`VG-H10-cytoplasm`, `VG-H8-membrane`) —** the spindle |G*| and liposome-yield numbers are the same *class* of observable but on the *wrong system* (spindle / reconstituted vesicle, not MCF7 cytoplasm/membrane); they do not confirm, tighten, or challenge those gates' bands.

## New gate candidates

All below are **candidate, PI-gate review** — staged only, since new gates are PI-authored. I am conservative: only propose where the observable is measurable at OUR single-cell scale AND the band is defensible from a primary source (not invented). I do **not** propose spindle or sarcomere gates (out of scope).

1. **proposed_vg_id: VG-DCM-cytokinetic-ring-confinement** (candidate, PI-gate review)
   - observable: does a single equatorial contractile actin(-myosin) ring self-organize under cell-sized confinement?
   - band_or_value: ring forms when confinement diameter **< 20 µm** (≈ actin L_p 5–15 µm); for diameter ≫ L_p, no single ring (bulk bundle network). Threshold is a *qualitative transition*, not a tunable number.
   - condition: confined actin + depletion bundler ± HMM; DCM/FF cytokinesis or confined-cortex geometry.
   - engine: DCM (division) or FF (confined filament) — **primary DOI Miyazaki 2015 Nat. Cell Biol. [5]**, Fig 3-3c (p20).
   - caveat: reconstituted-system datum → **overlay/cross-check only**, and the 20 µm is a persistence-length-set geometric threshold, so it should be *predicted* by the model (ring emerges when R_confine ≲ L_p) rather than fit. Reasonable as a mechanism-existence gate.

2. **proposed_vg_id: VG-DCM-ring-constriction-scaling** (candidate, PI-gate review, weaker)
   - observable: cytokinetic-ring constriction rate vs initial ring diameter.
   - band_or_value: constriction rate **∝ initial ring diameter** (recapitulates in-vivo scaling) at high HMM.
   - condition: confined actomyosin ring, HMM 25 µM.
   - engine: DCM division. Primary [5]. Weaker because the review gives only proportionality, no absolute rate/units — register only if DCM cytokinesis is a live target.

No FF-scale gate proposed for L_p because it is better handled as a **parameter + bending oracle cross-check** (below), not a standalone gate.

## Oracle candidates

- **No new closed-form oracle.** The review transcribes no equations; the SPOC unit-sarcomere ODEs and the myofibril coupling live in refs [2,3,38] and are mean-field cross-bridge population dynamics (not our fine-grained target), so even if fetched they would be a *muscle* model, not a single-cell-cortex oracle.
- **Cross-check only:** (a) the **worm-like-chain bending energy** E_bend = (κ/2)∫(∂θ/∂s)² ds with κ = k_B·T·L_p — this is already our FF bending physics; item 1 supplies the actin L_p input, reinforcing the existing κL/2R² / κq⁴ Helfrich-style bending oracles rather than adding a new one. (b) The **ring-at-equator argument** (rigid rod when R_confine ≲ L_p minimizes bending energy at the droplet equator) is a qualitative bending-energy prediction the FF/DCM ring could be checked against, but it is not a closed form to implement.
