# TAG-validation lens — p08

**Paper:** Sasmal, Miyazaki, Carlier-Grynkorn, Tran (2026), *Chimeras of kinesin-6 and kinesin-14 reveal head-neck-tail domain functions and dysfunctions that lead to aneuploidy in fission yeast*, Current Biology 36:415–425. DOI 10.1016/j.cub.2025.11.073.

## Paper kind

**experimental** (in-vivo molecular genetics + quantitative live-cell fluorescence microscopy in *S. pombe*). Explicitly not a model: "No in-house code was used in this study." No governing equations, no force laws, no bond-kinetics, no in-vitro reconstitution/force-spectroscopy. Therefore every number below is, at most, an **overlay/cross-check target** — never a fit target, and none is a candidate analytic oracle (paper contains no closed-form law).

Domain-mismatch note up front: this paper is about the **mitotic spindle** (microtubules + kinesin-6/-14 motors) inside a **closed-mitosis yeast nucleus**. `ffn_cellsim`'s validation surface is single-cell **actomyosin cortex + focal-adhesion clutch + cadherin junction + fibrous ECM + spheroid/necrosis** (DCM surface-mesh cell; FF fine-grained *actin* filaments). There is **no MT-spindle module, no kinesin motor, no nuclear-envelope-tension module** in the current gate/param/contract set. Relevance to the TAG/KB is therefore **Low** and no gate/param/oracle is proposed.

## Coverage vs existing gates

None of the paper's observables is covered by, confirms, tightens, or challenges any current gate:

- Spindle sliding velocities (6–40 nm/s) are **MT/kinesin** speeds, not the **actin retrograde-flow / myosin** speeds behind `PARAM-v_unloaded` (100 nm/s, actin) or `VG-U2` traction gates. Different filament, different motor, different mechanism — not comparable.
- Final spindle length (9.75 µm) and MTs/pole (~25) are yeast-mitotic-apparatus geometry; the KB's length scales are MCF7 cell radius (`PARAM-cell_radius` 10 µm), cortex thickness, ECM mesh size — unrelated.
- The speculated closed-mitosis **nuclear-envelope tensile resistive force** touches `MC-H9-nucleus` / `VG-H9-nucleus` only *conceptually*; the paper gives **no magnitude, no modulus, no equation**, so it neither confirms nor challenges the E_nuc 1–10 kPa band.
- Aneuploidy / mini-chromosome-loss frequencies are genetic-segregation phenotypes with no mechanobiology observable at our single-cell scale.

## New gate candidates

**None.** No proposed ValidationGate. Every candidate observable requires a **spindle/microtubule–kinesin module that does not exist** in DCM or FF; a gate written now would have no engine to measure it and no defensible band at our (actomyosin single-cell) scale. Proposing one would violate the "a gate must be a real, measurable observable at OUR scale" rule. If the project ever adds an MT-spindle engine (Cytosim's native domain — FF's physics class covers filaments + directional motors + antiparallel sliding), items #1/#3/#5/#7 above would be the natural overlay targets — flag as **future-module, PI-gate review**, not staged now.

## Param candidates

**None registerable.** The paper's numeric "parameters" are (a) imaging/assay settings (temperature, laser power, Z-step — not physics) and (b) protein domain boundaries in residues (aa 2–399 head, etc. — sequence annotation, not a mechanical constant).
