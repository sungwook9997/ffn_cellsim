# TAG-validation lens — p47

**Paper:** Yu, Kim, Rajagopal (2022) "Role of actin filaments and cis binding in cadherin clustering and patterning," *PLOS Comput Biol* 18(7):e1010257. DOI 10.1371/journal.pcbi.1010257.
**Lab:** Taeyoon Kim (Purdue) + Vijay Rajagopal (Melbourne). Same Kim-lab lineage that seeds the current DCM cadherin-cluster+maturation redesign ([[project-dcm-cadherin-cluster-redesign]]).

## Paper kind

**simulation** (agent-based / Brownian-dynamics, 2D rule-based kinetic model). This is decisive for how its numbers may be used: **every headline quantity is a MODEL OUTPUT, not a measurement** — cluster sizes, %trans/%cis, MSD, K_D^cis, τ_cluster∝[cad]² are all emergent from the authors' own swept rate constants. Per project philosophy these are **comparison-only, never a truth source and never a fit/gate target**. The only validation-grade *experimental* numbers in the paper are values it **re-cites from other papers** (Mander's 0.076, α-catenin ABD 1 µM, F-actin turnover ~20 s / ~2 min, PALM ~50 nm clusters) — those are secondary citations and, if wanted, should be registered against their **original** sources, not against p47.

Additional caveat: the model is **force-free** (hard-rejection excluded volume, stochastic rate firing) with **rigid/immobile** projected 2D actin and **no maturation escalation / no T1 rearrangement**. It cannot serve as a stress, tension, compaction, or junction-force oracle. It is a *mechanism + parameter-range* reference.

## Coverage vs existing gates

- **Cell-cell junction** is held by **MC-U4-junction-cadherin** (E-cadherin **catch-bond**, full KU-4.2 Rakshit-2012) with gates VG-U4-contact-angle, VG-U4-junction-force (~1–10 nN mature contact), VG-U4-monolayer-stress. **None of p47's observables overlap these** — our gates are junction *force / contact geometry / stress*; p47 is *cadherin cluster count, cluster spacing, cadherin diffusivity, %trans/%cis*. p47 neither CONFIRMS nor CHALLENGES any existing band; it is **orthogonal** (a finer, sub-junction observable layer our contracts do not yet resolve).
- **PARAM-N_cad** (default 100 cad/contact, KU-4.11/4.17) — p47's cluster-size outputs (up to ~600–700 monomers per cluster, multiple clusters per µm²) are same-order-of-magnitude *context* for N_cad but are **model outputs**, so they do not tighten or supersede it.
- **PARAM-gamma_junction** (0.3 mN/m) — p47 gives no junction tension; no interaction.
- No existing gate covers **cadherin cluster size**, **cadherin diffusion / sub-diffusion**, or **cluster spacing**. Those are new observables — see below.

## New gate candidates

All are flagged **candidate, PI-gate review** and additionally **NOT MEASURABLE AT OUR SCALE YET** (our DCM junction is a coarse mesh-contact interface, not resolved cadherin agents; FF does not model cadherins). They are staged as *future* mechanistic-comparison gates only if a cadherin-resolved junction module is built. Because the target values are simulation outputs, any such gate would be a **cross-check against p47's mechanism, not a lit-anchored band** — so bands below are deliberately left as "reproduce trend," not hard numbers.

1. **proposed VG-junction-cad-cluster-density-law** — observable: max cadherin cluster size vs cadherin surface density [cad]. Band: **monotonic increase with a threshold onset around [cad]≈800–1200 µm⁻²** at weak cis affinity (trend, not magnitude). Engine: DCM (cadherin-resolved junction, not yet built) / FF. Source: Fig 2C, p7. *Comparison-only — p47 is simulation.*
2. **proposed VG-junction-cad-cluster-lifetime-scaling** — observable: cluster lifetime vs cadherin density. Candidate law **τ_cluster ∝ [cad]²** (quadratic). Engine: DCM. Source: Fig 2F, p7. Note: this is an *emergent scaling from the model*, attractive as a mechanistic cross-check but **not an analytic ground truth** — do NOT elevate to an oracle.
3. **proposed VG-cortex-cad-subdiffusion** — observable: membrane-protein MSD exponent under cortical-actin corralling. Band: **sub-diffusive (MSD slope < 1) at increasing cortical actin density**. Engine: FF (fine-grained cortex + diffusing membrane protein). Source: Fig 3C/D, p8. This one is the most defensible as a *native FF* target because corralling can emerge from FF's real dynamic cortex (p47's actin is rigid, so FF would be a stronger model, not a copy).

None of these should be authored as contracts now — stage as candidates pending (a) a cadherin-resolved junction module and (b) PI sign-off on whether a simulation-sourced band is acceptable at all.

## Oracle candidates

- **L_F = h_actin·tan φ** (Eq 4) — trivial geometric projection, not a physics oracle. Skip.
- **p_ass = 1 − exp(−kΔt)** (Eq 3) — standard Poisson per-step firing; this is exactly the discretization our Bell-Evans/kinetic firing already uses. It is a *method-consistency note*, not a new oracle.
- **τ_cluster ∝ [cad]²** — emergent simulation scaling, **not closed-form ground truth**; per the "oracles are analytic ground truth, not simulation" rule it **must not** become a sanctioned oracle. Keep as an optional cross-check only.
- **Net: no new analytic oracle.**
