# DCM lens — p22

**Paper:** Nam, Lin, Kim, Chaudhuri — *Cellular Pushing Forces during Mitosis Drive Mitotic Elongation in Collagen Gels*, Adv. Sci. 2021, 8, 2000403 (DOI 10.1002/advs.202000403).

**One-line for DCM:** This paper is a near-exact conceptual twin of the DCM engine — a coarse-grained closed **membrane with a volume-conservation constraint** deformed by an **applied cytokinetic-ring inward force** plus an **applied spindle outward polar force**, with node-vs-ECM excluded-volume repulsion — and it hands us a *validated, mechanistic division-force protocol* (the single biggest open DCM lever: cell division / cytokinetic ring) with concrete force magnitudes (100 pN/side) and elongation targets (~20% ring-only, ~40% ring+spindle). It does NOT help cortical-γ generation or cadherin T1 compaction; its power is division + confinement/ECM-pushing.

---

## 1. MECHANISMS to adopt

### M1 — **Division module: cytokinetic-ring line-force + spindle polar-force under volume conservation** (HIGH — the marquee lever)
DCM lists "cell division / cytokinetic ring" as an *open* problem. This paper gives the exact fine-grained recipe DCM is missing:
- **Cytokinetic ring** = a constant **inward** force `F_Cyto` applied only to membrane nodes inside an equatorial band (width ±0.5 µm about the equatorial plane), with the force **switched off** for nodes that have already come within 1 µm of the mitotic (division) axis — i.e. the ring stops pulling once the furrow closes there. Total 100 pN per side.
- **Spindle (interpolar + astral MT)** = a constant **outward** force `F_Is` applied to membrane nodes inside a polar band (within 5 µm of the mitotic axis), pointing along ±(division axis). Total 100 pN per side.
- **Volume conservation** couples them: the ring's equatorial pinch, at constant enclosed volume, *elongates* the cell along the division axis; the spindle adds direct axial push. Ring-alone gives ~20% elongation, ring+spindle ~40%.

**Why it beats DCM now:** DCM currently has no division mechanism at all. This is not a lumped "prescribe two daughter spheres" shortcut — it is a mechanistic, node-level force field (band geometry + constant force + on/off furrow rule) that lets the daughter shape *emerge* from membrane elasticity + volume constraint + ECM back-reaction, which is exactly the DCM fine-grained ethos. The ring force is applied to the *actual furrow nodes*, and cytokinesis proceeds by furrow ingression, not by imposed topology.

**Fine-grained upgrade beyond the paper (respect the mechanistic rule):** the paper applies F_Cyto as a *constant* prescribed force. DCM should instead source it from a **contractile-ring line tension** `T_ring` acting on the equatorial edge loop, so the local nodal force = `T_ring · (curvature vector of the ring loop)` — i.e. a Young-Laplace-style line-tension pull that self-adjusts as the ring shrinks (higher curvature → higher inward force → runaway furrowing), rather than a fixed 100 pN. That converts the paper's phenomenological constant into a derivable line-tension law and matches DCM's Rakshit/Bell mechanistic posture. Calibrate `T_ring` so total equatorial force ≈100 pN/side at the initial 10 µm radius (see §3).

### M2 — **ECM back-reaction / confinement via node-face excluded-volume repulsion** (MEDIUM — DCM already has the machinery, this validates it)
The paper's Eq 8 one-sided harmonic repulsion between membrane elements and ECM fiber/crosslinker segments is *exactly* DCM's node-face penalty (Ericson) contact model. The paper shows that a dividing/pushing cell embedded in a collagen network buckles fibers along the mitotic axis and pulls them inward perpendicular — a clean confinement/durotaxis-adjacent scenario. DCM can reproduce "division against a confining ECM" by running M1 inside a fiber-ECM (the FF collagen-Mikado already used in FF crawl work) coupled through the existing node-face penalty. **Why it helps:** gives DCM a *physiological-baseline confinement BC* (real collagen at 1/3/5 mg mL⁻¹, G′≈10/100/400 Pa) rather than a rigid wall, and a measured matrix-deformation field to validate the contact forces against.

### M3 — **Volume-conservation-driven shape change as an explicit test of the enclosed-volume constraint** (MEDIUM)
The paper's central physical claim — equatorial pinch at constant volume ⇒ axial elongation — is a direct stress test of DCM's turgor/enclosed-volume machinery. If DCM's volume constraint is correct, applying M1's ring force with no spindle should yield ~20% elongation *emergently*. This is a free, high-value validation of an existing DCM compartment (the Π₀/enclosed-volume constraint), independent of the division story.

### M4 — **Astral-MT coupling of spindle force to the cortex/membrane** (LOW/OPTIONAL)
Laser-ablation shows the interpolar spindle couples to the ECM via astral microtubules and contributes ~half the deformation. DCM already has a microtubule compartment. A fine-grained version routes `F_Is` through astral MT tips that push on the polar membrane, rather than a raw membrane force — deferrable, but the mechanistic-preferred implementation.

## 2. EQUATIONS to port

Map to DCM state: membrane/cortex triangulated surface = nodes `x_i` (positions), edges (stretch), face-pairs (bending), enclosed volume `V`. DCM symbols: area-elasticity `K_A`, bending `κ_m`, cortex tension `γ`, turgor/osmotic `Π`, cytoplasm viscosity `η`.

**Eq 6 — Extensional (stretch) potential** `U_s = ½ κ_s (r − r_0)²`
→ DCM already has membrane in-plane elasticity via `K_A` (area) and edge springs. The paper's per-edge `κ_s,m` with rest length `r_0,m = 400 nm` is the mesoscopic analog of DCM edge stiffness. No new physics; confirms harmonic edge model. If DCM uses area-elasticity only, add/confirm an edge-length term consistent with `K_A`.

**Eq 7 — Bending potential** `U_b = ½ κ_b (θ − θ_0)²`, rest angle `θ_0,m = 0` (flat)
→ DCM membrane bending `κ_m`. Same functional form (dihedral-angle harmonic). The paper sets membrane rest angle 0 (locally flat) — matches a Helfrich-style κ_m with spontaneous curvature 0. Direct correspondence, no change needed; use as a cross-check that DCM `κ_m` uses the same `½κ(θ−θ0)²` convention.

**Eq 8 — Excluded-volume repulsion** `U_r = ½ k_r (r_12 − r_c)²` for `r_12 < r_c`, else 0
→ DCM **node-face penalty** contact. `r_12` = min distance from membrane node/face to ECM (or neighboring-cell) segment; `r_c` = contact cutoff; `k_r` = penalty stiffness. Identical one-sided harmonic to Ericson penalty. Use to parameterize cell-ECM contact stiffness for the confinement scenario.

**Volume-conservation constraint** (paper: rotate the symmetric 2D shape about the long axis each step, compare to `V_0`, apply transverse corrective forces)
→ DCM enclosed-volume constraint. DCM already computes true 3D mesh volume (divergence/tetrahedron sum) — *strictly better* than the paper's surface-of-revolution trick (the paper is quasi-2D, height 1 µm). Port the *coupling logic* only: `F_vol,i = −k_V (V − V_0) · n_i` (outward normal `n_i`, or the DCM turgor form `Π = −K_V (V−V_0)/V_0` acting on faces). This is exactly DCM's turgor; the paper validates that a constant-volume pinch elongates the cell. Keep DCM's real-3D volume, not the rotation approximation.

**Active mitotic forces (applied, not potential-derived):**
- `F_Cyto`: for node `i` with `|z_i − z_eq| ≤ 0.5 µm` AND `dist(x_i, division-axis) > 1 µm`: apply `f_i = −(F_Cyto,total / N_band) · r̂_⊥` (inward radial, toward axis). `F_Cyto,total = 100 pN` per side.
- `F_Is`: for node `i` with `dist(x_i, division-axis) ≤ 5 µm`: apply `f_i = +(F_Is,total / N_polar) · â` (outward along division axis `â`). `F_Is,total = 100 pN` per side.

**Recommended DCM-native replacement for F_Cyto (line-tension form, §M1):**
`f_i = T_ring · κ_ring · n̂_i` summed over the equatorial edge loop, where `κ_ring` is the local ring curvature (≈ 1/R_furrow) and `n̂_i` the in-plane inward normal — so as the furrow radius `R_furrow → 0` the pull intensifies. Set `T_ring` so `∮ f ≈ 100 pN` at `R_furrow = 10 µm`: `T_ring ≈ F_Cyto,total / (2π) ≈ 100 pN / 6.28 ≈ 16 pN` (line tension, N/m: `16 pN / (band arc) ` → derive at implementation; anchor to keep total = 100 pN at t=0, no free tuning).

**Eqs 2–5 (overdamped Langevin, fluctuation-dissipation, cylindrical drag Eq 4, Euler Eq 5):** these are the ECM/BD substrate — relevant to FF, not DCM's runtime (DCM is Warp implicit/large-dt). Skip for DCM except to note the ECM these forces push against is a Kim-lab BD network (belongs to the FF lens).

**Eqs 10–11 (fiber concentration ↔ mg/mL):** ECM calibration — FF lens, not DCM.

## 4. GEOMETRY / PROTOCOL to reproduce as DCM scenarios

### P1 — **Division-in-free-space** (validation of volume-constraint + ring, no ECM)
- Single DCM cell, MCF7 R=7.5 µm, full compartments at physiological baseline (membrane K_A, κ_m, cortex γ, cytoplasm η=65 Pa·s, turgor Π₀=40 Pa, nucleus).
- Apply M1 ring force alone (F_Cyto), volume conserved. Expect ~20% axial elongation. Then add spindle F_Is, expect ~40%.
- Run to T=2000 s. Observable: axial length / equatorial width vs time.

### P2 — **Division-in-collagen** (confinement, the paper's main experiment)
- Embed the P1 cell in an FF collagen ECM (1/3/5 mg/mL → G′ 10/100/400 Pa), coupled via node-face penalty (M2).
- Apply ring+spindle. Observables: (i) cell elongation reduced vs free space and *decreasing with fiber density*; (ii) outward matrix deformation along mitotic axis, inward perpendicular; (iii) fiber buckling along axis (~32% incidence).

### P3 — **Volume-conservation unit test** (cheap, no ECM, no division story)
- Apply only an equatorial inward pinch to a resting cell; confirm enclosed-volume constraint produces axial bulge with |ΔV|≈0. Direct check of DCM turgor/volume machinery.

### P4 — **Spindle-ablation analog** (mechanism decomposition)
- Run P2 with F_Is turned off mid-run (mimics laser ablation of interpolar spindle): expect partial relaxation of matrix deformation (spindle contributes ~half). Validates additivity of the two drivers.

## 6. RISKS / caveats

- **Cell type & regime:** MDA-MB-231 (mesenchymal, highly invasive) and MCF10A — *not* MCF7. MCF7 is more epithelial/less contractile; ring/spindle force scales may differ. **Adapt:** treat 100 pN/side as the *literature-anchored order of magnitude*, not an MCF7 datum; if we run MCF7, flag the number as cross-type provisional and prefer deriving `T_ring` from myosin-II ring-tension literature. Do NOT fit 100 pN to a DCM gate.

- **Quasi-2D model:** the paper's simulation is a 1-µm-thick slab with a 2D membrane (nodes have only x,y) and volume computed by rotating the 2D outline. DCM is fully 3D with true mesh-volume — **strictly better**; port only the coupling logic, never the surface-of-revolution shortcut. The 158-element / 400-nm mesoscale membrane is coarser than DCM native — informational only; keep DCM native resolution (physiological-baseline rule: validate at full/native mesh, not the paper's coarse 158-node ring).

- **Applied-force phenomenology:** F_Cyto and F_Is are *prescribed constant forces*, a lumped stand-in for actomyosin ring contraction and spindle-motor pushing. That is exactly the abstraction the DCM mechanistic rule forbids as a *runtime mechanism*. **Adapt:** implement the ring as a **line-tension law** (§M1/§2) and, optionally, route the spindle force through astral-MT tips on DCM's microtubule compartment (M4). Use the paper's constant-force version only as an *acceptance oracle* / first smoke test, then replace with the mechanistic form before reporting.

- **ECM ≠ our current DCM contact target:** the confinement is a discrete collagen fiber network (Kim-lab BD), not DCM's usual cell-cell aggregate. The node-face penalty maps cleanly, but the ECM itself belongs to the **FF engine** (Cytosim/Mikado collagen already built). P2 is a *DCM⊗FF coupled* scenario, so schedule it only once the DCM–FF contact bridge is solid; P1/P3 are pure-DCM and can land first.

- **Physiological-baseline compliance:** run P1–P4 from a `--from-resting` full-compartment MCF7 baseline (turgor Π₀=40 Pa ON, cytoplasm η=65 Pa·s, nucleus, microtubules) — never a floppy zero-pressure shell. The ring/spindle forces are *perturbations from the pressurized resting state*, matching how the paper's cell rounds (turgor-pressurized) before dividing.

- **Not a lever for:** cortical-γ generation (no cortex-tension mechanism here — forces are applied, not myosin-sourced) and cadherin/T1 aggregate compaction (no cell-cell junctions; single dividing cell in ECM). Do not over-claim relevance to those open problems.

- **Table S1 gap:** the complete sim constant set (κ_s, κ_b, k_r, k_0,u, λ_u, μ, Δt) is in Supporting Information Table S1, absent from the PDF. Fetch S1 before any full KB registration of the numeric block; the main-text values above (100 pN, band geometries, 2000 s, 10 µm) are sufficient to build the division scenario.
