## Paper kind

**simulation** (3D Brownian-dynamics / agent-based model output), with embedded **analytic** sub-relations (persistence-length↔bending, Damköhler-collapse scaling laws, bundle-diameter geometry) and a small set of **literature-anchored calibration constants** (L_p≈20 µm, torsional stiffness).

Consequence for how the numbers may be used (hard rule: *oracles are cross-checks, simulation is comparison-only, never a truth source*):
- Every emergent **output** of this paper (cross-angles, L_c distributions, pore-size scaling, connectivity, N_ACP dynamics, effective off-rates) is a **sim-vs-sim CROSS-CHECK** for the FF engine — a candidate comparison target, **never** a truth contract and **never** a fit target.
- Its **rate constants** (k_A,n, k_A,e) and its **stretch stiffness** k_s,f are *deliberately non-physiological* (accelerated / softened for timestep affordability, stated explicitly in the paper) → **must NOT be registered as physiological parameters.**
- Only the **calibration targets it inherits from prior measurement** (F-actin L_p≈20 µm from Gittes/Isambert; torsional stiffness from Tsuda/Yasuda) are candidate Parameters, and those must be cited to their **primary** source, not to this paper.

This paper's dominant value to the project is as a **method/architecture blueprint for the FF crosslinked-network engine**, not as a validation-data source. Its strict TAG-validation yield is modest.

## Coverage vs existing gates

- **Bell's-law force-dependent off-rate (Eq.16, k_ACP,− = k⁰·exp(γF/kT)).** Already an **acceptance oracle** in the project (Bell-Evans) and the mechanistic contract for `MC-U2-fa-motor-clutch` / `VG-U2-catch-slip-bond` (integrin) and `MC-U4-junction-cadherin`. This paper **CONFIRMS** the functional form for *actin-crosslinker* (ACP) unbinding — a new *application domain* (crosslinker, not integrin/cadherin) for the same oracle. No band change. The effective off-rates ~100/30 s⁻¹ are sim outputs, not a band.
- **Persistence-length ↔ bending-stiffness (Eq.8, L_p = k_b σ/kT).** The project already derives fiber bending from L_p·kBT (`PARAM-kappa_fiber` derived from `PARAM-ell_p`, KU-1.1/1.24, ECM collagen). This paper **CONFIRMS** the identical relation for actin filaments and would be the analytic basis for FF cortex-filament bending calibration. No tighten; it re-uses a relation we already hold.
- **Force-propagation exponent (`VG-U1-force-propagation`, σ(r)~1/r for sub-isostatic Mikado).** Tangential: this paper's connectivity/percolation analysis is the same Kim line that the project's DCM⊗ECM 1/r recovery cross-checks, but it reports connectivity qualitatively (adjacency-matrix powers), not a σ(r) exponent → no direct band comparison.
- **Cortex multi-filament reconstruction (`VG-U3-cortex-multifilament`, blocked Phase-2 entry gate: N_filaments≥50, Bell-Evans crosslink dynamics, Q_ij measurable).** This paper is the **direct methodological template** for that gate (bead-spring filaments + explicit ACP crosslinks + Bell unbinding + measurable adjacency matrix Q). It does not supply a *band* but it supplies the **morphology observables** (L_c, cross-angle, pore size, connectivity, bundle diameter) that gate could measure. Strengthens the contract; no numeric change.
- **No existing gate covers** crosslink cross-angle, inter-crosslink spacing, network pore size, or bundle diameter as observables → candidates below.

Net: **0 gates tightened, 0 challenged.** The paper confirms two mechanisms we already hold (Bell off-rate form, L_p↔k_b) and supplies new *cross-check* observables.

## New gate candidates (all sim-vs-sim CROSS-CHECK — flagged, PI-gate review; NOT truth contracts)

These are legitimate **comparison** targets for the FF crosslinked-network engine once it builds ACP classes, but because the reference is a *simulation*, they must be staged as cross-checks (compare FF's emergent morphology to Kim's), never as truth contracts and never fit to. Each is measurable at OUR single-cell / cortex-patch scale.

1. **`VG-FF-crosslink-angle` (candidate, cross-check).**
   Observable: mean crosslink angle between the two filaments an ACP bridges, per crosslinker archetype.
   Reference bands: bundler (α-actinin/fascin/fimbrin-type) ≈ 0° (Kim −2.39±3.98°); orthogonal (filamin-type) ≈ 90° (Kim 87.5±10.5°).
   Engine: **FF**. Source: p98. Note: bands are *sim outputs* — use as a shape/archetype discriminator (bundler bimodal-parallel vs orthogonal), not a tight numeric contract.

2. **`VG-FF-network-pore-scaling` (candidate, cross-check).**
   Observable: mean network pore size vs actin concentration.
   Reference: ⟨L_pore⟩ ∝ C_A^{−1/3} (Φ^{−1/3}), i.e. pore size tracks the mean inter-filament spacing r₀ ∝ C_A^{−1/3}. Range ~4–10 σ_A over 151 µM–1.21 mM.
   Engine: **FF** (or oracle — the C^{−1/3} law is analytic). Source: Fig.8a, p98. This is the most defensible candidate because the *exponent* (−1/3) is a first-principles geometric scaling, not a fitted magnitude.

3. **`VG-FF-crosslink-spacing` (candidate, cross-check).**
   Observable: inter-active-ACP spacing L_c distribution along a bundle.
   Reference: bundler → **bimodal** at ~5 σ_A and ~10 σ_A (half/full 74-nm helical turn); orthogonal → monotonic, no peaks.
   Engine: **FF**. Source: Fig.6, p98. Tests that FF's helical binding-site registry reproduces the half-turn periodicity. Qualitative (bimodal vs monotonic), not a band.

*(Bundle effective diameter σ_b=√(N_f,b)·σ_A is an analytic geometric identity — see Oracle candidates, not a gate.)*

## Oracle candidates

1. **Pore-size / mean inter-filament spacing law:** ⟨L_pore⟩ ∝ r₀ = (500·N_A·C_A)^{−1/3} ∝ C_A^{−1/3}. A closed-form geometric cross-check for FF network initialization at a given G-actin concentration — analytic, first-principles (packing), implementable as an oracle. **Strongest oracle candidate** (exponent is theory, not a fit).
2. **Bundle effective diameter:** σ_b = √(N_f,b)·σ_A (N_f,b = effective #filaments in cross-section). Trivial analytic identity; usable as a bundle-geometry sanity check.
3. **Damköhler-collapse scaling laws** (Eq.20 t̃_p~Φ^{−5/6}Da_n^{1/2}; Eq.21 ⟨L_f/σ_A⟩~(Da_n Φ^{1/3})^{1/2}): analytic collapses of *polymerization kinetics*. Since FF does not run on-the-fly nucleation/elongation with these accelerated rate constants, these are **not** actionable oracles for FF — comparison-only, low value.
4. Persistence-length↔bending (Eq.8) and Bell off-rate (Eq.16) are **already** project oracles — this paper confirms, adds no new oracle.
