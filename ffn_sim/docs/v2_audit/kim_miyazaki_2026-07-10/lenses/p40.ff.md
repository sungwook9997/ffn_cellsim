# FF lens — p40

**Paper:** Zhou, Jung, Farhana, Fujimoto, T. Kim, Yokokawa (2022) — *Durability of Aligned Microtubules Dependent on Persistence Length Determines Phase Transition and Pattern Formation in Collective Motion.* ACS Nano 16, 14765–14778. DOI 10.1021/acsnano.2c05593.

**One-line for FF:** An in-vitro MT–kinesin gliding assay + a Kim-lab agent-based Brownian-dynamics model that is *architecturally identical to FF* (serially-connected cylindrical segments + extensional + bending + excluded-volume + motor-propelling forces under Langevin). It hands us (a) a calibrated microtubule compartment (Lp, κ, length, motor velocity, stall force), (b) a depletion/bundling force with a numeric magnitude, and (c) a clean, purely-mechanistic *validation scenario* — the emergent finding that alignment **durability**, not alignment **probability**, is set by bending rigidity (tip thermal fluctuation ∝ 1/Lp). Directly usable for the FF `--microtubules` compartment and for a 2D self-propelled-filament FF regression test. Weakly relevant to actin cortex directly (regime is purified MT-on-glass, 2D).

---

## 1. MECHANISMS to adopt

### M1 — Microtubule compartment as an explicit bending beam (filament module)
FF already carries a `--microtubules` compartment in the native cell. This paper is the cleanest single-source calibration of MTs *as semiflexible beams*: MT = serially connected cylindrical segments with plus/minus polarity, bending κ set from persistence length, excluded-volume repulsion, and a directed motor force. That is exactly the FF bead-rod + angular-bending + LJ-EV representation. **Change:** set the MT compartment's bending modulus from a *measured* Lp (0.65–3.54 mm) rather than a generic value, and represent MT polarity (plus/minus) explicitly so motor directionality and growth are well-defined.
- **Why it beats current FF:** it replaces any lumped/nominal MT stiffness with a source-anchored κ = Lp·k_BT spanning a 5× range that is physiologically meaningful, and it gives us the *tip-fluctuation ∝ 1/Lp* observable to check that our bending term produces the right thermal orientational noise (a direct sanity gate on the bending force + BAOAB thermostat).

### M2 — Directed motor-propelling force as a per-segment body force (myosin/kinesin/clutch module → generalize to a "surface-anchored motor" law)
The gliding assay's kinesin is modeled not as an explicit two-head walker but as a **constant-magnitude propelling force oriented toward the filament pointed (minus) end**, mimicking a bed of surface-fixed motors (SI "Propelling Force", Fig S6a). This is the correct minimal law for a *substrate-anchored* motor field (gliding assay / traction lawn), distinct from the Stam–Hocky bipolar minifilament FF uses for cytoplasmic myosin.
- **Why adopt:** FF's crawl/substrate work (motor-clutch traction lawn) needs a substrate-motor propulsion law; the paper gives its functional intent (constant force toward pointed end, magnitude bounded by single-motor stall 5–7 pN) plus the emergent gliding speed (601 nm/s) to calibrate it. This is a mechanistically-faithful "many small anchored motors → net directed force per segment" that we can validate against a measured glide velocity.

### M3 — Depletion (bundling) attraction as an explicit short-range inter-filament force (crosslinker/adhesion module)
Methylcellulose induces an attractive depletion force between adjacent MTs (0.11 pN at 0.3 wt% MC) that bundles filaments and — crucially — **suppresses leading-tip thermal fluctuation**, extending alignment lifetime. This is a *non-specific, transient, force-magnitude-defined* lateral attraction, complementary to FF's specific α-actinin/filamin catch/slip crosslinkers.
- **Why adopt:** FF bundling currently comes only from explicit crosslinker bonds. A depletion-style weak lateral potential is the mechanistic route to *cortical crowding/bundling* driven by macromolecular crowding of the cytoplasm — and this paper quantifies it (0.11 pN) and shows its *effect is on durability, not on binding probability*. Adds a real, missing crowding force at a source-anchored magnitude.

### M4 — "Durability, not probability" as the design principle for emergent alignment (validation/mechanism framing)
The central result: collision-induced reorientation statistics (alignment *probability* ~30%, dispersion η_d = 16.3°, polar/antipolar ~50/50) are **independent of Lp and depletant** — they are set purely by incoming angle θ_in. What varies with rigidity is the **duration** an aligned bundle survives before tip fluctuation peels it apart. Nematic order and bundle size then self-amplify from durable seeds.
- **Why adopt:** this is a directly testable emergent property of any FF filament network. It tells us that if FF bundle/order metrics come out wrong, the lever is the *bending term + thermostat noise* (durability), not the crosslinker on-rate (probability). It also gives FF a non-trivial emergent-order regression target that stresses bending, EV, thermal noise, and motor force *simultaneously*.

## 2. FORCE LAWS / KINETIC SCHEMES to port

> Caveat: the explicit BD update, extensional/bending potentials, EV form, and propelling-force functional form live in the **Supporting Information (SI Text + Table S7 + Fig S6a)**, which is NOT in the provided text. Below are the printed governing equations (Lp measurement + order metrics) plus the qualitative force laws with FF symbol mapping. Retrieve SI Table S7 before a literal port of the force magnitudes/constants.

### L1 — Persistence length ↔ bending modulus (maps Lp to FF's κ)
Bending energy of a beam gives Lp = κ / k_BT, with κ = E·I the flexural rigidity. FF stores an angular bending stiffness per hinge; convert:
```
κ_MT = Lp · k_BT          # flexural rigidity [N·m²]
k_bend(discrete) ≈ κ_MT / l_seg    # per-hinge angular stiffness for segment length l_seg
```
At T = 298 K (assay 25 °C), k_BT = 4.11e-21 J:
- Lp = 0.65 mm → κ = 2.67e-24 N·m²
- Lp = 1.93 mm → κ = 7.93e-24 N·m²
- Lp = 3.54 mm → κ = 1.45e-23 N·m²

**FF map:** MT compartment `bending_kappa` (or per-hinge angle-harmonic constant); T = physiological baseline; l_seg = MT segment length.

### L2 — Cantilever mode analysis → validation of FF's thermal bending (Eqs 1–3)
```
(Eq1)  y(s) = Σ_n sqrt(1/L) · a_n · W_n(s/L)
(Eq2)  W_n(s/L) = [(cosh q_n − cos q_n)/(sin q_n + sinh q_n)]·[sin(q_n s/L) − sinh(q_n s/L)] + cos(q_n s/L) − cosh(q_n s/L)
(Eq3)  Lp = (1/var(a_n)) · (L/q_n)^4      q_n = 1.875, 4.695, 7.855, (n−0.5)π  (n=1,2,3,≥4)
```
**FF use:** clamp one end of an FF MT beam, run BAOAB at physiological T, decompose the free tip's transverse fluctuation y(s) into these modes, invert Eq3, and check the recovered Lp equals the input Lp = κ/k_BT. This is a *self-consistency sanity gate* on the bending force + Langevin thermostat — mechanistic, no fitting.

### L3 — Nematic order + bundling metrics (Eqs 4–6) as FF observables
```
(Eq4)  S  = (1/N)·sqrt[ (Σ_i f_i cos2θ_i)^2 + (Σ_i f_i sin2θ_i)^2 ]     # nematic order 0→1
(Eq5)  Sk = (1/N)·Σ_n [ (I_n − Ī)/σ ]^3                                 # bundling (skewness of density)
(Eq6)  σ  = sqrt[ (1/N)·Σ_n (I_n − Ī)^2 ]
```
**FF map:** compute S over filament orientation histogram, Sk over a rasterized density field, at native scale. These are the emergent-order readouts for M4's regression scenario.

### L4 — Motor-propelling force (qualitative form to port from SI)
Constant-magnitude force **F_prop** per gliding segment, directed toward the pointed/minus end, bounded above by single-motor stall (5–7 pN) and producing steady glide at 601 nm/s against Stokes drag. In an over-damped Langevin, F_prop ≈ γ_seg · v_glide fixes the effective force from the measured velocity and per-segment drag γ_seg = drag on a cylinder in the assay medium.
**FF map:** substrate-motor body force on cortex/filopodium segments in contact with a motor lawn; magnitude calibrated so isolated-filament glide = 601 nm/s. (Explicit SI form needed for exact per-motor kinetics.)

### L5 — Depletion attraction (short-range lateral potential)
Attractive inter-filament force, magnitude 0.11 pN at 0.3 wt% MC, range ~ filament diameter; monotone-increasing with depletant concentration; its *mechanistic action* is to damp transverse tip fluctuations (raises effective Lp of the aligned pair).
**FF map:** a weak short-range lateral attractive well between near-parallel filament segments in the crosslinker/crowding module, force-scale 0.1 pN. Not a specific bond — no Bell-Evans off-rate; it is a soft potential toggled by a crowding parameter.

## 4. NETWORK ARCHITECTURE / PROTOCOL to reproduce (FF scenario)

**Scenario "MT-glide-nematic" (2D self-propelled filament regression):**
- 2D domain 100×100 µm, periodic or open; FF MTs = serially-connected polar cylindrical segments; excluded-volume LJ on; BAOAB thermostat at 298 K.
- Filaments nucleated at random position/orientation (one segment), elongated by segment addition (polymerization rate k_+, nucleation rate k_n tuned to mean length 9.6 µm) — reuses FF's barbed-end growth machinery.
- Per-segment propelling force toward pointed end → steady glide at 601 nm/s (calibrate F_prop against per-segment drag).
- Two rigidity conditions: κ from Lp = 0.63 mm and 1.93 mm (matching the paper's simulation inputs).
- Densities: collective 5.5 filaments/µm² (pattern formation) and 0.022 filaments/µm² (binary collision statistics).
- Optional depletion lateral attraction 0.11 pN toggled to test durability.
- Measure S(t), Sk(t), bundle size N_B(t), formation vs disintegration times, collision θ_in→θ_out.

**Value to FF:** this is a *purely mechanistic* emergent-order benchmark exercising bending + EV + motor force + thermal noise together, with published targets. It stress-tests the same kernels the cortex uses, in a geometry small enough to run to convergence, and it validates that FF's bending/thermostat reproduce the "durability ∝ Lp" law before we trust MT behavior in the native MCF7 cell.

## 6. RISKS / caveats

- **Regime mismatch — purified MT-on-glass, 2D, kinesin; not actin cortex, not 3D.** These parameters belong to the **MT compartment** and a **substrate-motor lawn**, not to the actin cortex. Do NOT let Lp=0.65–3.54 mm, 601 nm/s, or 5–7 pN leak into actin/myosin configs. Register as MT/kinesin provenance only.
- **Lp is enormous vs actin (0.65–3.54 mm vs ~17 µm).** MTs are ~40–200× stiffer than actin; at MCF7 R=7.5 µm an MT is effectively rod-like over the cell. Keep the MT bending term stiff but watch CFL — high κ shortens the stable dt; may need the MT compartment integrated with a smaller sub-step or stiffer-safe scheme. Sanity-gate dt against κ_MT.
- **Motor law is a lumped surface-force, not an explicit walker.** The propelling force is a *proxy* for a bed of anchored kinesins. Under FF's "mechanistic over lumped" hard rule, the constant-force form is acceptable ONLY for a substrate motor lawn where individual heads are unresolved; do not use it in place of the explicit Stam–Hocky/Hill cytoplasmic myosin. Prefer resolving heads where the cytoplasmic motor matters.
- **Depletion force is a crowding proxy.** 0.11 pN is a specific-buffer (methylcellulose) number; in the MCF7 cytoplasm the crowding depletant differs. Use the *form and force-scale* (~0.1 pN weak lateral attraction that damps tip fluctuation), not the literal MC concentration mapping. It is a soft potential, not a Bell-Evans bond — do not give it an off-rate.
- **SI force forms not in hand.** Exact extensional/bending/EV/propelling functional forms + Table S7 constants are SI-only. A literal port needs SI retrieval; until then treat the force *magnitudes and the Lp→κ conversion* as the transferable content, and derive the discrete stiffnesses ourselves (grid-invariant, no fitting).
- **2D vs 3D + native-scale rule.** The pattern-formation scenario is 2D by construction; use it as a *component regression test*, not as a substitute for native-scale full-compartment conclusions. Any MT-mechanism finding used in the MCF7 story must be reconfirmed in the native `--cortex-fil 38000 --from-resting --microtubules` cell.
- **No fitting to reproduce S(t)/timescales.** Emergence times (50/20/10 min) depend on density, drag, and domain; match the *ranking and the durability∝Lp law*, not absolute minutes, and never tune κ or F_prop to hit a specific S value — that would violate the no-fitting/no-gate-loosening rules.
