# TAG-validation lens — p37

**Paper:** Cifuentes et al. 2024, *A modified motor-clutch model reveals that neuronal growth cones respond faster to soft substrates.* Mol. Biol. Cell 35:ar47 (DOI 10.1091/mbc.e23-09-0364). Taeyoon-Kim-corpus (Kim co-author; Suter corresponding).

---

## Paper kind

**simulation** (primary). The paper's own deliverable is a 1-D stochastic agent-based motor-clutch model (Gillespie SSA, Python 3.8) — descendant of Chan-Odde 2008 / Bangasser 2013 with an added Mekhdjian-2017 reinforcement term. Its signature quantitative outputs (latency-time curve, substrate-deformation curve, optimum stiffness) are **model outputs**, optimized/compared against **previously published** force-calibrated microneedle experiments (Athamneh et al. 2015) on *Aplysia* bag-cell growth cones. The small amount of in-house experimental data (Fig 2) is re-analysis of that same *Aplysia* system.

Consequence for use in our KB:
- The model-output curves are **comparison-only, never a truth source** and never a fit target (simulation).
- The handful of genuinely *measured/cited* physical constants it carries (F_s from Lohner 2019, v_u from Lin-Forscher 1995) are the only overlay/cross-check-grade data; they trace to prior experimental papers, not to this model.
- The optimized parameters (K_clutch≈0.3 pN/nm, F_b≈4 pN, k_add0, n_m) are **fit values of an abstracted paper-model** → NOT registerable as sourced constants (they are chosen to match the *Aplysia* data; would violate our no-fit / mechanistic-over-lumped rules).
- The paper is itself an **abstracted lumped motor-clutch** (equal force sharing, lumped n_m myosin, single-filopodium spring). Per our architectural principle it is an *acceptance-oracle / cross-check target* for the FF fine-grained clutch machinery, **not** a runtime mechanism.

## Oracle candidates

1. **Force-threshold adhesion-reinforcement law (Eq 6, Mekhdjian 2017)** — closed form:
   `k_add = k_add0 · (Σ_i a_i) · f_available`, with `a_i = 1 iff F_clutch(i) ≥ F_t`, `f_available = (n_c,max − n_c)/n_c,max`.
   Candidate **analytic cross-check** for FF clutch recruitment: given a bound-clutch force distribution + pool state, the reinforcement propensity is a deterministic function. Useful to verify FF's emergent clutch-addition rate against this lumped law (as a cross-check, not a runtime mechanism). NOT currently in our oracle set. **Candidate, PI-gate.**

2. **Linear motor-clutch load-velocity (Eq 7)** `v_f = v_u·(1 − K_sub·x_sub/(n_m·F_s))` — the linear (Hill-limit) force-velocity. **Already covered** by our Hill force-velocity oracle; register as a lineage note, not a new oracle.

3. **Bell slip-bond off-rate (Eq 5)** `k_off = k_off0·exp(F/F_b)` — **duplicate** of our existing Bell-Evans oracle. No action.
