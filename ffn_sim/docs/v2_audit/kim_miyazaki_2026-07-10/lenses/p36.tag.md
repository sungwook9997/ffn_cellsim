# TAG-validation lens — p36

**Paper:** Jung, Fillenwarth, Matsuda, Li, Inoue, Kim — "Collective and contractile filament motions in the myosin motility assay." *Soft Matter* 2020, 16, 1548–1559. DOI 10.1039/c9sm02082a.

## Paper kind

**simulation** (agent-based Brownian-dynamics model output — the canonical Kim-lab explicit-agent actomyosin framework applied to the in-vitro gliding assay). This is a *model paper*, not a wet-lab measurement paper and not a closed-form theory paper. Under our philosophy this means: **its numbers are comparison-only — never a truth source, never fit targets.** They tell FF what *emergent phenomenology* to reproduce and give a few *citable rate constants* (which themselves trace to prior experiments/PCM, not to this paper), but the paper itself cannot supply a validation band as a contract. Everything below is therefore staged as **candidate, PI-gate review**, and the "engine" for any adoption is **FF** (this is FF-family physics: explicit cylindrical-segment F-actin with stretch+bend, load-dependent myosin walking/unbinding, Bell's-law crosslinker off-rates, harmonic volume exclusion, Langevin dynamics). DCM relevance is low.

Important caveat that governs the whole lens: the paper's actual force-law constants (segment stretch k_{s,A}, bend k_{b,A}, drag, dt, r_{c,A}, and the full PCM rate table) live in **ESI Tables S1/S2, which are NOT in the provided 12-page PDF**. Do not quote exact stiffness/timestep numbers until the ESI is harvested.

## Coverage vs existing gates

- **Item 1 (motor unloaded speed ~140 nm/s) ↔ PARAM-v_unloaded (default 100 nm/s, range 10–100 nm/s, KU-2.4/3.12).** Directly relevant. The paper's 140 nm/s (NMMII) sits just above the current range ceiling of 100. This is a **cross-check / potential range-extension note**, NOT a conflict to act on unilaterally: 140 nm/s is the paper's model output *calibrated to* non-muscle myosin II lit (refs 28,29), and our v_unloaded is the retrograde-flow/motor speed for a different context. Flag for PI: consider whether the FF motor unloaded speed band should admit ~100–140 nm/s for NMMII. Do not silently overwrite.
- **Items 6/7/8/9 (crosslinker percolation + intermediate-crosslink contractility optimum) — NOT covered by any existing gate.** We have MC-U2 (FA motor-clutch, Bell-Evans) and MC-U4 (cadherin catch-bond), and the cortex MC-U3 mentions Bell-Evans crosslink dynamics (VG-U3-cortex-multifilament, blocked Phase-2), but **no gate tests emergent network-scale contractility vs crosslinker density/off-rate**. This is the paper's central result and is genuinely new territory for FF. → new-gate candidates below (staged, PI-authored).
- **Items 10/11 (Bell's-law k⁰_{u,ACP}=0.115 s⁻¹, PCM k₂₀=20 s⁻¹) — no existing Parameter row.** Our params cover FA clutch on-rate, F_stall_motor (2 pN), v_unloaded — but there is no ACP/crosslinker off-rate parameter and no explicit myosin ATP-unbinding rate. → param candidates below.
- **Item 13 (stall ∝ 1/k₂₀) ↔ PARAM-F_stall_motor (2 pN).** The paper does not give a single-motor stall in pN in the main text (it lives in the PCM/ESI); it only gives the *scaling*. No conflict; note only.
- **Item 3 (biphasic speed vs k₂₀), Item 5 (length threshold) — not covered.** Candidate emergent cross-check gates for FF motor stack, but weaker as contracts (dimensionless, model-only). Staged as low-priority candidates.
- **Nothing here touches** the DCM gates (VG-U5-*, VG-H7/H8/H9/H10, cortex-tension floor). No overlap, no challenge to those bands.

**Verdict on existing bands:** this paper CONFIRMS/EXTENDS the family the FF motor-clutch (MC-U2) and cortex-crosslinker (MC-U3) contracts already commit to (Bell-Evans off-rates, load-dependent walking), and CHALLENGES nothing. It does not tighten any current band (all its numbers are model outputs, not measurements).

## New gate candidates (all: candidate, PI-gate review — engine FF, simulation-comparison only)

> Philosophy note: because p36 is a *simulation*, these can only ever be **cross-comparison gates** ("FF reproduces the same emergent phenomenology as an independent agent-based model"), never primary truth gates. They are worth staging because they exercise FF's motor+crosslinker+volume-exclusion stack end-to-end at network scale, which no current gate does. Primary truth for each should remain analytic where one exists (e.g. single-motor Hill force-velocity oracle).

1. **proposed_vg_id: VG-FF-motility-glide-speed** — observable: mean F-actin gliding speed in a motility-assay geometry at reference motor kinetics. band_or_value: → unloaded motor speed ~0.10–0.14 µm/s when ⟨L_f⟩ ≫ motor spacing (NMMII regime); condition: quasi-2D slab, dense motors, no ACP, no volume exclusion. engine: FF. Cross-check against the single-motor **Hill force-velocity oracle** at zero load as primary. source: p1549, Fig 1/2b.
2. **proposed_vg_id: VG-FF-glide-biphasic-k20** — observable: gliding speed vs ATP-dependent unbinding rate k₂₀. band_or_value: qualitative — monotonic rise then fall (peak, not saturating), bound-motor fraction falls ~65%→~14% across the swept range; condition: no ACP/VE. engine: FF. source: Fig 2b,c.
3. **proposed_vg_id: VG-FF-acp-percolation-arrest** — observable: crosslinker density R_ACP at which network gliding speed → 0. band_or_value: ~3% of actin count (order-of-magnitude, C_A≈60 µM), with permanent (k⁰=0) crosslinks; condition: C_A fixed, VE off. engine: FF. source: p1555, Fig 5a. (Contract framing: percolation threshold is a topology invariant, defensible; band is order-of-magnitude only.)
4. **proposed_vg_id: VG-FF-contractility-intermediate-optimum** — observable: network tensile force / morphological heterogeneity Q_A vs (R_ACP, k⁰_{u,ACP}). band_or_value: **non-monotonic — maximum at intermediate R_ACP and intermediate k⁰_{u,ACP}** (percolation-vs-deformability balance); force is buckling-limited. condition: ACP unbinding on. engine: FF. source: Fig 5, p1556. (This is the highest-value candidate — it is the "intermediate crosslink density optimizes contractility" law, independently corroborated across many actomyosin studies, so a *qualitative shape* gate is defensible even though this paper is only one simulation.)

Do NOT propose a numeric contractility magnitude band from this paper — the tensile force is reported only as scalings (∝ R_ACP, ∝ 1/k⁰), the absolute constants are in ESI, and it is model output.

## Oracle candidates

**None from this paper as a primary oracle.** The paper's laws are either (a) *emergent phenomenology* (biphasic glide, intermediate-crosslink optimum) that arise from the simulation, not closed forms, or (b) *reused mechanistic laws we already treat as oracles from their original sources*: Bell's law (already our Bell-Evans off-rate oracle), the PCM motor cycle (our Hill force-velocity oracle covers the motor force-velocity primary). Two secondary/definitional forms could be implemented as measurement-protocol helpers rather than truth oracles:
- **Persistence length ℓ_p = k_{b,A}·r_{0,A}/(k_B T)** (Eq. 3) — standard WLC relation; we already derive PARAM-kappa_fiber from ℓ_p·k_B T. Definitional, not new.
- **Velocity autocorrelation / pairwise velocity-direction correlation** (Eqs. 1–2) and **heterogeneity Q_A** (Eq. 5) — these are *measurement protocols* FF could adopt to quantify collective motion, not oracles. Worth noting as analysis-tooling if VG-FF-contractility-* is ever authored.
