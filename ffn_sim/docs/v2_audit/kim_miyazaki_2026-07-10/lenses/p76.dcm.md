## 1. Mechanisms to adopt  (moderate–high — a deformable membrane-bounded cortex droplet migrating under confinement)
- **Cortex-contraction → interfacial-friction → propulsion under confinement.** A membrane-bounded actomyosin droplet migrates ONLY when the cortex binds the membrane (PIP2): rear-directed actin flow transmits contractile stress through the membrane to the substrate as sliding friction, propelling the front. This is the adhesion-free analogue of DCM's membrane+cortex+contact model and gives a clean physical law for confined cell motility that DCM can reproduce.
- **Geometric trade-off sets speed.** Propulsion (friction) ∝ contact area ∝ h⁻¹, viscous drag ∝ h⁻² → non-monotonic speed with an optimum chamber height (~60 µm). A DCM confined-migration scenario should reproduce this optimum, testing the DCM contact + drag coupling.
- **Actin–membrane binding, not polarity, is the essential requirement** (magnetic-bead control). DCM cortex–membrane coupling strength is thus a decisive knob for motility.

## 2. Equations / laws to port (surface/continuum — this IS a continuum active-gel theory)
- **Interfacial friction constitutive law:** `σ_act = α·v_act` — transmitted contact stress ∝ actin-flow speed, geometry-independent α. Directly usable as the DCM cortex–substrate friction law.
- **Contact-area force law:** `F_fric^contact = −2(α v_act)(π R_c²)`, with contact radius `R_c = R − (h/2)f(θ)`, `f(θ)=−(|tanθ|+1/cosθ)`, θ=154.7°.
- **Drag:** `F_drag ≈ η_oil[(D/2)⁻²+(h/2)⁻²]V·π(D/2)²h`.
- **Master migration speed (Eq.2):** `V_drop = (α v_act)/(2η_oil)·h·[1+(h/D)²]⁻¹·[1−(h/D)f(θ)]²` — closed form; a DCM analytic cross-check for confined motility.

## 4. Geometry / protocol to reproduce
- Cell-sized deformable droplet, membrane-bound cortex, confined between plates at height h=30–300 µm, D=80–650 µm → measure V vs h,D. DCM confined-migration benchmark.
- 1D microchannel (w=100/150 µm) → elongated shape boosts friction.

## 5. Validation targets for DCM observables
- Non-monotonic V(h), peak ~60 µm; V rises with D; ~4× faster confined vs unconfined; MSD ballistic (∝Δt²) confined vs diffusive unconfined.
- Symmetric contraction (no membrane binding) → no motion; asymmetric (bound) → migration. Φasym 0.15→0.50.
