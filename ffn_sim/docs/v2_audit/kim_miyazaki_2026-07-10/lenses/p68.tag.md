# TAG-validation lens — p68

**Paper:** Li, Szymanski & Kim (2023), *Probing stress-regulated ordering of the plant cortical microtubule array via a computational approach*, BMC Plant Biology 23:308. DOI 10.1186/s12870-023-04252-5.
**Lab:** Taeyoon Kim (Purdue BME) — the same agent-based-cytoskeleton lab already cited in our ECM/1-r line.

## Paper kind

**simulation** (agent-based, stochastic Monte-Carlo 2D cortical-MT model in MATLAB), with a small **experimental** validation overlay (confocal imaging of stage-4 *Arabidopsis* trichomes, morphology only, no extracted mechanical numbers). It also carries one **analytic** relation (the continuum stress-transformation projection, Eq 1) and one nematic order-parameter definition (Eq 2).

Consequence for use: the model *outputs* (order parameters S_p, reorientation timescales, MT-length/lifetime tables) are **comparison-only, never a truth source**. The only truth-grade quantitative numbers here are the plus-end dynamic-instability rate constants, which are themselves **cited from Shaw 2003 [11]** (experimental) — Shaw2003 is the primary source for any KB registration, with this paper as the model-implementation reference. All values are **plant (Arabidopsis) cortical MTs**, not animal/MCF7.

## Coverage vs existing gates

**No existing gate covers any of this.** Our validation state has zero microtubule observables — the closest cytoskeletal gates are all **actin cortex / crosslinker** (VG-H3-KU35 cortex tension, VG-U3-cortex-multifilament, VG-H7-gate-a/b), FA motor-clutch, ECM Mikado, junction cadherin, and spheroid/necrosis. MTs enter our system only as the DCM `--microtubules` compartment flag, which currently has **no sourced dynamic-instability parameters and no gate**.

- Nematic order parameter S_p: conceptually adjacent to VG-U1 alignment_S (ECM fiber alignment, PARAM-alignment_S) and to Q_ij in VG-U3-cortex-multifilament, but that is **actin-network orientation**, a different filament system. No overlap of band or condition. Not covered.
- MT length / lifetime: no existing param or gate. Not covered.
- Stress→alignment feedback (1.5:1 threshold, 50 min reorient): a plant-trichome-specific mechanism with **no analogue** in our MCF7 single-cell contracts. Not covered.

Net: this paper does not CONFIRM, TIGHTEN, or CHALLENGE any current band — it is orthogonal (plant cortical MT array vs animal actin cortex + ECM).

## New gate candidates

I propose **no new ValidationGate contracts**. Rationale under the philosophy:

1. **Scale/organism mismatch.** Every observable is a *plant* (Arabidopsis) cortical-MT-array property. Our validated basis is the MCF7 animal single cell (`--cortex-fil 38000 --from-resting --microtubules`, Nc≈266k). A stress→MT-alignment order parameter at the whole-plant-cell-wall scale (3–15 MPa turgor-driven wall stress) is not a defensible contract at our single-animal-cell scale, and inventing a band to fit it would violate the no-magic-number / defensible-contract rules.
2. **Simulation outputs, not truth.** S_p ranges, τ_p, and the model MT length/lifetime table are model outputs — comparison-only. They cannot become gate bands.
3. **The one contract-worthy quantity we lack (MT dynamic-instability rates for a microtubule module) is animal-cell-specific**; these plant values would be the *wrong* anchor. If/when FF or DCM builds an explicit animal microtubule compartment, register mammalian DI constants (e.g. from mammalian in-vitro / Verde-type sources), not Shaw2003 plant values.

**Staged (PI-gate review, low priority, only if a microtubule module is built):** a *cross-check-only* MT-length sanity band. `proposed_vg_id=VG-MT-length-sanity` — mean single cortical MT length **2–4 µm** (lit range, cite [54]); engine=FF or DCM microtubule compartment; verification-type sanity check, overlay-only, explicitly organism-agnostic (order-of-magnitude, holds broadly for cortical MTs). Flag: candidate only, not needed until a microtubule module exists; band is order-of-magnitude, not a tight contract.

## Oracle candidates

Two closed forms, both **weak** as oracles for our system:

1. **Orientation-projected normal stress (Eq 1):** σ_MT = (σ_x+σ_y)/2 + (σ_x−σ_y)/2·cos(2θ). This is the standard 2D Mohr/stress-transformation projection — generic continuum mechanics, not novel to this paper. It could serve as a trivial cross-check for any stress-projection code (e.g. projecting a virial stress onto a filament orientation), but it is textbook and not MT-specific. Low value as a registered oracle.
2. **Nematic order parameter (Eq 2):** S_p = Σ lᵢ cos[2(θᵢ−θ_p)] / Σ lᵢ (length-weighted). This is the standard 2D nematic scalar order parameter. **Mildly useful**: if we ever quantify actin-cortex or ECM-fiber alignment (adjacent to PARAM-alignment_S / Q_ij), this is the correct length-weighted definition to compute it — a *measurement-protocol* reference, not a truth oracle. Worth noting in the alignment-metric code, not worth a standalone oracle config.

Neither rises to the level of the analytic oracles we treat as ground truth (Helfrich, Young-Laplace, Bell-Evans, Hill, Kelvin-Voigt).
