## Paper kind

**simulation** (primary) **+ experimental verification** (secondary) **+ one analytic scaling law** (σ∝r⁻¹).

The quantitative *curves and exponents* (peak stress ∝ k_s,c, relaxation ∝ R_xl⁻¹, 80%/20% relaxation, >50% at zero unbinding, ~60 Pa magnitude) are **model output of a competing agent-based Brownian-dynamics code** (the Taeyoon-Kim engine). Per our philosophy these are **comparison-only, never a truth source** — Kim's engine is an on-demand cross-check, not an oracle. The in-vitro 3T3/collagen panel (Fig 6) is **experimental → overlay-only** but is *semi-quantitative* (normalized confocal-reflectance intensity vs distance, trend-only; no clean band). The one genuinely **analytic** result is the far-field stress-decay geometry (σ ∝ 1/area(r): r⁻¹ in the 2-D cylindrical slab, r⁻² in 3-D) — a candidate oracle.

**Provenance caveat (hard):** the full numeric parameter table is in **ESI Table S1, which is NOT in this PDF**. Only k_ub,0, x_ub, and the in-vitro concentrations are numerically stated in the main text; k_s,c*, C_f*, R_xl*, ζ, Δt, κ_b are ESI-deferred. **Do not KB-register reference sweep values until ESI is retrieved.**

## New gate candidates *(all: candidate — PI-gate review; new gates are PI-authored)*

1. **`VG-DCM-cell-matrix-stress-decay` (candidate)** — extend `VG-U1-force-propagation` from a Mikado point-force network to a **contractile CELL embedded in matrix**.
   - Observable: exponent n of far-field radial σ(r) from a central contractile DCM cell in a fiber matrix.
   - Band: **n ≈ 1 (2-D slab) / n ≈ 2 (3-D)** for fully-transmitted steady stress; distinguish "transmitted-to-boundary" (matches 1/area) from "attenuated" (steeper) as the diagnostic.
   - Engine: DCM (cell) ⊗ FF/ECM matrix. Analytic ground truth = 1/area(r).
   - Source: Figs 2B/C etc. insets, p4–7. *This is essentially the published version of the existing passing result — recommend folding as a scenario under VG-U1 rather than a wholly new contract.*

2. **`VG-FF-ecm-fiber-orientation-split` (candidate, soft)** — under a central contractile source, **tensed fibers align radial (~0°), buckled fibers align circumferential (~90°)**.
   - Observable: joint distribution of (end-to-end ratio, orientation angle) of matrix fiber elements post-contraction.
   - Band: tensed (ratio>1) peak orientation < ~20° from radial; bent (ratio<1) peak > ~70° (circumferential). Qualitative/geometric — mechanistic signature, not a magnitude band.
   - Engine: FF (fiber network). Source: Fig S1G/H (ESI — retrieve before staging).

**Not recommended as gates** (comparison-only, sim-sourced): peak σ ∝ k_s,c, relaxation ∝ R_xl⁻¹, 80/20 relaxation split, >50%-at-zero-unbinding poroelastic split, ~60 Pa magnitude. Use these as **cross-check overlays** when running a DCM cell in an FF matrix, not as bands.

## Oracle candidates

**`oracle: central-source transmitted stress decay`** — closed-form geometric cross-check.
- Law: for a central contractile source whose net radial force F is fully transmitted to radius r, σ_radial(r) = F / A(r), with A(r) = 2πr·h (2-D cylindrical slab, h = thickness) ⇒ **σ ∝ r⁻¹**; A(r) = 4πr² (3-D) ⇒ **σ ∝ r⁻²**.
- Use: analytic ground-truth exponent for any DCM-cell-in-matrix or FF-Mikado point-force run; already the implicit basis of `VG-U1-force-propagation`. Worth codifying alongside the existing `young_dupre.py` / `aa0_law.py` oracles as an `ecm/stress_decay.py` helper returning the expected exponent by dimensionality. Low effort, high reuse.
- This is a *geometry* oracle (dimensional), NOT a Kim-engine value — safe to adopt as truth.
