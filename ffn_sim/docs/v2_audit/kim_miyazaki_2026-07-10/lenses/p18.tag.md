## Paper kind

**simulation** (agent-based model output — overdamped 2D discrete mechanical model, `Mu=f` linear solve + Adams–Bashforth-4).

Consequence for use (hard philosophy): every number in this paper is **model output, not a truth source**. Under our rules (oracles/analytic = primary ground truth; lab = overlay-only; **simulation = comparison-only, never a fit target and never a gate band**), nothing here may become a quantitative ValidationGate band. Its value is (a) **model-form confirmation** for the FF ECM and DCM cell–cell contact physics (both share this paper's lineage — Mikado network + SimuCell3D Eq. 8), and (b) **qualitative behavior cross-checks** (correct sign/trend) for the FF⊗ECM durotaxis and DCM aggregate-contraction/escape tracks. Additionally, the parameter table proper (Table S1) is **absent from this PDF** — most constants (drag coeffs, κ⁰_s,M, F_tot, Δt) are unavailable, further limiting param registration.

The equations that *look* analytic (Eqs. 17–21) are **measurement protocols / trivial decompositions**, not closed-form physical laws:
- Eq. 17 `E∥/E⊥ = ⟨D⊥⟩/⟨D∥⟩` — a stiffness-probe definition (stiffness ∝ 1/displacement).
- Eq. 18 durotactic index `⟨(D∥−D⊥)/(D∥+D⊥)⟩` — a directional-bias metric.
- Eqs. 19–20 `F_r=F cosθ`, `F_θ=F sinθ` — trigonometric projection, not a law.
- Eq. 21 radial migration bias `D_r/D_s` — Cortay-2020 protocol.
None qualifies as a candidate analytic oracle.

## New gate candidates

All are **candidate, PI-gate review** and — because the paper is simulation — must be registered (if at all) as **qualitative sign/trend cross-checks against a peer model, NOT quantitative bands**. I do not propose importing any of the paper's numbers as a band.

1. **`VG-FF-durotaxis-index-sign` (candidate, qualitative)** — engine: **FF⊗ECM**.
   Observable: durotactic index (Eq. 18) of a cell/cluster on an anisotropically-stiffened fibrous matrix.
   Contract (defensible at our scale): **DI > 0 and increases monotonically with imposed stiffness anisotropy E∥/E⊥; DI ≈ 0 when matrix nodes are frozen (vᵢ=0 control).** No numeric band — the *sign, monotonic trend, and the frozen-matrix null* are the mechanistic content (the paper's own control, row 8/9, is what makes this durotaxis rather than contact guidance). Source: Fig. 3C/E, p16–17.
   Rationale: this is a real, measurable single-cell/aggregate observable at our scale, and the frozen-matrix null is a legitimate contract written before the run. Flag: peer-model comparison only.

2. **`VG-DCM-radial-tension-emergence` (candidate, qualitative)** — engine: **DCM aggregate**.
   Observable: radial/circumferential tension ratio F_r/F_θ (Eqs. 19–20) in a contracting cohesive cluster on a remodelable matrix.
   Contract: **F_r/F_θ > 1 (sustained radial tension) emerges with cell–cell adhesion ON and collapses toward isotropy (≈1) with adhesion OFF; a single cell cannot sustain it.** Qualitative only. Source: Fig. 4D/F, p17–19.
   Rationale: ties directly to the DCM cadherin-cluster + escape-memory work. Flag: simulation-comparison; the ~2 magnitude is NOT a band.

3. **`SANITY-FF-fiber-buckling-asymmetry` (candidate sanity check, not a gate)** — engine: **FF ECM fiber mechanics** (`oracles/ecm/fiber_mechanics.py`).
   Content: fibers must be **tension-stiff / compression-soft** (paper uses κ ÷10 in compression, Eq. 13, sourced to Storm 2005 / Notbohm 2015). This is a *model-form* requirement our fine-grained fibers should already satisfy via WLC/finite-extensibility; register as a sanity-gate assertion (compressive branch softer than tensile), not a quantitative gate.

*(No candidate for E∥/E⊥ magnitude: the effect is tiny (~1.05–1.07), model-specific, and would be an invented band — declined.)*
