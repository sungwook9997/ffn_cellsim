## Summary
The paper asks how a cross-linked actomyosin **network** is structurally reorganized into a contractile **bundle** (motivated by transverse-arc formation at the lamellipodium/lamella interface in migrating cells) and how tension is built up during that transformation. Using the Kim-lab coarse-grained agent-based Brownian dynamics model — actin filaments, actin cross-linking proteins (ACPs, filamin-A-like slip-bond), and non-muscle myosin II motors (multi-arm minifilaments with catch-bond kinetics via the parallel cluster model) as cylindrical segments in a thin 4×8×0.5 µm slab with a periodic y-boundary — they self-assemble a homogeneous network, then start motor walking and watch it condense into a bundle spanning the domain in y. They systematically sweep motor density (R_M) and ACP density (R_ACP), actin bending stiffness/buckling (reference κ_b,A giving 9 µm persistence length vs 100×κ_b,A), initial filament orientation (isotropic vs biased ±x/±y mimicking lamellipodia), and actin turnover (treadmilling rate k_t,A 30–120 s⁻¹ with a depolymerization-inhibition factor ξ_d,A). Key results: **maximum bundle tension rises with both R_M and R_ACP** (up to ~4 nN); **tension sustainability rises with R_ACP but falls with R_M** — because more ACPs share load → smaller per-ACP force → slower slip-bond unbinding → stable bundle, whereas high R_M/low R_ACP overloads ACPs → bundle disintegrates into aggregates and tension relaxes to ~0. **Filament buckling is required** for tight bundle formation and large tension in isotropic networks (suppressing it via 100× bending stiffness abolishes tension), but **not** required when orientation is highly biased (then polarity sorting can still bundle parallel pairs, though with ~zero/compressive tension). Compaction occurs in ~10–20 s (comparable to experimental ~20 s transverse-arc condensation), faster at higher R_M and lower R_ACP but yielding looser bundles. Actin turnover with **intermediate** depolymerization inhibition (ξ_d,A ~0.6) stabilizes tension at low ACP density but is purely destabilizing at high ACP density.

## Model & Methods
- **Model type:** Agent-based / coarse-grained **Brownian dynamics** (Langevin, inertia neglected / overdamped). This is the Kim-lab canonical actomyosin model (from Mak et al. Nat Commun 2016 [ref 13]; Kim 2015 [16]; Kim/Hwang/Kamm lineage [24–27]). Cytoskeletal elements are interconnected cylindrical segments with harmonic extensional/bending springs, force-dependent bond kinetics, and stochastic thermal forcing.
- **Elements:**
  - *Actin filament:* serially connected cylindrical segments with polarity (barbed/pointed ends); equilibrium segment length r₀,A = 140 nm, equilibrium inter-segment angle θ₀,A = 0.
  - *ACP:* pair of cylindrical arms (2 segments); r₀,ACP = 23.5 nm; θ₀,ACP = 0 between arms.
  - *Motor (NM-II minifilament):* backbone with symmetric (bipolar) polarity and **8 arms**, each arm representing **8 myosin heads** (N_h = 8). Backbone segments r_s,M1 = r_s,M2 = 42 nm; transverse arm spring r₀,M3 = 13.5 nm; longitudinal arm spring r₀,M4 = 0 nm.
- **Integrator:** explicit **Euler** scheme (Eq 4). **Time step Δt = 1.5×10⁻⁵ s.**
- **Domain / BCs:** 3D rectangular slab **4 × 8 × 0.5 µm** (thin). **Periodic BC in y** only; x- and z-boundaries exert repulsive forces to confine segments. Bundle forms spanning the domain in the y-direction.
- **Thermal forcing:** stochastic force satisfies fluctuation-dissipation theorem (Eq 2).
- **Drag:** per-segment drag from an approximated cylinder form (Eq 3, from Clift/Grace/Weber [35]).
- **Excluded volume:** repulsive harmonic when actin segments overlap (Eq 7).
- **Bond kinetics:** ACPs bind at constant rate to sites every 7 nm; unbind force-dependently via **Bell's equation** (slip bond, Eq 8, tuned to filamin A). Motors bind at 40·N_h s⁻¹; walking (k_w,M) and unbinding (k_u,M) governed by the **parallel cluster model (PCM)** of Erdmann & Schwarz [20,21], giving catch-bond behavior (both rates decrease with load).
- **Actin dynamics:** nucleation, polymerization, depolymerization; treadmilling by equal barbed-polymerization / pointed-depolymerization rates; turnover rate k_t,A = 30–120 s⁻¹; nucleation rate dynamically adjusted to hold mean filament length ~1.56 µm; nucleation biased to y-direction within bundle. Monomeric actin, free ACP, free motor treated implicitly by local concentration.
- **Algorithm outline:** (1) self-assemble homogeneous network (motors bound but not walking; ACPs cross-linking); optionally bias nucleation direction for anisotropic networks; (2) at t=0 start motor walking → network condenses to bundle; (3) measure macroscopic bundle tension (10 y-cross-sections, average of summed extensional-force normal components) and microscopic per-ACP / per-motor forces at peak tension; measure compaction via σ_x (std dev of actin x-positions).

## Governing Equations
1. **Langevin (overdamped) equation of motion (Eq 1):** `F_i − ζ_i (dr_i/dt) + F_i^T = 0` — force balance on element i: deterministic force minus drag plus thermal force = 0 (inertia neglected).
2. **Fluctuation-dissipation for thermal force (Eq 2):** `⟨F_i^T(t) F_j^T(t)⟩ = (2 k_B T ζ_i δ_ij / Δt) δ` — variance of stochastic force set by drag and temperature; δ_ij Kronecker delta, δ a second-order (identity) tensor.
3. **Drag coefficient (Eq 3):** `ζ_i = 3π μ r_{c,i} (3 + 2 r_{0,i}/r_{c,i}) / 5` — cylinder drag from medium viscosity μ, segment length r₀,i and diameter r_c,i.
4. **Euler position update (Eq 4):** `r_i(t+Δt) = r_i(t) + (dr_i/dt)Δt = r_i(t) + (1/ζ_i)(F_i + F_i^T)Δt`.
5. **Extensional harmonic potential (Eq 5):** `U_s = ½ κ_s (r − r₀)²` — stretching of a segment about equilibrium length r₀.
6. **Bending harmonic potential (Eq 6):** `U_b = ½ κ_b (θ − θ₀)²` — bending about equilibrium angle θ₀ between adjacent segments.
7. **Repulsive (excluded-volume) potential (Eq 7):** `U_r = ½ κ_r (r₁₂ − r_{c,A})²` if `r₁₂ < r_{c,A}`, else `0` — volume exclusion between two actin segments; r₁₂ = minimum inter-segment distance.
8. **ACP force-dependent unbinding — Bell's equation (Eq 8):** `k_{u,ACP} = k⁰_{u,ACP} · exp(λ_{u,ACP} |F⃗_{s,ACP}| / k_B T)` if `r ≥ r₀,ACP`, else `k⁰_{u,ACP}` — slip-bond: unbinding accelerates exponentially with tensile arm force F⃗_{s,ACP} = −∇U_{s,ACP}; λ = mechanical sensitivity (reactive compliance).
9. **Depolymerization inhibition (Eq 9):** `k_{d,A} = k⁰_{d,A} (1 − ξ_{d,A})` — actual depolymerization rate reduced by inhibition factor ξ_d,A (0 = no inhibition, 1 = fully blocked; models ACP/motor protection of bound filament regions).
10. **Microscopic force on ACP (Eq 10):** `f^max_{ACP} = F⃗_{s,ACP} · u⃗` — projection of ACP arm force onto barbed-end unit vector u⃗, evaluated at peak tension (sign: + toward barbed end).
11. **Microscopic force on motor (Eq 11):** `f^max_M = (F⃗_{s,M4} · u⃗)/N_h` — per-head motor force = longitudinal-spring force projected on barbed direction, divided by heads per arm N_h.
- **Motor kinetics (PCM, text, not numbered):** k_w,M (walking) and k_u,M (unbinding) both governed by F⃗_{s,M4} = −∇U_{s,M4} on the longitudinal spring; both decrease with load ⇒ catch-bond. Unbinding assumed very large when F⃗_{s,M4} points toward the pointed end (motor detaches if pushed backward).
- **Buckling criterion (text):** filament counted "buckled" when end-to-end distance / contour length < **0.6**.
- **Compaction time (text/SI):** time at which the rate of change of σ_x exceeds 0.01 × (average rate of change of σ_x during the first 5 s).

## Force Laws & Potentials
- **Stretching:** linear (Hookean) via U_s = ½κ_s(r−r₀)² → force κ_s(r−r₀), applied to actin (κ_s,A), ACP arms (κ_s,ACP), motor backbone (κ_s,M1, κ_s,M2 = κ_s,A), motor transverse arm spring (κ_s,M3), motor longitudinal arm spring (κ_s,M4).
- **Bending:** U_b = ½κ_b(θ−θ₀)² → restoring torque; actin κ_b,A set so persistence length L_p = 9 µm; motor backbone κ_b,M > κ_b,A (kept straight); ACP κ_b,ACP.
- **Excluded volume:** one-sided harmonic repulsion (Eq 7), strength κ_r, active only within cutoff r_{c,A}.
- **ACP off-rate:** Bell slip-bond (Eq 8), exponential in |force|.
- **Motor on/off/walk:** binding 40·N_h s⁻¹; walking & unbinding from parallel cluster model (catch-bond, load-decreasing); unloaded velocity ~140 nm/s; stall force ~5.7 pN.
- **Force distribution:** forces from bound ACPs/motors on an actin segment distributed to the segment's barbed and pointed end nodes (per Kim 2015 [16]).
