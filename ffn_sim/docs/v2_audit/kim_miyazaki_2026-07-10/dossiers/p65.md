## Summary
The paper introduces a three-dimensional Brownian dynamics (BD) simulation model of actin monomers that polymerize into filaments and become cross-linked by two idealized classes of actin cross-linking proteins (ACPs) — short parallel bundlers (ACP^B, modeling fimbrin/fascin/α-actinin) and long perpendicular cross-linkers (ACP^C, modeling filamin) — with the goal of understanding how monomer/cross-linker properties (nucleation and elongation rate constants, G-actin concentration C_A, ACP-to-actin ratio R, ACP binding-site orientation/stiffness, filament bending stiffness, and ACP size) determine emergent network morphology (filament length, inter-cross-link distance, cross-linking angle, connectivity/percolation, pore size, and bundle geometry). Each molecule (monomer or ACP) obeys an inertia-free Langevin equation integrated by explicit Euler in dimensionless units (Δt̃=3×10⁻⁵ ≈ 20.13 ps); filaments are single-stranded bead-spring chains (one 7-nm bead = two G-actins) endowed with harmonic stretch, bending, and torsion potentials plus truncated-LJ excluded volume, and ACPs bind via distance+angle+dihedral gates with force-dependent Bell's-law unbinding. Running 51 parameter combinations to a 99%-polymerized cutoff, the authors find robust scaling laws insensitive to detail: polymerization time t̃_p ~ Φ⁻⁵/⁶ Da_n^{1/2} ~ (k_A,n^{1/2} C_A)⁻¹ and mean filament length ⟨L_f/σ_A⟩ ~ (Da_n Φ^{1/3})^{1/2} ~ k_A,n^{-1/2}; ACP^B produce bundles with bimodal inter-cross-link spacing (~5σ_A and ~10σ_A, half/full helical turn) and near-0° cross-link angle (−2.39±3.98°), whereas ACP^C produce denser, more homogeneous, higher-connectivity networks with ~87.5±10.5° cross-link angle; mean pore size scales as C_A^{-1/3}; and the number of active cross-links keeps evolving as a weak power law t^{-0.05} after polymerization completes, which they connect speculatively to cytoskeletal fluidization/healing and power-law rheology. The model is explicitly positioned as a base BD framework to be extended (myosin motors, membranes, focal adhesions, mechanotransduction).

## Model & Methods
- **Model type:** Coarse-grained 3D Brownian dynamics (BD), agent/particle-based. Explicit particles = actin monomer beads and ACP beads. Filaments = single-stranded bead-spring chains (NOT the double helix; one bead = two G-actin monomers = 7 nm). Overdamped (inertia neglected). Reactive: on-the-fly nucleation, elongation, depolymerization, ACP binding and force-dependent unbinding.
- **Integrator:** Explicit (forward) Euler on the inertia-free Langevin equation.
- **Timestep:** Δt̃ = 3×10⁻⁵ (dimensionless) = 20.13 ps in SI; chosen to correspond to the diffusion time over one actin diameter σ_A.
- **Units:** Dimensionless, using k_BT, ζ_A (monomer friction), and σ_A (monomer diameter) as the three primary scales. Tildes denote dimensionless variables.
- **Boundary conditions:** Periodic boundary conditions on a cubic box.
- **Domain / geometry:** Cubic box holding 8,000 actin monomers (production size, chosen from a 512→27,000 monomer convergence study, Fig. 3). Box width depends on C_A: 40σ_A (=280 nm) at C_A=1.21 mM up to 80σ_A (=560 nm) at C_A=151 µM. (σ_A=7 nm.)
- **Initial condition:** Free actin monomers uniformly distributed; ACPs randomly placed. No pre-existing filaments.
- **Termination criterion:** Run until only 1% of free actin monomers remain (i.e., 99% incorporated into filaments) — a computational-cost cutoff, acknowledged as not a true cross-linking steady state (ACP binding still slowly evolving, Fig. 11).
- **Algorithm outline:**
  1. Place monomers (uniform) + ACPs (random) in periodic cube.
  2. Each BD step: compute deterministic forces (LJ excluded volume, stretch, bend, torsion, ACP potentials) + stochastic thermal force; advance all positions by explicit Euler.
  3. Nucleation: when two free monomers are within r̃₁₂=1.1·σ̃₁₂, form a dimer nucleus with probability P_n.
  4. Elongation: a free monomer within r̃₁₂=1.1·σ̃₁₂ of a barbed end is incorporated immediately and aligned to the filament axis (diffusion-limited).
  5. Depolymerization allowed at rate k_A,− (rare on this timescale).
  6. Cross-linking (two-step): (i) ACP binds one monomer if distance + θ₁ + θ₂ gates satisfied; (ii) the filament-bound ACP binds a monomer on a *different* filament if the same gates + dihedral φ₁ gate satisfied → active cross-link.
  7. ACP unbinding: force-dependent via Bell's equation.
  8. Continue to 99% incorporation; then characterize morphology (L_f, L_c, cross-angle, connectivity via adjacency-matrix powers, pore size via sphere-inflation, bundle length/diameter).
- **Visualization:** VMD (ref [47]).
- **Rate-constant provenance:** k_A,n and k_A,e were themselves *measured from the simulation* (empirical fits, eqs 12–13), not imposed a priori; nucleation is reaction-limited, elongation diffusion-limited.

## Governing Equations
1. **Langevin equation (Eq. 1):**
   m_i · d²r_i/dt² = F_i − ζ_i · dr_i/dt + F_i^B
   — Newton/Langevin balance for molecule i: inertia = deterministic force − drag + thermal (Brownian) force.
2. **Fluctuation–dissipation for the stochastic force:**
   ⟨F_i^B(t) F_i^B(t)⟩ = (2 k_B T ζ_i δ_ij / Δt) · δ
   — zero-mean Gaussian random force with variance set by temperature, friction, and timestep (δ_ij Kronecker delta; δ unit 2nd-order tensor).
3. **Stokes friction (Eq. inline):**
   ζ_i = 3 π η σ_i
   — molecular friction coefficient from Stokes drag on a sphere of diameter σ_i in medium viscosity η.
4. **Inertia-free (overdamped) dimensionless Langevin (Eq. 2):**
   dr̃_i/dt̃ = F̃_i^B + F̃_i
   — with inertia dropped, velocity = total (thermal + deterministic) reduced force.
5. **Explicit Euler update (Eq. 3):**
   r̃_i(t̃+Δt̃) ≈ r̃_i(t̃) + (dr̃_i/dt̃)·Δt̃
   — position integration scheme.
6. **Truncated (shifted) Lennard-Jones excluded-volume potential (Eq. 4):**
   Ũ_LJ(r̃₁₂) = 4ε̃[ (σ̃₁₂/r̃₁₂)¹² − (σ̃₁₂/r̃₁₂)⁶ ]  for r̃₁₂ ≤ 2^{1/6} σ̃₁₂ ; = 0 otherwise
   — purely repulsive WCA-style excluded volume; σ̃₁₂ = ½(σ̃₁+σ̃₂) is the average diameter.
7. **Harmonic bond stretch (Eq. 5):**
   Ũ_s = ½ k̃_s (r̃₁₂ − r̃₀)²   with r̃₀ = σ̃₁₂
   — filament backbone extensional spring (equilibrium bond length = bead diameter).
8. **Harmonic bending (Eq. 6):**
   Ũ_b = ½ k̃_b (θ − θ₀)²
   — semiflexibility; θ = angle of three consecutive beads, θ₀ = equilibrium angle.
9. **Filament orientation correlation / persistence length (Eq. 7):**
   ⟨C(s)⟩ = ⟨cos[θ(s) − θ(0)]⟩ = e^{−|s|/L_p}
   — tangent-tangent correlation decays exponentially with contour length s over persistence length L_p (used to calibrate k̃_b,f).
10. **Persistence-length ↔ bending-stiffness relation (Eq. 8):**
    L_p = k_b,f · σ_A / (k_B T)
    — links measured L_p (~20 µm) to the bending constant.
11. **Harmonic torsion (Eq. 9):**
    Ũ_t = ½ κ̃_t (φ − φ₀)²
    — torsional/dihedral rigidity maintaining binding-site angular registry (φ dihedral angle, φ₀ equilibrium).
12. **Nucleation rate constant (empirical, Eq. 12):**
    k_A,n = 2.51×10¹³ × P_n  [M⁻¹ s⁻¹]
    — linear in the per-encounter nucleation probability P_n (reaction-limited).
13. **Elongation rate constant (empirical, Eq. 13):**
    k_A,e = 1.41×10⁹  [M⁻¹ s⁻¹]
    — diffusion-limited barbed-end addition rate (measured from sim).
14. **Bell's-law force-dependent ACP unbinding (Eq. 16):**
    k_ACP,− = k⁰_ACP,− · exp( γ F_b / k_B T )
    — off-rate increases exponentially with bond force F_b; γ = mechanical compliance (reactive length) of the ACP–monomer bond.
15. **Damköhler number (Eq. 17):**
    Da_n ≡ t_n / t_d = (1/(k_A,n C_A)) / (ζ_A r₀² / (6 k_B T)) ≫ 1
    — ratio of nucleation time t_n to diffusion time t_d over the initial monomer separation r₀; with r₀ = (500 · N_AVO · C_A)^{−1/3} (Avogadro's number N_AVO).
16. **Dimensionless polymerization time (Eq. 18):**
    t̃_p ≡ t_p / (ζ_A σ_A² / k_B T) = t_p[s] / (6.711×10⁻⁷)
    — nondimensionalization of polymerization time.
17. **Solid / volume fraction as dimensionless concentration (Eq. 19):**
    Φ = 54.06 × C_A[M]
    — maps molar actin concentration to a solid fraction.
18. **Polymerization-time scaling law (Eq. 20):**
    t̃_p ~ Φ^{−5/6} Da_n^{1/2} ~ C_A^{−5/6} (k_A,n^{−1} C_A^{−1/3})^{1/2} ~ 1/(k_A,n^{1/2} C_A)
    — master collapse of t̃_p·Φ^{5/6} vs Da_n (slope 0.5 in log-log, Fig. 4).
19. **Mean-filament-length scaling law (Eq. 21):**
    ⟨L_f/σ_A⟩ ~ (Da_n Φ^{1/3})^{1/2} ~ k_A,n^{−1/2}
    — collapse of ⟨L_f/σ_A⟩ using Da_n·Φ^{1/3} (Fig. 5c); both L_f and t_p scale as k_A,n^{−1/2}.
20. **Bundle effective diameter (Eq. inline):**
    σ_b = √(N_f,b) · σ_A
    — effective bundle cross-section diameter; N_f,b = (sum of filament lengths in bundle)/(bundle end-to-end contour length) = effective #filaments in cross-section.
21. **Cross-link binding gates (Eqs. 14–15):**
    Bind step 1: r̃_{A–ACP} ≤ 1.1·(σ̃_A+σ̃_ACP)/2 ; |θ₁−θ₁,eq| ≤ 0.1745 (10°) ; |θ₂−θ₂,eq| ≤ 0.1745
    Bind step 2 (cross-link): additionally |φ₁−φ₁,eq| ≤ 0.1745
    — geometric acceptance windows for ACP attachment and for forming a two-filament cross-link.
22. **Connectivity via adjacency-matrix powers:** For N×N upper-triangular binary matrix Q (Q_{i,j}=1 iff filaments i,j cross-linked), Σ_{i,j} Q^k_{i,j} counts sets of (k+1) filaments connected by ACPs → percolation/connectivity measure.

## Force Laws & Potentials
- **Excluded volume:** truncated/shifted Lennard-Jones (WCA), purely repulsive, cutoff 2^{1/6}σ̃₁₂, well-depth ε̃ (ε = 4.142×10⁻²¹ J = k_BT). Applies between all molecule pairs.
- **Backbone stretch:** harmonic, k̃_s,f = 2,000 (dimensionless) = 0.1691 N/m; equilibrium length = bead diameter. Intermediate value chosen so timestep stays affordable; gives 0.05 strain at 60 pN (vs measured ~0.002 — softer than real, but shown to not affect analyzed properties).
- **Bending:** harmonic in bend angle, k̃_b,f = 3,000 = 1.243×10⁻¹⁷ N·m → L_p ≈ 20 µm (matches F-actin). Reduced tenfold (→300) in a sensitivity test.
- **Torsion:** harmonic in dihedral, κ̃_t,f = 1,000 = 4.142×10⁻¹⁸ N·m; maintains helical binding-site registry (π/5 rotation per bead → full turn per 10 beads).
- **ACP potentials:** each ACP carries its own harmonic stretch (κ̃_s), two bending (κ̃_b,1, κ̃_b,2) and torsion (κ̃_t,1) springs with prescribed equilibrium geometry (see ACP parameter block).
- **Bond rupture:** Bell's law (Eq. 16), force-dependent exponential off-rate.
- **Thermal force:** Gaussian white noise satisfying fluctuation–dissipation (Eq. 2).

## Relevance flags
- **DCM?** No (weakly). This is a fine-grained filament-scale BD model, not a surface-mesh continuum cell model; it does not inform membrane/cortex-tension/turgor/node-face-contact physics that DCM needs. Only tangential relevance (it discusses cortical orthogonal networks near the plasma membrane).
- **FF?** YES — strongly. This is essentially the ancestral blueprint of the FF (Filament-FEM / Cytosim-style) engine's cross-linked actin network: bead-spring filaments with harmonic stretch/bend/torsion, truncated-LJ excluded volume, explicit ACP cross-linkers (bundler vs orthogonal), Bell's-law force-dependent unbinding, on-the-fly polymerization/nucleation. Directly relevant to FF's crosslinker kinetics (Bell off-rates), filament bending calibration (L_p↔k_b via Eq. 8), ACP geometry parameterization, and network-morphology metrics (L_c, pore size, connectivity, cross-angle, bundle diameter). The two ACP archetypes (α-actinin/fascin-type bundlers vs filamin-type orthogonal) map onto FF's crosslinker classes.
- **TAG-validation?** YES. Rich source of quantitative, dimensionally-explicit validation targets: k̃_s,f→0.1691 N/m, k̃_b,f→1.243×10⁻¹⁷ N·m ↔ L_p≈20 µm, κ_t,f=4.142×10⁻¹⁸ N·m, Bell off-rate form + effective rates ~100/30 s⁻¹, cross-angle −2.39±3.98° / 87.5±10.5°, L_c bimodal 5σ_A/10σ_A, pore size ∝ C_A^{−1/3}, scaling laws t̃_p~Φ^{−5/6}Da_n^{1/2} and ⟨L_f⟩~(Da_n Φ^{1/3})^{1/2}, bundle diameter 1.5–2.3σ_A. Good candidate for KB SourceEvidence rows anchoring FF crosslinker/filament-mechanics parameters (with the caveat that k_s,f is deliberately softened and rate constants are computationally accelerated, not physiological).
