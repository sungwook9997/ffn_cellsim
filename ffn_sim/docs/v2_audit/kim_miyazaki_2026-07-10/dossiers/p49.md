## Summary
This study resolves the long-debated reaction scheme by which zipper-interacting protein kinase (ZIPK / DAPK3) phosphorylates the myosin II regulatory light chain (MRLC / LC20) at its two activating sites, Thr18 and Ser19. Using quantitative LC–MS/MS on wild-type human MRLC calibrated against synthetic monophosphorylated peptides, the authors show ZIPK phosphorylates **sequentially — Ser19 first, then Thr18** (monophospho Ser19:Thr18 ≈ 35:1), settling the sequential-vs-random debate in favor of sequential. Using phosphomimic/unphosphorylatable MRLC point mutants (T18A ≈ "0P" substrate reporting Ser19 rate; S19D ≈ "1P" substrate reporting Thr18 rate) resolved by urea/glycerol PAGE, they fit each step to Michaelis–Menten kinetics, finding equal affinities (Km = 4.2 µM for both sites, ZIPK-FL) but a **1.5× faster catalytic rate at Ser19 (kcat = 0.60 s⁻¹) than Thr18 (kcat = 0.40 s⁻¹)**. Two sequential Michaelis–Menten reactions ("Model 1") reproduce the full 0P/1P/2P time courses of isolated MRLC. Native smooth-muscle myosin (SMM, chicken gizzard) is phosphorylated much more slowly than isolated MRLC; a cosedimentation assay shows ZIPK's C-terminal domain binds the myosin heavy chain (MHC) with dissociation constant KI = 0.30 µM (≈10× tighter than the MRLC Km), so competitive, mutually-exclusive binding of ZIPK to MHC vs MRLC sequesters the kinase and slows MRLC phosphorylation. Adding this competitive term ("Model 2") reproduces SMM time courses. A C-terminal deletion mutant (ZIPK-ΔC, 1–291) validates the model: it has higher catalytic rates (kcat 0.63/0.95 s⁻¹) and tighter MRLC affinity (Km 2.5 µM) but weaker MHC binding (KI = 0.65 µM), and Model 2 again predicts its SMM time courses. The work delivers a compact, parameterized kinetic model of myosin activation.

## Model & Methods
- **Model type:** Experimental in-vitro enzyme kinetics + a *deterministic ODE-style Michaelis–Menten kinetic model* (no spatial dynamics, no Langevin/Brownian dynamics, no FEM). Reactions treated as bulk well-mixed initial-rate kinetics.
- **"Simulation":** Time courses (fraction 0P/1P/2P vs time) are computed by numerically integrating the two coupled Michaelis–Menten rate laws (eqs 1–2 for isolated MRLC = Model 1; eqs 4–5 with competitive MHC term = Model 2). No integrator/timestep is reported (deterministic kinetic simulation of a small ODE system). No boundary conditions / geometry (bulk solution).
- **Enzyme:** Recombinant human ZIPK (UniProt O43293); full-length ZIPK-FL (1–454 a.a.) and C-terminal deletion ZIPK-ΔC (1–291 a.a.), both GST-tagged, expressed in E. coli Rosetta 2(DE3), IPTG-induced, GSTrap-purified, dialyzed into A50 buffer. Kinase domain = residues 13–275; C-terminus predicted α-helix (αH; 413–450) with leucine zipper motif (LZM; 427–441).
- **Substrates:** Recombinant human MRLC isoform 2 (UniProt O14950) — wild-type + mutants T18A, T18D, S19A, S19D, T18DS19D (phosphomimic D / unphosphorylatable A). Native SMM purified from chicken gizzards (hexamer 474,000 Da: 2 MHC + 2 MRLC + 2 MELC). Native chicken MRLC+MELC mixture (37,000 Da) isolated by 8 M urea dissociation.
- **Assay conditions:** A50 buffer (50 mM HEPES–KOH pH 7.6, 50 mM KCl, 5 mM MgCl₂, 1 mM EGTA). Reactions at 25 ± 1 °C, 1 mM ATP + ATP-regenerating system (10 mM phosphocreatine, 0.1 mg/mL creatine phosphokinase), 10 mM DTT, 0.05 mg/mL BSA. Ca²⁺-independent (EGTA present; ZIPK has no calmodulin-binding domain).
- **Phosphorylation-state separation:** urea/glycerol PAGE (10 mA, 230 min) separates 0P/1P/2P MRLC bands; SYPRO Ruby stain; densitometry (Image Lab).
- **Site identification / quantitation:** LC–MS/MS on Orbitrap Eclipse (nanoflow UHPLC). Lys-C + pepsin double digest → target peptide RPQRATSNVF. Synthetic mono-phospho peptides RPQRApTSNVF (Thr18) and RPQRATpSNVF (Ser19), precursor 628.3 m/z. Absolute quantitation by parallel reaction monitoring (PRM) using neutral-loss (−98) MS2 fragment 579.3 m/z, calibrated with synthetic peptide standard curves (0,1,2,5,10,20,50 fmol). Skyline analysis; MASCOT search, FDR 0.01.
- **Binding assay:** Cosedimentation — phosphorylated SMM assembles into filaments that pellet at 627,000g (20 min, 25 °C). ZIPK partitions between pellet (bound) and supernatant (free); band ratios (Whole W vs Supernatant S) by SDS-PAGE give [SMM·ZIPK] and [ZIPKfree]; slope of [SMM·ZIPK]/[SMMfree] vs [ZIPKfree] = 1/KI.
- **Kinetic-constant fitting:** Initial-rate V₀ measured at fixed 5 nM ZIPK, 5 min (linear regime confirmed R²>0.99 over first 15 min), MRLC swept 1–16 µM (T18A and S19D mutants). Nonlinear least-squares fit of Michaelis–Menten to V₀ vs [MRLC]; cross-checked with Lineweaver–Burk. For ZIPK-ΔC, assumed Km(S19)=Km(T18) and kcat(S19)/kcat(T18)=1.5 to reduce free parameters.

## Governing Equations
Reaction scheme (Model 1, two sequential Michaelis–Menten steps):
- ZIPK + 0P-MRLC ⇌[Km^T18... note: Ser19 step] ZIPK·0P-MRLC →[kcat^S19] ZIPK + 1P-MRLC
- ZIPK + 1P-MRLC ⇌[Km^T18] ZIPK·1P-MRLC →[kcat^T18] ZIPK + 2P-MRLC
(0P→1P is Ser19 phosphorylation; 1P→2P is Thr18 phosphorylation on the Ser19-phospho species.)

**(Eq 1)** Rate of 0P→1P (Ser19 phosphorylation of unphosphorylated MRLC):
V^{0P→1P} = kcat^{S19}[ZIPK·0P-MRLC] = kcat^{S19} [ZIPK_tot][0P-MRLC] / ( Km^{S19} + [0P-MRLC] )
— Michaelis–Menten initial rate for the first (Ser19) step.

**(Eq 2)** Rate of 1P→2P (Thr18 phosphorylation of Ser19-mono-phospho MRLC):
V^{1P→2P} = kcat^{T18}[ZIPK·1P-MRLC] = kcat^{T18} [ZIPK_tot][1P-MRLC] / ( Km^{T18} + [1P-MRLC] )
— Michaelis–Menten initial rate for the second (Thr18) step.

**(Eq 3)** Cosedimentation binding isotherm (linearized 1:1 binding):
[SMM·ZIPK]/[SMM_free] = [ZIPK_free]/KI
where [SMM·ZIPK]/[SMM_free] = [SMM·ZIPK]/([SMM_tot] − [SMM·ZIPK]).
— slope of plot vs [ZIPK_free] = 1/KI; gives dissociation constant of ZIPK–SMM (via MHC).

**(Eq 4)** Model 2, Ser19 step with competitive ZIPK–MHC sequestration:
V^{0P→1P} = kcat^{S19} [ZIPK_tot][0P-MRLC] / ( Km^{S19} + [0P-MRLC] + (Km^{S19}/KI)[MHC] )
— competitive inhibition term (Km^{S19}/KI)[MHC] added to denominator; MHC binding lowers effective free kinase.

**(Eq 5)** Model 2, Thr18 step with competitive ZIPK–MHC sequestration:
V^{1P→2P} = kcat^{T18} [ZIPK_tot][1P-MRLC] / ( Km^{T18} + [1P-MRLC] + (Km^{T18}/KI)[MHC] )
— analogous competitive term for the second step.

**(Derived) Total phosphorylation metric** used in all time-course plots:
Total P (%) = 1P (%) + 2 × 2P (%)  (max 200% at full diphosphorylation).

## Force Laws & Potentials
None. This is an enzyme-kinetics paper; there are no mechanical force laws, potentials, or motor force–velocity relations. (The biological *significance* is that di-/mono-phosphorylation of MRLC activates myosin II force generation and filament assembly, but no force model is presented here.)

## Relevance flags
- **DCM?** No (indirect only). DCM's cortical tension comes from a lumped actomyosin σ; ZIPK phosphorylation kinetics are one biochemical layer above and are not part of the surface-mesh continuum. Could inform a future myosin-activation modulator but not needed for current DCM physics.
- **FF?** Weak-yes (context, not equations). FF uses Hill force-velocity myosin minifilaments as *active* force generators; this paper supplies the *activation switch* (mono-/di-phospho MRLC → active myosin). If FF ever models a myosin activation fraction / turnover gated by phosphorylation, kcat (0.40–0.95 s⁻¹) and Km (2.5–4.2 µM) set physiological activation timescales (seconds). Not a filament-mechanics or off-rate source; no Bell-Evans / bending / motor-stall content.
- **TAG-validation?** Yes (as a parameter/citation source). Clean DOI-verified quantitative constants (kcat, Km, KI, Ser19:Thr18 ratio) suitable for a KB SourceEvidence row if myosin-activation kinetics enter the model. These are *upstream regulatory* kinetics, not force-law validation targets, so tag as myosin-activation/kinase-kinetics rather than a cortical-tension or motor-stall gate.
