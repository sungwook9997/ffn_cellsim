## Summary
The paper asks how myosin motors and passive actin cross-linking proteins (ACPs) collectively, and across timescales, set the accumulation, magnitude, and long-term maintenance of mechanical tension (and the frequency-dependent viscoelasticity) of a disordered, cortex-like actomyosin network. Using a 3D agent-based Brownian/Langevin-dynamics model (F-actins as jointed cylindrical segments, ACPs as two-armed cross-linkers with force-dependent Bell-type unbinding, myosin bipolar thick filaments with catch-bond walking/unbinding), a thin (8×8×0.5 µm) network is self-assembled, then clamped to elastic (E = 3×10⁴ Pa) x-boundaries that act as a stress sensor via σ = Eε. Sweeping the molar ratios of motors (R_M) and ACPs (R_ACP) and the ACP zero-force off-rate (k⁰_u,ACP), they find: motors accumulate large stress fast by acting as *temporary* cross-linkers (σ_max ∝ R_M^0.6 at low R_M, higher motor density → higher σ_max), but this stress *relaxes* over time unless enough passive ACPs stabilize the network; ACPs let motors reach forces near their stall force (σ_max plateaus ~600 Pa, limited by motor number/stall), sharply raising the sustainability S; the force-dependent (Bell) unbinding of ACPs is the key switch — high R_M/R_ACP ratio or high k⁰_u,ACP loads ACPs enough to accelerate their off-rate, causing large-scale remodeling (mesh coarsening, motor aggregation) and stress dissipation. ACPs behave as a molecular clutch generating effective friction between F-actins. E′/E″ are shown to be roughly predictable from σ_max and S, consistent with prior rheology experiments. The mechanism is universal across bundle vs. network vs. 3D cubic geometry.

## Model & Methods
- **Model type:** 3D agent-based (coarse-grained particle) Brownian/Langevin dynamics of explicit F-actins, ACPs, and myosin thick filaments. Inertia neglected (overdamped Langevin).
- **Integrator:** Euler integration scheme. Velocity of each segment = (sum of deterministic forces + stochastic force) / drag; positions updated by Euler step. (Timestep value not given in this paper — deferred to Table S1 / ref 26.)
- **Governing dynamics:** Overdamped Langevin equation per segment; deterministic forces = extensional + bending + repulsive (all harmonic potentials); plus thermal stochastic force satisfying fluctuation–dissipation.
- **Geometry / domain:** Thin rectangular slab 8 × 8 × 0.5 µm (a quasi-2D "thin cortex-like" network). Also a 3D cubic control 3 × 3 × 3 µm (Fig S5).
- **Boundary conditions:**
  - During preassembly: PBC in x and y; two z-boundaries exert purely repulsive (z-only) forces to confine elements to the slab.
  - After preassembly: PBC deactivated in x; F-actins crossing the x-boundaries are severed and irreversibly clamped to the x-boundaries. PBC retained in y (simulates infinite network).
  - x-boundaries then modeled as elastic substrate, Young's modulus E = 3×10⁴ Pa; network stress read out via substrate strain.
- **Network preassembly (self-assembly):** G-actin polymerizes into F-actins; motor bipolar TF forms by nucleation of a bare zone then symmetric backbone assembly (arms bind F-actin, no walking during assembly); ACPs bind to form functional cross-links. Reference initial state: C_A = 25 µM G-actin, ⟨L_f⟩ ≈ 1.44 µm, R_M = 0.014, R_ACP = 0.1.
- **Stress measurement:** σ(t) = E·ε(t), ε = normal strain of the elastic substrate in x. σ_max = peak; sustainability S = ⟨σ⟩/σ_max, with ⟨σ⟩ time-averaged from the time of σ_max to t = 100 s.
- **Microscopic force readouts:** f_M^max = (F⃗_s,M4 · u⃗)/N_h on motors; f_ACP^max = F⃗_s,ACP · u⃗ on ACPs (u⃗ = unit axial vector toward barbed end; positive toward barbed ends), measured when σ ≈ σ_max.
- **Viscoelastic moduli:** small oscillatory 5% sinusoidal strain applied to +x boundary (−x fixed) → storage E′ and loss E″ vs frequency; phase delay δ = tan⁻¹(E″/E′). Frequency range explored ~0.1–10³ Hz.
- **Morphology metrics:**
  - Motor aggregation: distribution of centroid separation distance between each motor TF and its nearest-neighbor motor TF; rise in relative frequency of the smallest-distance bin = aggregation.
  - Mesh size: domain gridded 400×400 pixels in x–y; pixel = 1 if any actin segment present else 0; per-column (400 px in y) nearest non-zero-pixel distance distribution fit by decaying exponential; decay constant ↓ over time ⇒ mesh size ↑. (z ignored → underestimates absolute mesh but captures relative change.)
- **Simulation duration:** 100 s of network dynamics per run.

## Governing Equations

**(Eq. 1) Force-dependent ACP unbinding (Bell / slip-bond):**
```
k_u,ACP = { k⁰_u,ACP · exp( λ_u,ACP · |F⃗_s,ACP| / (k_B T) )   if r ≥ r_0,ACP
          { k⁰_u,ACP                                            if r < r_0,ACP
```
Meaning: ACP off-rate rises exponentially with extensional tension on the ACP arm (Bell slip-bond); only stretched arms (r ≥ rest length r_0) feel the force acceleration; compressed arms unbind at the zero-force rate.

**(Eq. 2, from Methods §2.5) Substrate stress:**
```
σ(t) = E · ε(t)
```
Meaning: network-generated normal stress = elastic-substrate Young's modulus × substrate normal strain in x.

**(Eq. 3) Sustainability:**
```
S = ⟨σ⟩ / σ_max ,  ⟨σ⟩ = time-average of σ(t) from t(σ_max) to t = 100 s
```
Meaning: fraction of peak stress retained over the run (1 = perfectly sustained).

**(Eq. 4) Microscopic motor force (per head):**
```
f_M^max = (F⃗_s,M4 · u⃗) / N_h   (evaluated at σ ≈ σ_max)
```
Meaning: axial force per myosin head carried by a motor arm at peak stress (F_s,M4 = force on the κ_s,M4 spring).

**(Eq. 5) Microscopic ACP force:**
```
f_ACP^max = F⃗_s,ACP · u⃗   (evaluated at σ ≈ σ_max)
```
Meaning: axial force on an ACP at peak stress, signed relative to barbed-end direction.

**(Eq. 6) Phase delay:**
```
δ = tan⁻¹( E″ / E′ )
```
Meaning: loss/storage phase angle characterizing viscous vs elastic response.

**(Implicit) Overdamped Langevin equation of motion** (inertia neglected): for each segment, drag·v = F_det + F_stoch, with F_det = F_extensional + F_bending + F_repulsive (harmonic), and F_stoch the thermal noise; positions Euler-updated. Exact harmonic forms + κ values deferred to Table S1 / ref 26.

## Force Laws & Potentials
- **Extensional (stretching):** harmonic springs with stiffness κ_s maintaining equilibrium segment lengths. Labeled springs include κ_s,A (actin), κ_s,ACP (ACP arm), κ_s,M and κ_s,M4 (motor; M4 is the specific spring whose force F_s,M4 defines the motor head force in Eq. 4). Functional form F = −κ_s(r − r_0).
- **Bending:** harmonic angular potentials with stiffness κ_b maintaining equilibrium angles at the elastic hinges between segments of F-actin (κ_b,A), motors, and ACPs. Functional form U = ½κ_b(θ − θ_0)².
- **Repulsive (excluded volume):** harmonic repulsion between segments (only repulsive branch), and z-boundary repulsion during preassembly.
- **ACP unbinding:** Bell slip-bond, Eq. 1 (off-rate ↑ with tension).
- **Motor arm kinetics (catch-bond):** walking rate k_w,M and unbinding rate k_u,M both *decrease* with higher applied force (catch-bond, opposite to ACP slip behavior; Fig S1). Binding: a free arm binds an available site at rate 40·N_h s⁻¹. Walking: arm steps to next barbed-ward site (~7 nm/step); slides off after reaching the barbed end. Net unloaded walking velocity ≈140 nm/s; stall force f_M^stall ≈ 5.7 pN.

## Relevance flags
- **DCM?** Weak/indirect. DCM is a surface-mesh continuum (cortex tension as a lumped γ), not filament-resolved; this paper's σ_max ~600 Pa and E~10²–10⁵ Pa emergent moduli could serve as *reference targets* for what a cortex should generate, but the mechanism is below DCM's abstraction level.
- **FF?** YES — strongly. This is essentially an FF-class model: explicit F-actins (bending beams), two-armed cross-linkers with Bell-Evans off-rate (Eq. 1, k⁰*=0.115 s⁻¹, λ=1.04×10⁻¹⁰ m, filamin A), myosin bipolar minifilaments (64 heads, catch-bond walking, v≈140 nm/s, f_stall≈5.7 pN). Direct parameter provenance + force laws for FF's crosslinker and myosin modules; the σ_max ∝ R_M^0.6 and ACP-clutch-stabilization results are candidate emergent-behavior validation targets for FF cortex contractility.
