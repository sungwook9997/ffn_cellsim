## Summary
The paper asks how the *relative activity* of three microscopic cytoskeletal processes — actin turnover (treadmilling), actin-crosslinking-protein (ACP) (un)binding, and myosin-II motor walking — jointly sets the network-scale mechanical state (internal tension magnitude, its sustainability over time, and network morphology). Using a previously validated 3D Brownian-dynamics model (Kim et al.) of semi-flexible actin filaments + force-sensitive ACPs (Bell's-equation unbinding) + myosin-II thick-filament motors (parallel-cluster-model kinetics), the authors show that motor-driven crosslinked networks occupy **two morphological phases**: (i) a *homogeneous* phase with sustained steady-state prestress, and (ii) an *aggregated* (clustered/foci) phase whose stress peaks then collapses as connectivity is lost. **Actin turnover is the decisive stabilizer**: below a critical turnover rate the network collapses into aggregates; above it, it stays homogeneous with sustained (but lower) stress. They map peak stress, a sustainability factor S, and a clustering factor CF over (actin-turnover-rate × %ACP × %motor), find the critical turnover rate falls with more ACPs (→0 near ACP:motor ≈ 100) and rises with more motors, and show that a *constant ratio* of turnover-rate to motor-walking-rate collapses stress profiles onto a master curve. Pulsing turnover across the phase boundary reproduces reversible actomyosin foci formation/dissolution with transient stress spikes on a ~100 s timescale (Drosophila-gastrulation-like pulsed constrictions). A reduced "active kinetic spring" analytic model qualitatively reproduces the peak-force and S phase maps. Finally, live MDA-MB-231 cells expressing LifeAct show actin clusters after Cytochalasin-D (barbed-end capping, turnover suppression) with cluster-formation time constant **τC = 28 ± 5 s** and declustering (washout) **τD = 155 ± 42 s**, consistent with the simulated ~100 s pulse timescale; Latrunculin A (monomer sequestration) gives diffuse actin, few clusters — distinct from Cytochalasin D.

## Model & Methods
**Model type:** 3D coarse-grained **Brownian dynamics** (over-damped Langevin) agent-based cytoskeleton — every actin filament is a chain of cylindrical segments with bending + extensional (stretching) elasticity; ACPs and myosin thick filaments (TF) are explicit mechanical + kinetic elements. This is the Kim-lab engine validated in refs 35–39 (Borau 2012; Kim, Hwang & Kamm 2011; Kim, Hwang, Lee & Kamm 2009; Kim 2015; Kim, Gardel & Munro 2014). Full equations/constants are in *Supplementary Note 1 / Table 1* (not in this PDF).

**Governing dynamics (from the model family, as described):** over-damped Langevin — displacement of each bead from the balance of elastic (bending + stretch), motor, thermal (Brownian), and drag forces; ACP unbinding is force-dependent via **Bell's equation** (ref 41); motor (myosin-II TF) walking/binding/unbinding governed by the **parallel cluster model** of Erdmann & Schwarz (ref 42) for force-sensitive myosin thick filaments.

**Active processes added onto passive crosslinked gel:**
1. **Motor walking** — myosin-II thick filaments = a backbone with multiple arms (multiple heads); processive; bind multiple actin filaments and walk toward barbed ends generating tension. Motors have 64 heads by default; **256 heads** used for the foci-pulse run (Fig. 4) to aggregate faster.
2. **Actin turnover = treadmilling** — polymerization only at barbed ends, depolymerization only at pointed ends at similar rates → steady-state per-filament turnover rate. On depolymerization, bound ACPs and motors detach spontaneously. "Actin turnover rate" throughout the paper = this steady-state treadmilling rate (per filament), reported in s⁻¹ (0–300 s⁻¹). Details in Kim, Gardel & Munro 2014 (ref 39).

**Domains / boundary conditions:**
- **3D networks:** 3 × 3 × 3 µm cube, **periodic BCs in all directions**. Primary geometry for the paper.
- **2D ("cortical") networks:** 8 × 8 × 0.5 µm slab, periodic BCs only in x and y (shallow z ⇒ filaments lie in-plane). Mimics cortex / cells on 2D substrates; used to probe larger length scales cheaply.
- Actin concentration **25 µM** (unless noted); ACP and motor amounts given as % relative to actin (e.g., 5% ACP, 1% motor).
- Initial condition: a homogeneous, well-connected network is polymerized in presence of ACPs/motors BEFORE motors are switched on (motor walking starts at t = 0).

**Stress measurement protocol:** internal stress = sum of tension on constituents crossing a cross-section ÷ cross-section area, averaged over multiple cross-sections through the domain. **Peak stress** = maximum network stress after motor activation, averaged over a 10-s interval. Stress fluctuations measured over total area 27 µm² (9 µm² in each orthogonal axis).

**Metrics (SI-defined):**
- **Sustainability S** (Supplementary Note 2 / Fig. 6): S≈1 ⇒ stress sustained over long times; S≈0 ⇒ stress decays rapidly. Rate of stress collapse ∝ (1 − S). Measurable experimentally by traction-force microscopy.
- **Clustering factor CF** (Supplementary Note 3 / Fig. 7): CF≈1 ⇒ homogeneous morphology; CF≈0 ⇒ highly aggregated. Measurable by fluorescence actin imaging.
- S and CF mapped at t = 40 s after motor activation (Fig. 3); representative morphologies at 51 s.

**Reduced analytic model — "active kinetic spring"** (Supplementary Note 6, Fig. 6): actin–ACP construct = a spring with force-sensitive binding kinetics + dynamic turnover (disintegration/regeneration); effective spring constant ∝ number of currently-bound springs; motors contract springs; total wall force = sum of contractile forces of bound springs. Parameterized by spring:motor:actin ratios and relative turnover rate kd/ku0. Reproduces peak-force and S phase maps qualitatively.

**Live-cell experiments (Methods):**
- Cells: MDA-MB-231 (metastatic breast cancer) expressing LifeAct fluorescent F-actin (gift, Lauffenburger Lab), 37 °C, 5% CO₂, DMEM + 10% FBS + 1% pen-strep, plated on 2D glass-bottom wells, imaged after 1 day.
- Drugs: **2.5 µM Cytochalasin D** (barbed-end capping ⇒ blocks polym+depolym); **1 µM Latrunculin A** (sequesters G-actin monomers). Concentrations at ~maximal saturating effect.
- Imaging: spinning-disk confocal, 63× / 1.4 NA oil objective (Leica); z-stacks, time resolution 10 s, z step 2.5 µm, x–y pixel 0.179 µm; ImageJ + custom Matlab.
- Analysis: 5.4 × 5.4 µm ImageJ box on clusters; max-intensity z-projection; intensity normalized (subtract min, divide max); fit to sigmoids (eqs 1,2) to extract τC, τD.

## Governing Equations
The core BD equations of motion, bending/stretching potentials, Bell off-rate, and parallel-cluster-model motor kinetics are in *Supplementary Note 1 / Table 1* and prior Kim-lab papers (refs 35–42) — NOT printed in this main text. The only equations explicitly transcribed in this PDF are the two empirical sigmoid fits used to extract experimental cluster time constants:

**(Eq 1) Clustering sigmoid (Cytochalasin-D-induced cluster growth):**
```
Intensity = A + B / ( 1 + exp( −(t − t0)/τC ) )
```
Meaning: normalized F-actin intensity rises sigmoidally from baseline A to plateau A+B; τC = clustering time constant (rise); t0 = per-cluster onset offset (clusters don't start simultaneously).

**(Eq 2) Declustering sigmoid (Cytochalasin-D washout, cluster decay):**
```
Intensity = A + B / ( 1 + exp( +(t − t0)/τD ) )
```
Meaning: intensity falls sigmoidally as clusters disperse after drug washout; τD = declustering time constant (decay). Same fitting constants A, B, t0 as Eq 1.

**Referenced (not transcribed) mechanistic laws:**
- **Bell's equation** (ref 41, Bell 1978) — force-dependent ACP off-rate: koff = koff0·exp(F·x/kBT) form; the actual ACP rupture parameters are anchored to Ferrer et al. 2008 (ref 40) single-molecule actin/ABP rupture-force measurements.
- **Parallel cluster model** (ref 42, Erdmann & Schwarz 2012) — force-sensitive walking/binding/unbinding of myosin-II thick filaments.
- **Active-kinetic-spring** constitutive relation (Supplementary Note 6) — net force = Σ over bound springs of motor contractile force; spring population governed by force-dependent binding + turnover kinetics parameterized by kd/ku0.

## Force Laws & Potentials
Functional forms and constants are in the SI (not this PDF). Qualitative content present in main text:
- **Actin filament:** semi-flexible; bending stiffness (persistence length) + extensional (stretch) stiffness — harmonic bending + stretching potentials (Kim-lab standard).
- **ACP:** mechanical crosslinker with its own bending + extensional stiffness; binds actin dynamically; unbinds force-dependently via Bell's equation (koff rises with load) — anchored to Ferrer 2008 rupture forces. Limiting case of **permanent bonds** (non-unbinding, à la fascin/scruin) simulated separately (Supplementary Fig. 10).
- **Motor (myosin-II TF):** backbone + multiple arms (heads: 64 default, 256 for Fig. 4); processive walking toward barbed ends; walking/binding/unbinding force-sensitive per parallel cluster model; generates internal tension.
- **Thermal / drag:** Brownian (thermal) forces + viscous drag (over-damped Langevin).
- **Excluded volume / repulsion:** standard in the Kim engine (SI).
