## Summary

This is a wet-lab protocol for an in-vitro optogenetic system that assembles branched (Arp2/3-mediated) actin networks with spatially and temporally controlled geometry, density, and thickness on a supported lipid bilayer (SLB) mimicking the plasma membrane. His-mEGFP-iLID is pre-anchored to a Ni-NTA (DGS-NTA(Ni))-containing POPC bilayer; the nucleation-promoting factor construct SspB-mScarlet-I-VCA (VCA from WAVE) is supplied in bulk along with actin (10% Alexa647-labeled), Arp2/3 complex, profilin, and capping protein plus an ATP-regenerating energy mix. Blue light (470/40 nm, delivered through a DMD for arbitrary patterns) drives iLID–SspB heterodimerization, recruiting SspB-VCA to the membrane; the local increase in VCA density triggers Arp2/3-branched actin polymerization confined to the illuminated region, and turning the light off halts polymerization (reversible dark dissociation). Network density is set by light intensity (tunable range ~0.04–0.18 nW/µm², saturating ~0.18 nW/µm²) and by the DGS-NTA(Ni) mol fraction (protocol uses 6%); network thickness is set by illumination duration. The protocol details lipid vesicle prep, flow-cell fabrication, iLID-SLB formation, DMD intensity calibration, the illumination timing scheme (500 ms pulses every 20 s), imaging, quantification via fluorescence-intensity calibration to known F-actin concentrations, plus depletion-effect, phototoxicity, and troubleshooting notes. It is a purely experimental/protocol paper — no simulation, no governing equations.

## Model & Methods

- **Type:** Experimental in-vitro reconstitution + optogenetics. No computational model, no integrator, no timestep. "Method" content is a physical protocol.
- **Optogenetic actuator:** iLID (improved light-induced dimer, ref [16] Guntas 2014) anchored via His-tag to Ni-NTA lipid; SspB (fused to mScarlet-I-VCA) binds iLID under blue light and dissociates in the dark (reversible). Blue light → membrane recruitment of the NPF.
- **Nucleation chemistry:** VCA (from WAVE) — V domain binds G-actin, CA domain binds Arp2/3 complex → Arp2/3 branch nucleation. Two membrane-proximal VCA domains + Arp2/3 + an actin filament fragment stochastically assemble to trigger branched polymerization. Profilin suppresses spontaneous bulk nucleation; capping protein (CP) limits filament elongation (controls mesh/branch density and prevents runaway filaments).
- **Geometry / substrate:** Handmade flow cell (~10–15 µL): 24×60 mm bottom coverslip + 18×18 mm top coverslip separated by 0.3 mm double-sided tape (2–3 mm channel gap), sealed with VaLaP. SLB is POPC + 6% (mol/mol) DGS-NTA(Ni), formed by vesicle fusion (0.33 mM vesicles, 10 min RT), then His-mEGFP-iLID (7 µM, 5 min).
- **Illumination / patterning:** Nikon ECLIPSE Ti2 + DMD module (TI-LA-DMD) delivers arbitrary 2D patterns; SOLA light source; ND filter (NE220B) attenuates so that 100% software output ≈ 1 nW/µm² at the objective. Spinning-disk confocal (CSU-W1), 60× NA 1.42 objective, EMCCD (iXon Life 888). Imaging channels 405/488/561/640 nm.
- **Stimulation protocol (Figure 4):** Blue-light stimulation pulses 500 ms every 20 s to continuously hold SspB on the membrane. Cycle structure: ×2 pre-cycles then ×10–30 cycles; acquisition at 640/561 nm interleaved with 470/40 nm stimulation; SspB recruitment appears ~20 s after light onset, then actin polymerization begins.
- **Density/thickness control knobs:** (1) light intensity (0.04–0.18 nW/µm²), (2) illumination duration (thickness), (3) DGS-NTA(Ni) mol fraction (VCA anchor site density), (4) illumination area.
- **Quantification:** Fluorescence intensity of Alexa647-actin and mScarlet-VCA over time; absolute density via a calibration curve from F-actin solutions of known concentration under identical microscope settings. ≥3 independent reconstitutions recommended.
- **Algorithm outline:** (A) make vesicle stock → (B) calibrate DMD light intensity → (C) build flow cell → (D) form iLID-SLB → (E) load polymerization mix, illuminate patterns, image.

## Relevance flags

- **DCM?** No (weak). This is a molecular in-vitro actin-network reconstitution; DCM is a surface-mesh continuum cell model — no direct coupling. Only tangential (branched cortex-like actin sheets on a membrane).
- **FF?** Weakly yes (context, not parameters). FF is a Cytosim-style fine-grained actin engine with Arp2/3 branching, capping, profilin, VCA/NPF nucleation — this paper's system (Arp2/3 branched network, CP-limited length, profilin-suppressed nucleation, membrane-anchored NPF) is exactly the biology FF models. But the paper gives NO rate constants, force laws, or mechanistic parameters to import — it is a wet-lab protocol. Useful as an experimental *scenario/validation concept* (density set by NPF surface density; thickness set by growth time; depletion-limited overshoot), not as a parameter source.
- **TAG-validation?** No. No mechanistic parameters or closed-form results to register as KB validation targets. The quantitative datasets (density-vs-intensity, thickness-vs-duration) live in companion Nat Commun [30] and would be sourced from there, not this protocol.
