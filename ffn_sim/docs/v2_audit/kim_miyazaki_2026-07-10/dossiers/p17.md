## Summary
The paper dissects the temporal organization of the RhoA→ROCK/formin→actomyosin signaling cascade that drives pulsed cortical contractions in the early *C. elegans* embryo (2-cell stage, AB cell, interphase). Using two-color near-TIRF/HILO microscopy of endogenous fusion reporters, the authors measure a stereotyped **~4.5 s delay** between upstream activators (RhoA proxy GFP::AHPH, ROCK/GFP::LET-502, formin CYK-1::GFP) and downstream Myosin II (NMY-2::mKate2) accumulation, while F-actin (UTR::GFP) precedes Myosin II by a small but significant **~0.5 s**. Single-molecule particle-tracking resolves the effective binding rate *k_app* (molecules·s⁻¹) and unbinding rate *k_off* (s⁻¹) of Myosin II and actin during pulses, and a simple weakly-activated-cascade ODE model (d[Myo*]/dt ≈ k_app − k_off·[Myo*]) shows the ROCK→Myosin delay (~4.25 s of the 4.5 s) arises almost entirely from **Myosin binding/unbinding kinetics acting as a capacitor/high-pass filter**, not from slow phosphorylation biochemistry. A "signaled concentration" [Myo⁰]=k_app/k_off quantifies how far out of equilibrium the system sits. Genetic perturbations (myosin phosphatase *mel-11(it26)*; cofilin *unc-60(RNAi)*) compress the dynamic range as predicted. Finally, agent-based Brownian-dynamics simulations of a cortex-like actomyosin meshwork (Kim model, 20×20×0.1 µm) show the F-actin-before-myosin **delay (0/5/10 s) non-monotonically tunes force deployment** — a ~5 s delay maximizes net contractile force by giving filaments time to cross-link before motors engage, avoiding premature network collapse.

## Model & Methods

Three coupled approaches:

**1. Experimental (in-vivo single-molecule microscopy).**
- System: early *C. elegans* embryo, 2-cell stage, AB cell during interphase; RhoA-driven pulsed cortical contractions (period ~30 s).
- Imaging: near-TIRF / HILO (Highly Inclined thin illumination, Tokunaga 2008) on inverted Nikon Ti-E, CFI Apo 1.45-NA/100× oil TIRF objective, 488 nm 50-mW sapphire laser, laser angle 65°, Perfect Focus. Cameras: Andor iXon3 897 EMCCD (107 nm pixel) or Photometrics Prime 95B (73 nm pixel), 1.5× extra magnification.
- Reporters: endogenous/CRISPR GFP & mKate2 knock-ins — GFP::AHPH (RhoA biosensor, Anillin C-term), CYK-1::GFP (formin), GFP::LET-502 (ROCK), MLC-4::GFP (myosin reg. light chain), NMY-2::mKate2 / NMY-2::GFP (myosin heavy chain), UTR::GFP (F-actin/utrophin CH domain), Actin::GFP, CAP-1::GFP (capping), MEL-11::GFP (myosin phosphatase).
- Pulse imaging: 50 ms exposure, no interframe delay (100 ms/channel effective), average 5 frames → 2 fps.
- Single-molecule imaging: GFP RNAi to lower fluorophore density; 500 ms exposure, 5% of 90 mW 488 nm.
- Tracking: MATLAB Crocker–Grier (Kilfoil lab implementation); particle size 3 px, max displacement 4 px, memory 3 frames.
- Adaptive ROI: convex-hull ROI seeded at pulse "seed time," advected by tracked molecules / local velocity field (radius 30 px), removing cortical advection confounds (residual effect <5%). Amount of contraction per pulse ~5%.
- Perturbations: *mel-11(it26)* temperature-sensitive null (restrictive 25°C, hypercontractile); *unc-60(RNAi)* (cofilin, slows actin turnover; Ahringer library).
- Statistics: one-sample & paired Student t-tests; N(embryos)≥5–10, N(pulses) 38–200+.

**2. ODE kinetic model** (weakly-activated cascade, à la Heinrich/Neel/Rapoport 2002). See Governing Equations.

**3. Agent-based Brownian-dynamics simulation of actomyosin** ("well-established" Kim model; Li 2017; Jung, Murrell & Kim 2015; T. Kim 2009; Mak 2016).
- Coarse-graining: actin filaments (F-actin), motors, and actin cross-linking proteins (ACPs) represented as **cylindrical segments**.
- Dynamics: **Langevin equation for Brownian dynamics** for every cylindrical segment.
- Deterministic forces: bending force (maintains equilibrium inter-segment angle) + extensional force (maintains equilibrium segment length) + repulsive volume-exclusion force between neighboring segment pairs.
- Filament kinetics: nucleation → barbed-end polymerization + pointed-end depolymerization (treadmilling).
- ACP: bind at constant rate (no cross-linking-angle preference); unbind at force-dependent rate via **Bell's law** (Bell 1978).
- Motors: each arm binds at constant rate, walks toward barbed end, or unbinds at force-dependent rate via the **parallel cluster model** (Erdmann, Albert & Schwarz 2013; Erdmann & Schwarz 2012). Motors self-assemble into thick-filament (minifilament) structures.
- Domain: thin slab **20 × 20 × 0.1 µm**; periodic BC in x,y; repulsive walls in z. Initial thin network self-assembled from F-actin + ACP.
- RhoA activation protocol: domain split into **16 subdomains (4×4)**; every 30 s one random subdomain activated. In it, a fraction of barbed ends polymerize faster by factor ρ_f for duration τ_f (reference ρ_f=10, τ_f=10 s → ~10 µm elongation; alt statement: elongation rate raised to 1.2 µm/s held ~10 s → filaments reach ~12 µm). After time delay d_M, motors self-assemble into thick filaments for duration τ_M (reference d_M=5 s, τ_M=15 s), contract the subdomain, then disassemble into non-binding monomers.
- Control variable: the **delay** between formin activation and myosin activation, swept at 0, 5, 10 s.

## Governing Equations

**(Eq. 1) Full weakly-activated myosin accumulation (p.10, line 246):**
$$\frac{d[Myo^*]}{dt} \approx k_{on}\cdot[ROCK]\cdot[Myo_T]\cdot\left(1-\frac{[Myo^*]}{[Myo_T]}\right) - k_{off}\cdot[Myo^*]$$
Meaning: rate of phosphorylated (active/cortical) myosin = ROCK-driven recruitment from the total pool (saturating in [Myo*]/[MyoT]) minus first-order unbinding.

**(Eq. 2) Reduced linear form under [Myo*]≪[MyoT] (p.10, line 251):**
$$\frac{d[Myo^*]}{dt} \approx k_{on}^{app} - k_{off}\cdot[Myo^*]$$
Meaning: with the pool undepleted, dynamics reduce to a constant effective binding rate k_app (= k_on·[ROCK]·[MyoT], hence **linear in [ROCK]**) minus first-order unbinding — a leaky-integrator / RC-capacitor.

**(Eq. 3) Signaled (instantaneous steady-state) concentration (p.10, line 257):**
$$[Myo^0] = \frac{k_{app}}{k_{off}}$$
Meaning: the concentration the system would reach if k_app,k_off froze — a proxy for instantaneous upstream ROCK signaling intensity; its gap from the effective concentration measures how far out of equilibrium the system is.

**(Eq. 4) High-pass-filter attenuation factor (Supplementary Note 1; p.11, line 303):**
$$\sqrt{1+\frac{\omega^{2}}{k_{off}^{2}}}$$
Meaning: for sinusoidal upstream forcing at angular frequency ω, myosin turnover attenuates/phase-shifts the output like a high-pass filter; slow signals (ω≪k_off) pass near-equilibrium, fast signals drive the system far out of equilibrium.

**(Eq. 5) Turnover master equation, single population (Methods, p.24, line 666):**
$$\frac{dN}{dt} = k_{app} - k_{off}N$$
Meaning: number of molecules N(t) in a tracked region; k_app = molecules binding per unit time (molecule·s⁻¹), k_off = fraction unbinding per unit time (s⁻¹).

**(Eq. 6) Discrete update (p.24, line 668):**
$$N_{t+1}-N_t = (k_{app}-k_{off}N)\,dt$$

**(Eq. 7) Step-change (state1→state2) analytical solution (p.25, line 676):**
$$N(t)=\frac{k_{app}^{2}}{k_{off}^{2}}+\left(\frac{k_{app}^{1}}{k_{off}^{1}}-\frac{k_{app}^{2}}{k_{off}^{2}}\right)e^{-k_2 t}=N_{eq}^{2}+\left(N_{eq}^{1}-N_{eq}^{2}\right)e^{-k_2 t}$$
Meaning: exponential relaxation between two equilibria N_eq=k_app/k_off with time constant 1/k₂ (k₂ = k_off of state 2). (A more general sinusoidal-forcing solution is in Supplementary Note 1.)

**(Eq. 8) Out-of-equilibrium fold-difference descriptor (p.25, line 682):**
$$\Delta=\frac{max_{target}-min_{target}}{max_{measured}-min_{measured}}$$
Meaning: ratio of signaled-concentration amplitude to effective-concentration amplitude (Fig. 2M metric).

**(Eq. 9) Intensity normalization (Methods, p.22, line 611):**
$$I_{normalized}=\frac{I-I_{min}}{I_{max}-I_{min}}$$

**Langevin equation (agent-based model, p.26):** the motion of every cylindrical segment obeys the overdamped Langevin/Brownian-dynamics equation with deterministic bending + extensional + volume-exclusion forces plus thermal noise (functional form not written explicitly in this text; references Li 2017 / Jung-Murrell-Kim 2015 / Kim 2009 / Mak 2016).

## Force Laws & Potentials
(Agent-based model; explicit constants are in the cited prior papers, not tabulated here.)
- **Extensional (stretching) force:** harmonic restoring force toward equilibrium segment length (maintains filament/ACP/motor arm rest length).
- **Bending force:** harmonic restoring toward equilibrium angle between consecutive segments (filament bending rigidity).
- **Volume-exclusion (repulsion):** repulsive force between neighboring non-bonded segment pairs (steric/excluded volume).
- **ACP unbinding — Bell's law:** k_off = k_off⁰·exp(F·λ/k_BT) form (force-dependent off-rate; Bell 1978). Binding at constant rate, no angular preference.
- **Motor force-dependent kinetics — Parallel Cluster Model (Erdmann & Schwarz):** motor arms bind at constant rate; walk toward barbed end and unbind at force-dependent rates governed by the multi-head parallel-cluster stochastic model; motors act cooperatively as bipolar thick filaments (minifilaments).
- **z-boundary:** repulsive wall force on segments crossing the slab boundary.
- **RC/leaky-integrator (kinetic model):** myosin cortical concentration behaves as a first-order low-pass in state but a high-pass in the input→out-of-equilibrium mapping (attenuation √(1+ω²/k_off²)).

## Relevance flags
- **DCM? — LOW/indirect.** This is a cortex-mechanics + signaling paper, not a surface-mesh continuum cell model. The pulsed-contraction period (~30 s), ~5% local contraction, and cortical-tension modulation could inform DCM's actomyosin cortex-tension time-dependence, but nothing maps directly to node-face/membrane mechanics.
- **FF? — YES (strong).** The agent-based Brownian-dynamics actomyosin network (Kim/Jung/Murrell) is essentially the same physics class as FF (Cytosim-style): cylindrical filament segments under Langevin dynamics, harmonic bending+extensional potentials, excluded-volume repulsion, **Bell's-law** cross-linker off-rate, and **parallel-cluster-model** motor force-velocity/detachment — all directly comparable to FF's beam filaments, Bell-Evans cross-linkers, and Hill/minifilament myosin. The formin barbed-end elongation (1.2 µm/s, ~10 s, ~12 µm) and RhoA-gated local activation protocol are concrete recipes for FF's protrusion/turnover machinery.
