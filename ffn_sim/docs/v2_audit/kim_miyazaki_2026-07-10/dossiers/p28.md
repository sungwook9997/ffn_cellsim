## Summary
This is a review of the Ishiwata group's multi-decade program on "bio-motile systems" viewed as **biological liquid crystals**, spanning three subsystems. (1) **Striated-muscle sarcomeres** are treated as a smectic-A liquid crystal (thick/thin myofilaments on a hexagonal cross-sectional lattice; thick:thin = 1:2 frog skeletal, 1:3 fly flight muscle, with genuine liquid-crystal dislocations/point defects). The muscle's auto-oscillatory **SPOC** (Spontaneous Oscillatory Contraction) — a saw-tooth sarcomere-length oscillation (quick stretch / slow shortening) that is the "third state" between contraction and relaxation — is reported for Ca-SPOC (pCa ≈ 6) and ADP-SPOC (high MgADP + Pi, no Ca²⁺), plus a three-equation **mathematical unit-sarcomere model** (cross-bridge fraction P(ξ,t) whose attach/detach rates depend on the myofilament lattice spacing) extended to myofibril (metachronal wave) and muscle-fiber (lateral k_z spring) scales. In-vivo cardiac **SL-nanometry** in living mice (20 nm / 100 fps) measures sarcomere shortening (2.00→1.72 µm, ~14%) and a positive ΔSL–ΔLVP correlation (Frank–Starling). (2) **Mitotic-spindle micromechanics** via microneedle/cantilever manipulation of Xenopus-egg-extract spindles: the spindle is an "active nematic liquid crystal / tactoid" with rigidity 10–100 Pa, ~2× pole-to-pole anisotropy, an anomalous Poisson ratio ~2.0, cut/fuse shape-memory, and active microrheology showing elastic-solid (<1 s) → viscous-fluid (~10 s) → active elastic-restoring (~minutes) behavior; external mechanical impulses to mitotic HeLa cells bias chromosome-segregation timing. (3) **Artificial-cell reconstitution** (Miyazaki): purified actin + bundler (methylcellulose/PEG) confined in cell-sized water-in-oil droplets spontaneously forms a single equatorial **contractile actin ring** when droplet diameter < 20 µm (≈ actin persistence length L_p = 5–15 µm); HMM myosin promotes ring formation and drives constriction (rate ∝ ring diameter); the inverted-emulsion (transfer) method yields >90% uni-lamellar giant liposomes for future deformable cell models.

## Model & Methods
Review — no single unified model; multiple experimental + theoretical methods are summarized:

**SPOC mathematical model (refs [2] Sato 2011 PBMB; [3] Sato 2013 PRL — described, not transcribed in full):**
- Model type: deterministic **ODE unit-sarcomere model** → visco-elastically coupled 1D chain (myofibril) → laterally coupled bundle (muscle fiber). Mean-field cross-bridge population dynamics, not particle-based.
- Unit model = three coupled linear ODEs; nonlinearity enters solely through the cross-bridge proportion P(ξ,t).
- Assumptions: (i) myosin is two-state (attached / detached) with attach & detach **rates that depend on the inter-myofilament lattice spacing** (the paper's "original assumption"); lattice volume ~constant so inter-filament distance grows as the sarcomere shortens; (ii) longitudinal force balance among external load, cross-bridge active force, and viscous molecular friction (cross-bridge drag); active force ∝ number of cross-bridges in the thick/thin overlap; (iii) transverse (vertical) force balance from cross-bridge + lattice + Z-disc elasticity.
- Myofibril model: sarcomeres connected in series through Z-discs/M-lines; **Local coupling** (adjacent lattice constants tend to equalize) + **Global coupling** (equal longitudinal force, sarcomere inertia neglected) → reproduces in-phase, metachronal-wave, disrupted-traveling-wave, out-of-phase SPOC patterns.
- Muscle-fiber model: lateral mechanical spring (constant k_z) between Z-discs/M-lines of adjacent myofibrils; end compliance k_a governs phase difference of traveling SPOC.

**In-vivo cardiac sarcomere imaging (SL-nanometry, refs [42,43,45]):** adenoviral α-actinin-AcGFP labeling of Z-discs in open-chest anesthetized mouse LV; confocal/EMCCD; simultaneous ECG + left-ventricular-pressure (LVP) + camera-shutter signals synchronized at 5 kHz; 10.02 ms/frame (100 fps); spatial resolution 20 nm; multi-peak Gaussian Z-disc fitting → sub-pixel SL. Neonatal-cardiomyocyte version adds fluo8 Ca²⁺ indicator and IR-laser microscopic heating (RT→40 °C) with FFT of SL and F.I.

**Mitotic-spindle micromanipulation (refs [4,19,20,22,60]):** spindles self-assembled in undiluted Xenopus laevis egg cytoplasmic extracts; inverted fluorescence microscope; force-calibrated glass microneedles / MEMS cantilevers; cut-and-fuse assays; quasi-static force–deformation (rigidity, anisotropy, Poisson ratio); sinusoidal **active microrheology** (Lissajous force–deformation, dynamic stiffness |G*| and phase δ over ω = 0.001–10 rad/s); single sub-100-ms mechanical impulses to metaphase HeLa cells between cantilever pairs.

**Artificial-cell reconstitution (refs [5,62,76]):** purified actin ± bundler (methylcellulose / PEG, depletion effect; or α-actinin) ± HMM (heavy meromyosin, 2-headed, filament-incompetent) or full-length myosin, encapsulated in **cell-sized water-in-oil droplets** (phospholipid monolayer, inverted emulsion / vortex-tap emulsification); polymerization triggered by raising salt; optical microscopy of ring formation vs droplet radius. Liposome route: transfer/inverted-emulsion method (Pautot–Weitz 2003) → giant uni-lamellar liposomes; lamellarity quantified from membrane fluorescence-intensity vs diameter (discrete tiers).

## Governing Equations
This review transcribes no equations in closed form; the models are described verbally (full forms are in refs [2],[3],[38]). Faithfully reconstructed relationships:

1. **pCa definition:** pCa = −log₁₀[Ca²⁺]. — activation coordinate; SPOC at intermediate pCa ≈ 6.
2. **SPOC active force (sliding-filament, described):** F_active ∝ N_xb(overlap) = P(ξ,t)·N_total, where ξ = thick/thin overlap distance, P = attached-cross-bridge fraction. — active longitudinal force scales with number of engaged cross-bridges.
3. **Longitudinal force balance (unit sarcomere, described):** F_external_load = F_crossbridge(active) − F_viscous_friction(cross-bridge drag). — 1D sarcomere force ledger.
4. **Cross-bridge rate–lattice coupling (key assumption, described):** k_attach = k_attach(d_lattice), k_detach = k_detach(d_lattice), with d_lattice ↑ as sarcomere shortens (constant-volume lattice). — inter-filament spacing feeds back on kinetics → generates oscillation.
5. **Transverse force balance (described):** F_vertical = elastic(cross-bridge) + elastic(lattice) + elastic(Z-disc) = 0. — radial equilibrium sets d_lattice.
6. **Myofibril coupling (described):** Local coupling d_lattice,i → d_lattice,i±1 (adjacent equalize) + Global coupling F_long,i = const ∀ i (inertia ignored). — series chain producing wave patterns.
7. **Muscle-fiber lateral coupling (described):** spring constant k_z between adjacent-myofibril Z-discs; end compliance k_a controls inter-myofibril phase difference (ref [38]).
8. **In-vivo ΔSL–ΔLVP linear fit (Fig. 1-8a):** ΔLVP = 287.3 · ΔSL (mmHg, µm), P < 0.05, R = 0.60. — sarcomere-dynamics → cardiac-output coupling (Frank–Starling correlate).
9. **Confinement–ring criterion (described, Fig. 3-3d):** ring self-organizes when R_droplet ≲ L_p (actin persistence length) — for R > L_p filament acts as flexible polymer; for R ≤ L_p filament acts as a rigid rod that minimizes bending energy at the droplet equator.

## Force Laws & Potentials
- **Cross-bridge active force:** proportional to engaged-cross-bridge number P(ξ,t)·N (Eq. 2); no explicit force–velocity (Hill) law given in this review — the mechanism is population-based.
- **Molecular friction:** viscous drag of cross-bridges (linear friction) opposing sliding (Eq. 3).
- **Elastic restoring:** Hookean elements for cross-bridge, myofilament lattice, Z-disc (transverse) and lateral k_z / end k_a springs (fiber). Linear-spring potentials.
- **Bending-energy minimization (actin ring):** rigid-rod filament (L_p ≥ R_droplet) localizes to droplet equator to minimize elastic bending energy — implicit worm-like-chain bending potential E_bend = (κ/2)∫(∂θ/∂s)² ds with κ = k_B T · L_p.
- **Depletion (bundling) force:** Asakura–Oosawa depletion attraction from methylcellulose/PEG (ref [63]) bundles actin filaments.
- No Bell/Evans or catch-bond off-rate laws, no LJ/excluded-volume potentials, no explicit integrator/timestep are given (review level).
