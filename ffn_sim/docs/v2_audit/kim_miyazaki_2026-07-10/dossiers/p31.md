## Summary
The paper asks how the *mobility* of myosin motors — as realized experimentally when heavy meromyosin (HMM) is anchored to a fluid supported lipid bilayer rather than fixed to glass — reshapes the collective behavior of gliding F-actin in a myosin motility assay. Using a well-established Kim-lab agent-based Brownian-dynamics model with **explicit** motors (each motor a single cylindrical segment representing N_H=4 myosin heads, walking/unbinding governed by the Parallel Cluster Model, PCM), the authors simulate a 50×50×0.1 µm quasi-2D domain with periodic BCs, varying motor mobility via the motor drag coefficient ζ_M (parameterized by a relative diffusion coefficient D̄_M = D_M/D*_M, D*_M = 5.11×10⁻¹⁴ m²/s). The central result: **higher motor mobility drives F-actin into distinct, large, round clusters with strong polar ordering** (P high), whereas **immobile/low-mobility motors produce long thin bundles with only nematic ordering** (P low, S high in both). Clusters organize with F-actin pointed ends toward the center, stabilized by **+1/2 nematic "wedge" defects** that trap filaments; motors that bind then walk toward barbed ends and are expelled, producing robust **phase separation** between motors and F-actin (quantified by a grid-based mixing metric M/M₀ and by a declining bound-motor fraction). Cluster formation is enhanced by **shorter filaments** (reference ⟨L_f⟩=2.58 µm; clusters at 1.29–2.58 µm, rings at 7.43 µm) and by **higher actin density** (903–12040 monomers/µm²; negligible collective behavior at 903, strong clusters at 6020–12040). Bausch-lab bilayer experiments (biotinylated HMM on DOPC SLB, gelsolin-controlled filament length, TIRF) qualitatively reproduce the length/concentration trends: short + high-concentration → clusters, long + low-concentration → bundles.

## Model & Methods
- **Model type:** Agent-based **Brownian dynamics** (overdamped Langevin, no inertia), quasi-2D. Both F-actin and motors are chains of cylindrical segments; explicit motors (not implicit force application). Established Kim-lab model (refs [29–31]).
- **Domain / geometry:** 3D rectangular box **50 × 50 × 0.1 µm**. F-actin nodes constrained to plane z = 13.5 nm; motor bodies at z = 0; the 13.5 nm gap = motor-segment equilibrium length. Displacements computed only in x, y (z fixed).
- **Boundary conditions:** Periodic in x and y.
- **Integrator:** Explicit **Euler** scheme for the overdamped Langevin equation.
- **Timestep:** Δt = **1.15 × 10⁻⁵ s**.
- **Total simulated time:** 200 s (all figure time-series run to 200 s).
- **Two-phase protocol:**
  1. **Initiation phase** — actin nucleates and polymerizes (no depolymerization) to build a distribution of filament lengths uniformly in the xy plane; motors bind but do NOT walk. Sets ⟨L_f⟩ via nucleation rate.
  2. **Production phase** — actin (de)polymerization stops (length distribution frozen); motors begin walking toward barbed ends, propelling F-actin (and moving themselves if mobile).
- **Motor representation:** each motor = one cylindrical segment standing in for N_H = 4 myosin heads (stall force + kinetics lumped). Binding sites on F-actin every 7 nm; binding rate k_{+,M} = 40·N_H s⁻¹. Walking (k_{w,M}) and unbinding (k_{u,M}) rates from the **Parallel Cluster Model (PCM)** (Erdmann & Schwarz, refs [33,34]): 3 mechanochemical states, 5 transition rates (Table S2, in SI).
- **Motor mobility control:** vary motor drag ζ_M; report as diffusion coeff D_M = k_BT/ζ_M and relative D̄_M = D_M/D*_M with reference D*_M = 5.11×10⁻¹⁴ m²/s. Values used: D̄_M ∈ {0, 0.0625, 0.25, 1, 4}. D̄_M = 0 ⇔ ζ_M = ∞ ⇔ fixed motors (glass-surface analog). MSD without F-actin used to verify mobility (Fig. S1b).
- **Forces:** F-actin governed by extensional + bending + repulsive (excluded-volume) harmonic potentials; motors governed by extensional force only (no motor–motor repulsion — explicitly noted as a limitation).
- **Order-parameter analysis:** nematic S and polar P computed over all F-actin pairs within 1 µm; velocity correlation ⟨cos α⟩ over endpoint pairs within 1 µm sampled every 1 s; phase-separation metric M/M₀ on a 100×100 grid.
- **Experiments (Bausch lab, Appendix B):** rabbit skeletal-muscle actin + HMM; biotinylated HMM bound via streptavidin to a supported lipid bilayer (98% DOPC / 2% DSPE-PEG(2000)-biotin / 0.05% Texas Red DHPE); filament length set by gelsolin:actin ratio (short 1:50, medium 1:190, long 1:500); Alexa-488-phalloidin labeling; 2 mM ATP + 0.2% methylcellulose crowding + oxygen scavenger; imaged by TIRF (Leica DMi8, 100× TIRF objective) ~30 min after ATP.

## Governing Equations
1. **(A1) Overdamped Langevin (force balance, no inertia):**  F_i − ζ_i (dr_i/dt) + F_i^T = 0.
   — Deterministic force + drag + thermal force sum to zero on endpoint i.
2. **(A2) Fluctuation–dissipation (thermal force statistics):**  ⟨F_i^T(t) F_j^T(t)⟩ = (2 k_B T ζ_i δ_ij / Δt) δ.
   — Gaussian white noise; variance set by drag and temperature; δ_ij Kronecker, δ unit 2nd-order tensor.
3. **(A3) Cylindrical-segment drag coefficient:**  ζ_i = 3π μ r_{c,i} · (3 + 2 r_{0,i}/r_{c,i}) / 5.
   — μ = medium viscosity; r_{0,i} = segment length, r_{c,i} = segment diameter.
4. **(A4) Euler position update:**  r_i(t+Δt) = r_i(t) + (dr_i/dt)Δt = r_i(t) + (1/ζ_i)(F_i + F_i^T)Δt.
   — Explicit forward-Euler step.
5. **(A5) Actin extensional (stretch) potential:**  U_{s,A} = ½ κ_{s,A} (r − r_{0,A})².
   — Harmonic spring on actin segment length about rest length r_{0,A}.
6. **(A6) Motor extensional potential:**  U_{s,M} = ½ κ_{s,M} (r − r_{0,M})².
   — Harmonic spring on motor segment length about r_{0,M} (rest 13.5 nm).
7. **(A7) Actin bending potential:**  U_{b,A} = ½ κ_{b,A} (θ − θ_{0,A})².
   — Harmonic angular spring between adjacent actin segments; θ_{0,A} rest angle (straight).
8. **(A8) Excluded-volume (repulsive) potential:**  U_r = ½ κ_r (r₁₂ − r_c)²  if r₁₂ < r_c; else 0.
   — Soft harmonic repulsion between overlapping actin segments; r₁₂ = min inter-segment distance; cutoff r_c = actin diameter r_{c,A}.
9. **(1) Nematic order parameter (2D):**  S = ⟨2cos²θ⟩ − 1, θ ∈ [0°,180°] between neighbor F-actin pairs. S=0 isotropic, S=1 perfect (anti)parallel, S<0 orthogonal.
10. **(2) Polar order parameter:**  P = ⟨cos θ⟩, range [−1,1]. P=0 no alignment, P=1 perfect parallel.
11. **(3) Velocity correlation:**  ⟨cos α⟩ = ⟨ (v_i·v_j)/(|v_i||v_j|) ⟩ over endpoint pairs within 1 µm. +1 same direction, −1 opposite, 0 uncorrelated.
12. **Motor diffusion relation:**  D_M = k_B T / ζ_M; relative mobility D̄_M = D_M / D*_M.

## Force Laws & Potentials
- **Extensional (actin & motor):** linear Hookean springs, F = −κ_s(r − r₀) from U_{s} (A5, A6).
- **Bending (actin):** angular Hookean, torque = −κ_{b,A}(θ − θ_0) from U_{b,A} (A7) — encodes semiflexible persistence length.
- **Excluded volume (actin–actin):** one-sided harmonic repulsion, active only when segments approach within r_c = actin diameter (A8). No motor–motor repulsion (limitation).
- **Motor propulsion:** emergent from PCM walking kinetics — motor heads bind sites (every 7 nm, rate 40·N_H s⁻¹), walk toward barbed end, exert extensional force on bound actin via the motor spring (A6); reaction force pushes mobile motors back. No explicit constant "propelling force" (this is what distinguishes explicit from implicit-motor models).
- **Thermal (both):** stochastic FDT-consistent noise (A2).

## Relevance flags
- **DCM?** No — this is a quasi-2D motility-assay / active-matter model of gliding F-actin on a surface, not a 3D deformable-cell / cortex-tension model. No membrane, turgor, or cell shape. Not directly usable by the DCM engine.
- **FF?** Yes (moderate/high, mechanism-and-numerics relevance) — the FF engine (Cytosim-style fine-grained) uses the *same primitives*: filaments as bending beams (harmonic stretch A5 + bending A7), overdamped Langevin/Brownian dynamics (A1–A4), excluded-volume soft repulsion (A8), and explicit myosin motors with binding-site kinetics. This paper gives a concrete, cited **Parallel Cluster Model** motor treatment (Erdmann–Schwarz [33,34]) as an alternative/benchmark to Hill force–velocity, plus explicit numeric anchors: motor binding rate 40·N_H s⁻¹, 7 nm binding-site spacing, N_H=4 lumping, cylindrical-segment drag ζ = 3πμr_c(3+2r_0/r_c)/5, and the emergent polar-ordering / +1/2-defect trapping physics. Useful as a cross-check oracle for FF collective-behavior tests, motor kinetics, and drag formulas — though it is surface-assay, not cortex.
- **TAG-validation?** Yes — carries clean quantitative anchors worth registering as KnowledgeClaims/validation targets: motor D*_M=5.11e-14 m²/s, timestep 1.15e-5 s, binding rate/site-spacing, actin density thresholds (≈903 monomers/µm² for collective onset), length crossover (clusters ≤2.58 µm vs rings 7.43 µm), and the drag-coefficient closed form. Same Kim-lab model lineage as refs [29–31] already in-project; good corroborating source for FF motor/filament parameterization. Note exact spring/bending stiffnesses are in the SI (not this PDF).
