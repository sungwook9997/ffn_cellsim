## Summary
The paper asks how the well-studied in-vitro **myosin motility assay** (F-actins gliding on a surface of immobilized myosin heads) produces its rich collective/contractile phenomenology from molecular interactions, and builds the first rigorous multi-filament, explicit-motor computational model of it. Using the Kim-lab **agent-based Brownian-dynamics** framework (cylindrical-segment F-actin governed by Langevin dynamics; motors fixed in space with a **parallel-cluster-model (PCM)** mechanochemical cycle; ACP cross-linkers unbinding via **Bell's law**), the authors run three campaigns. (1) **Base study, no volume-exclusion, no ACPs:** gliding speed depends on filament length ⟨L_f⟩, motor density R_M, and the ATP-dependent myosin-head unbinding rate k₂₀. F-actins glide near the unloaded motor speed (~0.14 µm s⁻¹) only when they are long enough relative to the mean motor spacing; gliding speed is **biphasic in k₂₀** (rises with walking rate, then falls when detachment outpaces walking). (2) **Volume-exclusion on (no ACPs):** purely steric repulsion between F-actins generates **collective motion, thick bundles, and ring-like structures** whose morphology depends on actin concentration C_A and repulsive strength κ_{r,A}; weak repulsion → large thick bundles (small-angle alignment), strong repulsion → many small rings (no crossover). Networks reach a "frozen" steady state at the network level while remaining dynamic at filament level. (3) **ACPs on (no volume-exclusion):** density R_ACP and zero-force unbinding rate k⁰_{u,ACP} of cross-linkers control contractility; **maximal heterogeneity/contraction at intermediate R_ACP and intermediate k⁰_{u,ACP}** (a percolation-threshold-gated balance between connectivity and deformability), consistent with the classic actomyosin "intermediate-crosslink" contractility optimum.

## Model & Methods
- **Model type:** Agent-based / coarse-grained **Brownian dynamics** with the Langevin equation (the established Kim-lab framework, refs 19–22). F-actin, motor, and ACP are all represented by **cylindrical segments**. This is a fine-grained, explicit-agent model (every filament segment, motor arm, cross-linker is a discrete object) — directly FF-family physics.
- **Integrator / dynamics:** Overdamped Langevin (Brownian dynamics) update of the positions of cylindrical segments representing F-actin and motors; motor anchor points are fixed in space (mimicking heads immobilized on the assay surface). Extensional (stretch) and bending harmonic forces maintain equilibrium segment lengths and inter-segment angles. Timestep, drag coefficients, and thermal-noise amplitudes are inherited from the previous Kim-lab models and tabulated in ESI Tables S1/S2 (not in this PDF).
- **Boundary conditions / geometry:** Thin quasi-2D slab domain **10 × 10 × 0.1 µm** with **periodic boundary conditions in x and y**. F-actins self-assemble from actin segments placed at the same z; motors placed at random (x,y) at a single z. The z-offset between F-actin plane and motor plane equals the **motor-arm equilibrium length (13.5 nm)**, so a motor can bind an F-actin only when their (x,y) coincide → captures the surface-tethered geometry of the assay.
- **Actin dynamics:** actin undergoes nucleation + polymerization to build F-actins of a prescribed average length ⟨L_f⟩.
- **Motor model ("one-arm motor"):** each motor arm represents the cooperative kinetics of **N_h = 8 myosin heads** (chosen to give slightly more processive motors + higher stall force than a single head). Binding rate = **40·N_h s⁻¹** to a single actin binding site. Walking rate k_{w,M} and unbinding rate k_{u,M} are produced by the **parallel cluster model (PCM)** of Erdmann & Schwarz (refs 24, 25): 3 mechanochemical states, 5 rates. One rate — the **ATP-dependent head unbinding rate k₂₀** — is the swept control variable. k_{w,M} and k_{u,M} scale ∝ k₂₀ and both **decrease under load** (catch-bond-like); **stall force ∝ 1/k₂₀**.
- **ACP (cross-linker) model:** ACPs bind F-actin at a constant on-rate and form functional cross-links between filament pairs; they **unbind force-dependently via Bell's law** (ref 23) with zero-force rate k⁰_{u,ACP}.
- **Volume-exclusion:** pairwise **repulsive harmonic potential** between overlapping actin segments (Eq. 6), strength κ_{r,A} (tunable; models crowding pushing filaments onto the motor surface).
- **Protocol:** while F-actins assemble, a fraction of motors bind without walking; once assembly completes, all motors begin walking toward barbed ends. Filament-endpoint velocities v_{i,A}(t) sampled every 2 s; runs ~100 s; morphological/force quantities time-averaged over last 50 s.
- **Compute:** XSEDE / Comet supercomputer (SDSC).

## Governing Equations
Transcribed from the rendered pages (Eqs 1–6 are the only numbered equations in the main text; the core force laws and rate laws reside in ESI).

**(Eq. 1) — Velocity autocorrelation (motion persistency):**
⟨cos φ(τ)⟩ = ⟨ (2/(T−τ)) · Σ_{t=0}^{(T−τ)/2} [ v_{i,A}(2t) · v_{i,A}(2t+τ) ] / ( |v_{i,A}(2t)| · |v_{i,A}(2t+τ)| ) ⟩
Meaning: time-lag τ autocorrelation of an endpoint's velocity direction; ≈1 if the filament keeps its heading, exponential decay for pure motor-propelled gliding. T = run duration.

**(Eq. 2) — Pairwise velocity-direction correlation (collective motion):**
⟨cos α(r,τ)⟩ = ⟨ [ v_{i,A}(t) · v_{j,A}(t) ] / ( |v_{i,A}(t)| · |v_{j,A}(t)| ) ⟩, for endpoint pairs with r ≤ |r_{i,A}(t) − r_{j,A}(t)| < r + Δr, i,j on different F-actins.
Meaning: alignment of velocities of endpoints on neighboring (different) filaments separated by distance r → measures collective/parallel gliding. Time-averaged over last 50 s → ⟨cos α(r)⟩.

**(Eq. 3) — Persistence length from bending stiffness:**
l_p = k_{b,A} · r_{0,A} / (k_B T)
Meaning: nominal persistence length of F-actin; k_{b,A} = bending stiffness (N·m), r_{0,A} = equilibrium actin-segment length, k_B T = thermal energy.

**(Eq. 4) — Tangent–tangent correlation (measured curvature):**
⟨cos θ⟩ = ⟨ u(s) · u(s + Δs) ⟩
Meaning: correlation of unit tangent vectors along the filament contour vs arclength separation Δs; faster decay ⇒ higher motor-induced curvature (effective persistence length can differ from Eq. 3 when motors bend the filament).

**(Eq. 5) — Network morphological heterogeneity Q_A:**
Q_A = (1/(2 N_G)) · [ Σ_{i=1}^{N_G} std( ρ_A^{i,j} |_{j=1..N_G} ) + Σ_{j=1}^{N_G} std( ρ_A^{i,j} |_{i=1..N_G} ) ]
Meaning: domain split into N_G × N_G grids; ρ_A^{i,j} = # actin segments in grid (i,j); Q_A = mean over rows/columns of the std of segment counts → higher Q_A = more heterogeneous (bundled/clustered). N_G = 20 chosen; time-averaged over last 50 s.

**(Eq. 6) — Volume-exclusion repulsive harmonic potential:**
U_r = ½ κ_{r,A} (r₁₂ − r_{c,A})²   if r₁₂ < r_{c,A};   0   if r₁₂ ≥ r_{c,A}
Meaning: soft harmonic repulsion between two actin segments; κ_{r,A} = repulsive strength, r₁₂ = minimum distance between two segments, r_{c,A} = actin-segment diameter (cutoff).

**(Not numbered) — Bell's law for ACP unbinding** (ref 23; functional form in ESI): k_{u,ACP} = k⁰_{u,ACP} · exp(F·λ / k_B T), force-dependent off-rate with zero-force constant k⁰_{u,ACP}. (Stated in text as "unbind … in a force-dependent manner following Bell's law.")

**(Not numbered) — Extensional & bending harmonic potentials** for F-actin (segment stretch + inter-segment bending) — standard Kim-lab forms; constants in ESI Table S1 (k_{s,A} stretch, k_{b,A} bend).

**(Not numbered) — PCM mechanochemistry** (refs 24/25): 3 states, 5 rates; k_{w,M}, k_{u,M} ∝ k₂₀ and load-dependent; stall force ∝ 1/k₂₀.

## Force Laws & Potentials
- **F-actin stretching:** harmonic, equilibrium segment length r_{0,A}, stiffness k_{s,A} (ESI).
- **F-actin bending:** harmonic in inter-segment angle, stiffness k_{b,A}; sets nominal l_p via Eq. 3.
- **Volume exclusion (actin–actin):** harmonic repulsion Eq. 6, strength κ_{r,A}, cutoff r_{c,A} (segment diameter).
- **Motor force generation:** PCM cross-bridge cycle; walking + unbinding rates load-dependent (catch-bond-like), stall force ∝ 1/k₂₀; unloaded walking speed 140 nm s⁻¹ at reference k₂₀ = 20 s⁻¹.
- **ACP cross-link:** elastic cross-link (spring) between two F-actins with Bell's-law force-dependent unbinding; zero-force off-rate k⁰_{u,ACP}.
- **Thermal force:** Langevin random force (fluctuation–dissipation, ESI).

## Relevance flags
- **DCM? — No (low).** This is filament-level actomyosin in a quasi-2D assay slab, not a surface-mesh continuum cell. No membrane/cortex-tension/turgor/nucleus content. Only tangential relevance: contractile-network intuition (intermediate cross-link optimum) that DCM abstracts as cortical tension.
- **FF? — YES (high, core).** This IS the FF engine's physics family: explicit cylindrical-segment F-actins with stretch+bend potentials, myosin motors with load-dependent walking/unbinding (Kim-lab PCM ↔ FF's Hill/Bell motor stack), ACP cross-linkers with **Bell's-law force-dependent off-rates**, harmonic volume-exclusion (Eq 6 ↔ FF LJ/soft repulsive), Langevin/Brownian dynamics. Directly informs FF motor/crosslinker parameterization and the emergent bundle/ring/contractility phenomenology FF should reproduce.
- **TAG-validation? — YES.** Concrete validation targets: unloaded gliding speed 140 nm/s (non-muscle myosin II), biphasic speed-vs-k₂₀, ~3% ACP percolation threshold to arrest gliding at C_A=60 µM, intermediate-R_ACP/intermediate-k⁰ contractility optimum, bundle↔ring morphology map vs κ_{r,A}/C_A, buckling-limited tensile force. k⁰_{u,ACP}=0.115 s⁻¹ and PCM k₂₀ reference are citable rate constants.
