Flagship results

Emergent dynamics

DCM engine · deformable cells & spheroids

SimuCell3D physics on NVIDIA Warp: membrane + cortex + cytoplasm (turgor, viscosity) + nucleus, scaled to thousand-cell spheroids with aggregation, compaction, faceting, division and necrosis.

FF engine · explicit filament networks

Cytosim physics on NVIDIA Warp: individual actin filaments, myosin motors and cross-linkers woven into cortex, filopodium and lamellipodium — validated against Taeyoon-Kim fiber-network mechanics.

Foundation · validation-first Phase 0 / 1

Before any production run, every physics module passes literature-anchored sanity gates. ECM Mikado (H.1), single filament (H.2), actin cortex & cortical tension (H.3, KU-3.5), and the A/A₀ spreading law all cross-checked against oracles.
